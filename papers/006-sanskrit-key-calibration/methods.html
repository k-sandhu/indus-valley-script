Evidence guide for Paper 006

5 min read

Every number in the manuscript comes from a saved output in experiments/2026-10-12-published-claim-tests/. Paths in the output table are relative to that directory. The claim–evidence map traces every principal claim to its exact file and JSON key, and the submission checklist gives the ordered reproduction steps and human-review items. The study's report, protocol, amendments 1–4, adversarial review and response record the order in which analyses were designed and which were added after their results had been seen.
