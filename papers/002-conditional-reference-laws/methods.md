The exact examples use complete enumeration or integer/rational probabilities. Main calibration uses 99 references per population. Marginal rate intervals are Wilson intervals across the stated independent populations: 1,000 for the original main studies and agreement diagnostic, and 300 for the shared-quality nested comparison. Noisy and quality paired intervals resample their populations 20,000 times. Literal-copy paired intervals resample the 500 linked seed clusters 20,000 times under the [paired-stream amendment](../../experiments/2026-10-02-six-hour/null-calibration/PAIRED_UNCERTAINTY_AMENDMENT.md).

The main fitting studies and exact unique/guard calculations use unchanged nested method snapshots and grid 5/20/100/500/2,000. Copy-tail enumeration uses its declared integer adjacency statistic, and channel recovery uses a penalized observation likelihood. The shared-quality agreement diagnostic uses a new observed-sign integer statistic with fresh populations under its post-outcome protocol. Its exact reference tails are independently checked by hypergeometric convolution. The domain guard and selected-law reweighting are separately specified follow-ups. They reuse frozen scores where possible and retain the original failures.
