The exact examples use complete enumeration or integer and rational probabilities. Main calibration uses 99 reference corpora per population. Per-method rate intervals are Wilson intervals across the stated independent populations: 1,000 for the original main studies and the agreement diagnostic, and 300 for the shared-quality nested comparison. Paired intervals for the imperfect-face and shared-quality studies resample their populations 20,000 times. Literal-copy paired intervals resample the 500 linked seed clusters 20,000 times under the paired-stream amendment.

The main fitting studies and the exact unique-table and score-extension calculations use unchanged nested method code and the smoothing grid 5, 20, 100, 500 and 2,000. Copy-tail enumeration uses its declared integer statistic counting equal neighbouring signs, and channel recovery a penalized observation likelihood. The agreement diagnostic uses a new integer statistic on observed signs, with fresh populations under its post-outcome protocol; hypergeometric convolution independently checks its exact tails. The score extension and the deduplication reweighting are separately specified follow-ups that reuse saved scores where possible and keep the original failures. See the mathematics for the observation premises and the retrospective for the development record.
