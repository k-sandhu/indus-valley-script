# Quantitative chance calibration of Dravidian rebus interlocks in the Indus script

The [manuscript](manuscript.md) evaluates the evidential weight of Asko Parpola's interlocking Dravidian rebus readings for the Indus script against matched random-word baselines, cross-dictionary tests, unselected sign pairs, and a Sanskrit cross-language control. We measure single-homophone base rates across five language lexicons, test Parpola's 2010 five-link compound network in the Madras *Tamil Lexicon* and in Miron Winslow's 1862 dictionary, summarize phylogenetic and sound-correspondence checks on the foundational *mīn* "fish / star" pun, repair the Sangam frequency proxy and lookup fallback in the 38-pair blind-template test, and score standard Sanskrit nouns for the exact same 16 concrete pictures on those 38 pairs in Cologne Monier-Williams.

The work synthesizes six dated packages in `experiments/`: [REBUS-BASE-RATE-2026-10-21](../../experiments/2026-10-21-rebus-base-rate/REPORT.md), [DRAVIDIAN-ROOT-MEANINGS-2026-10-21](../../experiments/2026-10-21-dravidian-root-meanings/REPORT.md), [NORTH-B-CORRESPONDENCE-2026-10-21](../../experiments/2026-10-21-north-b-correspondence/REPORT.md), [REBUS-BLIND-TEMPLATES-2026-10-22](../../experiments/2026-10-22-rebus-blind-templates/REPORT.md), [REBUS-WINSLOW-2026-10-22](../../experiments/2026-10-22-rebus-winslow/REPORT.md), [READING-EVAL-READINESS-2026-10-22](../../experiments/2026-10-22-reading-eval-readiness/REPORT.md), [LEXICAL-EXTRACTION-EXTENSION-2026-10-24](../../experiments/2026-10-24-lexical-extraction-extension/REPORT.md), and [REBUS-REPAIRED-2026-11-22](../../experiments/2026-11-22-rebus-repaired/REPORT.md). Detailed procedures are documented in [methods.md](methods.md), and every quantitative claim is traced to its saved output file in [claim-evidence-map.md](claim-evidence-map.md).

Prepared with AI research and writing assistance; author names, affiliations, and a submission venue have not been assigned. This working paper calibrates the lexical and statistical support for published rebus interlocks and assigns no authenticated sound value, meaning, or language to the Indus script.
