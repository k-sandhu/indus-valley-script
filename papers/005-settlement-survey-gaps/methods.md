# Methods and evidence guide

The paper uses a structured narrative review, an audit of published aggregate counts, and proposed investigations. Searches followed current archaeological survey and excavation studies, environmental records, institutional inventories and statistical-method papers. The [final search record](../../experiments/2026-10-11-settlement-prospection/SEARCH-AND-ACCESS-FINAL.md) describes scope and actual access. A complete search-hit denominator and duplicate-screening count were not retained.

The final [source register](../../experiments/2026-10-11-settlement-prospection/sources-v6.json) has 72 records and 50 local witnesses. Each observation has an R-number, locator, reading depth and canonical pointer. Local files carry hashes and remain outside the reusable package. Some observations were read in publisher or institutional extracts; the manuscript identifies those limits. The 2026 field-participant Mohenjo-daro account traces an original preliminary report that was not obtained, so its precise foundation-date claims are not adopted. The historical 1,022-site teaching source cites Possehl (2002) but does not identify the exact work in the acquired excerpt.

The counting units are a locality, physical mound or scatter, phase-specific occupied footprint, and investigated deposit. Calendar BCE, calibrated calendar BP, conventional radiocarbon BP and OSL ages retain their different meanings. Historical excavation counts and descriptive remote proposals are not combined into a present excavation percentage.

Published-count arithmetic, the reconstruction of Green's Table 1 and local repeat-survey comparisons are in [run-v3](../../experiments/2026-10-11-settlement-prospection/run-v3/manifest.json). The source-register input for that run remains the frozen v2. A [comparison](../../experiments/2026-10-11-settlement-prospection/review/final-register-numeric-comparison.json) checks the source metadata used by the run; final package verification extends it through v6. Numerical constants are curated in the preserved script rather than extracted from changing prose. Seven separately recorded [inline calculations](../../experiments/2026-10-11-settlement-prospection/run-v3-inline-arithmetic/arithmetic.json) cover feet, hectares and calendar orientation. The [claim map](../../experiments/2026-10-11-settlement-prospection/claims-v3.json) joins assertions to observations and exact output paths.

The [region register](../../experiments/2026-10-11-settlement-prospection/candidates-v5.json) gives 21 overlapping editorial rectangles, evidence classes, units, comparisons and demotion rules. It has no predicted site count, site area or artifact total. Four published points have containment checks. Source footprints and mapped landforms must define an actual sampling frame. Any eligible cell with several labels is selected once in a shared frame. Known-context calibration units and P02 margin transects cannot enter negative comparison draws.

Information, preservation-evidence and feasibility levels are separate editorial judgments by the AI-assisted drafting process. P2 documents archaeological survival somewhere in a region, including other periods; it predicts neither an untested parcel's survival nor Indus affiliation. All field feasibility is F0, unverified. There is no combined priority score or expert-rating exercise.

The [buried-surface pilot](../../experiments/2026-10-11-settlement-prospection/PILOT-DESIGN-v4.md) proposes 24 blocks across four landform strata, with profiles nested inside blocks. Adequacy requires spatial coverage, depth and chronological resolution. Descriptive survival reporting uses all adequately resolved blocks. Eight blocks are held aside only to evaluate a prediction rule locked before their labels are released. The workload has no power calculation, fitted confidence interval or field authorization. A stratum with no adequate blocks remains unresolved.

The first complete draft and all review endpoints remain preserved. Claude used supplied authored packets with tools disabled. The final verification and separate PDF inspection record cover the released files.
