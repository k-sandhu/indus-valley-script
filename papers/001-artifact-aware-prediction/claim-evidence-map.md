## Interpretation and review

The measured targets are fine-code predictive likelihood, compatible candidate-event likelihood, exact accuracy for one sign hidden at a known slot, and accuracy in recovering the number of signs in artificial gaps. Each denominator, weighting rule and target alphabet stays attached to its result. Bootstrap intervals are conditional on fitted predictions, and permutation probabilities refer to the stated shuffling rule. Archaeological source quality, alias completeness and generalization to new populations need separate review.

The paper makes measurement claims about this dataset. Related work addresses publication priority, once, for Markov restoration, duplicate analysis, shared-context methods and template and control tests. Before claiming that one predictor ranks above another, a submission should compare older and newer predictors under a shared protocol.

The companion manual numeric review reads frozen results without refitting. Its [saved review](numeric-review.json) records source hashes and checked values; [the review script](verify_numeric_claims.py) repeats the check in a fresh checkout with the required files available.
