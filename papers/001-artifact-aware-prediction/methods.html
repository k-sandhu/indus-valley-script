Models and normalization

Training vocabulary V comprises the observed training codes plus UNKNOWN. Smoothed unigram U adds 0.5 to every state. For count vector C, total n and prior Q,

Smooth(C,Q;α)(c) = [C(c)+αQ(c)]/[n+α].

The independent models are U, P(c|L,i)=Smooth(C[L,i],U;α), and PM(c|L,i,s)=Smooth(C[L,i,s],P;α), where s is site/type. Let B(c|a)=Smooth(C[a→·],U;α) and R(c|a)=B(c|a)/U(c). The dependent distribution is

PMB(c|L,i,s,a)=PM(c|L,i,s)R(c|a)/Σb PM(b|L,i,s)R(b|a).

At the first position PMB equals PM. The denominator depends on the predecessor and target slot; restoration retains it. The baseline and dependent models can have different selected α values. Unseen test codes map to UNKNOWN for likelihood scoring; exact glyph accuracy uses the original code.

Nested selection and uncertainty

Five outer and three inner component-held-out folds use seed 20261001. Independent candidates include U and P/PM at α∈{5,20,100,500,2000}; dependent candidates are PMB at those values. Selection maximizes inner noninitial held-out log likelihood. Each outer fold owns its training vocabulary and fitted choices.

The principal contrast is

G=Σrecords Σi≥1[log2 qdependent(xi|history)−log2 qbaseline(xi|metadata)]/Nnoninitial.

The main fit selects PM@500 and PMB@100 in all five outer folds, with 8,199 noninitial tokens. Unique weighting has 6,413 noninitial tokens. Five thousand component-bootstrap draws resample already fitted held-out component scores, sum numerator and denominator, then take their ratio. These intervals condition on the corpus and fitted models; they do not refit selection or propagate all transcription uncertainty.

The main endpoint is nested_primary/nested_results.json. The corrected unique endpoint is nested_unique_preserved_aliases/nested_results.json. The earlier fixed-20 pilot remains archived because its metadata baseline was insufficiently regularized.

Full-procedure permutation references

Independently permute signs within each exact (length,slot,site/type) cell, preserving its multiset and row metadata. Record cell counts and token mobility because singleton cells cannot change. For each null corpus, nested_joint_null.py rebuilds data-dependent groups, reallocates folds, retunes smoothing and computes held-out scores. Earlier fixed-group/fold references are retained conditional diagnostics.

The main metadata reference has 1,000 corpora; its extension preserves the original 100 outcomes and seed prefix. Its position-only reference remains at 100. The completion audit verifies counts, unchanged observed values and preserved outcomes.

For unique weighting, nested_unique_conditioned.py rejects proposed column shuffles that create any repeated whole string. It samples the original reference conditional on complete-string uniqueness. The completed post-pilot run accepts 1,000 corpora from 6,569 proposals, preserving every accepted seed. It reruns the nested fit on each accepted corpus. Its audit verifies source/input hashes and seed agreement with the independently audited rejection sampler.

The one-sided probability is (1+exceedances)/(1+accepted trials). Zero exceedances in 1,000 gives 0.000999000999. Negative fitted-null gains can arise from dependent estimation and regularization. Significance is evaluated against the fitted reference distribution under the stated exchangeability law.

Independent annotation and uncertain events

independent_annotator.py excludes all training components touched by CISI M1–M199 source artifacts or by an eligible test sequence compatible with a fine-code complete string. It removes 170 training strings, leaving 1,426 strings and 1,362 components. The test has 86 eligible faces, five unmapped and seven without a supported candidate in at least one event, leaving 74 faces, 73 artifact/P-text groups and 315 noninitial events.

For candidate sets Ai, the forward state is initially f1(c)=PM(c|L,0,s)1[c∈A1], then

fi(c)=1[c∈Ai]Σa fi−1(a)PMB(c|L,i,s,a).

The final sum gives the specified whole-event probability. Intermediate normalization with accumulated log scale prevents underflow. Singleton sets recover ordinary fine-code scoring; short-sequence brute force checks the recursion. Candidate sets can overlap across P labels, and each is scored on the fine-code alphabet. Unsupported fine candidates receive zero event probability. Twenty scored faces contain some unsupported alternatives, so a 54-face fully supported sensitivity is retained. Main metadata assumes Mohenjo-daro/SEAL:S; subtype-unspecified scoring is separate. The 10,000-draw group bootstrap conditions on the fitted training model. See the complete event output.

Known-slot restoration

restoration.py masks each of the 8,009 slots across 1,596 unique strings in 1,526 components. The fixed-length local posterior multiplies the candidate's predecessor transition by the next survivor's candidate-dependent transition, removing absent factors at the boundaries. Only factors common to every candidate cancel. Three inner grouped folds choose α separately by exact top-one accuracy; both families select 20 in all outer folds. Stable ranking resolves ties. OOV fine targets remain exact failures even if an UNKNOWN bucket receives mass.

Report slot-weighted top-one and top-k inclusion, paired component-bootstrap accuracy differences, equal-text means and frequency strata. Calibration compares maximum posterior mass with observed exact correctness. benchmark.json contains complete summaries; cross-site tests remove whole components using the raw site populations.

October 2 conventional comparator

The new protocol specifies a position-independent first-order model with learned start probabilities and an end state E. Its state vocabulary matches each existing outer training fold. For internal masks between a and b, score candidate c by A(a,c)A(c,b); use π(c)A(c,b) initially and A(a,c)A(c,E) finally. Normalize over candidate glyph states, excluding E. Outgoing rows shrink toward a training destination unigram; start counts shrink toward the training glyph unigram.

The 20261006 grouping/fold construction reproduces all 8,009 saved identities, targets, groups and folds. Strength is selected from 20/100/500/2000 by three grouped inner folds maximizing exact top-one; ties favor greater strength. The PM/PMB predictions are reused. Ten thousand paired component draws with seed 142 produce conditional contrast intervals. Confidence bins are fixed at width .2. Macro-code weights use inverse true-code frequency in the complete benchmark and remain fixed during component resampling. Results record these choices, and an independent counter verification checks every ranking and target loss. The smoothing rule differs from Rao et al.'s modified Kneser–Ney rule.

Unknown gap counts and observation law

gap_restoration.py draws one internal gap per intact unique string: k∼Uniform{1,…,min(3,L−2)}, then a valid internal start uniformly. The predictor observes both surviving sides and compares candidate counts 1–3. It sums hidden fine-state paths using a dynamic program, a training-derived full-length prior and frozen α=20.

For survivor count m, candidate length is L=m+k. The artificial observation law contributes 1/[min(3,L−2)(L−k−1)]. Since L−k−1=m−1, cancellation leaves the clipping term. gap_design_matched.py verifies every query and source hash, then reweights unchanged saved posteriors by 1/min(3,m+k−2). The historical endpoint remains preserved.

Sampled counts are 815 ones, 454 twos and 327 threes, yielding the constant-one control 815/1596=51.0652%. Query seed is 20261012, fold seed 20261006, and the matched-law 10,000-draw component bootstrap seed is 142. The original and reweighted results use the same queries and fitted models.

Templates, controls and frame transfer

template_comparison.py selects finite independent-slot mixtures from 16,32,64 components with minimum component size five. The known nonphonetic routing control tests whether residual dependence beyond that fitted family is specific to language. Source-published controls preserve author order and simultaneous composite units. Totem OOV coverage and weather's one-component closure prevent treating them as matched language classifiers.

Exact substitution frames have one wildcard and identical remaining length/slot/context. Training components supply discoveries; held-out transfer requires a new frame. Support counts distinct frames, with shared mapped graphic classes separated from disjoint classes. substitution_joint_null.py repeats discovery and transfer on every shuffled corpus. Aggregate counts and pair-level maximum statistics have separate reference distributions. Frequency-adjusted maxima are post-pilot sensitivities.

Extra shrinkage of two-state histories

The adaptive follow-up reuses the same masks and component folds. First-order strength remains twenty. For a training history count N>0, multiply the second-order weight by N/(N+κ), yielding effective strength α+κ+ακ/N. Zero histories use first order. The grid contains seventeen distinct finite factors from α=5/20/100/500/2000 and κ=0/20/100/500, plus exact first-order backoff. Three inner component folds minimize unknown-bucket loss. Complete masked probabilities include beginning/end events and all later factors involving the candidate.

Every outer fold selects backoff. The endpoint preserves all ninety candidate/fold losses and paired component intervals. Independent flat-count review reconstructs every query, verifies rational effective-strength identities and checks full-chain probabilities. Detailed source-linked predictions remain local. This protocol follows earlier outcomes and supplies a comparison on reused development records.
