Choosing record models before symbolic mapping: reference-conditioned recovery controls

Complete private working manuscript, 2 October 2026. Authorship, affiliations and venue are pending. Computational work and writing used AI assistance. Protocols, generated worlds, source snapshots, independent reviews and all outcomes are preserved in the private repository.

Abstract

Recovering a bijection between two symbol inventories depends on the distinctions retained by the reference model. We compare graph mapping, length/slot assignment and latent-form mixtures on generated short records. In a first grid of 48 new systems, slot assignment recovers 55 of 96 twenty-symbol administrative maps, compared with graph mapping's 25; graph mapping recovers 74 transition maps, compared with zero for slot profiles. A second fresh grid chooses between models by three-fold reference prediction before mapping truth is opened. Both likelihoods condition on observed length through normalized record probabilities. Selection recovers 309 of 384 maps, compared with 310 under a supplied-family diagnostic. A further matched grid makes administrative forms the same length and adds shared symbol mass. A post-outcome mixture fitted on those frozen records receives three components but no record labels. At reference size 400 it recovers 24 of 24 maps at both zero and .35 shared mass, compared with 22 under the preceding selector. Coincident-component smoothing controls recover 21 and 22. At .70 mass, reference validation prefers ordinary slot prediction and recovery stays 16 of 24. Independent counts, conditional likelihoods, exact small-inventory optima, assignment duals and EM bounds accompany the results. Six failed mixture maps score below truth, identifying search misses; 56 score above it. The study supplies recovery controls for choosing a record model under an explicit external reference, inventory correspondence and finite fitting budget.

1. A mapping search needs a record model

A frequency profile, a transition graph and a position-specific form preserve different information. They can each be transformed by a symbol bijection and compared with a reference population. A search succeeds when the retained information distinguishes the true assignment and the optimizer finds it. Those are separate empirical questions.

The project earlier enumerated eight-symbol maps under reference uncertainty, erroneous supplied identities and changes in record mixture. A subsequent graph-mapping experiment scaled the search to twenty symbols and checked eight-symbol optima exhaustively. It exposed both optimization gaps and training objectives that preferred wrong identities. Reference experiment, graph search.

This study follows one observed difficulty: twenty-symbol administrative mappings often had high average symbol accuracy but low complete recovery under the pooled transition objective. The generated records had typed slots and forms with distinct lengths. We therefore specified a model retaining length and slot, then tested whether the reference population could choose between that model and a transition model. Both stages use fresh generated systems after their respective protocols.

Linear assignment, graph matching and held-out model selection are established methods. The graph search uses the FAQ and 2-opt implementations; the assignment uses SciPy's modified Jonker–Volgenant solver. Neural decipherment work also uses matching or flow constraints with known-system evaluation. These precedents provide algorithms and evaluation principles. The present contribution is a matched recovery comparison, a reference-only selection procedure and preserved optimization/identity failures under declared short-record mechanisms. FAQ primary article, assignment implementation, Luo, Cao and Barzilay 2019.

2. Data and methods

2.1 Known systems and withheld identities

Each grid has twelve systems in each combination of two generator families and inventories of eight or twenty symbols. A transition family draws a start distribution and transition rows from Dirichlet distributions, then generates records of length three through eight. An administrative family has four disjoint symbol pools and three forms of lengths four, five and six. Each form draws independent symbols from its typed slot distributions. Form probabilities vary across generated systems.

A random bijection encodes the training and test identities. The reference remains in its original category names. Reference sizes are 40 and 400 records; encoded training sizes are 160 and 640. Each system contributes a baseline process and a .35 change. For transition records, the change mixes a shifted start distribution and a shifted successor distribution into each row. For administrative records, it changes the mixture of forms while retaining their slot distributions. Four hundred independently generated encoded records supply the test set for each change condition.

The two sample sizes and two changes yield eight conditions per system, or 384 per grid. Systems are the resampling units; repeated conditions remain together. The first grid uses seed 810261002 with declared inventory/family/replicate offsets. The selector grid uses fresh seed 910261002 and the same offset design. Generated records and hidden maps are retained; source snapshots, seeds and library versions reconstruct the generator parameters. First protocol, selector protocol.

2.2 Two mapping objectives

For length/slot mapping, estimate each reference cell distribution with one pseudocount per symbol. An absent cell is uniform. Let C[L,j,c] count encoded training code c at slot j in records of length L. The assignment weight for mapping code c to reference symbol r is W[c,r]=Σ C[L,j,c] log₂ q(r|L,j). Maximizing the sum of selected weights is a linear assignment. The decoder receives observed lengths, slots and the common inventory size, while hidden role pools and true identities remain in evaluation.

The graph objective sums encoded transition counts multiplied by reference log probabilities after a bijection. Fixed start/end matches include boundary likelihoods. Each glyph row has one pseudocount for each of the k glyph destinations and END; the start row has one per glyph. The comparator retains two deterministic FAQ initializations, ten randomized starts and 2-opt refinements, then chooses the largest training score across all refined and unrefined candidates. Solver seeds are declared by condition. The slot and graph decoders use the same records and inventory.

In the first grid, enumeration of all 40,320 eight-symbol bijections verifies the linear maximum. At every inventory size, a separate continuous assignment relaxation provides a dual bound and inequalities checked against the selected score. The independent reviewer forbids each selected code match in turn to find tied alternative assignments. Numerical equality uses a declared 10⁻⁸ score tolerance; the dual checks use 10⁻⁷. The selector grid has separate dual programs and exact prime-factor certificates for retained alternative maps.

2.3 Select the model from reference prediction

The second grid assigns known-reference records to three random folds using seed system seed + 50. Each model fits on two folds and scores the third. The selector receives only reference records, inventory size and that seed. It receives no generator family, encoded test records, mapping answer or hidden probabilities.

Both scores are probabilities for an entire record conditional on its observed length. The slot model multiplies its cell probabilities. The first-order model multiplies start, interior transitions and end, then divides by its probability of the specified length. If π is its start row, A its glyph-to-glyph transition matrix and e its end-probability column, that normalizer is π A^(L−1) e. It puts both models on the same conditional sequence space. A tiny fully enumerated sequence space checks that each conditional distribution sums to one.

Choose the model with the larger pooled held-out reference log score; ties choose graph. Then fit both mapping methods on the full reference and encoded training sample, and retain the chosen map. Mapping accuracy is opened only in evaluation. A supplied-family diagnostic selects slot for administrative records and graph for transition records. That diagnostic uses the known generator family; it measures the cost of reference-only selection in this design.

2.4 Outcomes and uncertainty

Report complete identity recovery, the fraction of symbols correctly mapped and optimal-score ties. A correct returned map is scored as point recovery; tied compatible maps are reported separately. In the selector grid, each test record is scored under both conditional reference models using the same selected map and a common token denominator. Their conditional whole-record likelihoods can be compared. The first grid retains unconditional graph loss per edge and conditional slot loss per token; those scores and denominators remain separate.

Twenty thousand bootstrap draws resample the twelve systems within a family/inventory, after averaging the eight repeated conditions within each system. Intervals describe the generated design and chosen fitting procedure. Model-development decisions followed preceding outcomes, while each new grid had fresh systems and a pre-generation protocol.

2.5 Same-length and overlapping-form stress test

A third fresh grid generates twelve matched twenty-symbol systems. It compares the separated-length design with three six-slot forms at shared masses 0, .35 and .70. Equalizing length adds two slots to the first form and one to the second. Their new slots draw declared role-pool distributions. In the six-slot variants, each cell distribution is (1−alpha)p + alpha*b, with one shared twenty-symbol background b per system. The shared-mass comparison therefore holds record length fixed. Each variant has reference sizes 40 and 400, encoded training size 640 and mixture changes 0 and .35, giving 192 conditions. Common form and slot uniforms couple variants within each system.

The graph, slot and reference-selection rules remain fixed. A separate stronger-input diagnostic supplies observed form labels on both reference and encoded records and conditions its slot profiles on those labels. That comparison holds the symbolic records fixed. Twenty thousand paired bootstrap draws retain each system's variants and repeated conditions. Linear objectives have independent dual bounds and exact tied-map certificates; the twenty-symbol graph search has no global optimum certificate. Stress protocol, independent review.

2.6 Unlabelled form mixtures

The stress result motivated a three-component mixture fitted from the known reference: p(x)=Σ_z π[z]∏_j θ[z,j,x[j]]. Each component has six independent categorical slot profiles. The component count is supplied as three; fitting reads reference symbols without their generated form labels or role pools. EM is an established latent-inference method, including in noisy-channel decipherment. Dempster, Laird and Rubin 1977, Knight et al. 2006.

MAP-EM adds one pseudocount per mixture component and per categorical cell, corresponding to Dirichlet(2) priors. Eight fixed starts have at most 150 iterations. The retained start maximizes the saved penalized objective. Of 2,304 individual starts across 288 reference fits, 178 reach the iteration limit. Raw likelihood and penalized trajectories are both saved. For encoded records, the reference mixture stays fixed. Each current map gives soft form responsibilities, whose expected counts produce a linear assignment update. The implementation checks actual likelihood ascent and retains the best initial or intermediate map across the declared starts, with at most 25 updates per start. Mixture protocol and equations.

This is a post-outcome comparison on the frozen common-six-slot worlds: twelve systems, three shared-mass levels, two reference sizes and two mixture conditions, for 144 conditions. Three-fold reference prediction adds mixture to the earlier slot/graph selector. Encoded test records and true mappings remain outside selection. A subsequent separately specified control uses the coincident-component profile (count+3)/(N+60) to match the mixture's collapsed smoothing. Here count is a symbol's reference frequency at one slot, and N is the number of reference records. Its four-model selector adds that profile to the three-model comparison. Paired intervals resample twelve systems, retaining repeated conditions, with 20,000 draws. Mixture methods, collapsed-profile protocol.

2.7 Identification at the stored population parameters

A separate post-outcome calculation fixes the grouping (0,1), (2,3), (4,5) before rank checks. Each pair is a 400-state variable whose conditional distribution has three form rows. The six-slot product generator makes the pairs conditionally independent given form. Allman, Matias and Rhodes's Corollary 2 identifies the three weights and pair distributions up to a common form-label permutation when the weights are positive and the three row Kruskal ranks sum to at least eight. Marginalizing each pair recovers its two original slot distributions. Primary theorem, fixed-group protocol and proof.

The calculation uses exact rational normalization of stored binary64 probabilities and, separately, probabilities from rounded cumulative thresholds under continuous independent uniforms. Saved nonzero minors certify rank; singular values are diagnostics. Both conventions define population laws for a fixed three-component product model. A separate shared-mass-one control makes every component identical. Protocol.

3.5 Exact form parameters remain identifiable in the overlap controls

All 36 profiles, twelve systems at each of zero, .35 and .70 shared mass, have pair ranks three/three/three under both conventions. Their sum nine exceeds the required eight. Unshifted and rotated mixture weights remain positive. Exact population probabilities therefore determine the form weights and six slot distributions within the specified model, up to a common form permutation. The alpha-one controls have identical pair rows and rank one. Distinct weight vectors (.2,.3,.5) and (.3,.3,.4) give the same collapsed population law. Exact witnesses and controls.

The independent audit reconstructs 345,600 rational pair entries, checks 34,560 slot marginals and 144 mixing vectors, and certifies 288 minors by elimination. It retains the first audit's failed support assertion: a rounded zero-mass cumulative sum can leave a tiny positive interval for the final code, increasing support from five to six. The corrected review leaves the main ranks and witnesses unchanged. The result identifies the population-model parameters; finite reference fitting and cross-alphabet mapping remain the empirical tasks evaluated above. Independent audit and repair, repair record.

4. What this supplies for an unfamiliar script

The study starts with an external reference whose symbols and record process are known. The inventory correspondence is a bijection, record boundaries and length are observed, and generator changes have a declared form. Those premises define the current recovery problem. An Indus application would need an independently justified reference or functional observation, plus a model for graphic identity, segmentation and mixtures. The current experiment supplies a way to test those model choices on a known control before investing in an archaeological mapping.

The most useful observation can be the record's form or physical role. Here an observable length preserves an administrative form, and held-out reference prediction chooses the associated score. In other collections, a container class, face role, operational stage or measured quantity could provide the conditioning variable. Its source must be recorded separately from the symbolic mapping so its predictive value can be checked.

The same-length stress test and mixture follow-up make the conditioning field a measurable research target. Fitted latent forms improve several conditions under the supplied count of three. The next evaluation can vary form count, adapt encoded-population mixture weights and use fresh systems with uncertain boundaries. The six search misses also provide a fixed optimization diagnostic. An acquired reference or independently recorded object role can replace a declared generator premise in a new experiment.

5. Verification and availability

The first grid's independent implementation rebuilds every cell count and profile weight, checks all training/test likelihoods and answer-key comparisons, reenumerates eight-symbol optima, verifies dual inequalities and retains all code-exclusion alternatives. The selector review passes 6,489 checks: every fold and choice, all 1,536 returned-row likelihoods, 384 independent slot dual programs and exact conditional mass at lengths one through four. Its state-vector recursion independently reconstructs the graph length normalizer. Prime-factor cancellation certifies 32 alternative likelihood equalities across eleven tied slot conditions. The failed first pilot is retained: a NumPy boolean prevented JSON serialization after fitting, and a Python-boolean conversion allowed the deterministic pilot to complete into a new endpoint.

Code, generated inputs, hashes, protocols, outcomes and retrospectives are project artifacts in the private repository. Methods and claim files give exact paths. Primary literature provides the existing algorithm precedents. New source observations and future trials should retain the current endpoints and state the changed premise before execution.
