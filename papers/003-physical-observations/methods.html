Methods package

The manuscript combines physical acquisition simulations with controls on a known numerical system, the khipu. Each branch keeps its own design, answer keys and record of source values seen in advance.

Branch Design Current output Checks and research record
PHYS-01 Six positive labels, complete or incomplete physical membership, one count, and a finite diagnostic told the six values Corrected results Review, retrospective
PHYS-02 Public action descriptions, a budget, complete or missing group members, and exact, interval or inaccurate measurements Main, fresh interval refinement Arithmetic, classification, retrospective
KHI-01 Fixed published account alignment; separately generated concealed positional values Source aggregate, simulation aggregate Source check, simulation check, retrospective
KHI-03 Exact maximum consensus with unreliable membership, keeping positive solution families and ties 300-seed control Methods and audit paths, retrospective
KHI-04 Independent inventory of a complete or partial pool, two views of scale and fixed support gates 200-seed control Independent check, retrospective
KHI-05 Choice of validation checks designed after the outcomes, on fixed KHI-04 observations; sealed check rows, exact subset expectations and retained families Validation aggregate Main review, additional family review, retrospective

The corrected PHYS-01 run replaces the diagnostics that used faulty ownership counts; original equations, ranks and output remain. PHYS-02's interval rule is likewise a post-outcome refinement, tested on fresh worlds. KHI-01 records prior exposure to the published targets and keeps them from the predictor in code. Its generated answer keys are separate from the source replication.

Reproduction instructions and dependencies are in the branch READMEs and KHI-01 RUNS. Every completed run requires a fresh output directory. Most calculations used local Python 3.13 with NumPy and SciPy; the manifests of the result directories record actual versions. Exact arithmetic certificates form part of the output.

The source package keeps original access routes, local paths, source hashes, field coverage and rights observations. Raw acquired khipu data and detailed source vectors are excluded from Git. The redistribution record governs future packaging. The tracked, reproducible part is the generated controls and our own observations and code.

Before submission, we must supply authorship and affiliations, select a venue, resolve access and data-use requirements for the source package, and obtain independent review of the archaeological observation design. The computational paper can be reviewed from its declared controls. A claim about Indus sign values would require a suitable physical relation and a separate measurement to test it.
