# Publication screen of the 20 October packages, 21 October 2026

Before the first commit of the 20 October work we screened the 508 files it adds or changes with the sequence scanner of the [13 October screen](../2026-10-13-publication-screen/README.md), run unchanged through [run_scan.py](run_scan.py) against the local Lipi index, and with `tools/audit_git_payload.py` and `tools/review_prose.py`. The payload audit passed: no credential pattern and no file under a declared local-only path. The prose review raised only judgment flags on slash compounds and template paragraphs in protocols, which we left to their authors.

The sequence scan ([batch 1](sequence-scan-batch1.json)) found one file with a strict match: the seal-legend units report quoted one complete four-sign Lipi text, in the passage explaining how the rules cut a legend, and two four-code excerpts of catalogued legends with their CISI numbers. The 11 October rule allows complete texts of at most three signs. We replaced the four-sign text with a description of the case and shortened the two excerpts to three codes, appended a dated publication note to the report, and kept the original bytes under `data/raw/2026-10-21-publication-screen/originals/`. The [rescan](sequence-scan-batch1-after-edit.json) finds no strict match. The remaining matches are lists of separate closing formulae counted across groups and short complete texts of two or three codes, which the bounded-example rule allows; the matches in `civilisations.json` are calendar years read as codes.

Later batches of 21 October packages are screened the same way before their commits; their scan files are added here.
