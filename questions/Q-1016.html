Question

What do the Kalibangan final reports record for each inscribed object (registration, findspot, stratum, period, material, dimensions), and how do those records join to the 197 local Kalibangan objects and the 122 CISI 1 entries?

Why it matters

Kalibangan has full CISI coverage in the local export but weak context: 25.9% of objects have a depth and 36.0% a shape. The census found 26 CISI 1 Kalibangan entries with no local match. The final report gives excavators' stratigraphy for the Early and Mature phases, which the chronology fields need.

What is needed

Read DATA.md. Held: Excavations at Kalibangan: The Harappans (1960–69), Part II (ASI PDF, 876 pages) at data/raw/2026-10-06-evidence-census/sites-deep/c-north-india/kalibangan_report2020.pdf. Not held: The Early Harappans (1960–1969) (2003, MASI 98), which is scanned on the Internet Archive; Part I of The Harappans, known only from a PDF address in a search result and a bookseller listing; and the Kalibangan section of CISI 3.2 (Q-1009). Acquire the 2003 volume and, once confirmed, Part I; record each in the manifest with its hash; locate the seal, sealing and inscribed-object tables; and transcribe them with page locators. Keep transcriptions local.

How to decide

Done when the report's inscribed objects are tabulated with locators, each is joined to a local object and CISI number or listed as unmatched, and a coverage table reports gains in stratum, period and dimensions. Unresolved joins are listed with the evidence that blocks them.

Prior attempts

The north-India census batch identified the reports and read selected locations for counts; it did not extract an object table.
