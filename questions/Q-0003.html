Question

How do field availability and conflicts change when the export's face rows are grouped into provisional objects (faces the export numbers as one object, with physical identity unchecked)?

Why it matters

Metadata repeated on every face can overstate coverage, and placeholder strings once inflated the count of objects with a depth.

What is needed

List the fields that occur and their raw placeholder values; keep the states observed, catalogued, inferred, unknown, conflicting and not applicable apart. Report row and object denominators. Read DATA.md for the gaps this task depends on.

Sources: knowledge/directions/context-data-plan.md.

How to decide

Account for every row and provisional object in the fixed input. Count a field as present only under a declared rule; have the counts replayed independently and inspect every uncommon placeholder.

Prior attempts

CONTEXT-01; DATASET-LANDSCAPE-01. Read their outcomes and retry_when conditions before running this task.

Outcome

13 October 2026: the data inventory recounted field coverage per object and per face, by object type and site (summary.json); headline figures are in DATA.md section 2.
