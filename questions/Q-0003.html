Question

How do field availability and conflicts change when the held export’s face rows are collapsed to provisional object families?

Why it matters

Repeated face metadata can overstate coverage, and placeholder strings previously inflated depth availability.

What is needed

Inventory the actual fields and raw placeholder values; preserve observed, catalogued, inferred, unknown, conflicting and not-applicable states. Report row and object denominators. Read DATA.md for the gaps this task depends on.

Sources: knowledge/directions/context-data-plan.md.

How to decide

Account for every row and provisional family in the pinned input. A field is present only under a declared rule; independently replay the counts and inspect every uncommon placeholder.

Prior attempts

CONTEXT-01; DATASET-LANDSCAPE-01. Read their outcomes and retry_when before running this task.

Outcome

13 October 2026: the data inventory recounted field coverage per object and per face, by object type and site (summary.json); headline figures are in DATA.md section 2.
