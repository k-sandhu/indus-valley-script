Q-1020: Record the unit, precision and source of every dimension value

Question

For each local dimension value, what unit and precision was it recorded in, which source does it come from, and where do the local sources disagree?

Why it matters

The Lipi export, our local transcription corpus, has an inch field and a millimetre field for each size. The millimetre fields are mostly conversions: where both are present, 5,206 of 5,434 millimetre values equal the inch field times 25.4 within 0.6 mm, so their real precision is a tenth or a twentieth of an inch. Another 228 differ from the conversion, 452 inch values have no millimetre value, 530 thickness values are printed ranges such as 5 - 9, and zeros stand for missing values in 1,810 horizontal, 1,681 vertical and 3,586 thickness fields. Against the older export of the Indus website, 2,018 objects agree and 206 disagree. Size comparisons, such as the seal-dimension test in conjecture C-0004, need the precision and the source of each number.

What is needed

Read DATA.md. Inputs: data/normalized/lipi_observed_faces.jsonl, data/normalized/indus_website_observed.jsonl, and the coverage outputs cited above. Classify each value as converted-from-inch, metric, range or placeholder; list the 228 conversion mismatches and the 206 disagreements between the two exports with both values; check 30 of them against the CISI basic data or the excavation tables once Q-1005 or Q-1003 is available.

How to decide

Done when every dimension value has a unit class and a precision, and the mismatch lists carry a likely cause (transcription, rounding, different measurement) with the sample checks reported.

Prior attempts

The October context audit separated placeholder zeros from measurements; the 12 October data inventory measured the conversion pattern and the agreement between the two exports.

Outcome

13 October 2026, image index and units: each of 17,040 size values (one for each axis of each face, a face being the record of one inscribed side) has a source, unit of record, precision, conversion fit and flags (the record-level table is local; counts are in summary.json). 5,643 values were recorded in inches (2,295 to 0.1 in, 3,298 to 0.01 in), 389 in millimetres and 102 in centimetres, 4,250 exist only in millimetres, and 6,656 are empty or zero. Among the seals measured in Shot A, the 12 October test relating seal size to the closing sign, 135 of 590 in the discovery half (used for exploration) and 146 of 582 in the confirmation half (used for the frozen test) have both face sizes rounded to 0.1 in. The check against an independent source waits for the Mackay and Marshall tables (Q-1003).
