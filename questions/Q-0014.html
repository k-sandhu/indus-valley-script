Question

Do the views we hold independently show the elephant on L-161 to L-170 and the unicorn the catalogue gives the M-895 fragment?

Why it matters

The test of whether the text belongs to an office or travelled on one seal assumes the two objects show different animals.

What is needed

Inspect the complete views and captions we kept. Record which features are visible and which are inferred from the fragment or catalogue. Read DATA.md for the gaps this task depends on.

Sources: knowledge/conjectures/C-0004-m895-office-text.md; experiments/2026-10-12-seal-text-identity/REPORT.md.

How to decide

Use C-0004 P2 and its credence rule. A pass requires both animals visible or independently confirmed by a source; an unidentifiable fragment remains unresolved.

Prior attempts

SEAL-TEXT-IDENTITY-LOCALITY-2026-10-12. Read its outcome and retry_when condition before running this task.

Progress

13 October 2026: in the plate check both AI readers saw an elephant on the sealings at low to moderate confidence and disagreed on M-895 (unicorn against unidentifiable). A specialist reading of the photographs or the objects is still needed.
