Q-0009: Distinguish a missing motif record from a seal with no motif

Question

Can the motif fields we hold distinguish a catalogued absence of motif from a motif that is unknown, damaged, unreported or not acquired?

Why it matters

Seals bearing only text and seals whose animal was not observed play different roles in motif comparisons.

What is needed

Inspect each placeholder type in the export and in a fixed source sample. Keep the source's label and a versioned mapping to a value status and a reason for the missing value. Read DATA.md for the gaps this task depends on.

Sources: knowledge/directions/context-data-plan.md.

How to decide

Stop once every placeholder category and the fixed sample are reviewed. Reject any normalization that merges a recorded absence with an unknown observation.

Prior attempts

SIGN-CONTEXT-SEALS-01; OBJECT-LEDGER-PILOT-01. Read their outcomes and retry_when conditions before running this task.
