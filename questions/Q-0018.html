Question

Which excavation contexts and dates can the sources we hold assign to the Banawali seals that share texts?

Why it matters

Same-site duplicates could be one holder's seals, one institution's deposit or objects from separate periods.

What is needed

Trace item numbers to find units, stratum, phase and deposit status. Record unavailable fields in the data-gap list. Read DATA.md for the gaps this task depends on.

Sources: experiments/2026-10-12-seal-text-identity/REPORT.md; knowledge/directions/context-data-plan.md.

How to decide

Finish the whole saved duplicate group. Accept that seals are contemporary only within the interval the source supports; leave pairs without context unclassified.

Prior attempts

SEAL-TEXT-IDENTITY-LOCALITY-2026-10-12. Read its outcome and retry_when condition before running this task.
