Question

Which locally established crop groups survive the urban transition and later settlement changes, how much does their composition change, and which independently dated weed and processing observations support continuity in management?

Current evidence

The pilot audits Jognakhera and extracts nine published phase summaries at five comparison sites. A Bahola Late Harappan-to-PGW comparison retains winter and summer crops while their proportions change. The focal supplement, recovery denominators and layer-to-phase joins remain missing. These observations do not supply a regional causal estimate.

Next evidence and decision

Acquire source-linked sample counts, sediment volumes, sieve sizes, deposit types, local phases and direct-date identifiers. Compare crop occurrence, density and composition within sites before estimating regional change, retaining dependence between samples and publications. Freeze the model and precision target before examining a reserved set of new assemblages. If the source frame supports only pooled rounded percentages, stop at descriptive comparisons. The design records the acquisition queue and eligibility rules.

Prior attempts

The 11 October institutional study required recovery-controlled intermediate farming contexts before a transmission claim. The 25 October pilot adds a bounded archaeobotanical audit without repeating its rice-domestication calculation. The new question remains unclaimed; a read-only issue search found no Jognakhera match on 25 October 2026.
