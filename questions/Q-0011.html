Question

Why it matters

The conditional numeral equations depend on this physical object and both its faces.

What is needed

Check the plate we hold, excavation registration J213 and Vats number 477 against the historical identifier. If local sources cannot settle it, record the specific missing source in DATA.md. Read DATA.md for the gaps this task depends on.

Sources: knowledge/directions/decipherment-readiness-2026-10-02.md; research/literature/metrology/numerical_source_audit.md.

How to decide

Accept only an explicit historical concordance, or a matching registration plus source images of both faces. Stop when our evidence is exhausted; leave the identity conditional if the author's figure or register is missing.
