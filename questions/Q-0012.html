Question

Does any source we hold connect M-1383, as named in the numeral proposal, to the reconstructed bangle assembly and its count?

Why it matters

The proposal's additional range for the long-stroke value picks one arithmetic solution, so it needs an independent physical basis.

What is needed

Compare source-captioned identity, material, figure and the accounts of refitting and counting. Keep the match with our export's M-1383 record separate from the proposed bangle referent. Read DATA.md for the gaps this task depends on.

Sources: knowledge/directions/decipherment-readiness-2026-10-02.md; research/literature/metrology/numerical_source_audit.md.

How to decide

Pass only with a link between this inscription and the object, plus documented uncertainty in the count and reconstruction. Stop at the sources we hold; if no link is supported, record the missing source in DATA.md.
