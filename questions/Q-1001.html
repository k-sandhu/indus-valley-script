Question

Where in the held CISI scans are the photographs and basic-data entries for the ten Lothal sealings L-161 to L-170 and the Mohenjo-daro seal M-895, and what do those entries record (sides and impressions photographed, museum and excavation numbers, findspot, material, dimensions)?

Why it matters

Conjecture C-0004 and its verification cards Q-0013 (text order), Q-0014 (animals) and Q-0015 (seal dimensions) all read the same eleven objects. Both volumes are already held as complete scans, so the plate check needs no acquisition; what is missing is a located, hashed witness set that independent readers can open without searching. The Lipi image index has crops for L-161, L-162, L-164 (shared with L-165), L-166 to L-170 and only an impression crop for M-895, with no entry for L-163; the crops are derivatives and the plates are the witnesses.

What is needed

Read DATA.md. The Lothal entries are in CISI 1 (Joshi and Parpola 1987), Lothal section printed pp. 238–297 with basic data on pp. 372–374, local copy research/literature/sources/joshi_parpola_1987_cisi1.pdf; the scan repeats the book at a 431-page offset, so cite printed and PDF pages. M-895 is in the CISI 2 Mohenjo-daro range M-621 to M-1659, local copy data/raw/2026-10-06-evidence-census/sites-deep/a-pakistan-afghanistan/common-revision/cisi2-archive.pdf. For each object record the printed and PDF page, the plate position, every photograph caption (seal, impression, bis photographs), and every basic-data column. Save rendered page images locally with hashes; the tracked record holds locators and the transcribed metadata, not the images and not a reading of the signs.

How to decide

Done when each of the eleven objects has its photographs and basic-data row located, or a recorded absence with the page range searched, and the witness list is recorded where Q-0013 to Q-0015 can cite it. Leave sign readings and animal identifications to those cards.

Prior attempts

The seal-text identity study found the match in catalogue transcriptions and named this plate check as its first next step. No plate has been located yet.

Outcome

13 October 2026: witnesses assembled and read in the plate check. L-163 is in the Lipi crop L-164_A_L-165_A.jpg, and M-895 also has an object crop M-895_A.jpg. Neither CISI basic-data page gives dimensions.
