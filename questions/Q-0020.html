Question

Which stroke counts and face arrangements on tablets H2218–H2239 can be independently confirmed from the original views we hold?

Why it matters

These short texts fall outside the main restoration analysis's length filter, but the objects are complete and keep their structure across faces.

What is needed

Keep all objects and faces, source order, visible stroke height, candidate counts and published engraver groups. Review unresolved pairs before testing numerical relations. Read DATA.md for the gaps this task depends on.

Sources: knowledge/directions/decipherment-readiness-2026-10-02.md; experiments/2026-10-02-six-hour/harappa-object-frame/README.md.

How to decide

Finish all 22 objects in the source set. Accept a count difference only between two resolved physical faces; alternative transcriptions remain repeated observations of those objects.

Prior attempts

HARP-SHORT-01; ARCH-01. Read their outcomes and retry_when conditions before running this task.
