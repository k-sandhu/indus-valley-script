Question

Obtain the catalogue and plates of Corpus of Indus Seals and Inscriptions, volume 3, part 3, Indo-Iranian borderlands (Eastern Iran, Turkmenistan, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, Afghanistan), edited by Parpola and Koskikallio (Helsinki 2022, Humaniora 386) and cited in the project as CISI 3.3, and record its objects against the local records of finds made outside South Asia.

Why it matters

The local corpus has 42 faces (inscribed sides, each recorded as one row) from sites outside South Asia. The census batch on finds abroad found that the catalogue entries for Shortughai (84 entries) and Tepe Yahya (112) are mixed populations that need to be classified object by object. The project holds only a 40-page author excerpt of this volume, without plates. A review describes 3.3 as the penultimate part, to be followed by 3.4; no publication of 3.4 was found by 12 October 2026 (search snippet only).

What is needed

Read DATA.md. The volume is print only, through Tiedekirja; no open-access edition and no record in the Helsinki research portal were found. Acquisition is a maintainer decision. Once the volume is held, record its ISBN, pages and hash, and classify each entry as Indus inscription, Indus-style seal without script, or local glyptic (seals carved in the local tradition).

How to decide

Done when the volume is held with an entry in the project's data manifest and each entry for the borderland sites has a class and a match status against the local records, or when the decision not to acquire is recorded.

Prior attempts

The census batch on finds abroad and the Oxus source recovery used the excerpt and original excavation reports.
