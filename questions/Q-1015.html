Question

What do Rao's Lothal: A Harappan Port Town (1955–62), Volumes I (1979) and II (1985), record for each seal, sealing and inscribed object: registration, findspot, phase, material, dimensions and plate, and how do these join to the 182 local Lothal objects and the 290 CISI 1 Lothal entries?

Why it matters

Lothal has the largest quantified catalogue gap in India: 111 of 290 CISI 1 entries have no local identifier match, 52 of them in pottery or graffiti sections and 59 in seal, impression and tablet sections. Sealing counts disagree between sources (Rao 1985 p. 305 gives 91, as opened by the census reviewer; Frenez and Tosi 2005 give 93; CISI 1 has 93 sealing entries; Mahadevan 1977 selects 75). Only 14.3% of Lothal objects have all three dimensions and 50.5% a spatial unit. The excavation report supplies registrations and phases for the sealings that the Lothal attachment studies and conjecture C-0004 use.

What is needed

Read DATA.md. Both parts are scanned on the Internet Archive (Part 2 as masi78lothalaharappanporttownraos.r.part2_531_D; the Part 1 scan is labelled as missing its plates); an earlier batch copy saved a challenge page instead of the book, so check the title page and hash of whatever is downloaded. Record each volume's identifier, pages and hash in the manifest; tabulate the seal and sealing catalogue with page locators; join to CISI L-numbers through the CISI 1 basic data (Q-1005) and to the local objects. Keep the scans and the transcription local.

How to decide

Done when both volumes are identified and held or their absence is recorded with the routes tried, the inscribed-object tables are transcribed with locators, and each of the 111 absent CISI keys has a disposition that uses the report. The sealing count is reported with every source's figure and the reason for any difference.

Prior attempts

The Gujarat census batch and the synthesis identified Rao 1985 and the count conflicts; the Lothal physical-use frame coded Frenez and Tosi's table, and the registered-view audit recovered CISI panels and museum registrations for selected sealings.
