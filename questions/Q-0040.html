Question

Which similarities in town design, materials and infrastructure persist through rebuilding, and do changes in shared routes and access alter the distribution of seals, sealings, tablets and other writing?

Current evidence

The first comparison records 287 source assertions across twelve core towns and a hinterland relation. It distinguishes occupation followed by replanning from later local additions within retained frameworks. The sources provide ordered architectural changes and bounded documentary contexts, but no cross-town population of independently dated primary-use deposits with matched recovery denominators.

Next test

Recover phase-keyed plans, construction relations, restoration masks, source samples and object-context joins for the same named parts. Code architecture and documentary operations independently. Freeze models that distinguish common rules, supervision, engineering convergence, household activity and redeposition, then score new information after source and identity review. The linked test design states the power and exposure gates.
