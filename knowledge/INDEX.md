# Knowledge index

Use this index to find a research programme by date and follow its results to reports, reviews and exact outputs. The [findings guide](findings/README.md) groups individual results by question; the dated summaries below preserve the longer research record. Attempts and findings use the [status vocabulary](registry/VOCABULARY.md).

We distinguish four levels of claim: observed graphics, statistical structure, possible function, and phonetic or lexical interpretation, that is, sounds and words. We have useful evidence for the first two. No authenticated phonetic or lexical interpretation has been established.

### Two approaches beyond the Bonta work

The [October 11 parallel investigation](../experiments/2026-10-11-two-approaches/REPORT.md) develops two earlier recommendations: Rao and colleagues' proposal to infer a grammar from the inscriptions, and Mukhopadhyay's proposal to narrow the possible meanings before assigning values to signs. Its [20-source census](../experiments/2026-10-11-two-approaches/literature/LANDSCAPE.md) records 22 recommendations and recent precedents using hidden Markov models, contextual sign classes and administrative systems. In the frozen comparison a learned grammar of sign classes predicts worse than a tuned Markov model; an exploratory mixture of the two lowers loss by 0.0557 bits per sign against an equally tuned mixture of the Markov model and sign position. Whole objects repeat combinations of face texts, but a control added after the outcome, which fixes the role of each face, removes the evidence for particular sign pairs across faces. ## Status vocabulary

Each attempt and finding has one status class. An endpoint is the result an attempt was designed to report; a reference is the baseline or chance model it is compared with.

- Proposed: not run.
- Synthetic: simulated or constructed data.
- Exploratory: a result or refinement chosen after earlier outcomes were seen.
- Frozen evaluation: the reference, data split and method were fixed before that particular outcome, not necessarily before the whole project.
- Failed: the stated approach did not pass its test or beat its reference.
- Superseded: a documented defect or design change prevents use of the endpoint as the current result.
- Verified computation: numerical and source-integrity checks passed; this does not establish archaeological meaning or peer review.

Source review (`source-review`) records an audit, survey, census or literature review. Record (`record`) preserves coordination, release or research history. Contributor verification tiers are separate from these classes; see the [controlled vocabularies](registry/VOCABULARY.md).
