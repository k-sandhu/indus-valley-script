## Status vocabulary

Each attempt and finding has one status class. An endpoint is the result an attempt was designed to report; a reference is the baseline or chance model it is compared with.

- Proposed: not run.
- Synthetic: simulated or constructed data.
- Exploratory: a result or refinement chosen after earlier outcomes were seen.
- Frozen evaluation: the reference, data split and method were fixed before that particular outcome, not necessarily before the whole project.
- Failed: the stated approach did not pass its test or beat its reference.
- Superseded: a documented defect or design change prevents use of the endpoint as the current result.
- Verified computation: numerical and source-integrity checks passed; this does not establish archaeological meaning or peer review.

## Two approaches beyond the Bonta work

The [October 11 parallel investigation](../experiments/2026-10-11-two-approaches/REPORT.md) develops two earlier recommendations: Rao and colleagues' proposal to infer a grammar from the inscriptions, and Mukhopadhyay's proposal to narrow the possible meanings before assigning values to signs. Its [20-source census](../experiments/2026-10-11-two-approaches/literature/LANDSCAPE.md) records 22 recommendations and recent precedents using hidden Markov models, contextual sign classes and administrative systems. In the frozen comparison a learned grammar of sign classes predicts worse than a tuned Markov model; an exploratory mixture of the two lowers loss by 0.0557 bits per sign against an equally tuned mixture of the Markov model and sign position. Whole objects repeat combinations of face texts, but a control added after the outcome, which fixes the role of each face, removes the evidence for particular sign pairs across faces. [Grammar](../experiments/2026-10-11-two-approaches/grammar/REPORT.md), [carrier analysis](../experiments/2026-10-11-two-approaches/administrative/REPORT.md).

The proposed [token life cycle](../experiments/2026-10-11-two-approaches/administrative/HYPOTHESES.md) treats a licence and a ration token as stages of one operation: the token states what its bearer may receive or do, and after use certifies that the right was used. Its predictions require independent evidence of handling, cancellation, attachment, contents or issuing group. An [exact synthetic anchor example](../experiments/2026-10-11-two-approaches/ANCHORS.md) shows that a new contextual observation adds information and a copy of one already counted does not. The [adversarial review](../experiments/2026-10-11-two-approaches/review/REVIEW.md), [response](../experiments/2026-10-11-two-approaches/REVIEW-RESPONSE.md) and [retrospective](retrospectives/2026-10-11-two-approaches.md) preserve the failed outcomes, corrections to the chance comparisons and the fixed tests for the next data.

## Published-claim tests, 12 October

The [published-claim tests](../experiments/2026-10-12-published-claim-tests/README.md) evaluate Yajnadevam's Sanskrit key and his unicity-distance argument, that a corpus this long allows only one key that yields readable Sanskrit, ([Study A](../experiments/2026-10-12-published-claim-tests/sanskrit-key/REPORT.md)), Indus-script texts found outside South Asia ([Study B](../experiments/2026-10-12-published-claim-tests/foreign-finds/REPORT.md)) and the key's groups of variant signs (allographs) and same-sound signs (homophones) ([Study C](../experiments/2026-10-12-published-claim-tests/allographs/REPORT.md)). Our emulation of his dictionary readability criterion accepts random strings. The key scores far below Sanskrit text and 1.2 to 2.4 standard deviations above rearrangements of its own values, while a power analysis on synthetic Indus-like corpora shows that a correct key with homophones would stand 27 to 72 standard deviations clear. Unrelated languages fit the corpus equally well, also after near-duplicate texts are removed. An adversarial review and response accompany Study A. The [key calibration memo](directions/key-calibration-2026-10-12.md) turns these checks into a procedure for any future key; the [retrospective](retrospectives/2026-10-12-published-claim-tests.md) records failures and repairs.


## Structure before meaning, 12 October

The [diagnosis and plan](directions/structure-first-2026-10-12.md) explains why earlier work did not reach a substantive result. The [six-step program](../experiments/2026-10-12-structure-first/README.md) tests the sign list, image–text pairing, line divisions, stroke numerals, method calibration on readable scripts and a structural language comparison. Graphic sign families improve prediction of rare signs, mostly through information already in the Wells order. Harappa tablet texts pair with their images within each kind of tablet, while sign groups specific to copper tablets show no detectable transfer to seals. Catalogued line divisions fall between recurring units. Stroke values are more tied to neighbouring signs than Ur III counts are. Unsupervised segmentation and the search for alternating endings do not discriminate at the size of the Indus corpus. Indus texts close on a small sign class, a descriptive property that leaves every candidate language tied. Each of the four candidate descriptions of the script in the program's hypothesis ledger failed at least one frozen prediction. [Integrated report](../experiments/2026-10-12-structure-first/REPORT.md), [review and responses](../experiments/2026-10-12-structure-first/review/RESPONSE.md), [retrospective](retrospectives/2026-10-12-structure-first.md).
