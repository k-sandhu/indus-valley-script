# Claim

Indus sealings recorded responsibility at handovers between urban groups. Their recurring legends connected institutions across districts and towns, while the particular stamp identified an authorising holder. The relevant architectural settings were controlled passages, storage access and points where goods changed custody.

# Evidence and extension

The project has physical fastening evidence from Lothal and repeated texts carried by different stamps across sites. The [new study](../../experiments/2026-10-24-urban-form-and-inscription-ecology/REPORT.md) adds the distinction between a use context, a workshop, a discarded assemblage and a building associated only by a reused label. A sealing's fastening trace supports closure or attachment; responsibility and institutional handover remain the proposed function. No sound value or sign gloss follows from this claim.
