# Claim

Harappa's repeated tablet series recorded obligations or entitlements for work on shared infrastructure. The repeated descriptor identified a work group or service and the varying stroke group identified an obligation class. Production batches made comparable records available to several participants.

# Why take this shot

Serial production, recurring faces and varying stroke groups make a repeatable administrative instrument plausible. [Rao 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.00049), printed pp. 518-525, already proposes rations for porters or labourers and repetitive economic transactions. Our extension identifies obligations for shared urban works and asks for a phase-linked distinction from ordinary goods allocation. The [source record](../../experiments/2026-10-24-urban-form-and-inscription-ecology/modules/inscriptions/RESEARCH.md) gives manufacturing and secondary-discard contexts; none independently identifies a labour account. This specific connection is the long shot.

These are acquisition targets, not a ready-to-run confirmation protocol. P1 alone supports the broader administrative model and leaves this labour-specific credence at 0.10. P1 and P3 passing moves it to 0.18. P1 and P2 passing with P3's independent distinction moves it to 0.35. A powered failure of P1 or P2 moves it to 0.03. All overlapping observations receive one joint update.

# Kill condition

Abandon this specific labour reading if a new, independently coded sample ties the varying series to stored goods or another activity that explains their variation and defeats the shared-work prediction, or if a sufficiently powered prospective test yields no useful association. Failure leaves the broader proposition of serial administrative tablets available for separate tests.
