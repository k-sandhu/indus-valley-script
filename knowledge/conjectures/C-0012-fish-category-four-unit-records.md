# Claim

The short-stroke, plain-V and plain-fish Harappa tablet series specifies a standard four-unit record indexed to the Fish category. V supplies the unit role; fish supplies a category role that is also expressed in the longer legend on the other face. The category can identify contents, an account or a recipient. Four is this construction's denomination.

# Why it might be true

H-302, H-912 and H-1191 have four–V–fish, while H-1192 has four–fish–V. All four records have fish in the related longer legend on the other face. H-912 and H-1191 have identical transcriptions and the same mixed export reference. They remain separate provisional families under identity review. The [source observations](../../experiments/2026-10-20-h912-tablet-series/SOURCE-OBSERVATIONS.md) and [core identifiers](../../experiments/2026-10-20-h912-tablet-series/register-v1/core-identifiers.json) retain every case and its missing image status.

The [frozen comparison](../../experiments/2026-10-20-h912-tablet-series/confirmation-v1/comparison.json) has one confirmation dependency group and fails its minimum-size rule. Literal contents and fixed allocation have the same graphic likelihood. The [bench freeze](../../experiments/2026-10-20-h912-tablet-series/bench-freeze-v1.json) and [inspection](../../experiments/2026-10-20-h912-tablet-series/bench-inspect-v1.json) record that limitation. This proposal was written after the outcome and has no separate confirmation pass.
