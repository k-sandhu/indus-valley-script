# Societal persistence and renewal

The [October 11 program](../../experiments/2026-10-11-societal-persistence/README.md) develops minimum functional requirements for a specified population, service and horizon. A purposive primary-source survey bounds the contribution alongside economic reproduction, maintenance, viability, collective action and archaeological persistence research.

The [retrospective](../../experiments/2026-10-11-societal-persistence/RETROSPECTIVE.md) records theory revisions, access failures, source-identity corrections, archived Dholavira recovery, frozen finite examples and independent review. The [report](../../experiments/2026-10-11-societal-persistence/REPORT.md) and [manuscript](../../experiments/2026-10-11-societal-persistence/MANUSCRIPT.md) distinguish exact invented results from source-observed renewal and prospective empirical work.

A continuation needs independently supported delivered quantities, wear and supply bounds, dated work windows or evidence for skill succession and participation. Broader duration or architectural resemblance alone does not identify an institution. Earlier endpoints and raw witnesses remain preserved; no CI/CD or recurring process was added.
