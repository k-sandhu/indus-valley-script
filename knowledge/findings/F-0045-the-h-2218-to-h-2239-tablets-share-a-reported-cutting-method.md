# Finding

Meadow and Kenoyer report the same cutting direction and within-character stroke order across the 22 H-2218–H-2239 tablets, attributed to at least three engravers from microscopic examination of moulds. Sixteen tablets were recovered in a clump, two nearby and four scattered, all in secondary debris outside Mound E's curtain wall.
