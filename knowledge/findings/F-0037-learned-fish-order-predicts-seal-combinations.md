# Finding

An order of seven fish-family codes learned on discovery agrees with more pairs than it reverses in 43 reserved dependency groups, against eight opposing groups and two ties. The exact one-sided sign-test p-value is 0.0000003434. There are 45 forward pairs among 55 pairs in total.

# Evidence

[The frozen result](../../experiments/2026-10-20-fish-context/confirmation-v1/results.json) gives the order, pair counts, component votes and strict-code sensitivity. [The arithmetic check](../../experiments/2026-10-20-fish-context/verification-v1.json) independently reconstructs the votes and exact tail.

# Scope

The source corpus had earlier fish and whole-corpus analyses. This is a frozen evaluation of the specified order on reserved rows, with historical exposure declared. The result measures ordering among graphic forms; it does not establish a language or meaning. A later independent-position comparison and a Mohenjo-daro exclusion are exploratory and appear separately in the report. Verification tier: submitted.
