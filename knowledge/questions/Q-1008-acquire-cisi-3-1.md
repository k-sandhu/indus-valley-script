# Question

Obtain a usable copy of *Corpus of Indus Seals and Inscriptions, Volume 3: New material, untraced objects, and collections outside India and Pakistan, Part 1: Mohenjo-daro and Harappa* (Parpola, Pande and Koskikallio, eds, with Meadow and Kenoyer; Helsinki 2010; Annales Academiae Scientiarum Fennicae Humaniora 359; Memoirs of the Archaeological Survey of India 96), cited in the project as CISI 3.1, and record which local objects it adds or documents.

# Why it matters

Harappa and Mohenjo-daro, the two largest sites, hold 3,716 of the 4,695 objects in the local corpus. CISI 3.1 publishes the finds made at Harappa since 1986 by the Harappa Archaeological Research Project (HARP), and Mohenjo-daro material later than CISI 1 and 2, the two volumes the project holds. It gives photographs of every side and excavation numbers; its publisher lists 3,761 black-and-white and 125 colour photographs and 154 line drawings. The project holds only the 15-page and 17-page versions of its Harappa introduction. Objects that earlier studies used without a photograph from a primary publication (H697A, H1897, H2139, H2200, H1100) are in this range, and the Harappa tablet tests of conjecture C-0005 need its find contexts.

# What is needed

Read [DATA.md](../DATA.md). The volume is print only, sold by Tiedekirja in Helsinki; in 2020 the Finnish Academy offered volumes 1, 2, 3.1 and 3.2 together for 300 EUR, and no open-access edition was found ([sources.json](../../experiments/2026-10-12-data-inventory/sources.json)). Purchase is a maintainer decision; library access or an interlibrary scan of the catalogue and basic-data sections (the tables of each entry's excavation, museum and concordance numbers) is an alternative. Record edition, pages, ISBN 978-951-41-1040-5 and, for any scan, its hash and the rights notice. Then extend to this volume the column layout that Q-1005 uses to transcribe the CISI 1 basic data.

# How to decide

Done when a copy is held with an entry in the project's data manifest, or the maintainers have declined purchase and the library route is recorded as tried. Report how many CISI 3.1 entries match local objects and how many are new to the local corpus.

# Prior attempts

The project's evidence census and its tables of Harappa objects drawn from published sources worked from the introduction only; the source-identity study ROOT-19 left several Harappa matches between catalogue and object conditional on this volume.
