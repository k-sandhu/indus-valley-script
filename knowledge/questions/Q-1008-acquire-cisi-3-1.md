# Question

Obtain a usable copy of *Corpus of Indus Seals and Inscriptions, Volume 3: New material, untraced objects, and collections outside India and Pakistan, Part 1: Mohenjo-daro and Harappa* (Parpola, Pande and Koskikallio, eds, with Meadow and Kenoyer; Helsinki 2010; Annales Academiae Scientiarum Fennicae Humaniora 359; Memoirs of the Archaeological Survey of India 96), and record which local objects it adds or documents.

# Why it matters

The two largest sites hold 3,716 of the 4,695 local objects. CISI 3.1 publishes the HARP finds from Harappa since 1986 and Mohenjo-daro material later than CISI 1 and 2, with photographs of every side and excavation numbers; its publisher lists 3,761 black-and-white and 125 colour photographs and 154 line drawings. The project holds only the 15-page and 17-page versions of its Harappa introduction. Objects studied before without a primary photograph (H697A, H1897, H2139, H2200, H1100) are in this range, and the Harappa tablet tests of C-0005 need its contexts.

# What is needed

Read [DATA.md](../DATA.md). The volume is print only, sold by Tiedekirja in Helsinki; in 2020 the Finnish Academy offered volumes 1, 2, 3.1 and 3.2 together for 300 EUR, and no open-access edition was found ([sources.json](../../experiments/2026-10-12-data-inventory/sources.json)). Purchase is an owner decision; library access or an interlibrary scan of the catalogue and basic-data sections is an alternative. Record edition, pages, ISBN 978-951-41-1040-5 and, for any scan, its hash and the rights notice. Then extend the basic-data schema of Q-1005 to this volume.

# How to decide

Done when a copy is held with a manifest entry, or the owner has declined purchase and the library route is recorded as tried. Report how many CISI 3.1 entries match local objects and how many are new to the local corpus.
