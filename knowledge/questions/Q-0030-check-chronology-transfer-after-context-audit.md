# Question

Can a cohort dated through audited source links support a held-out comparison of sign use across periods, after controlling for genre, material and recovery method?

# Why it matters

Period differences found so far vary with the source of the labels and the mix of mounds.

# What is needed

Use only audited dates and object–context links. Before seeing the outcome, freeze rules for residual objects (older than their layer), secondary deposits, grouping and holding out whole sources or sites. Read [DATA.md](../DATA.md) for the gaps this task depends on.

Sources: [knowledge/directions/research-roadmap.md](../directions/research-roadmap.md); [knowledge/directions/context-data-plan.md](../directions/context-data-plan.md).

# How to decide

A pass requires the declared effect on a supported reserved cohort and a check against dating uncertainty. Stop if date labels are confounded with source or manufacture beyond the available controls.
