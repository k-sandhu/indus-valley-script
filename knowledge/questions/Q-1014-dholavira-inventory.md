# Question

Which entries of the seal and sealing inventory in Bisht's *Excavations at Dholavira (1989–90 to 2004–05)* correspond to the 230 local Dholavira objects, and what registration, findspot, stage and dimensions does each carry?

# Why it matters

Dholavira is the third-largest site in the local corpus and its worst described: 0.9% of its objects have a CISI number, 18.7% an excavation number and 0.9% a local image, because the finds post-date CISI 1. The report gives 225 recovered seals and 60 sealings on p. 233, with other populations on pp. 242 and 308 that disagree; the census recorded these conflicts. The inventory is the only route held locally to registrations and stages for these objects.

# What is needed

Read [DATA.md](../DATA.md). Input: `data/raw/2026-10-11-societal-persistence/evidence/acquisition-v5/bisht2015-preliminary-original.pdf`, a 127 MB scan that the Internet Archive labels a preliminary January 2015 Dholavira report (938 pages, no title page); it was acquired after the census, which worked from a mirror transcription. First confirm that its text matches the published ASI report, then use the census route to the inventory, pp. 246–333. Transcribe registration number, findspot, stage, material, dimensions, motif and plate reference for each inscribed object, then join to the local Dholavira faces by text, motif and dimensions, keeping text-only matches as candidates. Keep the transcription local.

# How to decide

Done when every local Dholavira object has a joined inventory entry, a candidate set or a recorded failure, and every inventory entry with an inscription has a local object or is listed as missing from the local corpus. Report the counts against the p. 233 totals and explain any difference.

# Prior attempts

The Gujarat census batch and the synthesis recorded Dholavira's population counts and their internal conflicts from a mirror; no object-level join exists.
