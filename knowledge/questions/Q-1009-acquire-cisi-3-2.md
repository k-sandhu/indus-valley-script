# Question

Obtain *Corpus of Indus Seals and Inscriptions: 3.2 Shahr-i Sokhta; Mundigak; Mehrgarh, Nausharo, Sibri, Dauda-damb; Chanhudaro; Ahar, Balathal, Gilund; Kalibangan; Rojdi* (Parpola, Pande and Koskikallio, eds; Helsinki 2019, released January 2020; Humaniora 383; MASI 116) and record what it adds to the local objects from those sites.

# Why it matters

The volume covers sites where the local record is thin or incomplete: Kalibangan (197 objects, 26 CISI 1 entries unmatched, depth for 25.9%), Chanhu-daro (no chronology label on any of its 72 objects), Nausharo, Mehrgarh (18 CISI 2 entries unmatched) and the Iranian and Afghan sites. The publisher lists 1,472 black-and-white and 32 colour photographs and 535 line drawings, including material from the Museum of Fine Arts, Boston.

# What is needed

Read [DATA.md](../DATA.md). Print only, 160 EUR hardbound from Tiedekirja (2020 notice), ISBN 978-951-41-1134-1; the north-India census batch inspected selected pages without holding a copy. The decision to buy belongs to the owner. Once held, record the hash and rights notice, locate each site section and extend the basic-data transcription of Q-1005 to it.

# How to decide

Done when a copy is held with a manifest entry, or the decision not to acquire is recorded; then report, per site, the entries that match local objects and those new to the local corpus.
