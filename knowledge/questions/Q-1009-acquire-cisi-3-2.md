# Question

Obtain *Corpus of Indus Seals and Inscriptions: 3.2 Shahr-i Sokhta; Mundigak; Mehrgarh, Nausharo, Sibri, Dauda-damb; Chanhudaro; Ahar, Balathal, Gilund; Kalibangan; Rojdi* (Parpola, Pande and Koskikallio, eds; Helsinki 2019, released January 2020; Humaniora 383; MASI 116), cited in the project as CISI 3.2, and record what it adds to the local objects from those sites.

# Why it matters

The volume covers sites where the local record is thin or incomplete: Kalibangan (197 objects; 26 entries in CISI 1, the volume for Indian collections, have no local match; depth recorded for 25.9%), Chanhu-daro (no chronology label on any of its 72 objects), Nausharo, Mehrgarh (18 CISI 2 entries without a local match) and the Iranian and Afghan sites. The publisher lists 1,472 black-and-white and 32 colour photographs and 535 line drawings, including material from the Museum of Fine Arts, Boston.

# What is needed

Read [DATA.md](../DATA.md). The volume is print only: 160 EUR hardbound from Tiedekirja in Helsinki (2020 notice), ISBN 978-951-41-1134-1. The north-India batch of the project's evidence census inspected selected pages without holding a copy. The decision to buy rests with the maintainers. Once the volume is held, record its hash and rights notice, locate each site section, and extend to it the transcription of CISI basic data (each entry's excavation, museum and concordance numbers) set up in Q-1005.

# How to decide

Done when a copy is held with an entry in the project's data manifest, or the decision not to acquire is recorded; then report, per site, the entries that match local objects and those new to the local corpus.

The north-India batch of the evidence census opened selected pages to count Kalibangan entries.
