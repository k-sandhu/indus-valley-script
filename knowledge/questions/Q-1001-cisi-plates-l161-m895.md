# Question

The *Corpus of Indus Seals and Inscriptions* (CISI) photographs each object on its plates and gives its concordance, excavation and museum numbers and photograph sources in a table of "basic data". We hold complete scans of volumes 1 and 2. Where in those scans are the photographs and basic-data entries for the ten Lothal sealings L-161 to L-170 and the Mohenjo-daro seal M-895, and what do those entries record (sides and impressions photographed, museum and excavation numbers, findspot, material, dimensions)?

# Why it matters

Conjecture C-0004 and the cards that test it, Q-0013 (text order), Q-0014 (animals) and Q-0015 (seal dimensions), all read the same eleven objects. Because both volumes are held, checking the plates needs no purchase. What is missing is a list of the exact pages, saved as hashed page images, that independent readers can open without searching; these pages are the witnesses, the primary evidence for each object. The image index of Lipi, our local transcription corpus, has crops cut from these plates for L-161, L-162, L-164 (shared with L-165) and L-166 to L-170, only an impression crop for M-895, and no entry for L-163.

# What is needed

Read [DATA.md](../DATA.md). The Lothal entries are in CISI 1 (Joshi and Parpola 1987): the Lothal section is on printed pp. 238–297 and the basic data on pp. 372–374, in the local copy `research/literature/sources/joshi_parpola_1987_cisi1.pdf`. That scan contains the book twice, the second copy starting 431 pages after the first, so cite both the printed and the PDF page. M-895 is in the CISI 2 Mohenjo-daro range M-621 to M-1659, local copy `data/raw/2026-10-06-evidence-census/sites-deep/a-pakistan-afghanistan/common-revision/cisi2-archive.pdf`. For each object record the printed and PDF page, the position on the plate, every photograph caption (seal, impression, and the extra photographs marked `bis`) and every basic-data column. Save rendered page images locally with their hashes. The record tracked in Git holds the page locators and the transcribed metadata; the images and any reading of the signs stay out of it.

# How to decide

Done when each of the eleven objects has its photographs and basic-data row located, or a recorded absence with the page range searched, and the list of pages is recorded where Q-0013 to Q-0015 can cite it. Leave sign readings and animal identifications to those cards.

# Prior attempts

The seal-text identity study found that the catalogue transcriptions give these sealings and M-895 the same text, and named this plate check as its first next step. No plate has been located yet.

# Outcome

13 October 2026: the pages were located and read in the [plate check](../../experiments/2026-10-13-plate-checks/README.md). L-163 is in the Lipi crop `L-164_A_L-165_A.jpg`, and M-895 also has a crop of the seal itself, `M-895_A.jpg`. Neither CISI basic-data page gives dimensions.
