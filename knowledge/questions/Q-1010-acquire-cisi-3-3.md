# Question

Obtain the catalogue and plates of *Corpus of Indus Seals and Inscriptions*, volume 3, part 3, Indo-Iranian borderlands (Eastern Iran, Turkmenistan, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, Afghanistan), edited by Parpola and Koskikallio (Helsinki 2022, Humaniora 386), and record its objects against the local foreign-find records.

# Why it matters

The local corpus has 42 faces from sites outside South Asia, and the foreign-find census found mixed catalogue populations at Shortughai (84 entries) and Tepe Yahya (112) that need object-level classification. The project holds only a 40-page author excerpt of this volume, without plates. A review describes 3.3 as the penultimate part, to be followed by 3.4; no publication of 3.4 was found by 12 October 2026 (search snippet only).

# What is needed

Read [DATA.md](../DATA.md). Print only through Tiedekirja; no open-access edition and no Helsinki portal record were found. Acquisition is an owner decision. Record ISBN, pages and hash once held, and classify each entry as Indus inscription, Indus-style seal without script, or local glyptic.

# How to decide

Done when the volume is held with a manifest entry and each entry for the borderland sites has a class and a match status against the local records, or when the decision not to acquire is recorded.

# Prior attempts

The foreign-find census and the Oxus source recovery used the excerpt and original excavation reports.
