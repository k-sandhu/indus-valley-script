## Graphic restoration on the selected Indus transcriptions

The restoration benchmark holds physical aliases and repeated whole strings together. It masks 8,009 known positions in 1,596 distinct strings and evaluates exact graphic-code recovery. The conventional first-order model reaches 34.59% top-one accuracy. Adding second-order context raises that to 37.27%, a paired gain of 2.6845 percentage points, with a component-bootstrap interval of 1.9902–3.3968.

Probability loss worsens from 5.1194 to 5.2472 bits per slot. Both loss-based second-order selection and a later extra-shrinkage family return first order in all five folds. The retained candidate tables make that tradeoff reusable. Boundary and training-order interventions also measure which information contributes: removing the end factor lowers final-position accuracy by 5.70 points, while an interior shuffle retaining endpoints lowers overall accuracy to 24.52% across twenty orders.

The earlier unknown-count experiment gives 46.30% after matching its artificial observation law, below the constant-one control's 51.07%. Physical gap width and surviving traces are the specified inputs for a useful retry. ## Which physical measurements resolve numerical ambiguity

Generated whole-part relations identify relative values, while independently counted objects constrain scale. A documented Puruchuco khipu control reproduces sixteen of twenty published account columns, with total absolute error sixteen. Its source alignment and generated hidden-value controls have separate status and preserved inputs.

Exact maximum consensus can accept a wrong vector when many equations repeat the same algebraic direction. On 200 fresh four-swap worlds, an independently recorded complete pool raises correct recovery from 133 to 190 and reduces accepted wrong values from nine to one at the declared 50% gate. Erroneously calling a partial pool complete produces fifteen accepted errors.

The validation study tests the kind of observation to acquire next. With a counted unit already fixing scale, one withheld count eliminates all nine accepted wrong local decoders, while rank-first selection needs two checks. With scale free, count-first needs two and rank-first three. Exact ray and family intersections preserve unresolved and wrong alternatives. This makes observation choice a question about feasible quantities and measurement type. ## Reference models and symbol-mapping recovery

The mapping program tests a bijection against an independent known reference in generated transition and administrative systems. Graph and slot profiles preserve different information. A fresh reference-only selector recovers 309 of 384 maps, compared with 283 for fixed graph and 168 for fixed slot. Exact small-inventory maxima, assignment duals and equality witnesses distinguish search misses from objectives favoring wrong mappings.

Making administrative forms the same length and mixing their symbols reduces recovery. A fitted three-component reference mixture restores all 24 large-reference maps at zero and .35 shared mass, compared with 22 under the preceding selector. A coincident-component smoothing control recovers 21 and 22. The component count remains a supplied prior. At .70 mass, reference validation chooses ordinary slot prediction and preserves the associated failures. Six failed mixture maps score below truth, providing a specific search diagnostic for another optimizer. [Paper 004](../papers/004-reference-model-selection/manuscript.md), [claim files](../papers/004-reference-model-selection/methods.md).

All 36 stored profiles satisfy a published population-identification condition under two declared exact probability conventions. Rational rank witnesses certify that the full population law determines the form weights and slot distributions, up to a common form-label permutation within the fixed three-component model. A collapsed-component boundary preserves distinct weights with identical observations. This structural calculation gives the finite-sample recovery program a precise parameter target.
