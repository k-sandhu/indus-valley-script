C-0019: Harappa tablet series recorded obligations for shared work

Claim

Harappa's repeated tablet series recorded obligations or entitlements for work on shared infrastructure. The repeated descriptor identified a work group or service and the varying stroke group identified an obligation class. Production batches made comparable records available to several participants.

Why take this shot

Serial production, recurring faces and varying stroke groups make a repeatable administrative instrument plausible. Rao 2018, printed pp. 518-525, already proposes rations for porters or labourers and repetitive economic transactions. Our extension identifies obligations for shared urban works and asks for a phase-linked distinction from ordinary goods allocation. The source record gives manufacturing and secondary-discard contexts; none independently identifies a labour account. This specific connection is the long shot.

Predictions

ID Prediction New data and pass rule Status
P1 Newly recovered tablet series occur in primary contexts associated with shared works In a new sample with at least 20 matched context pairs and a preregistered useful effect, tablet occurrence predicts a directly evidenced maintenance or provisioning context after controls for domestic storage, craft production and trade. Pass requires conditional odds ratio at least 2 and a cluster interval above 1. untested
P2 Varying stroke groups covary with independently measured work or provision classes Freeze the classes from physical evidence without reading inscriptions. A model using the frozen face units predicts those classes better than within-frequency rearrangements, with component influence checks and transfer to a new excavation block. untested
P3 Material associations distinguish shared work from ordinary container labelling New wear, residues, discard sequences or direct associations supply an independent distinction between labour provisioning and goods storage. The decision rule must be established from a blinded pilot and frozen before confirmation. untested

These are acquisition targets, not a ready-to-run confirmation protocol. P1 alone supports the broader administrative model and leaves this labour-specific credence at 0.10. P1 and P3 passing moves it to 0.18. P1 and P2 passing with P3's independent distinction moves it to 0.35. A powered failure of P1 or P2 moves it to 0.03. All overlapping observations receive one joint update.

Kill condition

Abandon this specific labour reading if a new, independently coded sample ties the varying series to stored goods or another activity that explains their variation and defeats the shared-work prediction, or if a sufficiently powered prospective test yields no useful association. Failure leaves the broader proposition of serial administrative tablets available for separate tests.
