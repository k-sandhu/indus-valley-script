Claim

Indus legends are built from seven word classes: numerals, links, the names of great households, personal-name elements, commodities and units, office nouns, and closing determinatives, signs that classify the word before them. A seal legend reads "of [household]: [personal name], [department] [office] [the man or the body]". The pair of short strokes after the opening signs 817, 861 and 820 is a particle meaning "of"; the fish signs are "star" elements of personal names; the jar 740 is the steward of the store named by the sign before it; the comb 400 marks an office as a body, and the man 090 an individual holder. Harappa's moulded and incised tablets are tokens of standard measures, offices and labour; Mohenjo-daro's copper tablets record named holders and their fodder or grain allotments; pottery marks give capacities in standard units or the store a vessel belongs to. Names and commodities belong to a city, while households, offices and grammar are shared, and office holders have larger seals bearing the emblem of the shared administration. The classes therefore predict where seals were found, their size, their animal and their date. The full map is GLOSS-MAP.json and the reading READING.md.

Why it might be true

A frame is a context of neighbouring signs; two signs that occur in the same frame can stand in for each other.

  • 817, 861 and 820 open legends and substitute for one another in 37 to 44 shared frames; the pair of short strokes 002 follows them in 134 of its 235 occurrences in distinct discovery texts and follows a stroke numeral 6 times (frames_summary.json, reading_counts.json).
  • 740 ends 259 of 704 distinct discovery seal legends and is bound to the signs before it: 760 precedes it in 34 of 37 texts and 100 in 38 of 44 (grammar_summary.json).
  • After 740, the man 090 and the comb 400 share 32 frames; seals end 740-090 in 23 legends and 740-400 in 5, while tablets end 740-090 in 8 texts and 740-400 in 39 (reading_counts.json).
  • On Harappa tablets, 203 of 237 intact faces with 700 consist of a stroke numeral plus 700; the sheaf signs 390, 405 and 407 follow a numeral in 51 of 145 occurrences, and the granary bin 590 in 33 (reading_counts.json).
  • The structure-first program found recurring units, a small class of closing signs and stroke groups fixed to neighbouring signs (REPORT.md), and the seal-text identity study found that long legends almost never repeat while short recurring legends travel between cities (what-12-october-established.md).

Kill condition
