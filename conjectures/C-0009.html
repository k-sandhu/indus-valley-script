Claim

H-912 B records four vessel-units of fish. The stroke group gives the number, the V-shaped sign names the vessel unit, and the fish-shaped sign names the contents.

Why it might be true

CISI 2 printed page339 shows this construction on H-912 B. Parpola's 2010 Figure 6 reproduces its photograph but calls it H-902. The separate H-902 photographs on page338 have other signs. Figure 5 compares the V-shaped sign with an object held by a person on M-478 A. This supplies a pictorial basis for the vessel reading. Source correction, observations S2–S4.

The current bench freeze and meaning-map result record the fish study's structural and carrier tests. They do not validate this vessel-and-contents mapping: its intervening V-shaped sign puts it outside the adjacent-stroke prediction. This is a source-based prospective branch, with no phonetic value assigned. Belcher's Harappa fish deposits have no sourced object-to-lot join to H-912.
