Claim

H-912 B records four vessel-units of fish. The stroke group gives the number, the V-shaped sign names the vessel unit, and the fish-shaped sign names the contents.

Why it might be true

CISI 2 printed page339 shows this construction on H-912 B. Parpola's 2010 Figure 6 reproduces its photograph but calls it H-902. The separate H-902 photographs on page338 have other signs. Figure 5 compares the V-shaped sign with an object held by a person on M-478 A. This supplies a pictorial basis for the vessel reading. Source correction, observations S2–S4.

The current bench freeze and meaning-map result record the fish study's structural and carrier tests. They do not validate this vessel-and-contents mapping: its intervening V-shaped sign puts it outside the adjacent-stroke prediction. This is a source-based prospective branch, with no phonetic value assigned. Belcher's Harappa fish deposits have no sourced object-to-lot join to H-912.

Record

20 October 2026: recorded Parpola's inherited functional proposal on the corrected object, with credence 0.35. The source-reference correction has direct photographic support. The content-and-unit prediction is untested. Verification tier: submitted.

20 October 2026, H-912 follow-up: H-302, H-912, H-1191 and H-1192 form the recovered source series. H-912/H-1191 have identical two-face transcriptions and a shared source reference, with no asserted physical merge. The original recovery range is 4–7 feet; the collection spans two squares and three months. The frozen quantity comparisons fail their minimum-size and p-value rules (one confirmation text component, p = 0.5), and the literal reading ties the fixed-quota rival. The physical prediction is not evaluable, so credence remains 0.35. A count of four vessels and an amount of four standard measures retain separate prospective physical tests. Report, bench freeze, bench inspection, exact comparison, excavation observations.
