Claim

Fish combinations form an ordered designation field within seal legends. Successive fish forms carry different parts of one compound label. The field can identify a person, affiliation, office or category; its component sounds remain unassigned.

Why it might be true

A discovery-learned fish order predicts the reserved seal texts in 43 dependency groups, against eight groups opposing it and two ties. The frozen p-value is 0.0000003434. A later comparison against independent position frequencies gains 0.343 bits per group, and excluding Mohenjo-daro leaves 13 favourable groups, two opposed and one tie. The most common directed relation, W235 before W240, has 14 occurrences against two in reverse. Primary result, stronger references.

The numeral success is less specific: preceding context predicts slightly better overall, and removing value two eliminates the fish-specific gain over a general quantity reference. The meaning-map carrier prediction fails (p = 0.4855). The bench output and freeze therefore support a structural field without selecting one social or economic meaning. The corpus has prior exposure; this is a frozen evaluation on that material.

Kill condition

Drop the compound-designation interpretation if a sufficiently sized new source block is explained by independent positional roles and the frozen order has no predictive gain, or if source images show that the apparent compounds are a recurring transcription or face-boundary error. Preserve the measured ordering of the old export.
