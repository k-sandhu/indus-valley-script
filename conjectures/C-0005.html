Claim

Harappa's moulded and incised tablets are standard tokens issued in series. One face names what the token is for, and the face with two, three or four tall strokes before sign 700 states its tier: two, three or four of a standard unit. On seals, by contrast, stroke groups are fixed parts of names and titles, and do not count goods.

Why it might be true

The three commonest texts in the corpus are two, three and four tall strokes followed by sign 700: 93, 127 and 111 faces, all but five on Harappa tablets. On multi-face tablets the W700 face takes several tall-stroke values against a fixed descriptor face, in 17 complete two-face classes (two approaches, administrative). Elsewhere stroke values are more tied to the neighbouring sign than Ur III counts are to their commodity: 3.61 values per item against 4.53–5.15 (structure-first step 4).
