Claim

The short-stroke, plain-V and plain-fish Harappa tablet series specifies a standard four-unit record indexed to the Fish category. V supplies the unit role; fish supplies a category role that is also expressed in the longer legend on the other face. The category can identify contents, an account or a recipient. Four is this construction's denomination.

Why it might be true

H-302, H-912 and H-1191 have four–V–fish, while H-1192 has four–fish–V. All four records have fish in the related longer legend on the other face. H-912 and H-1191 have identical transcriptions and the same mixed export reference. They remain separate provisional families under identity review. The source observations and core identifiers retain every case and its missing image status.

The frozen comparison has one confirmation dependency group and fails its minimum-size rule. Literal contents and fixed allocation have the same graphic likelihood. The bench freeze and inspection record that limitation. This proposal was written after the outcome and has no separate confirmation pass. The four-entry observation and its economic alternatives have the earlier authors listed above; no priority claim is made.

Kill condition

Drop the fixed-four claim after a secure new object with a different numeral in the exact short-stroke, plain-V and plain-fish construction. Drop the proposed unit role if an independent correspondence identifies V as an incompatible fixed name element or linked allotments contradict the numerical relation. M-928's long stroke and modified V and fish lie outside this declared construction and remain a broad comparison case.
