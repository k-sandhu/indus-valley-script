# Town-axis visual atlas

We made 32 graphics from the reviewed Q-1032 evidence. The atlas adds source plates and larger diagrams to the earlier presentation: the town-location frame, coverage matrices, published architectural axes, the Lothal discrepancy, Surkotada's wall picks and plan, phase and charcoal evidence, modern horizons, observer-location corrections and the deciding survey. The [scientific report](../../experiments/2026-10-24-q1032-town-axis-register/REPORT.md) remains the basis of the reading.

The current figure export is `run-v4`. The [35-page atlas](../../data/raw/2026-10-24-town-axis-visual-atlas/run-v4/town-axis-visual-atlas-2026-10-24.pdf) has a figure catalogue and bookmarks. The [graphics bundle](../../data/raw/2026-10-24-town-axis-visual-atlas/run-v4/town-axis-graphics-2026-10-24-complete.zip) contains each figure as PNG, SVG and an individual PDF, together with source notes, input hashes and the code. The PNGs are 3,040 pixels wide. SVG plots keep text and paths editable, while reproduced source plates retain their raster images inside the SVG.

All figures and build files live at `D:\projects\indus_valley_script\data\raw\2026-10-24-town-axis-visual-atlas`. The logical root is `data/raw/2026-10-24-town-axis-visual-atlas/`. Acquired imagery and scanned plans appear only in these local outputs. The whole atlas has mixed rights. Its source notes retain Esri attribution, the existing source-scan status and the Copernicus notice. Original project prose and aggregate plots follow CC0, and project code follows the Unlicense.

We acquired no new source and made no new archaeological measurement. The endpoint-error curve is an explicitly labelled sensitivity calculation. The horizon comparisons retain 30 usable profiles at 20 towns, with separate marks for different observer points. The scripts refuse to replace existing figure and atlas outputs.

The strongest reading remains that an architectural system in a building episode is the unit the evidence supports. A true-north survey with angular error below 0.5 degrees should measure the Moneer axes and HR First Street at Mohenjo-daro, Surkotada's IC ramparts and the Lothal dock. Freeze the wall identities and dated phase joins before reading. The reading predicts near-cardinal and turned systems at Mohenjo-daro and an anticlockwise turn greater than 5 degrees at Surkotada. A common axis within the surveyed errors would defeat that reading.
