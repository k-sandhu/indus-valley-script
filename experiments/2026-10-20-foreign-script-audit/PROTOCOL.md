# Source audit of circular seals found outside South Asia

We fix the frame to every `SEAL:C` face in the pinned 5,680-face Lipi export. This includes South Asian, foreign and unprovenanced objects. Cylinders are excluded from this bounded audit because they have a different geometry; the earlier result pooled them with circular stamps. The frame has 33 faces in 32 provisional families, including the already known Ur/Dilmun duplicate. `frame.json` and `frame-freeze.json` preserve this frame before new scoring.

We inspect every indexed image in the frame and every circular inscription reproduced in Laursen's Figure 11. Table 1 supplies object numbers, publication locators and contextual metadata. A figure drawing is a published transcription witness, and a photograph is a separate observation. We retain uncertainty about sign shape, gaps, reading order, physical seal versus impression and aliases. Figure 11 explicitly depicts impressions. A code change requires a joined identity and enough graphic evidence to identify the changed sign; a visual resemblance alone cannot establish a Wells-code mapping.

The old per-object scores were inadvertently displayed during orientation. This breaks blindness for the author. A separate reviewer receives opaque image IDs without scores or the identity key. The author's audit remains exploratory even when that review agrees. Revised annotations and any proposed code alternatives must be frozen before revised scores are computed. If the evidence cannot support a reliable transcription, we stop at the source audit and report the exact missing joins, forms and dates. We do not turn unresolved images into a forced sign string.

If a source-corrected analysis is feasible, we compare the original and revised score on the same objects, publish every inclusion and exclusion, match form, length, site and date where supported, and report leave-one-object and component influence. A comparison requiring absent dates is explicitly infeasible. Orientation alternatives are retained jointly with sign alternatives. The earlier foreign-minus-South-Asian round interval includes zero, so an unusual foreign sequence alone cannot identify a regional change.

We use the shared shots loader with a private copy of the spending ledger and explicitly allow spent access. We register one source-audit participant and freeze the protocol, frame and executable audit before confirmation access. This records procedural reuse, not fresh evidence: the full population and statistic were examined in October 12 work. No new seed restores freshness. The main endpoint is source review; no sounds, meanings or decipherment are submitted. The relevant versioned harness output is the shots synthetic check and spent-access record.

Primary endpoint: numbers of eligible faces, image-supported faces, secure Table 1 joins, reliable sign-code corrections, unresolved directions and dated matches. The pass rule for a corrected geographic comparison is complete source-supported variants on both sides with explicit comparable form and length; dated regional interpretation additionally requires comparable dates. Failure leaves a completed audit and an infeasibility statement. We propose no credence increase from spent scores. Sources, full strings, extracted text and detailed annotations stay under the local-only raw directory. Tracked files contain identifiers, source locators, our observations, aggregates and hashes.
