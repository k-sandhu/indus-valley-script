Indus survey hypotheses

Twenty-one regions of interest assembled from published research and additional preservation questions. The next task is to reconcile site records, then test selected landforms with equal-effort comparisons. Rectangles overlap and cannot be summed into a survey area. No new sites or field access were established. Source IDs resolve in sources-v5.json and the paper bibliography.

Shared sampling frame: A1km²cell eligible under more than one region belongs to one shared frame, carries every applicable label and is selected once. Correlated contexts remain together. P02known-margin transects are excluded from P01negative draws. Other overlaps follow this same shared-frame rule.

Assessments are editorial AI-assisted judgments; no expert ratings were elicited. I2/I1/I0 describe information, P2/P1/P0 describe preservation evidence, and F0/F1/F2 describe verified access. The full definitions are in the region register and paper methods.
