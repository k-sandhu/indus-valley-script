Indus survey hypotheses

Twenty-one coarse sampling windows assembled from published research and additional preservation hypotheses. These are proposed tests; this study inspected no new imagery and discovered no new sites. Rectangles overlap and cannot be summed into a survey area. Source IDs resolve in sources-v2.json / the paper bibliography.

P01 · Eastern Cholistan desert margins · Class A

Origin: published remote proposals

Missingness mechanism: dunes and incomplete legacy location records

Target: Khundowala and Mulhiawala proposals plus comparison desert margins

Competing explanation: Natural dune/soil anomalies or later mounds

Falsification test: Ground-check proposed clusters; date intact occupation and compare matched noncandidate tiles

Comparison stratum: Similar dunes and moisture settings without classifier peaks

Field feasibility: not assessed; permits, local collaboration, access and recent land use require verification

Sources: R08. Bounding rectangle west/south/east/north: [70.0, 27.8, 72.0, 29.8].

Missingness mechanism: different surface definitions and peripheral scatters
