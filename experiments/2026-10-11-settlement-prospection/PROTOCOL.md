## Evidence procedure

Follow source references and check recent work through 11 October 2026. Record publication and fieldwork dates separately, access depth, exact observation locators, and source limitations. Keep full downloaded witnesses in ignored `data/raw/2026-10-11-settlement-prospection/`; authored summaries and source pointers belong in the dated experiment. This is a structured narrative review, not an exhaustive systematic review with a complete bibliographic denominator.

Distinguish reported archaeological sites, remotely proposed mounds, rediscovered legacy entries, off-site activity areas, and geographic search hypotheses. A geographic window is a proposed sampling frame and must not be reported as a new archaeological discovery. Search priorities require a named place, a mechanism by which evidence may have been missed, supporting sources, competing explanations, a falsifiable field test, and a comparison stratum.
