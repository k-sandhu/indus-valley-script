# Historical denominator: bounded citation-lineage check

The Lucknow-hosted PDF was acquired in full and its count paragraph visually checked on printed p. 41 / PDF p. 3. It is a 37-page excerpt headed ‘CHAPTER-III HARAPPAN CIVILIZATION,’ running through printed p. 75. The institutional host and filename do not establish the original chapter author or publication date. [R01 teaching chapter](https://www.lkouniv.ac.in/site/writereaddata/siteContent/202003241550006972anil_kumar_HARAPPAN_CIVILIZATION%201.pdf)

Its attribution is exactly ‘According to Possehl (2002) and Setter and Korisettar (2002).’ It then reports at least 1,022 Mature-Harappan sites, comprising 406 in Pakistan and 616 in India, with about 96 excavated, comprising 41 and 55 respectively. Both subtotal sums agree. The rounded arithmetic ratio is approximately 9.4%.

The supplied excerpt has no bibliography and no title-bearing Possehl entry. A further in-text citation on printed p. 70 / PDF p. 32 gives Possehl 2002, p. 112, in a sculpture discussion, but does not identify the work either. Consequently this check cannot establish whether R01 means *The Indus Civilization: A Contemporary Perspective*, a chapter or another 2002 publication. The proposed book attribution remains unverified. Attempts to access the book's Google Books pages 20 and 112 failed; secondary search leads were not adopted as evidence.

Recommended manuscript wording: ‘The teaching chapter attributes its historical 1,022-site summary to Possehl (2002) and Setter and Korisettar (2002), without identifying titles in the supplied excerpt. Its approximately 96 excavated sites imply a historical ratio of about 9.4%. The relation between this compilation and R38's 1,056-site count has not been verified.’ This preserves the observed attribution while leaving the exact-work and common-inventory questions open.
