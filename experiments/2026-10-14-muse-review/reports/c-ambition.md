# Review: ambitious goals and research strategy

## 1. Verdict

The repository has built, in two weeks, the two things Indus research usually lacks: a measured account of what its own corpus does and does not contain, and a test discipline that can tell a real result from a fitted one. Its strongest results are structural and negative in a productive sense: sign order is real but not diagnostic of language, line breaks fall between recurring units, stroke values are tied to their neighbours more tightly than administrative counts are, long seal legends do not repeat while short ones recur across cities, and a published Sanskrit key does not stand clear of rearrangements of itself where a correct key, on synthetic corpora of the same shape, stands 27 to 72 standard deviations clear. The project has not yet produced a confirmed positive claim about what any sign or text means, and its own diagnosis explains why: meanings were assigned before the units of the script were fixed, the most informative objects were filtered out of the main populations, and breadth replaced accumulation. The turn of 12 October toward a complete object record, structure before meaning, and frozen two-stage tests is the correct response, and the first two shots show the machinery working, including the honest downgrading of a pass that rested on three seals. The ceiling for the next year is set by three scarcities, none of them computational: fresh inscription material that no analysis has seen, adjudicated object identity across catalogues, and specialist eyes on a small number of decisive photographs. A project that secures those three can credibly deliver, within twelve months, the field's reference object register, a tested segmentation of seal legends into recurring units, and one confirmed functional claim about a class of texts. Decipherment is not a credible aim on that horizon, and the repository is at its best when it says so plainly and aims at the step before it.

## 2. Observations

1. **The project knows its corpus quantitatively, which is rare and is its chief strategic asset.** The local corpus holds 4,695 provisional objects and 5,680 faces, with site known for 99.4% of objects but all three dimensions for only 28.8%, a period label for 55.8%, a local image for 54.6% after the 13 October work, a museum number for 11.5%, and a calendar date for none (`knowledge/DATA.md:3`, `knowledge/DATA.md:69-95`). Against the printed catalogues, 1,032 of 3,620 measured CISI 1 and 2 entries have no identifier match, and of the numbers that follow CISI 2 the export cites 1,291 of 2,040 for Mohenjo-daro and Harappa and 172 of 550 for four further sites (`knowledge/DATA.md:3`, `knowledge/DATA.md:93`). Object counts remain provisional because a Lipi family is not yet an adjudicated physical object (`knowledge/DATA.md:7`). Every structural claim in the working model is therefore a claim about a well-measured but incomplete and singly-transcribed sample, and the model itself puts complete data first among the things that would change it most (`knowledge/WORKING-MODEL.md:65`).

2. **The evidential machinery is stronger than the substantive results it has so far certified.** The two-stage design explores freely on a discovery half, freezes predictions with a recorded hash, and reads the confirmation half once through a logged loader (`experiments/2026-10-12-conjecture-shots/DESIGN.md:7-11`). The shared harness extends the same discipline to keys, meaning maps, restorations and segmentations, refuses to overwrite outputs, and keeps a ledger of statistic families already spent (`bench/README.md:19-21`, `AGENTS.md:12`). Applied honestly, it killed the project's own working reading: the 118-sign gloss map scored below the mean of rearrangements of its own labels on the confirmation half and also failed on the discovery half (`experiments/2026-10-12-conjecture-shots/shot-b-reading/REPORT.md:3`, `experiments/2026-10-12-conjecture-shots/shot-b-reading/REPORT.md:19`). It also produced the project's most exportable result, the calibration showing that dictionary readability accepts 92% to 100% of random strings while a likelihood criterion separates a true key from its rearrangements by tens of standard deviations (`papers/006-sanskrit-key-calibration/manuscript.md:7`, `knowledge/directions/key-calibration-2026-10-12.md:5-7`). No positive conjecture has yet passed this machinery cleanly: of five cards, one is dead at 0.02, one survives narrowed at 0.15, and three are open or strengthened on photographic readings that still await a specialist (`knowledge/conjectures/README.md:23-29`).

3. **The confirmation resource for the most-studied questions is already spent.** The October 12 split's confirmation half is recorded as spent for seal size, site and motif statistics, and new tests of those need new material or a new partition of unused objects (`knowledge/conjectures/README.md:17`, `experiments/2026-10-12-conjecture-shots/REVIEW-RESPONSE.md:27`). The design note is candid that the half was fresh only for the statistics the shots defined, since earlier packages had computed corpus-wide aggregates over it (`experiments/2026-10-12-conjecture-shots/DESIGN.md:17`). Shot B's report reaches the same conclusion from the other side: its reversed-locality pattern cannot be tested on either half, and the decisive test must be prospective, on texts none of the analyses has seen (`experiments/2026-10-12-conjecture-shots/shot-b-reading/REPORT.md:51`). This is the single most important strategic fact in the repository. Without a fresh population, further shots on seals can only re-partition examined objects, which the harness rules explicitly say does not establish freshness (`bench/README.md:21`).

4. **The strongest substantive cluster concerns what seal legends and sealings do, not what signs sound like.** No two of 445 seals with six or more signs share a text; the 65 recurring texts are mostly short and 41 occur at more than one site; 13 of 16 sealings matching a known seal match only seals from other sites (`README.md:11-12`, `knowledge/WORKING-MODEL.md:31-37`). The Lothal case gives this pattern a concrete instance: ten sealings carry the four-sign text of M-895 but were impressed by a different seal of equal face width with differently spaced signs, which weakens the single-travelling-seal account and leaves an office held in two cities, or one holder with two seals, as the live readings (`knowledge/conjectures/C-0004-m895-office-text.md:42`). The conjecture stands at 0.6, the highest in the set, with its animal prediction unresolved because two AI readers disagree on M-895 (`knowledge/conjectures/C-0004-m895-office-text.md:30`). The working model's three-jobs account, seals as identity legends, Harappa tablets as tiered tokens, pottery marks as labels, at 0.6, organises these results into a testable whole (`knowledge/WORKING-MODEL.md:11-13`).

5. **The strongest methodological lever is segmentation, and it is still unbuilt.** On 69 seals with catalogued line divisions, the text of each line recurs elsewhere more often than a stretch crossing the break, 23 seals to 4 with 42 ties (`README.md:13`). The structure-first diagnosis and the 12 October synthesis both identify the consequence: recurring units should be counted as units rather than as sign sequences, and a segmenter should be trained on the observed divisions (`knowledge/directions/what-12-october-established.md:23`, `knowledge/MAP.md:141`). Until that exists, every downstream question, alternation of stem and ending, the two-part name-and-title structure at credence 0.45 (`knowledge/WORKING-MODEL.md:17-19`), and any comparison of language families, is being asked of the wrong units. The rehearsal result counsels modesty about what segmentation alone will prove: at Indus scale the best unsupervised segmenter only slightly beats cutting everywhere, even on scripts that can be read (`knowledge/MAP.md:99`, `knowledge/directions/structure-first-2026-10-12.md:65`). Supervision from the 118 multi-line faces is therefore the difference between a usable tool and another null result.

6. **Two conjectures are testable now with held data and have simply not been run.** The unicorn-affiliation card's first prediction, that rarer animals are concentrated in fewer sites than their counts predict while unicorns follow site totals, needs only square seals with a known animal at adequately sampled sites (`knowledge/conjectures/C-0003-unicorn-affiliation.md:30`). The tablet-tier card's first prediction, stability of the two-, three- and four-stroke proportions across Harappa periods, needs only the period labels already in the corpus, with power stated (`knowledge/conjectures/C-0005-tablet-tiers.md:30`). Both cards have sat untested since 12 October while effort went to packaging and to new lines. Their second predictions are genuinely data-blocked: same-tier find-context clustering needs Harappa context registers (`knowledge/conjectures/C-0005-tablet-tiers.md:31`, `knowledge/DATA.md:214`), and the prospective predictions need new publications. The gap between what is blocked and what is merely unrun is where a week's work buys the most credence movement.

7. **The 820 result is a warning about small-group inference, correctly absorbed.** Shot A's frozen four-sign prediction passed at p = 0.021, but adversarial review showed the pass depends on three seals ending in 820, fails a rank version and a Holm correction, and that first signs carry as much size information as last signs on the confirmation half (`knowledge/conjectures/C-0001-rank-written-twice.md:67`). The project narrowed the claim to 820 after the outcome, recorded that it has had no test of its own, and set the review credence to 0.15 against a frozen-rule value of 0.24 (`knowledge/conjectures/C-0001-rank-written-twice.md:31`, `knowledge/conjectures/C-0001-rank-written-twice.md:68`). The seven rules added for later shots, joint credence rules for predictions sharing objects, influence criteria for small groups, split-half reliability before freezing, and site tests that exclude or stratify Mohenjo-daro, are the right institutional memory (`experiments/2026-10-12-conjecture-shots/REVIEW-RESPONSE.md:21-27`). The remaining risk is that the narrowed 820 claim, which is exactly the kind of post-outcome refinement the design exists to distrust, quietly acquires the status of a finding. Its only honest test is prospective, on the unphotographed 820 seals and on new material, with a rank or leave-one-out rule (`knowledge/WORKING-MODEL.md:67`).

8. **A large share of past effort went to measurements that cannot separate the hypotheses in play, and the project has already diagnosed this.** The structure-first memo counts 33 of 152 registry entries working only on invented or simulated data, 29 of the 46 entries on real material being variants of two statistics first computed on 1 October, and restoration accuracy rising from 33.8% to 41.2% without changing any belief about the script, because the same gains appear for an invented office-routing code and for every readable comparison corpus (`knowledge/directions/structure-first-2026-10-12.md:11`, `knowledge/directions/structure-first-2026-10-12.md:19`). It also records that 596 proposed meanings were constructed to cover 713 codes before the sign list itself was tested, and that the candidate language was inherited from sources rather than compared under matched freedom (`knowledge/directions/structure-first-2026-10-12.md:15`). Three of the six manuscripts, on conditional reference laws, physical observations in numerical decoding, and record-model selection, are methods papers whose evidence is synthetic (`papers/002-conditional-reference-laws/manuscript.md:7`, `papers/003-physical-observations/manuscript.md:7`, `papers/004-reference-model-selection/manuscript.md:7`). They are competent controls, but each further enumeration of this kind now has near-zero expected value for the script itself, a point the memo's pause list already makes (`knowledge/directions/structure-first-2026-10-12.md:55`).

9. **Breadth remains the standing temptation, and the question queue shows both the cure and the disease.** The priority-1 queue is well aimed: identity blocks, face completeness, dimension normalisation, chronology tracing, the CISI 2 basic-data table, CISI 3.1 acquisition, the identifier crosswalk and the register build (`knowledge/MAP.md:109-129`). That is the data-first priority of 12 October (`AGENTS.md:5`) correctly operationalised. But the wider tree still carries programmes outside the script, settlement prospection, societal persistence, early Indian continuity, and a settlement manuscript that is a narrative review with no new fieldwork or imagery analysis (`papers/005-settlement-survey-gaps/manuscript.md:3`, `knowledge/MAP.md:368-376`). The roadmap written on 2 October already warned that the next cycle should have one primary target, predicting an externally observed use or quantity on a new artifact family (`knowledge/directions/research-roadmap.md:25`). With one maintainer integrating many agents, every additional programme taxes the scarcest resource, which is not agent hours but freeze discipline, review attention and the maintainer's hand-run build and check cycle (`AGENTS.md:55`, `bench/README.md:25`).

10. **Publication readiness is uneven, and the most publishable work is not the most central.** The Sanskrit-key calibration is a complete, self-contained paper with a clear external audience: it reconstructs a public key exactly, measures the redundancy of what the key actually fixes at 0.167 against an assumed 0.7, and leaves behind a procedure any future key must pass (`papers/006-sanskrit-key-calibration/manuscript.md:7`). The prediction paper is likewise complete but states plainly that submission needs a reproducible access route for a derivative export with unresolved redistribution terms, and independent epigraphic review of transcriptions checked incompletely against photographs (`papers/001-artifact-aware-prediction/manuscript.md:3`, `papers/001-artifact-aware-prediction/manuscript.md:57-59`). That rights question is general: the Lipi export has no licence at the pinned commit and its image crops reproduce CISI photographs, so record-level publication of the planned register depends on a reuse basis that is still only a question card (`knowledge/DATA.md:15`, `knowledge/DATA.md:216`, `knowledge/DATA.md:257`). The project could therefore finish a technically complete register that it cannot publish at record level. Settling the reuse basis early, even if the answer is restrictive, is worth more than several analysis packages.

11. **The data that would unlock the largest step is mostly identified, priced and ranked already.** The acquisition table puts work on held sources first, CISI basic data, the crosswalk and the register, then purchases and requests (`knowledge/DATA.md:196-227`). CISI 3.1 is the pivotal purchase: 749 numbers in its range are absent from the export, 244 of them among seals and tablets, and the volume carries photographs of every side for the two largest sites (`knowledge/DATA.md:123`, `knowledge/DATA.md:207`). Those absent seals and tablets are, in effect, a pre-packaged fresh confirmation population, the one resource observation 3 shows the project has exhausted. ICIT, with 4,665 objects and some 16,000 photographs or drawings, would supply the denominator for completeness, but access is by request, the database currently returns an authorisation error, and no terms are stated (`knowledge/DATA.md:113`). HARP registers would unblock the tablet-tier clustering test but require a maintainer request with no public dataset to fall back on (`knowledge/DATA.md:141`). The ranking in the repository is sound; the risk is sequencing, since CISI 3.1 sits at rank 8 behind held-source transcription that does not create fresh test material (`knowledge/DATA.md:202-207`).

12. **Specialist judgement is a named dependency at exactly the decisive points.** The office-text conjecture turns on the animal of M-895, which two AI readers could not settle (`knowledge/conjectures/C-0004-m895-office-text.md:42`). The working model lists a specialist reading of that animal second among the observations that would change it most (`knowledge/WORKING-MODEL.md:66`). The chart atlas carries 687 cells of AI annotation without expert check (`knowledge/DATA.md:23`), the prediction manuscript awaits independent epigraphic review (`papers/001-artifact-aware-prediction/manuscript.md:3`), and the roadmap's paired-surface and manufacture lines both state that they need expert image review (`knowledge/directions/research-roadmap.md:33-34`). A project staffed by agents and one maintainer can manufacture analysis without limit; it cannot manufacture the authority to say what animal is carved on a worn seal. Small, specific, well-prepared requests to named specialists are therefore on the critical path, not an embellishment.

## 3. Recommendations

Ranked by value for effort. Each respects the standing constraints, no automation, frozen outputs never overwritten, local-only raw corpora, unless it says otherwise.

**R1. Treat CISI 3.1 as the next confirmation corpus, and buy it before finishing the held-source transcription queue.** Concrete steps: the maintainer takes the purchase decision on `knowledge/questions/Q-1008-acquire-cisi-3-1.md` now rather than at its current rank of 8 (`knowledge/DATA.md:207`); on arrival, an agent transcribes only the absent-number seals and tablets first, the 244 in seal and tablet blocks (`knowledge/DATA.md:93`), into a new dated package, and a second agent who has not seen the transcriptions freezes prospective predictions against them before any site, size or motif field is read. Register the frozen predictions for the narrowed 820 claim with a rank rule, for the reversed-locality statistic from Shot B with its direction fixed in advance (`experiments/2026-10-12-conjecture-shots/shot-b-reading/REPORT.md:51`), and for the office-text prediction that any further occurrence appears with a third animal or at a third site (`knowledge/conjectures/C-0004-m895-office-text.md:32`). Cost and breakage: a purchase, days of transcription, and discipline, since the material must stay unread for the frozen fields until predictions are hashed; nothing existing moves, so evidence links, the registry and `kb.py` are untouched, and the site needs no change. Success looks like this: at least three frozen predictions scored on objects no prior package has examined, with credences moved by the pre-stated amounts, whatever the outcomes. This single step converts the project's best machinery from re-partitioning spent data to genuine prospective testing, and it is the cheapest fresh population available anywhere in the field.

**R2. Finish the register spine: CISI 1 and 2 basic data, the identifier crosswalk, then the versioned object register, in that order, as one staffed programme.** Concrete steps: complete the remaining basic-data blocks, estimated in the repository at about 2.8 hours for CISI 2 and 2.4 for CISI 1 of double-read agent work (`knowledge/DATA.md:202`); build the crosswalk joining CISI, Mahadevan 1977, the 1979 texts, excavation and museum numbers with explicit relation types (`knowledge/DATA.md:172`, `knowledge/questions/Q-1013-cross-catalogue-identifier-crosswalk.md`); then build the register in the target schema with a value status on every field (`knowledge/DATA.md:167`, `knowledge/questions/Q-1030-object-register-build.md`). Staff it as paired lanes, one agent transcribing a 30-object block, a second independently reading the same block blind, disagreements adjudicated into the record rather than averaged away, following the pilot procedure that reached 90.5% cell agreement with 119 of 1,635 cells left bracketed (`knowledge/DATA.md:46`). Cost and breakage: this is the largest single block of agent labour in the queue, and it will surface identity conflicts that force re-counts of earlier coverage figures; those re-counts must go to new dated outputs, never edits of frozen runs. The register build will also require a maintainer `kb.py build` and check cycle and a site rebuild by hand. Success is checkable: one regenerable record per adjudicated object, museum-number coverage rising from 11.5% toward the ceiling the basic data allows, every earlier headline percentage reproducible from the register, and the 1,032 unmatched CISI entries each given a disposition (`knowledge/questions/Q-1007-absent-cisi-keys-disposition.md`). At three months this is the deliverable the project can most credibly put its name to: a complete, citable register of every catalogued inscribed object with sourced fields for identity, faces, images, dimensions with units of record, material, findspot, stratum, museum accession and publication, published at whatever record level the rights allow.

**R3. Settle the Lipi reuse basis in parallel with R2, before the register is finished.** Concrete steps: the maintainer writes to the Lipi maintainer seeking explicit terms for record-level metadata reuse, as scoped in `knowledge/questions/Q-1028-lipi-reuse-basis.md`; whatever the answer, record it in `knowledge/provenance/REDISTRIBUTION.md` and design the register's public form around it, full records if permitted, identifiers and project-observed fields only if not. Cost: one maintainer letter and possibly an unwelcome answer. Breakage: none to existing files; a restrictive answer changes only what the register may publish, which is far cheaper to learn now than after the build. Success: a written basis, or a documented refusal with a register design that complies with it, so that no completed work is stranded unpublished. This recommendation goes slightly against the grain of deferring maintainer decisions until cards have prepared them (`knowledge/DATA.md:196`); the card has done its preparing, and the dependency now sits underneath R2, R6 and every manuscript that uses the export.

**R4. Build the line-division segmenter, freeze a unit inventory, and make units the default population for structural work.** Concrete steps: audit the catalogue line-division locators against the plates (`knowledge/questions/Q-0027-audit-catalogue-line-division-locators.md`); train and score segmenters against the observed divisions with the harness's segmentation task, reporting precision, recall and line-break recall against the readable-script baselines (`bench/README.md:11`, `knowledge/questions/Q-0028-train-segmentation-on-observed-divisions.md`); publish the resulting recurring-unit inventory as a versioned project output; then re-run exactly two downstream tests on units rather than signs, the closing-formula structure behind the name-and-title claim and a Kober-style alternation count with its readable-script control, since the rehearsal showed alternation does not discriminate at Indus scale on signs and the question is whether units change that (`knowledge/directions/structure-first-2026-10-12.md:65`). Combine this with the base-plus-modifier recoding done blind to catalogue order (`knowledge/questions/Q-0026-recode-sign-families-without-catalogue-order.md`), so the unit inventory rests on a tested sign list rather than on the Wells numbering's built-in grouping, which already captures most of the family gain (`knowledge/WORKING-MODEL.md:57`). Cost: weeks of agent work and one careful freeze; breakage is nil for frozen evidence, but every later structural claim must state which unit version it uses, and `bench/spent.json` must record the new statistic families. Success: a segmenter that beats the cut-everywhere baseline on held-out line divisions by a pre-stated margin, and a unit inventory stable enough that two independent agents segment the same unseen seals identically. At six months, this plus R1 gives the project's best shot at a confirmed structural result of a stated kind: that seal legends compose a variable opening unit with a closing formula drawn from a small closed stock, demonstrated on fresh CISI 3.1 seals segmented by rules frozen before those seals were read.

**R5. Run the two unblocked conjecture tests immediately, and convert the seal-sealing pattern into a census with specialist-coded motifs.** Concrete steps: score the unicorn concentration prediction and the tablet-tier stability prediction (`knowledge/conjectures/C-0003-unicorn-affiliation.md:30`, `knowledge/conjectures/C-0005-tablet-tiers.md:30`) as ordinary frozen evaluations on held data, with the Mohenjo-daro stratification and reliability checks the new shot rules require (`knowledge/conjectures/README.md:14-15`); verify the animals on the matched sealings (`knowledge/questions/Q-0014-verify-the-animals-on-the-matched-sealings.md`); and prepare the M-895 animal question as a one-page specialist brief with the best available images, the two AI readings stated neutrally, and the exact credence consequence of each answer (`knowledge/conjectures/C-0004-m895-office-text.md:30`). Cost: days. Breakage: none; these use held data and existing cards, though the site and motif statistics must be logged against the spent-half rules and interpreted as frozen evaluations rather than fresh confirmation. Success: two cards moved decisively, up or down, and the office-text conjecture either at 0.65 with both animals confirmed or at 0.25 with a shared animal. The strongest lever toward a real advance is this cluster, recurring short legends as office titles operating across cities, because it is the only positive account that simultaneously explains unique long legends, cross-site duplicates, cross-site sealings and the unicorn exception, and because it makes risky predictions about objects rather than about sign statistics. The single decisive test that would move it most is a deposit-level one the working model already names: one excavated context containing both seals and their sealings, where the same issuer should appear on both (`knowledge/WORKING-MODEL.md:68`). Since no such deposit is in hand, the attainable version is the enlarged sealing census: every sealing joined to its text and motif from the register, testing whether cross-site matches survive complete data at anything like 13 of 16.

**R6. Publish in the order the evidence supports: the key calibration first, the register second, the prediction paper third.** Concrete steps: assign authorship and venue to `papers/006-sanskrit-key-calibration/manuscript.md` and submit it, since it needs no new data and its procedure is the project's most reusable export; release the harness publicly with its synthetic selftest as the citable companion, which a fresh clone can already run without restricted inputs (`bench/README.md:23`); write the register paper once R2 lands, aimed at a data or digital-archaeology venue, since a complete sourced object record is the contribution other researchers can build on regardless of decipherment views; hold `papers/001-artifact-aware-prediction/manuscript.md` until R3 settles its access route and a specialist has reviewed its transcriptions, as its own status note requires (`papers/001-artifact-aware-prediction/manuscript.md:3`). Cost: maintainer time on submission correspondence, and the discipline to let three methods manuscripts (`papers/002-conditional-reference-laws/manuscript.md`, `papers/003-physical-observations/manuscript.md`, `papers/004-reference-model-selection/manuscript.md`) wait or be folded into the harness documentation rather than submitted as separate claims on the field's attention. Breakage: none to the repository; publication will invite scrutiny of the AI-authorship disclosure, which the manuscripts already state. Success at six to twelve months: one paper in review or in print, a public benchmark that at least one outside group has run against its own key or map, and a register release with a persistent identifier that outside work cites. An open benchmark others actually use is worth more to this project than a further internal result, because it recruits the field's decipherment claims into the project's own test discipline.

**R7. Stop four lines of work explicitly, and record the stop in the registry so agents do not restart them.** The lines are: further restoration and Markov-order variants on the same hidden positions, which the roadmap already gates on new reviewed transcriptions or a specified new diagnostic (`knowledge/directions/research-roadmap.md:81-84`); further synthetic enumerations of null laws and artificial mapping systems beyond the harness's existing controls (`knowledge/directions/structure-first-2026-10-12.md:55`); extension of proposed sign meanings to unreviewed codes before R4 fixes the units (`knowledge/directions/structure-first-2026-10-12.md:15`); and new programmes outside the script, including further settlement and societal-persistence work, until the register and one prospective shot have reported. Concrete steps: add a short stopped-lines section to `knowledge/directions/research-roadmap.md` naming each line, its retry condition copied from the existing retry table, and the date; point `AGENTS.md` orientation at it; and let `python tools/kb.py next` surface only work inside the active lanes. Cost: some agent proposals will be turned away, and the settlement manuscript stays a working paper. Breakage: none to evidence; retry conditions keep every line revivable on genuinely new inputs. Success: over the next month, a clear majority of new dated packages fall in the data, segmentation and conjecture-test lanes, measurable from the registry areas.

**R8. Reorganise agent work into three lanes with a weekly freeze, adversarial pairing as the default, and a fixed confirmation budget.** The lanes follow the repository's own structure. A data lane works in 30-object blocks (`knowledge/questions/Q-0001-review-a-30-object-identity-block.md`), always in blind pairs, one producing and one independently reading, handing adjudicated blocks to the maintainer for the register build. A structure lane owns R4 and the conjecture tests, and may explore freely only on designated discovery material. A verification lane, staffed by agents who did not produce the work under review, reproduces one result per week from its tracked aggregates (`knowledge/questions/Q-0038-reproduce-one-manuscript-claim-from-tracked-aggregates.md`, `knowledge/questions/Q-0033-independently-reproduce-a-reference-calibration.md`) and writes the adversarial review for every shot before its credences are integrated, on the model that caught the 820 dependence. Cadence, without any automation: the maintainer declares one freeze day per week on which all pending predictions for shared material are hashed together, satisfying the rule that shots sharing a confirmation half freeze before any reads it (`knowledge/conjectures/README.md:11`); confirmation reads happen only after a freeze, against a budget the maintainer sets in advance, for example no more than two confirmation reads per month and none on a spent statistic family, checked by hand against `bench/spent.json` (`AGENTS.md:12`). Hand-offs are files, not conversations: every lane ends a task with its dated package, its registry entry and a proposed card edit inside its own directory, leaving generated files to the maintainer as now (`AGENTS.md:55`). Add one external role the current organisation lacks, a named specialist correspondent for image questions, engaged per question rather than as a co-author in waiting. Cost: slower starts, since nothing confirms mid-week, and some duplicate reading labour. Breakage: none to existing rules; this recommendation argues for tightening, not relaxing, the no-automation constraint, since the weekly freeze is a human calendar act. Success: zero unlogged confirmation reads, every integrated credence change traceable to a freeze hash and an adversarial review, and verification packages appearing at the same rate as discovery packages.

**R9. Open the outside engagements in dependency order: specialists, concordance holders, museums, then venues.** First, two or three named Indus specialists receive the narrow briefs from R5, the M-895 animal and a sample of chart-atlas cells for spot adjudication, because these unlock existing conjectures and cost the recipients an hour rather than a collaboration. Second, the maintainer writes to the ICIT administrator requesting access terms and to the Iravatham Mahadevan chair at the Roja Muthiah Research Library about the planned expanded concordance, which the working model already identifies as a source of unseen texts (`knowledge/WORKING-MODEL.md:69`, `knowledge/DATA.md:115`); both letters should offer the register schema and the crosswalk work as the project's contribution in return, since a request accompanied by a useful artefact is more likely to succeed than one asking only for data. Third, approach the two or three museums whose unjoined records and CC0 photographs are already inventoried (`knowledge/DATA.md:148-149`) for findspot information that would make their unprovenanced seals joinable, a small ask with a concrete payoff in the register. Fourth, submit and present, the calibration paper to a journal, the register to a Computer Applications in Archaeology audience of the kind that published the ICIT count the project relies on (`knowledge/DATA.md:3`). Cost: maintainer correspondence over months, with slow and uncertain replies; none of the first three steps should block R1, R2 or R4, which proceed on held or purchasable material. Breakage: sharing the schema before publication is a deliberate, low risk, since the project's licences already dedicate its own material to reuse (`README.md:90`). Success: at twelve months, at least one specialist adjudication integrated into a card, one institutional reply that changes a coverage figure or a denominator, and the benchmark run by someone outside the project.

**R10. Set the three horizons explicitly in the working model, with kill criteria for the project itself.** Three months: the register spine of R2 complete for CISI 1 and 2, the reuse basis answered, C-0003 and C-0005 first predictions scored, and CISI 3.1 in hand with frozen prospective predictions registered against its absent numbers. Six months: the register released in a citable version, the segmenter and unit inventory frozen, the first prospective shot on CISI 3.1 material scored, and the calibration paper submitted with the harness public.
