# Susa source recovery

This run follows the marked-weight census in `experiments/2026-10-02-connections-and-priority/regional-west/`. Its purpose is to recover independent dates, find contexts, original measurements and clear photographs for the exact objects already identified. The completed census and numerical endpoint remain frozen.

The targets are SB 13677 / AS 2615, SB 13323 / AS 9546 and SB 17774 / AS 5724; the limestone ducks SB 9342 / AS 13821, SB 9340 / AS 13820 and SB 13748 / SH 095373; the cross-marked ovoids SB 13552 / AS 9522, SB 13561 / AS 9520 and SB 13177; and the pebbles SB 13564 and SB 15535. Soutzo's 1911 descriptions and Ascalone's 2023 chapter are source targets. A Dholavira context join may be attempted after these acquisitions.

Museum inventory numbers supply the object join. Creation dates and deposition dates are separate fields. A material or unit label is retained with its source and the source's dating precision. Photographs are inspected for the marked face and recorded at their acquired resolution. A source that gives only a general period cannot supply a common excavation layer.

The analysis changes only if a new witness changes a measurement, graphic count, object identity or dated comparison group. Existing fit tolerances are not recomputed merely because another copy of an old interpretation was located. An authenticated unit inscription would provide a stronger calibration than the prior mass-based reconstruction.

Downloaded metadata, books and photographs remain local-only under the assigned raw directory. Manifests retain exact URLs, retrieval times, hashes, rights notices and transformations. Repository-facing tables contain our observations and source pointers. The final report distinguishes new acquisition from prior interpretation and identifies the observation needed to complete each join.
