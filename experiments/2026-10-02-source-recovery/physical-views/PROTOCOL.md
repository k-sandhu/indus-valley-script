# Registered physical-view recovery

This source-recovery branch asks whether new public records can resolve the Harappa count and variant exceptions or expose additional Lothal contact and cut surfaces. The earlier native drawing and halftone photograph readings are preserved. An acquisition qualifies as new information when it adds a registered photograph, mould, adjoining-face view, original scan edition, source registration or collection-record locator.

## Fixed targets and comparison observations

Harappa targets are H2238, H2230, H2234 and H2237. Their published excavation registrations are H95-2613/6560-01, H97-3311/8040-10, H97-3322/8040-16 and H96-3125/6937-16. All four belong to the published third engraver group. The remaining registered members of that group provide same-production comparison targets. H2239, which has a fully readable existing count profile, belongs to Group 2 and can serve as a separate cross-group visibility comparison.

Lothal targets are G01 members L190, L193 and L206, and G02 members L161, L162, L163, L165 and L168. G01 needs contact junctions, adjoining surfaces and whole impressions. G02 needs complete marked margins, side/rear coverage and whole impressions. Group membership describes the preserved inscription strings; a same-die match requires its own physical comparison.

## Acquisition and coding

Search exact catalogue and excavation identifiers, original excavation/catalogue repositories, author publications and institutional collection records. Inspect bibliographic witnesses and source captions before making any image join. Keep search terms, routes, access times, failed attempts, redirects and acquired-file hashes. Stop repeating an inaccessible route unless a new URL, edition or service changes the attempt.

For each new witness, record its source, exact registration basis, page/figure or record coordinate, image dimensions and parent/crop hashes. Record which surfaces are visible, whether adjoining faces establish orientation, and whether groove boundaries can be counted. Retain unreadable marks and candidate sets. A group photograph without individual registrations remains a group photograph.

Downloaded third-party documents, pixels, HTML and detailed extraction remain local under the matching raw directory. Tracked outputs contain our summaries, short necessary attributed examples, identifiers, hashes, access records, code and acquisition instructions. No contacts or requests will be sent.

## Bounded outcomes

An individually registered new Harappa view can test whether H2238 preserves three or four A-face grooves and whether H2237's C construction differs from the production controls. New Lothal views can test an additional contact junction or delimit a complete cut-count surface. If views remain unavailable, preserve concrete catalogue/holding/record locators and a locally written observation-request packet specifying the required views and registration checks.

This branch uses source-informed image observations. It acquires no external quantity or named referent by assuming one from a graphic count. Its output is a reviewed source packet and explicit next observation, with no prediction-model rerun.

## Verification and closure

Verify every acquired source hash and size, each target-registration join against the existing caption ledger, and every coded view against its caption/source record. Report new registered-view counts separately from source locators and reused witnesses. Save a report, retrospective, source manifest, access log, coded observations and manual verification. Preserve the protocol bytes before searching and retain failed routes.
