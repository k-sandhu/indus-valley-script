# Oxus original-source recovery protocol

Frozen before inspecting the recovered original figures on 2026-10-02. This is a source-informed exploratory read: the earlier Bonora et al. discussion has already supplied the proposed Gonur total of 26 and an interpretation of the Altyn Depe object.

Recover Sarianidi's 1998 publication and identify Fig. 41 from its own caption and nearby text. Record the publication's stated find location, inventory references, associated objects and chronology, preserving any distinction between a room description and a numbered archaeological context. A reported hoard total and the number of forms visible in a figure are separate measures. Count pictured forms only where their outlines are readable; record repeated views, overlap, damage and unresolved object joins. Use ordinary shape descriptions, and record holes or impressions without assigning numerical values.

Recover Kircho's 2008b chapter in the original collective monograph and inspect Plate 94.2. Link the image to its catalogue description, excavation area, room and layer only where the source supplies that link. Record the number of labelled objects and views, broad surface geometry and the visible organization of marks. If individual depressions cannot be separated reliably from texture, count only clearly bounded mark groups and explain the image limit.

Read bibliographic and contextual text before coding figures. Save unchanged page renders and crops under the owned local-only raw directory with parent-file hashes and coordinates. No image enhancement, synthetic examples, learned classifier, sign transcription or semantic interpretation is part of this test. Recheck each count against the full figure, and write any refinement as a separate observation field.

The output consists of source and access manifests, source-linked observations, a bounded report, a read-only checker and results, and a retrospective. Failed ordinary access routes remain in the manifest. Raw PDFs, HTML, extracted text and page images remain local-only under the repository redistribution policy.
