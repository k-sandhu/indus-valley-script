# Amendment 2: transliteration conversion by source

Written 21 October 2026 (UTC) while the transcription runs were in progress, before any record was normalised and before any statistic was computed. [SCHEMA.md](SCHEMA.md) and [Amendment 1](AMENDMENT-1.md) apply the conversion from the Survey's older transliteration to all three annual reports. The OCR draft of Bloch's list (*Annual Report 1903–04*, pp. 101–120) shows that Bloch writes the Indological convention instead: *Candragupta*, *Ghaṭotkacagupta*, *Kṛṣṇagupta*, *Kumārāmātyādhikaraṇa*, with `c` for the unaspirated palatal, `ch` for the aspirate, `ṣ` and `ṛ`. Marshall (1911–12: *Vichhi*, *Pushyavriddhi*, *Sreshthi*) and Spooner (1913–14: *Vishnudāsa*, *Sreshṭhi*) write the older convention. Applying the conversion to Bloch would turn his aspirate `ch` into `c`.

The conversion is therefore chosen per source: the older-convention mapping of SCHEMA.md (`chh` → `ch`, then `ch` → `c`, `sh` → `ṣ`, `ṛi` → `ṛ`, `ṁ` → `ṃ`, `ç` → `ś`) applies to `asi-ar-1911-12` and `asi-ar-1913-14`; for `asi-ar-1903-04` only `ṁ` → `ṃ` and `ç` → `ś` apply. If the transcription notes for a source record a different convention from the page images, the notes decide, and the choice is written into the normalisation output for each source.
