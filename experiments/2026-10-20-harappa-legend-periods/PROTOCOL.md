# Protocol: Harappa seal legends through dated periods

Package `2026-10-20-harappa-legend-periods`, written 20 October 2026 before the confirmation read. It tests conjecture [C-0010](../../knowledge/conjectures/C-0010-seal-legend-fixed-pattern.md), the successor reading of the [Mohenjo-daro stratigraphy shot](../2026-10-20-mohenjo-daro-stratigraphy/REPORT.md), with the two-stage design of the [conjecture shots](../2026-10-12-conjecture-shots/DESIGN.md) and its [seven later rules](../2026-10-12-conjecture-shots/REVIEW-RESPONSE.md#rules-for-the-next-shots). The predictions are in [PREDICTIONS.md](PREDICTIONS.md).

## Question

Did the seal legend change at Harappa between the end of Period 3B and the end of Period 3C, while the seal itself changed form? C-0010 says it did not: the closing stock, the choice of opening and closing sign, the variant cut within a sign class, the length of the legend and its sign inventory stayed as they were. At Mohenjo-daro no part of the legend changed with depth in either excavation, but Marshall's sections may span little time and the rise of rectangular seals seen in DK-G did not repeat there. The test here needs a sequence whose order is known independently of the legends and which demonstrably spans a change in the seals.

## Data and the sequence

The legends and the chronology fields come from the Lipi export `data/normalized/lipi_observed_faces.jsonl` (SHA-256 `1220f559…`). Harappa has 428 seal families in it. A seal's place in time is printed in two ways. Seals from M. S. Vats's excavations carry his stratum in the `phase` field (Stratum I is the top of a mound, so the latest), and the export assigns each stratum of each mound a HARP period in the `time` field. Seals from the Harappa Archaeological Research Project (HARP, CISI numbers H-1521 to H-2583) carry HARP's own period label (3A, 3B, 3B/C, 3C).

HARP's own seals are too few for a within-mound test of legends: 36 families carry a HARP period label, 18 of them from Mound E. The longest within-mound sequence of seals in the export is Vats's Mound F, where 175 seal families have a stratum (Mound AB, the next, has 46). Vats (1940, *Excavations at Harappa*, vol. I, pp. 9–10; archive.org `in.gov.ignca.18220`) placed Strata I to III of Mound F in his Late period and IV to VII in his Intermediate period, and observed that the lower strata hold an increasing share of "tiny seals and sealings", which Kenoyer and Meadow later identified as inscribed tablets. The export maps the Mound F strata to HARP periods as follows: Stratum VII to 3B-1, VI and V to 3B/C, IV to 3C-1, III to 3C-2, II to 3C-3 and I to 3C-4. The held files do not document the source of this mapping ([Q-1021](../../knowledge/questions/Q-1021-chronology-calendar-ranges.md)); we use only the order of Vats's strata, which is his own, and read the HARP labels as the calendar meaning of that order. Kenoyer and Meadow date Period 3B to about 2450–2200 BCE and 3C to about 2200–1900 BCE ("Inscribed Objects from Harappa Excavations 1986–2007", CISI 3.1, 2010, p. xlv, Table 1), and HARP checked Vats's sequence by cutting back his section in Trench I of Mound F (p. xlix). The Mound F sequence therefore runs from late 3B to the end of the Harappan occupation, roughly three to four centuries, through about 20 ft of deposit (the printed depths of the seals run from about 20 ft in Stratum VII to mostly under 3 ft in Stratum I).

## Partition

The confirmation population is every Harappa seal family from Mound F with a Vats stratum: one mound, one excavator and one label source. The discovery population is every other Harappa seal family with a chronology label: Vats's stratified seals from Mound AB, Mound D, Area J, Area G and Area H, HARP's period-labelled seals from Mounds E, F and AB, and six Area H seals with an export period only (109 families). Thirty Mound F seals from Vats's excavations carry an export period (3B/C) but no stratum; they belong to the confirmation mound and source without its clock, and are held out of both populations. The 114 Harappa seals with no chronology label are undated.

The split keeps every confirmation seal, and its stratum, out of the exploration. Parameters that need no date, the closing stock and the pool of legends for the power simulation, come from all 253 Harappa seals outside the confirmation population (discovery, held-out and undated), and from the closing stock of Mackay's DK-G seals frozen by the Mohenjo-daro package. [build_inputs.py](build_inputs.py) writes the two populations, one row per family, with the legend face chosen by the rule of the stratigraphy package (the longest complete text without unknown signs or blanks). The rows stay local under `data/raw/2026-10-20-harappa-legend-periods/`; counts and hashes are in [inputs/inputs-summary.json](inputs/inputs-summary.json).

The bench shots loader enforces the freeze. Its input is the confirmation file alone; it closes families over CISI aliases and identical texts and assigns components at random to two halves. Both halves are confirmation material. [confirm.py](confirm.py) reads the confirmation half, which the loader refuses before every registered prediction is frozen, then the other half, and scores the pooled population once. The two halves are also scored separately as a reported replication.

## The clock and the frames

A seal's clock value u is the mid-rank of its stratum among the Mound F seals, scaled from 0 (oldest) to 1 (latest). With the population's stratum counts the early third (u < 1/3) is Strata VII to V (HARP 3B-1 and 3B/C) and the late third (u ≥ 2/3) is Strata II and I (3C-3 and 3C-4); Strata IV and III form the middle. Vats's strata are building levels defined across the mound, so u is ranked over the whole mound.

Every null permutes u among the analysed units within frames. A frame is one of Vats's trenches in Mound F (Trench I, Trench III, Trenches IV and V together, Trench VI, the Granary area) or the cell of seals printed without a trench name, each with at least 15 seals; smaller cells are pooled and pools under five seals dropped, which removes one seal each from Trench A and Trench V. The trenches sample the strata unevenly (the census counts: Trench III holds mostly Strata VI and V, Trench VI mostly III and II, the Granary mostly IV), and legends may differ between neighbourhoods. The chronology package found that Harappa's 3B–3C differences shrank inside a single excavation frame ([CHRONOLOGY-REGION-01](../../knowledge/registry/attempts/CHRONOLOGY-REGION-01.json)). A within-trench null asks whether legends from older strata of a trench differ from legends from later strata of the same trench. Permuting over the whole mound is a reported sensitivity analysis, as is Vats's printed depth ranked within each trench in place of the stratum.

In the discovery population each frame is a mound together with its label source, since a Vats stratum scale and a HARP period scale are not compared with each other, and u is ranked within the frame.

## Legends

The export normalises every legend to be read right to left, so the leftmost sign of the visual code closes the legend and the rightmost opens it, and the code marks each edge as surviving (`+`) or broken (`[`, `]`). Many Harappa seals are broken. A legend counts for the closing position when it is written on one line, its left edge survives and the sign at that edge is read, and for the opening position on the same terms at the right edge; complete legends of one sign are left out of both. Length needs both edges and every slot observed. Variants and the sign inventory use every legend with its readable signs. Identical complete legends in one frame are merged at their median u, so a set of copies counts once. On the pool, the edge rule reads the closing sign of 198 legends where the complete-legend rule of the stratigraphy package reads 166; scoring complete legends only is a reported sensitivity analysis.

## The closing stock

The closing stock is the union of two stocks defined by one rule, a sign that closes at least five legends of two or more signs and stands last in at least half of its occurrences in them. Mackay's DK-G seals give 740, 520, 090, 400, 151, 156, 527, 617, 621, 226, 892 and 407; the Harappa pool gives 740, 520, 405, 154, 090, 151, 407 and 595. The union of fifteen signs closes 72.8% of pool legends; derived on one half of the pool and measured on the other, it closes 69.6% (5% to 95% over 200 halvings: 0.61 to 0.77). The DK-G stock alone closes 61.0%, and scoring with it alone is a reported sensitivity analysis. ([make_parameters.py](make_parameters.py), [discovery-v2/frozen-parameters.json](discovery-v2/frozen-parameters.json).)

## The positive control

A null result on drift means something only if the sequence spans a change. Kenoyer and Meadow (2010, p. xlix) date square seals with script alone first to Period 3B and confine long rectangular seals with script alone to Period 3C, from HARP's own stratigraphy; Andreas Fuls (*Corpus of Indus Inscriptions I*, 2022, p. 13, as quoted in the [harappa.com review](https://www.harappa.com/content/corpus-indus-inscriptions-i)) reports that rectangular seals increase in the later phases of Mound F. The positive control (PC) is the correlation between u and an indicator of a rectangular seal, permuted within trenches. On the discovery seals it gives r = 0.21, p = 0.015, with the rectangular share rising from 0.09 in the early third to 0.29 in the late third.

The census of the confirmation population, run before this design was fixed, printed object types by stratum at face level for Mound F: rectangular seals number 0 of 2 in Stratum VII, 0 of 19 in VI, 6 of 29 in V, 5 of 41 in IV, 7 of 37 in III, 5 of 40 in II and 9 of 15 in I. The positive control therefore confirms a known change under the frozen within-trench test, and is not a blind prediction. Its role in the credence rule is to gate the gains: a P1 pass or a "no drift" outcome adds its full gain only when the control passes, since a sequence that shows no change in seal form cannot show that the legend stayed fixed while the seal changed. When the control fails the gains count at half weight, because the within-trench format test is itself weak: it detects a linear rise from 10% to 25% rectangular seals in 38% of Mound F-shaped populations, and from 10% to 30% in 52% ([discovery-v2/power.json](discovery-v2/power.json)).

## Residual, intrusive and misassigned seals

Seals were kept, lost and redeposited. Vats's stratum for a seal follows its printed depth closely, and Kenoyer and Meadow found that he placed the inscribed tablets of Trench I too early. A residual seal, a seal fallen into a pit and a seal given the wrong stratum all have the same effect on these tests: they move a legend to a stratum where it was not made, which blurs a drift without creating one. Power is therefore stated after a quarter and a half of the seals in each trench exchange strata at random. Fixity statements (P1, P2) are shares over thirds of the sequence and must survive single deletions, and drift outcomes need the single-deletion influence rule, so one misplaced seal cannot decide them.

## Freshness and the spending ledger

[bench/spent.json](../../bench/spent.json) records splits of the full Lipi input spent for `seal_size`, `site` and `motif` (12 October and bench round 1) and for the fish families and `object_type` (fish-context, 20 October). None is a family of legends against period. This package declares one family, `harappa_period_sequence`.

Earlier packages computed statistics that touch these seals. [CHRONOLOGY-REGION-01](../../knowledge/registry/attempts/CHRONOLOGY-REGION-01.json) compared Harappa's Period 3B with 3C on all inscribed faces: sign frequencies with square seals as one of three object types, positional divergences and length. Its 3C arm included Mound F's Strata IV to I with their sub-phases pooled, its 3B arm held ten seals (two of them from Mound F's Stratum VII), and Strata VI and V, labelled 3B/C, were left out as intervals. The 3B-against-3C seal frequency contrast is spent, and we do not count its reproduction; the relation between legends and stratum order within Mound F's trenches was not computed there. [SIGN-CONTEXT-SEALS-01](../../knowledge/registry/attempts/SIGN-CONTEXT-SEALS-01.json) tabulated the unicorn share and the size dispersion of Harappa seals by period label, including the 3C sub-phases drawn from Vats's strata, so motif and size by period are spent; we use neither. [SEAL-LEGEND-UNITS-2026-10-20](../../knowledge/registry/attempts/SEAL-LEGEND-UNITS-2026-10-20.json) built its inventory of closing formulae from every clean seal legend in the corpus (family `seal_legend_closing_structure`). The level of the closing-stock share in any group of seals is therefore predictable from corpus-wide aggregates, and P2, which is such a level, is not counted as fresh confirmation; the change of that share through the sequence (P1) is fresh.

The loader runs with a package ledger, [shots/spent-ledger.json](shots/), copied from `bench/spent.json` as it stood in the working tree on 20 October. We leave the shared ledger to the maintainer and propose the entry in [DATA-UPDATE.md](DATA-UPDATE.md). Once read, Vats's Mound F stratified seals are spent for `harappa_period_sequence`, and the discovery seals are examined for it.

## Examined before the freeze

From the confirmation population we looked only at what the design needed: family counts by trench cell and by stratum, the stratum-to-period mapping, the trench-by-stratum face counts and the object types by stratum quoted above, the printed depths by stratum and trench, the overall motif counts (not by stratum), and the numbers of legends readable for length (107), opening (104) and closing (104) under the complete-legend rule. No Mound F legend, sign or legend length was tabulated against stratum. Everything in [EXPLORATION.md](EXPLORATION.md) was computed on the discovery seals and the undated pool.

## Procedure

1. Build the two populations ([build_inputs.py](build_inputs.py) → [inputs/](inputs/)).
2. Explore the discovery seals ([explore_discovery.py](explore_discovery.py) → [discovery-v1/](discovery-v1/)).
3. Derive the closing stock, choose the test constants on synthetic populations and rehearse the scorer on the discovery seals ([make_parameters.py](make_parameters.py), [power.py](power.py) `--quick` → [design-v1/](design-v1/); [rehearse_discovery.py](rehearse_discovery.py), [reliability.py](reliability.py) → [discovery-v2/](discovery-v2/)).
4. Estimate planted-effect power at the shape of Mound F ([power.py](power.py) → [discovery-v2/power.json](discovery-v2/power.json)).
5. Create the loader split on the confirmation file, register the shot, and freeze [PREDICTIONS.json](PREDICTIONS.json) with the scorer, the statistics modules, the protocol, the predictions text, the input builder and the frozen parameters as artifacts ([bench/](bench/), [shots/](shots/)).
6. Run [confirm.py](confirm.py) once; record the outcome, the credence change and the review in [REPORT.md](REPORT.md).
