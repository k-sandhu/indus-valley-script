## Complete inscription tests

Keep every eligible inscription intact during calculation, in normalized reading order. Use complete, readable, single-line strings of length at least two. Report all-string, length-at-least-three and one-representative-face-per-object summaries. Keep source disagreements and repeated-object dependence visible. Build object/identical-text closure before any deterministic discovery/transfer split; the exact previously exposed corpus makes the split a stability check rather than fresh semantic validation.

For each family, report its full-record frequency, positions, immediate stroke/quantity graphics, anchor co-occurrence and exact substitution templates that preserve the rest of the whole inscription. Include short and long template counts separately. Prefer analytic/descriptive counts to another broad permutation search. A structural random-label control may test whether role coherence arises from flexible assignment; it must preserve the declared support/position features and cannot be called a probability of a Sanskrit meaning.

A proposed role must make an explicit constraint, such as counted entity, internal connector, non-final entity or closing category. Score that constraint across every complete inscription containing the candidate, retaining contradictions and unknown signs. Construct complete-record semantic walks with the same global map, show unresolved slots, and compare competing account, issuer/name, commodity and grammatical interpretations where the available evidence leaves them equivalent. Coverage is the share of slots assigned a reviewed comparison, not translation accuracy.

Context-supported candidates require a documented picture, a selected sense, at least five distinct complete strings and supporting direction in both nonempty split frames. Sparse or mixed families retain visual-lead or context-compatible status. These thresholds are prioritization rules; no posterior probability is estimated. Any threshold or schema change after results requires a dated amendment and the earlier output remains frozen.
