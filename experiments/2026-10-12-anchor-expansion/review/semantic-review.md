# Semantic and complete-inscription review

The extension preserves a global meaning map and the complete input strings. I checked all 2,930 local faces in run-v1 against the context source register: code order, recorded length and role assignments agree throughout. Those faces contain 1,859 distinct strings. Rare member codes retain their own support tiers instead of inheriting a family’s pooled result. The remaining problems concern the strength and scope of the interpretation, several short labels and how rival record genres are displayed.

This review read the frozen seventy-family map, both Sanskrit ledgers, family-links-v2, context/run-v2, the run-v1 builder and local companion. It inspected twenty intact source records, with matching and contrary cases, and twenty-five exact font glyphs. These font images document components; they are not new archaeological object observations. Dictionary sense identities and literary citation scopes remain as selected in the earlier anchor checks. The review did not estimate probabilities of meanings or propose a phonetic reading.

## Findings to incorporate

**SE01, high priority: distinguish the narrow stroke proxy from all quantity graphics.** The declared `counted_entity` rule tests immediate predecessors W001–W005. In M-16, record **2544.1**, W130 follows W035, whose exact font has five long vertical strokes and whose global family is `quantity_strokes`. W130 fails the declared proxy, but it does have a preceding quantity-like graphic. Similarly, W383 in M-389, **2885.1**, follows W031, a single long stroke. Statements that these codes have no stroke/numeral predecessor would misdescribe the source. Preserve the existing endpoint and say “no selected W001–W005 predecessor.” A broader quantity-family sensitivity, if used, must be separately declared after these outcomes; it must not replace the registered proxy or establish the numeral values.

**SE02, high priority: the directional screen must not imply strong or universal semantic roles.** The toothed-tool family passes the short-stroke proxy in **3/22** target occurrences, compared with **1.369** expected; nineteen occurrences fail it. Both splits have a positive direction, which satisfies the stated prioritization rule. W130 itself passes **0/8**, while W132 has one passing occurrence in four strings. Binding/bowtie passes an internal-position rule in **84/153**, compared with **81.975** expected. Its transfer result is **29/57** compared with **28.935**, an excess of only **0.065** occurrence. The viewer’s “Two-split directional enrichment” badge accurately names the screen; calling every such family a strongest semantic result would exceed what that screen measures. Keep the badge, effect magnitude, member support and contrary cases together. A stricter tier would require a named amendment rather than retroactively altering the screen.

The converse cases also matter. Compartmented frames have a closing match in **6/62** target occurrences, below **14.204** expected and below expectation in both splits. Horned-animal graphics have **0/11** short-stroke matches. Cup-vessel graphics have **22/267** overall and reverse direction in transfer: **4/110** against **4.954** expected. Their pictures can remain semantic leads, but those declared roles have not supplied supporting two-split evidence. A nonmatching occurrence is an exception to this exact role rule; it does not independently falsify the pictured-object comparison.

**SE03, medium priority: remove service, material and authority refinements from the leading short labels.** W125’s image supports a person beside a curved implement, with bow and loop/apparatus alternatives. *Dhanus* and *dhanvin* support bow and bow-equipped person; neither supplies **bow-making service**. Retain person-with-bow as the leading comparison and bow-maker as an untested occupation scenario if needed. W383 and W825 show repeated closed cells in a strip or oval; use “reticulated or crosshatched object” before naming textile/net/lattice as rivals. The mesh family’s **2/5** proxy matches do not identify a material. W420 consists of parallel stems joined at a base; W426 adds forked upper ends. *Stamba* supplies a clump/bunch, *kāṇḍa* a stalk component, and *śūka* an awn. These permit a plant-bundle comparison without selecting harvested grain. W521’s shaft/head comparison does not select **authority** from its outline or nonfinal placement; retain a pointed implement and keep authority as a separate record-function scenario.

**SE04, medium priority: finish the competing complete-record interpretations.** The run-v1 record card shows the preferred semantic walk and an issuer/name relabelling. The template contains a commodity variant, but does not display it; a grammatical alternative is also absent. The protocol names account, issuer/name, commodity and grammatical interpretations where their roles are equivalent. Add a compact comparison explaining the same global slots under these scenarios, with unchanged contradictions and unresolved positions. A grammatical scenario should label possible name/nominal components, classifiers and linkers rather than fabricate a Sanskrit sentence. Equal role fits should remain visibly equal.

**SE05, medium priority: correct the local record counts and the W169 note.** `coverage.json` calls **2,930** `complete_local_strings`, although the local companion contains faces with **1,859** distinct code strings. Publish separate face and distinct-string counts. W169’s row correctly describes two horizontally joined ovals, has zero eligible complete-string occurrence, uses *dvaya* and the pair sense of *yuga*, and retains a visual-lead tier. Its `context_note` nevertheless says “pooled carrier family” and “closing fit.” Its actual family is `paired_suspended_ovals` and its role is unconstrained. Remove that inherited wording. The family heading should also describe paired ovals/linked units before suspended loads: W169, W170 and W171 contain joined ovals, while W172, assigned elsewhere, visibly supplies a carrying frame and hanging loops.

**SE06, medium priority: keep representatives’ individual tiers visible beside a pooled family badge.** Bound-stalks passes in **17/24** tokens, but its first shown representative W420 has four distinct strings and no proxy matches; W426 has **4/5**. Plant-branch’s first representative W411 has three strings. Binding/bowtie’s first representative W530 has eight strings and a mixed-context tier. The member panel records these differences, and code cards use the right tiers. Showing each representative’s own count/tier beside its thumbnail would prevent the family badge being read as that code’s result.

**SE07, low priority: preserve the unselected vocabulary requests in catalogue metadata.** Family-links-v2 explicitly records eight unselected request spellings: *śūla*, *nara*, *karmakara*, *pāla*, *krimi*, *parṇa*, *nadī* and *yāna*. Run-v1’s `unmatched_requested_anchor_words` is empty because the builder checks the already selected link words. Rename that field or carry the original unselected-request list separately. All seventy families do have exact selected comparisons; an empty selected-word lookup failure list does not mean every original hint was selected.

## Image and sense conclusions

The W272 image has a segmented center and multiple appendages. Bird, insect/scorpion, plant and composite-design rivals are suitable; *vṛścika* and *makṣikā* are comparison words without a selected species. W700 is one open U, not two cups. W940, W942 and W943 combine a fish-like body with a differently segmented/appendaged form inside curved boundaries. The run-v1 overlays already correct those three points and preserve the earlier map as evidence.

The best immediate Sanskrit extensions remain component-level: *śikya* and *śikyapāśa* for genuine suspension, *dhanus/dhanvin* for a bow-equipped figure, *stamba/kāṇḍa* for plant-like bunches and stems, *jāla/tantu* for mesh/thread alternatives, and the pair sense of *yuga* for joined ovals. Their full-record roles are separate tests. The checked local companion uses one map for all appearances and keeps every unresolved slot; no mapping-integrity blocker was found. The listed wording, metadata and rival-display changes should enter a new viewer run, preserving run-v1 and the anchor endpoints.
