# Six vocabulary gaps in the frozen visual map

The complete visual review froze seventy families covering 673 codes, including 533 previously unreviewed codes. Three families requested direct comparison words missing from the initial ninety-six-sense ledger: a branched shaft, an arrow/shaft and a woven mesh. The [six-sense supplement](supplement-v1.json) fills those gaps while preserving the initial forty additions and all earlier run-v6 senses. The [versioned family links](family-links-v2.json) cover every frozen family ID.

This supplement was chosen from specified graphic vocabulary gaps before the Sanskrit contributor inspected the new complete-context results. It does not select words for a favourable positional count or compound match. Together the two ledgers contain **102 selected senses**, comprising fifty-six reused senses and forty-six additions in separately recorded endpoints.

| Comparison | Exact MW record | Selected citation and distinction |
|---|---|---|
| **śākhā**, branch | 214950, p. 1062,3 | RV cited. Limb, door-post and Vedic school have separate sense records. |
| **daṇḍa**, staff/rod/pole | 89571, p. 466,2 | RV cited. Punishment, sovereignty, length and banner-staff records remain separate. |
| **iṣu**, arrow | 29463, p. 168,3 | RV, AV and VS cited. The mathematical and symbolic-number senses are excluded. |
| **śara**, arrow/shaft | 213454, p. 1056,2 | Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad, Manu and MBh cited. The neighbouring reed/grass record 213453 has the RV citation. |
| **jāla**, net | 79046, p. 419,3 | AV VIII/X, Kātyāyana Śrauta and Pāraskara Gṛhya cited. Generic woven texture (79051), lattice (79052) and window (79053) are separately indexed. |
| **tantu**, thread/warp | 82420, p. 436,1 | RV cited. Lineage and sacrificial-series interpretations belong to separate senses. |

The branch and staff words retain plant, implement and emblem rivals for forked or headed uprights. The arrow comparison requires an observed point/shaft structure; a tapering geometric sign alone cannot identify a weapon. The mesh family keeps net, textile, lattice and intersecting-line alternatives. Selecting the net sense of *jāla* and the thread/warp sense of *tantu* supplies two component tests without identifying the material or craft depicted.

The primary source remains the [pinned Cologne Monier-Williams digitization](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-orig/blob/55e8addbd96e8d9001b8789026b026763f4049c1/v02/mw/mw.txt). Exact headword, ID and page/column checks pass for all six additions. Internet checks resolved the original print-page URLs for [iṣu](https://sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/MWScan/MWScanpdf/mw0168-imaka.pdf), [daṇḍa](https://sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/MWScan/MWScanpdf/mw0466-dakSiNAzA.pdf), [jāla](https://sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/MWScan/MWScanpdf/mw0419-jAnevAdika.pdf), [tantu](https://sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/MWScan/MWScanpdf/mw0436-tanUhrada.pdf) and [śara](https://sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/MWScan/MWScanpdf/mw1056-zayanagRha.pdf). The [śākhā page](https://sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/MWScan/MWScanpdf/mw1062-zAkaMdheya.pdf) timed out. No new printed-image verification is claimed. [Source record](sources-supplement-v1.json), [verification](verification-supplement-v1.json).

Eight requested spellings remain unselected: *śūla*, *nara*, *karmakara*, *pāla*, *krimi*, *parṇa*, *nadī* and *yāna*. These are dictionary-selection gaps, rather than claims that the words are unattested. Each affected family has another exact selected comparison, such as *puruṣa* for a person, *pattra* for a leaf, *udaka* for water and *cakra* for a wheel. The family-link file records the original hints, exact selected matches and unresolved requests separately. For the many-appendage creature, *vṛścika*, *makṣikā*, *śakuni* and *pakṣin* keep scorpion, insect and bird competitors visible. No species is chosen by the lexical source check.

The family links preserve the frozen visual map’s roles and picture observations. Broad frames, parallel rows, crosses and other geometric designs retain indirect functional or emblem comparisons. They receive no mineral, crop species or material assignment solely to increase coverage.

This addition changes only the owned anchor package. It adds `build_supplement.py`, `supplement-v1.json`, `family-links-v2.json`, `sources-supplement-v1.json`, `verification-supplement-v1.json` and this addendum. The original `senses.json`, `family-links.json` and their verification outputs remain unchanged; full additional dictionary bodies remain in the ignored local companion.
