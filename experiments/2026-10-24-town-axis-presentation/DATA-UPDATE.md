# Sources and coverage

This package presents the existing Q-1032 evidence. We acquired no third-party source, created no archaeological measurement and changed no data coverage. No inscribed object or radiocarbon determination enters the register through this package. Its numerical inputs come from the 24 October register, the earlier Lothal comparison and the storage-migration summary. The input manifest records their byte counts and SHA-256 values.

The deck's notes retain the distinctions needed to use those observations. Published orientation rows keep their source pages and north references. The Lothal intervals show reported errors. The Surkotada intervals show digitizing errors with total uncertainty unknown. The radiocarbon table keeps the laboratory labels, conventional BP ages and reported errors beside the IA and IC building distinction. Modern horizon profiles retain their observer locations and unquantified palaeohorizon uncertainty.

The only new outputs are presentation prose, editable tables and charts, embedded workbooks containing the chart values, and local render and validation files. The workbook snapshots contain values rounded to twelve significant digits, which avoids binary floating-point tails that Excel cannot preserve. The full source precision remains in the register. No image, plan, PDF scan or DEM raster enters the deck.
