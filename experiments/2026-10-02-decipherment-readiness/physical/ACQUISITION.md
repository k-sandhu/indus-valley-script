# Specimen-specific acquisition checklist

Collect an accession-linked packet for each target: original publication page and caption, excavation and museum registration, all relevant faces with scale and orientation, find unit and dating basis, material and conservation history, and the particular independent quantity or operation being tested. Keep uncertain joins as candidate links until a source record resolves them. Record whether contents or detached parts were retained, whether membership is complete, and which fields were supplied by interpretation. The rows below specify what each packet must add.

The collection order favors observations that can answer a specified question with a small coherent object group. The complete Lothal matched-view packet can establish operational variation immediately. Quantity work should proceed through the named registration and count packets. Geometry and known-system controls then supply measured error and representation checks for the prospective physical designs.
