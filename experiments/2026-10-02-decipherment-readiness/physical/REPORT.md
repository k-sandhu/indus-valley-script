# Where physical evidence can support decipherment

The best attainable foothold is the role an inscription played in a documented operation. Lothal sealings preserve contact and fastening traces, and Kanmer pendants preserve a repeated stamped front alongside varying reverse marks. The reviewed Indus objects supply useful associations between surfaces and activities, while independently labelled quantities, commodities or named people remain missing. A next study can narrow the record's function and test a proposed field assignment before a particular sign acquires a lexical value.

This audit read all 101 registry entries and reviewed the original records or endpoints for the [22 physical branches listed in coverage.json](coverage.json). It followed source-defined objects, registrations, faces and find contexts. Published reconstructions, alternative transcriptions and repeated photographs retain their source status. The only new calculations are descriptive checks declared in [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md); their inputs and exact outputs are in [current-checks.json](current-checks.json). Acquired corpora, scans and unresolved-rights images remain local-only. The [acquisition checklist](ACQUISITION.md) names the missing specimen fields and source pages.

## Lothal: repeated text across different attachments

The inspected 2005 source table covers L-124 through L-216. Its 93 objects carry 133 listed impressions; 68 objects join the preserved transcription, and 29 complete single-line inscriptions of at least three signs form nine groups. G01 contains 11 objects spanning seven recorded physical categories, including two undiagnostic entries. G02 contains ten elephant-stamp objects classified as wooden-box sealings. The table and prose retain different impression and context totals. Context `W1` was assigned through a recurring stamp, so it cannot provide an independent warehouse label in a stamp prediction test. [Object joins and complete-string groups](../../2026-10-02-six-hour/lothal-physical-use/graphic-coverage/results.json), [independent table and trace review](../../2026-10-02-six-hour/lothal-physical-use/independent-review/README.md).

The new check asks how many objects could share one diagnostic physical category per complete inscription. After excluding the three undiagnostic objects, the largest within-group category counts sum to 20 of 26 objects. G01 accounts for all six required exceptions. Retaining undiagnostic as a recorded category gives 20 of 29 and nine exceptions. These source categories combine texture, support and fastening, and the calculation is an in-sample compatibility bound. A literal one-category-per-string rule already meets conflicting observations; a common authorization, holder or procedure field could occur across several attachments. [Exact group maxima and ties](current-checks.json).

The useful next observation is a matched front-and-reverse record of G01 and G02. Contact concavity, rope channels, support texture and clay removal surfaces can specify the closure action independently of the inscription. That would support an operational association such as repeated authorization across different closures. Identifying a particular sign as the word for an official, a box or a commodity would require further variation tied to independently identified referents.

Frenez's 2024 article develops the administrative-archive interpretation through stratigraphy and Western Asian comparisons. The title, bibliographic coordinates and institutional abstract were accessible; this audit's direct full-text request returned 403. Its argument is an acquisition lead for the original find contexts and object illustrations. [Institutional record](https://repository.iitgn.ac.in/entities/publication/8b5a98f0-16f1-4a53-b2f9-dd19c8bb4986), [publisher issue](https://www.tandfonline.com/toc/rsas20/40/2).

The caption joins tablets to excavation registrations; 16 came from the unit-8040 clump, two from nearby and four from scattered deposits. This establishes a physical production/deposition group. ## Kanmer: a handling test with missing geometry

The frame has four photographed clay pendants and three restored registrations. Their shared broad stamped front, varying reverse layouts and holes supply a production and handling question: how was a pendant presented, attached and inspected? The acquired source-relative views do not measure individual bore axes, wear or residues. The fourth registration remains missing. Scanner illustrations are available, while raw meshes and actual specimen sampling precision are absent. [Object frame and disagreements](../../2026-10-02-six-hour/kanmer-physical-use/README.md).

The proposed 42-replica experiment compares hanging, parcel attachment and hand inspection, with 36 used replicas and six controls. Original bore geometry, mass distribution and surface condition are prerequisites. Its observable outcomes are resting orientation and localized wear. Those measurements can distinguish handling proposals; a travel-pass interpretation needs additional evidence about the represented permission or holder. The source-history amendment identifies the exact missing final-volume pages and a library holding with CD-ROM. [Handling protocol](../../2026-10-02-six-hour/kanmer-physical-use/PHYSICAL-TEST-PROTOCOL.md), [page and holding audit](../../2026-10-02-six-hour/kanmer-physical-use/source-continuation-2026-10-02/REPORT.md).

## Quantities: repair the specimen joins and obtain a target

The capacity audit contains eight rows and zero eligible independent held-out tests of the specified 40.4-litre-per-stroke hypothesis. The capacities are geometric profile estimates. H-371 has a consistent excavation-ID join, while the H-370 and H-372 assignments differ between the inspected corpus and Fuls's table. For the three fitted vessels, capacity identifies the product of a stroke coefficient and a volume unit. An independent unit or mixed-mark relation is needed to separate those factors. [Capacity and identity audit](../../../research/literature/metrology/source_and_identifiability_audit.json).

Their proposed bangle expressions `7+L` are 11, 13, 17 and 43. Direct substitution confirms every equality. The source premise needing recovery is the independent bangle count and its association with M-1383. The inspected Vidale account discusses two actual bangles inside one bowl and a reconstruction of a larger assembly, without supplying the proposed M-1383 count. For H-904 and M-331, acquiring complete faces secures the graphics; the quantity operation connecting those faces also needs evidence. ## Known systems provide measurement criteria

Susa envelope SB 1940 offers an actual inside/outside observation: the Louvre records three round pieces and three rods with three external circles and three elongated impressions. Its bibliography points to six individual token entries in *Before Writing II*, Suse 223-225 and 330-332. The equal mixture supplies one conservation row, `(3,3,-3,-3)`. Both positive assignments `(1,2,1,2)` and `(1,2,2,1)` satisfy it. A documented unequal mixture or direct contact correspondence would distinguish these class assignments; an independently counted unit would address scale. [Louvre specimen record](https://collections.louvre.fr/ark:/53355/cl010175973), [existing source comparison and measurement fields](../../2026-10-02-six-hour/physical-acquisition/SOURCE_AND_MEASUREMENTS.md).

The six Puruchuco accounts supply a source-defined numerical hierarchy and known quantity readings. The published fixed alignment matches 16 of 20 entries; ordered flattening gives the same comparison. In the selected geometric grouping study, both largest-gap and equal-cord-count rules recover the two reference partitions. Source values, partitions and ranges were exposed before the protocols, and the CM009 reconstruction is a separate source witness. This control demonstrates which grouping observations survive particular representations. The generated physical and khipu branches then show why a prospective Indus collection needs complete membership, an independently grounded scale and validation equations that add rank. Their false unique answers and unresolved positive families are preserved.
