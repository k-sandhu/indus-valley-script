# Retrospective

We froze five cases and eight searches, then followed public author and HARP routes. The useful source gain came from the HARP caption series: the full excavation key joins H-1657 to a named floor and room. The surrounding captions show why the trench alone cannot settle the date: its collapsed-wall deposits also include a Period 4 vessel, without a published relation to the seal's lot.

Both 2009 filenames returned 403, while the public reader exceeded its size limit. The 1999 scan exposed no extractable text, and screenshot responses produced no inspectable pixels through this interface. We retained the Figure 4.2 search lead without resolving H-2587. The three early cases still require field records. The result is one new source-qualified context assertion and four bounded unsuccessful searches.

The broad search responses incidentally included inscription sequences from Wiktionary and a Hugging Face CSV. We recorded that exposure immediately in the package. Future confirmation design must carry it forward. No graphic payload was deliberately opened and no reserved confirmation data were read.
