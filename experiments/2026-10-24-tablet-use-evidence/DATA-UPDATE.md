# Proposed coverage update

We add a physical-use assertion register for H-2218–H-2239, covering 22 existing object identities and 66 previously joined displayed faces. No object, image join or excavation registration is newly discovered. The source is Meadow and Kenoyer (2000), *The 'Tiny Steatite Seals' (Incised Steatite Tablets) of Harappa: Some Observations on Their Context and Dating*, the same author PDF used by the October 2 Harappa source frame.

The new fields explicitly record the published common cutting direction and stroke order, distinguish the engraver interpretation from the mould observation, retain alternative production histories, and separate six unpublished physical-use fields from functional reconstructions. The 22-object register has 308 source-qualified assertions, including 132 unknown use assertions. Counts describe this bounded register and do not claim global additions to every earlier repository record.
