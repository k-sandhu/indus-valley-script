# The Harappa clump preserves a carved series and its disposal

The 22 incised steatite tablets H-2218–H-2239 preserve a shared method of cutting signs across at least three reported engravers. Meadow and Kenoyer describe the cutting direction and stroke order as the same for each character, based on microscopic examination of fine-grain moulds. Sixteen tablets were recovered in a clump containing members of all three manufacturing groups. The source places the whole series in secondary debris outside Harappa's Mound E curtain wall. This combination supports repeated production and subsequent gathering; the tablets' operational use still needs a physical relation to a transaction, container or impression.

We audited the complete 21-page author manuscript of Meadow and Kenoyer (2000), [*The 'Tiny Steatite Seals' (Incised Steatite Tablets) of Harappa: Some Observations on Their Context and Dating*](https://www.harappa.com/sites/default/files/pdf/Kenoyer2000_The%20Tiny%20Steatite%20Seals%20of%20Harappa.pdf). The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) preceded this extraction. The [source manifest](source-manifest-v1.json) records the held PDF, extraction, seven full-page renders and the six pages inspected visually. We reviewed all extracted pages, including the separate terracotta discussion and the endnote on production and reading direction. The PDF was already exposed in earlier work; this is an additional field audit of that source.

The group assignments identify cutting hands under the excavators' interpretation. They do not establish three workshops or three independent production events. The two nearby objects remain outside the observed sixteen-object clump; their possible former membership is an additional source interpretation. The entire series belongs to dumped debris deposited after wall 296 and its revetment fill, before Period 3C material arrived. Late Period 3B is a relative deposition assignment, not a direct date for manufacture or use.

## The useful null evidence

The chapter reports no object-specific attachment, contents, independent quantity, use-wear, physical cancellation or preserved pouch for this 22-object block. We encode 132 unknown assertions, with the reason and searched chapter attached. Unknown means that the field was not reported for these objects in this source. Examination of the objects could supply it.

The chapter does describe worn tablets on author p.11, PDF 12. Those are the moulded terracotta specimens in Figure 3.8–10. Transferring that wear description to the incised steatite series would manufacture evidence for circulation. Likewise, the microscopic moulds made for modern examination are research casts; they are not excavated ancient impressions.

Manufacture also keeps this block separate from two other physical classes already documented in the [archaeological-setting audit](../2026-10-20-archaeological-setting/REPORT.md). H-1727–H-1766 are impressions stamped into vessel walls before ceramic firing. H-1719–H-1724 are separate clay tags bearing seal impressions, while H-1725 is an unbaked jar stopper. Their impressions and attachment possibilities belong to different carrier histories. The present tablets are separately carved stone objects that were then fired.

## What would decide use

A registered ancient clay impression matching one of these tablets would directly test the chapter's assumption that the tablets were never intended to stamp. A convincing match needs the three-dimensional groove profile and accidental edge or cut details, together with secure object identities and deposit records; a shared sign string cannot identify the tool. The first acquisition target is therefore an archived impression or residue-bearing surface linked to an individual tablet, followed by a complete 3D record of all faces and ends. Absence of an impression in this publication does not answer that test.

The pouch reconstruction has a different deciding observation: documented fibres, leather residue or an enclosing impression at the clump boundary, tied to the excavation record. That would establish packaging of the group. It would not alone identify issue, redemption or a ration amount. Those operations need a registered tablet-to-allotment relation with independently measured contents and quantity. The [data update](DATA-UPDATE.md) keeps these requests separate.

No confirmation cohort was opened, no statistic was rerun and no reading or conjecture was submitted. C-0005, C-0009 and C-0012 retain their existing credences and untested physical predictions. The [validation](validation-v1.json) checks source hashes, the complete identifier block, relation accounting and the separation of observed recovery from inferred use; it is author validation.

The boldest physical reading supported here is a deliberately repeated carved series, with sixteen specimens surviving together in a discard deposit. A securely matched ancient impression would establish a stamping role; a registered attachment or allotment would decide whether these objects participated in a counted transfer.
