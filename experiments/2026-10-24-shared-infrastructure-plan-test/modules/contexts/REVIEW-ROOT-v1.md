# Review of the integrated plan and context study

The current drafts preserve the important source, date and denominator distinctions. We found no blocking numerical or dating error in the contextual statements reviewed. The 78 dating entries remain 76 determinations/TL results plus two Kanmer rice-age paragraphs without raw determinations. The 225 Dholavira rows remain catalogue rows; the 93 Lothal rows retain their table/prose disagreement. Zero qualified matched pairs describes the assembled evidence. It does not become a zero effect or an assertion that ancient institutions were absent.

This review follows the preserved context freeze. Its coder already knew the context sources and now read the new architecture narratives. It is an adversarial review of the integrated argument, not external reproduction of the context module. `review-root-v1.json` pins the reviewed draft hashes, checks the unchanged context endpoint and records exact source locators. The coordinator corrected the registration identification before the final review; that resolved issue is not an open finding.

The principal remaining wording issue concerns drainage renewal. Meadow and Kenoyer 1997, printed pp. 144 and 146, PDF indices 5 and 7, describes the earlier western drains, the last clogged with refuse, followed by the new central Drain 51 and its crossing street. The repaired report body now says this correctly. The opening still calls the connection continuing, and the strongest-reading summaries can suggest uninterrupted service. Carry the qualification into those summaries: some connections were retained or re-established while local access changed. A service hiatus and a preferential persistence fraction remain unmeasured. This is RCTX-01; it changes no measured width, eligible denominator or credence.

The prospective custody and labour criteria were improved during review. Access control, a storeroom and a fastening can describe security without a handover. Provisioning can distribute ordinary goods without identifying an obligation for work. Lothal's physical contacts and Harappa's manufacturing debris expose those alternatives directly. The revised decision and test design now require a physical custody-change criterion independent of tested sealing occurrence and an additional observable work-specific distinction. That resolves RCTX-02 for this acquisition design. The future discovery pilot must validate those criteria, preserve unknown classifications and include secured-storage and ordinary-provisioning controls before confirmation.

One smaller wording change remains. In REPORT.md, the descriptions of serial tablet administration and original administrative use can make a proposed function sound established. Kenoyer's manufacturing evidence identifies production, and the Wall 296 group identifies secondary discard; neither identifies the original institutional function of every tablet. State that C-0019 extends a proposal of serial administration, and use original use in the Wall 296 paragraph. This is RCTX-03.

The architecture-to-document chain remains properly incomplete. Farmana's IIA/IIB plan labels cannot be equated with the seal tables' numeric structural phases 4/5 without a crosswalk. Harappa's Area C geometry, Mound ET Drain 51, below-Wall 15 hearths, Trench 54 manufacture and Wall 296 discard are separate chains until the excavation record joins them. Dholavira's room rows, fallen gate inlays and broad stage-dated samples do not identify one emplacement episode. Lothal's outside-SRG3 samples cannot date its warehouse sealing deposit. The drafts keep those separations and do not infer an institution from orthogonality.

A later paragraph places a button-shaped steatite seal with a perforated knob in X30/layer 8 within its KMR IIB discussion. The held witness is gzip-compressed original-report JSONP text, SHA-256 `4560d6a057aee71325eef69bdcffaad80af19d3323fb9cfc2c393cfb7caab7b5`; no full original PDF or acquired page 94 image is asserted. The JSON review records the raw path, URL, authors, publication, access limit, digit-glyph normalization and exact text offsets.

The floor-level sealing is a stronger bounded recovery target than the earlier unheld Chapter 8 lead. It has no registration, named room, original application event or independent date, and it is not identity-linked to the three-impression cohort. The X30 seal also remains unregistered and layer-associated. These are two source mentions, not two additions to an adjudicated physical-object population. AA28/layer 9 and Z28/layer 12 dates cannot be transferred to a Z28/layer 9 find. Record the mentions in a new post-join lead addendum without altering the 32 frozen assessments, run-v1 or qualified-pair denominator.

The strongest supported reading remains architectural and functional: some connections were retained or renewed while local access changed, and writing-bearing objects participated in manufacture, handling, fastening, reuse and display. The institutional cause and the proposed custody/work relations remain open. A new comparison needs complete common windows, preservation controls, independently dated operational contexts and physical criteria that separate the rival functions before documents are scored.
