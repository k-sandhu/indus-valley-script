# Inspecting the eastern plan evidence

We began with prior exposure to these towns' published architectural and inscription evidence. We read the frozen protocol and measurement rules, checked the D storage target, and kept the new documentary overlay unopened. Our work examined the original report pages, not a reconstruction made from the earlier town comparison.

Several promising illustrations failed the required phase comparison. Farmana Figures 5.69 and 5.70 are adjoining halves of one IIB plan. Its IIA eastern plan covers another excavation window. Kanmer's residential illustration is an oblique photograph, and its fort overview has no residential subphase key. Banawali's site-plan publications combine periods and add exposure information. We froze these failures with five bounded windows before selecting measurement endpoints.

We then made local measurements on the actual drawings. Visual inspection corrected preliminary endpoint placement before the versioned endpoint file was saved. A later annotated review exposed a remaining Banawali error: the first angle endpoint lay above the curved defence band. We retained that failed read and withdrew its angle, without choosing a replacement after seeing the result. The original inventory remains unchanged; an erratum corrects the OP10 photo number from Figure 7 to Figure 8. Seven measurements remain, with doubled-radius sensitivity and explicit unknown source accuracy.

The source joins required as much care as the geometry. Farmana explicitly assigns figure ranges to IIA and IIB, while individual additions and the 4C cap have local order but no separate absolute date. Its 3I accounts give different addition lengths, which remain in one repair group. Kanmer's room counts refer to different exposed phases and cannot be added as a census. The two superimposed levels of eight rooms do not establish two independent phases in every individual room. Those repairs are now visible in the structured records.

The module adds six source records, four annotated plan boards, seven retained measurements, two unmeasured candidates, one withdrawn reading and fourteen source sequence records. Original imagery, facsimile pages, crops, transforms and endpoints are local-only on D. Tracked outputs contain authored aggregates, source locators and retrieval metadata. We changed only the assigned module and its D raw directory.

The strongest result is a difference between kinds of continuity: an enclosure or alignment can remain while a local connection disappears. A deciding study needs registered phase plans of the same ground and a complete inventory of surviving connections and partitions, each tied to an ordered surface. That would turn this source-supported pattern into a measurable comparison of shared-route and internal-partition survival.
