# The strongest reading and the deciding test

The plans support selective continuity, meaning retention or renewal of some connections through local architectural change. Farmana's capped compound passage and Harappa's partly blocked gateway show why street alignment, through-access, entry into a building and drainage must be measured separately. A drain can continue while a threshold closes. The published evidence makes continued coordination plausible, but it does not identify the institution that maintained it.

Writing participated in several spatially distinct activities. Harappa has bounded tablet manufacture and positive room, floor and sump associations. Lothal has physical fastening evidence and a concentrated later deposit. Dholavira has a gate-chamber inscription. A model of urban administration should explain that variety. The specific proposal that tablets recorded shared-work obligations still lacks a physical distinction from goods allocation.

We retain the existing exploratory credences: C-0017 at 0.55, C-0018 at 0.35 and C-0019 at 0.10. Reusing their published examples does not supply the new confirmation evidence required by their update rules. The zero qualified episode and matched-pair counts describe this source audit's eligibility, while the architectural sequences and documentary activities are positive observations in their own right.

## A comparison that can decide the architectural claim

Recover complete phase drawings, excavation records and sections for at least twelve independent rebuilding episodes in four towns. That is an entry minimum, with final sample size determined by a pilot that models missing exposure, preservation and town dependence. Before measuring persistence, freeze the common excavation windows and inventory every visible shared-route boundary, service line and internal partition. Use independent survey controls for registration. Match material, foundation depth, robbing and excavation exposure, and retain unknown features separately from removed ones.

The frozen target remains a retained fraction for shared routes at least 0.25 above the fraction for internal partitions, with an interval calibrated for the small number of towns that excludes zero. A powered result with no preferential continuity would defeat C-0017's core claim. If apparent continuity disappears when composite phases or restoration are removed, the corresponding examples fail even before a population-level comparison. Six directly joined repairs in three towns can then test whether locally different components still made usable connections. Independent construction dates must determine whether repairs group by connected infrastructure better than by proximity and engineering constraints.

## A comparison that can decide the documentary extension

The next useful unit is a registered object or adjudicated cohort on a demonstrable operating surface or fastening, related stratigraphically to a named construction or repair. Short-lived samples must bracket that event, and the object-use relation must be established separately from final deposition. The immediate recovery targets are Harappa's named floor and courtyard seals, Trench 54's production sequence, the conflicting Wall 15 tablet proveniences, Banawali's sitting room and Dholavira's explicitly named rooms. Lothal requires resolution of deposit formation as well as its provenance counts.

Freeze custody-change settings from physical handling sequences and independently justified access, storage and wear evidence before reading sealing occurrence. A gate, storeroom or threshold alone supplies a candidate setting, not evidence of a change in custody. The pilot must establish a reproducible physical criterion for that change without using the occurrence or interpreted meaning of the documentary objects being tested. Twenty matched context pairs in four towns are an entry minimum, subject to demonstrated sensitivity. C-0018 requires an odds ratio of at least two, an appropriate cluster interval above one and a relation that survives restriction to primary use. If adequately recovered household possession, manufacture and discard explain the distribution without a custody-change association, the handover claim fails. A repeated legend cannot repair a failed physical prediction.

C-0019 needs an additional distinction: evidence that the relevant records concern work rather than ordinary movable goods. Residues, direct provisioning associations, wear and an independently defined repair schedule are candidate evidence. Provisioning alone also fits goods allocation, so the pilot must demonstrate an observable distinction specific to work before inscription units are scored. If the varying series instead track goods classes that explain their variation and defeat the shared-work prediction, abandon the labour-specific claim. A new confirmation round must freeze identities, aliases, production groups, features and scoring on discovery material, pass the benchmark's exposure audit, and receive one joint update for overlapping observations.

Our strongest reading is that some connections were retained or renewed while local access changed. The decisive observation would be a replicated, preservation-controlled advantage in route continuity, joined to independently dated operational documents. Its disappearance, or a distribution fully explained by workshops and secondary dumps, would disprove the corresponding extension of the shared-infrastructure idea.
