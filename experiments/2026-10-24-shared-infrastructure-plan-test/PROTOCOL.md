## Architectural observation rules

The observational unit is a named architectural part through an explicitly ordered construction episode. Towns, repeated views of one feature and multiple catalogue rows are not independent episodes. Record the source's relative phase namespace and chronology separately. Distinguish a phase-specific plan, composite plan, reconstruction, modern restoration and source interpretation. Absence on a partial excavation plan is unknown unless the source and exposure justify removal.

For genuine paired phase plans, identify a bounded common window from the excavation grid or named feature before scoring. Record a complete visible candidate inventory in that window, independent of the documentary overlay, before computing persistence. Register the panels using survey or stable independent controls; list those controls and registration error. Match a feature by source identity and geometry, not by choosing the nearest line after seeing the result. Record retained, changed, removed and unobservable separately. Compare shared-route boundaries or service lines with internal partition walls using lengths or counts within the same source window. Preserve material, foundation, robbing, excavation and restoration qualifications. Without those controls the difference remains descriptive.

If paired plans cannot be registered, source-defined sequences can test ordered additions, blockages or clearance but cannot supply a numerical wall-survival rate. A single composite plan may support a scoped dimension or topology observation only when the relevant phase attribution is explicit. Unknown means unknown, never zero.

For plan dimensions store the untouched parent page hash, PDF page box, crop and rotation, image dimensions, digitized pixel endpoints, scale-bar endpoints and stated scale. Propagate endpoint and scale reading uncertainty; show a sensitivity bound. Local dimensionless ratios can be reported without a north correction. An unlabeled north arrow remains unspecified north; no true-north azimuth is inferred. Do not transfer the current DEM horizon or an old town-axis bearing to a small excavated wall.

## Documentary and dating joins

For each candidate object or explicitly defined source cohort, retain registration, catalogue identity, object type, precise context, named structure or grid, source layer and depth, phase, date basis and relation to construction or activity. A cohort stays a cohort until physical aliases are adjudicated. The minimum join chain is object to deposit to architectural part to episode to independent date. Distinguish a primary-use deposit, a manufacturing context, a fill, a dump, a surface find and an uncertain association. Recovery-stage labels cannot date manufacture or use.

A construction date is independent only if its sample and stratigraphic relationship are actually reported. Retain laboratory identifier, sample material, reported determination or interval, calibration convention and residuality or intrusive risk. Broad cultural date ranges, model-assigned centuries and dates inferred from the same infrastructure connection do not supply an independent repair date. Record the failed link in every ineligible chain.

Architecture and object modules are initially coded separately. Architectural coders do not receive the newly assembled documentary overlays before their inventory is saved. All coders already know earlier source examples, and this limitation is declared. A coordinator joins the saved records; shared site name alone never establishes an association.

## Predictions, controls and decision

C-0017 P1 predicts at least a 0.25 greater persistence fraction for shared routes than internal partitions. Its confirmation entry gate is twelve independently joined episodes in four towns with comparable preservation and adequate pilot-calibrated power. We report the actual eligible denominator and within-window descriptive differences. We do not substitute many walls in one plan for those episodes. Counterexamples include shared-route clearance, blockage and narrowed circulation, loss after independent rebuilding and apparent continuity created by composite plans.

P2 requires six directly joined repairs in three towns and local component differences across at least four usable connections. P3 requires independent repair dates and a frozen prediction comparison against distance and engineering. Report eligible and rejected cases for each condition. No result from a single composite plan passes those predictions.

C-0018 requires primary-use contexts and independently coded custody change, with matched recovery denominators, at least twenty matched pairs in four towns and adequate sensitivity. C-0019 additionally needs physical evidence distinguishing shared work from ordinary goods allocation. Test the data's ability to meet these gates. Where an association can be calculated on valid denominators, report an effect and source uncertainty; do not manufacture controls from unreported finds. A zero count of eligible joins is a result about this source frame, not proof that ancient institutions were absent.

The required benchmark is `python -m bench shots` in synthetic mode, with a versioned output for workflow guards. It supplies no archaeological score. A fresh confirmation read is not authorized by this exploratory protocol; a future round must freeze all participants and pass the population and power audit. Neither source reuse nor a new seed restores freshness.
