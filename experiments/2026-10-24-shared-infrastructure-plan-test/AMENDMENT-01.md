# Supplementary Mohenjo-daro availability control

Recorded on 24 October 2026 before acquiring or measuring the supplementary plan data. The six-town primary source frame and all prediction thresholds remain those of the original protocol.

The early source audit reports composite Harappa plans, a schematic Dholavira sequence and absent Lothal plates. A published GIS reanalysis at Mohenjo-daro offers a useful independent check of what reusable phase-resolved legacy data can supply. Green, Alam and Petrie's 2026 article and its university repository list supporting information; Green's 2018 study lists two supplementary files. The earlier comparative package already examined these papers and the 2026 residential statistics. None of that material becomes fresh confirmation.

We add one supplementary source-availability and method-control case at Mohenjo-daro. First inspect the supplementary file inventory and source provenance. If spatial and phase data are supplied, assess whether they contain explicit streets or only residence polygons, and whether the chronology and feature correspondence permit the pre-existing measurement rules. The control is retained even if it supplies no eligible route comparison. It does not replace an unsuccessful primary town or change the stopping rule to seek a preferred result.

The coordinator owns `modules/mohenjo-control/` and the corresponding D: raw directory. The architecture and documentary agents retain their original tasks. Any numerical analysis remains a declared exploratory reuse or a reproduction of published work. A table of house areas alone cannot measure street survival or identify an institution.
