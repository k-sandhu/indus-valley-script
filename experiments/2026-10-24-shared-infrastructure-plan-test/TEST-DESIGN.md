# The next discriminating comparison

Status: prospective acquisition design informed by this exposed pilot. This file does not register a fresh confirmation round. The core pass rules are inherited from C-0017 to C-0019 and the frozen protocol; final variance, sample size, scoring family and power require a blinded pilot on new admissible material.

## Recover the missing observation

For architecture, request the original phase drawings, survey grid and section sheets for a complete bounded building group and its connecting street or drain. Harappa's published Area C cart-track and upper street plans, Kenoyer 1991 Figures 4.12 and 4.16, show that independent grid registration can be feasible, while their narrow early exposure and combined upper levels show why geometric alignment alone cannot qualify the comparison. A complete later plan cannot repair an incomplete earlier window. Farmana's central Figures 5.69 and 5.70 are adjacent halves of one Period IIB plan and must remain a single phase rather than a fabricated before-and-after pair.

For documentary contexts, recover field registration, lot, locus, level and section references before assigning objects to buildings. Resolve the Harappa Wall 15 conflict between caption and prose against the field register rather than voting between printed passages. Recover the individual find records for the Trench 10E seals and Trench 54 tablets. At Banawali, find the sitting-room seal's registration and the house's structural subphases. At Dholavira, follow the three literal room labels into a room-specific floor sequence. A site or broad stage does not meet these requests.

For dating, require a sample's material identification, laboratory determination, pretreatment, calibration convention and the physical relation between the sample and the target event. Prefer short-lived material where available. Preserve all stratigraphically inconsistent results for an explicit residuality or intrusion assessment. A Bayesian sequence model can combine independently justified order and dates; it cannot create an object-to-event association absent from the excavation record.

## Distinguish the competing explanations

| Explanation | Observation it predicts | Observation that would weaken it |
|---|---|---|
| Common rules for connected services | Preferential route continuity after matched preservation; usable joins across locally different construction | No continuity advantage in a powered comparison; joins exist only in composite drawings |
| Settlement-wide supervision | Repair timing and component choices follow settlement-wide coordination beyond engineering constraints | Stable local differences and staggered repairs cluster by connected groups with better withheld prediction |
| Engineering constraints | Slope, flow, load, foundation and flood exposure account for retained alignments and repairs | Connected-group prediction improves on those measured variables in new episode groups |
| Household decisions with inherited streets | Local additions and closures accumulate while movement follows inherited constraints | Preferential joined repairs and independent cross-group timing remain after those constraints are controlled |
| Workshop or secondary-discard concentration | Tablet and sealing association follows production waste and deposition rather than operational custody settings | Primary fastening and use contexts retain an association under matched recovery and independent custody coding |
| Goods allocation | Physical goods classes explain documentary variation | Work-specific associations and externally established repair or provision classes predict new records beyond goods and style |

These alternatives can coexist. The test compares their predictions for the same units and does not assign an institution from a plan's regularity alone. Local construction differences by themselves cannot distinguish negotiation from delegated supervision.

## Freeze and score

1. Adjudicate physical identity, aliases, faces, impressions of one stamp and copied production series. Split discovery and confirmation at the relevant dependent group, preserving exposure history. Previously examined plans and objects remain discovery evidence.
2. Have architectural coders inventory a complete common window before receiving documentary finds. Use source grid controls independent of the tested features. Report control fit, held-out control error, transformation and source survey uncertainty. Do not warp a plan until the desired walls coincide.
3. Have context coders identify original operation, manufacture, reuse, destruction and later discard before receiving the architecture persistence result. Record sediment volume, recovery method and preservation for both members of every candidate pair. A gateway or storage room supplies a candidate setting; the discovery pilot must validate a physical custody-change criterion independently of the tested sealing occurrence. Ordinary provisioning also fits goods allocation. Require an additional observable distinction specific to work before creating labour labels.
4. Establish episode chronology from independent physical stratigraphy and samples. Separate laboratory replicates, one specimen, one deposit and one rebuilding event. Freeze a chronological model before using it to label the tested repairs.
5. Use the discovery pilot to simulate the intended clustered design across plausible missingness and measurement-error scenarios. Freeze a sample size with adequate sensitivity to the inherited 0.25 route difference and odds ratio of two. The minima of twelve episodes and twenty pairs do not certify power.
6. Freeze all registered participants, target measures, exclusion rules, influence checks and the one joint update for overlapping predictions. Run the actual benchmark only after its fresh-population and spent-family checks pass. Read confirmation once.

## Record a result that can fail

Publish retained, changed, removed and unobservable features with their source coordinates and reasons. Report the eligible denominator before the effect. For the architectural target, freeze the geometric matching definition before scoring shared-route minus internal-partition persistence within common windows and retain the town-level dependence. Code retained masonry, rebuilding on the same alignment, rerouting and interruption of service separately. Functional renewal does not automatically count as retention of the tested wall or boundary. For documentary targets, report matched recovery denominators and the primary-use subset. Leave unestimable effects null rather than returning zero.

The strongest model consistent with this pilot is retention or renewal of some connections through changing local access. It survives only if new fully joined evidence retains that preference after preservation and engineering controls. A powered absence of preferential continuity, or a documentary association produced entirely by manufacture and redeposition, decides against the relevant claim.
