## Result and limits

The work yields 18 scoped plan quantities, 29 architectural source cases, 32 frozen context assessments and 78 source-specific dating entries. It adds an auditable route from each result back to source geometry, depositional history or dated sample. No complete persistence contrast or matched documentary pair meets the frozen entry requirements. Prior exposure and overlapping source units prevent a fresh confirmation interpretation, and the current conjecture credences remain unchanged.

The next acquisition should recover a complete joined episode, not simply add another site or picture. Harappa's Area C full phase drawings, named floor finds and Wall 15 field register are immediate targets; Lothal's missing plates and Dholavira's encroached Lower Town street are specific counterexample targets. A result against common rules is useful if it survives preservation and recovery controls. A claim about labour additionally needs a physical distinction from goods allocation.

The strongest reading is selective continuity, including renewal after interruption, through changing local access. A powered comparison of complete phase windows and independently dated operational documents can decide it. If preferential route continuity disappears, or manufacture and discard fully explain the documentary pattern, the relevant shared-infrastructure extension fails.
