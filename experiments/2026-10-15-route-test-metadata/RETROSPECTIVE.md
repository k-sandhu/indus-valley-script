# Retrospective: Q-0009 pilot, 15 October 2026

We chose Q-0009 from the starter Priority 1 metadata questions because its inputs were all held and its stopping rule, a fixed sample with every placeholder category reviewed, could be met in one session. The route to it, and every point where the route was unclear, is in the [route-test report](../2026-10-15-route-test/REPORT.md); this note covers the data work.

The first obstacle was that the worktree had no local data. `python tools/fetch_data.py --verify lipi` reported both pinned files missing, and `--fetch lipi --dry-run` showed the clone it would make. Rather than download a second copy, we read the maintainer's existing local files from the main checkout, recorded their hashes and confirmed that the faces file matches the hash DATA.md gives for the 12 October inventory.

The second was rights. The Lipi export has no licence, and REDISTRIBUTION.md keeps record-level derivatives local. The CISI 2 pilot of 13 October tracks per-object field flags with CISI numbers and Lipi family ids, so we followed that precedent: the tracked sample table holds identifiers, the stratum, the crop's file name and hash, and our own reading; the crops, sign codes and named motif values stay local. A maintainer may judge that the stratum column, which records the placeholder each sampled object carries, is itself a record-level derivative; dropping that column would not change any count in the report.

The third was reading. Many CISI crops are small and dark, and the protocol had not planned for enlargement. We viewed 13 of the 38 crops at two or three times their size with automatic contrast and flagged each one. Four faces had large forms that could be signs or a device; for those we compared the reading with the number of signs the export transcribes and recorded that we did so. Two of the four control seals showed their animal only after enlargement, which is itself evidence for the main result: a worn face hides its motif from any single reader.

The outcome is a mapping that accepts `None` as "no motif" on rectangular seals and treats it as unknown elsewhere on seals, with seal motif coverage at 89.9% instead of 94.2%. Nothing in the 12 October inventory was changed; the proposed DATA.md and manifest text is in this package for maintainers. The next step is a blind second reading of every square-seal `None` and `-` face.
