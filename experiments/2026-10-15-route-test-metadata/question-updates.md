> 15 October 2026, `experiments/2026-10-15-route-test-metadata/README.md`. A fixed, hash-ordered sample of 38 seal faces was read against their CISI crops. `None` means no motif on rectangular seals (8 of 8 text-only) but not on square seals, where 6 of 12 sampled faces are broken or worn where the animal would be; `-` on square seals hides at least one recorded animal (M-1005). Mapping v1 keeps `None` as an asserted absence only on rectangular seals and lowers seal motif coverage from 94.2% to 89.9% of seal objects. Next: a blind second reading of all 76 square `None` faces and 58 square `-` faces, then the same check for tablets, whose 1,513 `None` faces include 663 on bas-relief tablets, a type that often carries an image.
