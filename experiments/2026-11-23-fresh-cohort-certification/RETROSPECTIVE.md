# Retrospective: fresh post-catalogue cohort certification

Date: 23 November 2026. Scope: non-purchase execution of GitHub Issue #20 (`Q-1014` and `Q-1026`), plus unsent institutional data-access drafts for `Q-1011` and `Q-1017`.

## Approach

We froze [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md) (`PROTOCOL.sha256`) before reading any candidate row, Lipi metadata record or held PDF page. To preserve blinding for future prospective tests, every script excluded sign-sequence columns from `lipi_observed_faces.jsonl`, refused to render PDF pages as images for visual viewing, and probed held PDFs strictly for file hash, page count, embedded raster count and figure or plate caption tokens.

## Challenges and fixes

1. **Dholavira false-absent edge case (`PCF-DHV-141`)**. The 21 October inventory marked `PCF-DHV-141` (registration `33693`, grid `35x63x4`, Lower Town, Stage VI) as `absent` because both candidate families inside its strict `Lower Town` filter conflicted. Checking all 230 Dholavira Lipi families revealed unregistered rectangular seal family `4277` in the exact same grid square (`35 x 63 x 4`) and Period `6` under `Middle Town-`, plus eight unregistered Dholavira seal families with blank spatial coordinates. We therefore reclassified `PCF-DHV-141` to `blocked_by_alias_ambiguity`, preventing a false certification.
2. **Non-PDF local holdings for *Man and Environment***. Two verified-absent objects (`PCF-MTL-001` at Mitathal and `PCF-BTD-002` at Bet Dwarka) have `local_path` entries pointing to DjVu OCR text files (`H06324_djvu.txt`, `H06258_djvu.txt`) with no local PDF or plate scan. Checking file extensions and bytes on disk caught this immediately and placed both rows in `conditionally_absent_needs_plate_check` until the actual PDFs are acquired.
3. **False positive and false negatives in the first blind PDF plate probe**. Our initial plate-probe script accepted a prose page in `Indian_Archaeology_1996_97_A_Review.pdf` (PDF page 31, zero raster images) because PDF border lines tripped a `drawings > 50` heuristic, while missing valid parent-figure and plate-range captions in `SHIKARPUR2009.pdf` (`Fig. 4` covering `fig.4a/4c/4d` on PDF page 8) and `iar-2004-05.pdf` (`Plates 13-14` covering `pl.13` on PDF page 42). A blind structural inspection of page text lengths, image counts and label tokens across those PDFs allowed us to replace the heuristic with a strict rule requiring embedded raster imagery (or more than 200 vector drawing primitives) on a confirmed figure or plate page, supporting parent-figure captions and plate ranges, and flagging image-only interleaved IAR plate leaves (`iar-1995-96` pages 27–29; `iar-1996-97` pages 32 and 34) separately. That refinement moved the certified count from an erroneous 5 (2 seals, including one false positive on an IAR text page) to a verified 8 (3 seals, 3 sealings and 2 tablets).
4. **PowerShell syntax and recursive-glob latency**. Initial PowerShell one-liners hit `&&` and here-string syntax differences on Windows PowerShell 5.1, and a recursive glob over all of `data/raw/` scanned large image trees on D:. Switching to PowerShell `@' ... '@ | python -` piping and targeted paths resolved both issues cleanly.

## Outcomes

- All 503 post-catalogue rows and 358 candidate-audit rows are deterministically classified into four mutually exclusive tiers (`8 certified_absent_sealed`, `88 conditionally_absent_needs_plate_check`, `230 blocked_by_alias_ambiguity`, `177 already_present_or_exposed`).
- Independent verification ([code/verify.py](code/verify.py)) passed 10 of 10 checks, confirming zero overlap with Lipi (4,695), Mackay and Marshall (1,146), CISI 1 and 2 basic data (3,680 rows, 2,602 joined) and `bench/spent.json`, verified embedded plate imagery on all 8 certified objects, and intact blinding invariants.
- Exact power analysis against frozen P1 to P4 proved that three certified seals are underpowered (`P2` power 0.000, `P1` shuffle-null pass rate 50.2%, kill condition inactive), motivating the two unsent institutional access requests ([REQUEST-ICIT-Q1011.md](REQUEST-ICIT-Q1011.md) and [REQUEST-HARP-Q1017.md](REQUEST-HARP-Q1017.md)) and targeted small-site plate checks before firing the first fresh shot.
