# Protocol: byproduct publication inventory

## Question
Which recorded project outputs outside the narrow question of deciphering the Indus script establish reusable archaeological, metrological, catalogue, computational, conservation, or reproducibility results, and what is the smallest credible publication form for each?

## Inputs
I will inspect the repository guidance, all four paper manuscripts, the 112-entry experiment registry, its retrospective index, the October 6 contribution assessment, and the recorded README/REPORT/FINDINGS/RETROSPECTIVE documents and result summaries under `experiments/`, `research/`, `results/`, `output/`, `knowledge/`, and `papers/`. Exact outcome claims will come from saved report fields or saved machine-readable outputs; I will not rerun an analysis. Large outputs may be sampled at summary fields. Existing predecessor assessments are starting points, not substitutes for source paths.

## Method and comparisons
I will group closely related registry entries into publication candidates while retaining the registry IDs that each candidate covers. For each candidate, I will record the measured object/population, result and status, exact evidence paths/fields, close precedents found in a bounded live-web search, opened/snippet status, remaining validation, smallest publication form, real candidate venue(s), and remaining effort. I will compare candidate readiness, field usefulness, source recoverability, and plausible novelty. Novelty is provisional where a search found only adjacent work or only snippets.

The bounded search will use targeted searches by candidate domain and publication form, then open selected result pages. It will not establish an exhaustive literature review. Candidate venues will be named only when a current journal or series page confirms that the venue exists and its scope fits the form.

## Selection rules
Do not merge distinct source claims just because they concern the same site. Keep raw versus prose totals, original versus later catalogue observations, and generated versus archaeological observations separate. Treat derived numbers as confirmed only when the cited saved output or report supplies them. Use repository status labels, mapping its legacy status wording to the closest label and preserving the original wording in the record. Mark proposed trials separately from completed simulations. Do not call source-informed recounting independent excavation evidence.

## Negative result and stopping rule
A candidate can be a negative result if it rejects a tested method, exposes an identifiability limit, demonstrates that a source claim does not match its comparator, or documents an acquisition failure that narrows future work. Negative evidence is publishable only when its protocol, comparison, denominator and retained endpoint are clear. If a source output does not confirm a number, record it as unverified rather than infer it. Stop each precedent check after the targeted searches and selected source openings listed in `sources.json`; unresolved novelty is reported explicitly.

## Amendment rule
Any change after inspecting candidate outcomes will be dated here and will mark affected judgments exploratory. This inventory is an assessment of existing outputs; no earlier analysis is rerun or altered.
