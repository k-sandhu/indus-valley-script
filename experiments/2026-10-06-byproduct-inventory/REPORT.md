**Priority 4: numerical premises and capacity, record 1.** In variable order `(L,S,v)`, the assumed equations `v=2S-L` and `vL+1=7S+5` have four positive-integer solutions: `(4,20,36)`, `(6,8,10)`, `(10,8,6)` and `(36,20,4)`. [NLP4DH 2026](https://aclanthology.org/events/nlp4dh-2026/) is an international conference; the next call needs a format check (SRC-16).
