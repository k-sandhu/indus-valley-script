# Sources and rights

## Lüders's list of Brāhmī inscriptions

Heinrich Lüders, *A List of Brahmi Inscriptions from the Earliest Times to about A.D. 400 with the Exception of those of Asoka*, Appendix to *Epigraphia Indica and Record of the Archaeological Survey of India*, vol. 10 (Calcutta: Superintendent Government Printing, India, 1912). The list numbers 1,353 inscriptions; its additions and corrections add new entries up to no. 1454, and indexes of personal names, geographical names and miscellaneous terms follow. In his preface Lüders says that he quotes every proper name in the texts and adds the Sanskrit equivalent of Prakrit names where he can; the calibration uses those equivalents.

We downloaded two independent scans from the Internet Archive on 21 October 2026 at 01:30 UTC. Both stay local in `data/raw/2026-10-21-birth-star-names/sources/`.

**Rights.** The work is in the public domain. It was published in 1912, before the 1931 cutoff for the United States; as a publication of the Government of India its Indian term of sixty years from publication ended in 1972; and Lüders died in 1943, so a term of the author's life plus seventy years ended in 2013. The Archaeological Survey copy is marked public domain by its uploader. Under [REDISTRIBUTION.md](../../knowledge/provenance/REDISTRIBUTION.md), large OCR files stay local. We track the extracted donor table, [calibration-v1/luders-donors.csv](calibration-v1/luders-donors.csv): the entry numbers, name readings and Sanskrit equivalents of both scans, both markings and their resolution, without the OCR context of the clauses. It is project material under CC0 1.0 drawn from a public-domain source.
