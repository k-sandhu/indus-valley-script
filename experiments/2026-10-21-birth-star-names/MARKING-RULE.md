# Rule for marking names formed from asterisms

Written on 21 October 2026 (UTC) before any donor name was extracted from Lüders's list or marked. Two markers apply this rule independently to the same extracted list; neither sees the other's marks before both are complete.

## What is marked

Each row of the extracted list is one donor occurrence: a Prakrit name as printed by Lüders, with the Sanskrit equivalent he gives in parentheses when he gives one. The OCR text loses or garbles diacritics, so `Asida (Ashagha)` stands for Āsāḍha (Āṣāḍha) and `Posi (Pushy2)` for Posī (Puṣyā). A marker reads through such errors. Each row receives three values:

- `asterism`: the asterism from the table below that forms the name, or empty.
- `strength`: `strict` when the stem is unambiguous, `ambiguous` when the stem also has a common non-astral meaning (marked in the table), empty when no asterism is marked.
- `note`: optional, for an uncertain reading.

## When a name is formed from an asterism

A name is formed from an asterism when, after an honorific prefix (Aya-, Ayya-, Arya-, Aryya-, Bhadanta-, Bhayamta-) is removed, the whole name or its first member is an asterism stem in the table, in its Sanskrit or Prakrit form or as its regular vṛddhi derivative (Phālguna, Āṣāḍha, Vaiśākha, Pauṣa, Taiṣya, Rauhiṇa, Kārttika, Māgha, Caitra, Śrāvaṇa, Śrāviṣṭha, Prauṣṭhapada, Raivata, Āśvayuja, Mārgaśīrṣa). The stem may stand alone or be followed by:

- a gender ending or a hypocoristic suffix (-a, -ā, -i, -ī, -u, -ka, -kā, -aka, -ika, -ikā, -ila, -iya, -inī, -iṇī);
- a second member of the usual kind (-datta/-data/-dina/-dinna, -mitra/-mita/-miti, -rakṣita/-rakhita, -pālita, -gupta/-guta, -deva, -sena, -bhūti, -nandin/-nandi, -śrī/-siri, -dāsa/-dāsī, -ghoṣa, -bala, -śarman, -varman, -yaśas/-yasa).

The Sanskrit equivalent Lüders prints decides the stem when it identifies one. The Prakrit form decides when he gives none. When the two conflict, the Sanskrit equivalent wins and the conflict goes into `note`.

A stem occurring only as the second member of a name does not mark it (Dhamarohiṇa would not count; no such name is expected). A place name, a family or gotra name, a king's dynastic name, a deity's name and the name of a month used as a date do not count; the extraction removes most of them, and a marker who sees one leaves `asterism` empty and writes `not a personal name` in `note`.

## Stems that are not marked

Bhadra alone ("auspicious"), Aśva/Asa/Assa alone ("horse"), Sava ("all"), Sāta and Sāti- in Sātavāhana and Sātakarṇi (dynastic names), Mitra alone and Nakṣatra alone are not marked. Śiva, Skanda and other gods' names are not asterisms.

## Strict and broad counts

The strict count includes rows whose stem has no mark in the "Ambiguous" column. The broad count adds the ambiguous stems Magha, Hasta, Citrā, Jyeṣṭhā, Mūla, Śravaṇa and Bahula. Both counts are reported; the strict count is primary.
