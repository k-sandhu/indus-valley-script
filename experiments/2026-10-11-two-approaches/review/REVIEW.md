## Probability, grouping and search

Its scores therefore concern that mapped alphabet; assigning its UNKNOWN mass separately to multiple unseen codes would cease to define a normalized distribution. The comparison is consistent because every fitted family uses the same training alphabet and mapping. The report now states this estimand and preserves the 257 UNKNOWN events among 8,009 targets.

The review also challenged the prototype law's interpretation. Directly permuting the selected prototype bag with a uniqueness constraint is a separately declared conditional law. Their total variation distance is 2/3. The 180 original slot allocations and 24 selected-bag allocations are exhaustively enumerated in [prototype_counterexample.py](prototype_counterexample.py) and [its output](prototype-counterexample.json).
