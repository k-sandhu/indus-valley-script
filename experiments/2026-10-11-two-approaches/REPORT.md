# Two developments beyond the Bonta extension

11 October 2026. The parallel investigation developed two recommendations into code and measured outcomes: learn a sign-class grammar, following Rao, Yadav and colleagues; and test competing administrative functions before selecting sign meanings, following Mukhopadhyay. The first supplies a small exploratory predictive improvement. The second establishes repeated whole-object designs under specified allocation controls, while a stronger control removes the apparent evidence for particular sign partners across faces. Together they support a more precise research target: learn how a record is organized, then test how its carrier was used.

The [literature census](literature/LANDSCAPE.md) records 20 sources and 22 source-anchored recommendations. The [grammar report](grammar/REPORT.md), [administrative report](administrative/REPORT.md) and [adversarial review](review/REVIEW.md) give the implementation, outcomes and challenges. This report integrates those packages and develops a proposed token life cycle. All new measurements use graphic transcriptions; the functional schema below remains a hypothesis.

## What other researchers proposed

The sources supply several ways to advance the work. Rao and colleagues proposed machine-learning grammar induction in the Discussion of *A Markov model of the Indus script*. Yadav's studies join sign contexts with stochastic grammar, site, object type and graphic variants. Mukhopadhyay proposes restricting the semantic domain before assigning individual values, with licensing, commodity control, taxation and access among her candidates. Frenez's corporate identity and branding model and Rao's ration/labour model supply competing explanations for related objects. [Rao et al. (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0906237106), [Yadav and colleagues](https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.3017), [Mukhopadhyay (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-023-02320-7), [Frenez (2018)](https://www.harappa.com/sites/default/files/pdf/private-person.pdf), [Rao (2018)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.00049).

Other recommendations include fixing pictorial components before a rebus search, resolving add-ons and composites, testing numerical series with explicit units and regional controls, and studying seal reuse under emblem interpretations. The [landscape table](literature/LANDSCAPE.md#what-the-approaches-ask-us-to-develop) links each prescription to its original passage and a practical test. Those paths are retained for later work; the two selected packages use the sequences and physical-object links already available here.

Recent precedents shape the comparison. Tiwari's July 2026 paper already uses a categorical hidden Markov model and positional states. Khanna and Merriam study contextual classes and request narrower semantic and archaeological filters. Raghavendra's SIGIL supplies a constructed compositional system with nonphonetic meanings. Sharman's July 2026 administrative grammar shares the corpus lineage and reverses the positional names of several codes relative to this repository's normalized direction. The present work therefore adds a particular predictive and carrier-allocation comparison, with source dependence and direction recorded. It makes no claim to originate HMM analysis or administrative field grammars. [Tiwari](https://aclanthology.org/2026.nlp4dh-1.28.pdf), [Khanna and Merriam](https://www.ijcaonline.org/archives/volume187/number64/khanna-2025-ijca-926075.pdf), [Raghavendra](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.02999v1), [Sharman's author page](https://www.souldriver.com.au/paper/the-indus-operating-system).

## Development 1: a reusable grammar of graphic classes

The model learns sign distributions for hidden classes and probabilities of passing from one class to another. Its contextual variant lets length and slot alter those transitions. Prediction sums over class paths and uses only the prefix and specified length. The resulting grammar describes preferred constructions and substitutions; smoothing gives every mapped sequence a positive probability.

The evaluation retains 1,596 distinct eligible texts with 8,009 tokens. Physical-object and identical-text links are closed across all 5,680 recorded faces before eligibility filtering. Five outer folds and three inner folds keep each resulting component together. Inner scores choose model settings and mixture weights. [Protocol](grammar/PROTOCOL.md), [population and hashes](grammar/run-v1/manifest.json).

The standalone grammar fails to improve on Markov. Its weakness suggested using shared classes to supplement particular sign transitions. The later convex mixture improves loss by **0.0557 bits per token over the matched Markov-plus-position mixture**, with a conditional component-bootstrap interval of **0.0436–0.0675**. The improvement is positive in all five folds. Both mixtures use the same weight grid, alphabets and original held-out predictions. Their design followed inspection of the primary results, so a fresh population must test transfer of the saved recipe. [Primary failure](grammar/run-v1/results.json), [mixture amendment](grammar/AMENDMENT-V3.md), [matched outcomes](grammar/run-v3-matched-mixtures/results.json).

Each fit predicts its training alphabet plus one UNKNOWN event. The 257 tokens whose original code is absent from that fit share that bucket. The table measures mapped-sequence likelihood conditional on length, rather than probabilities for individual unseen graphic identities. The class increment is 0.0760 bits on known targets and −0.5569 on UNKNOWN targets. The bootstrap holds fitted models and folds fixed; its interval excludes uncertainty from renewed search and transcription. These qualifications define the result's reach.

The recurring opening family contains 817/820/861, a concentrated internal family contains 002/060, and a common completion family contains 740/520. Several intermediate classes overlap. The selected twelve-state capacity is the upper edge of the declared grid; it does not establish twelve ancient functions. A descriptive audit also identifies a possible late attachment: 740→400 and 740→090 have 73 and 66 occurrences, with the right code final in 72 and 60; 520→400 adds seven final cases. These selected counts motivate a construction in which a completed field can take a later qualifier or operation. [Class inventory](grammar/run-v1/learned-grammar.json), [stability](grammar/run-v1/classes-stability.json), [post-outcome counts](grammar/post-outcome-diagnostics.json).

This development produces a reusable [prefix predictor](grammar/predict.py), frozen [carry-forward recipe](grammar/carry-forward.json) and [future predictions](grammar/FUTURE-PREDICTIONS.md). Constructed administrative fields and compositional clauses can share exactly the same graphic probability law. Their controls show why grammar recovery needs an external functional test. ## Development 2: whole-object records and competing uses

The administrative analysis represents a carrier as an unordered multiset of its whole recorded face strings. This preserves within-face order and avoids constructing a reading order between surfaces. The primary population has **417 objects and 867 faces**, including 312 distinct bundle prototypes within matched allocation cells. The mirror-invariant sensitivity has 543 objects and 1,124 faces. Eligibility requires every recorded row to pass the stated filter. Five primary objects and eight sensitivity objects conflict with their declared side count, so this is a census of recorded surfaces rather than a verified inventory of every surviving physical face. [Input accounting](administrative/run-v1/input-summary.json).

The first reference reallocates complete face modules among carriers matched on site, exact type, recorded face count and sorted length profile. It preserves the module bags and their internal sign order. In the primary population, **423 pairs of carriers have identical bundles**, compared with a reference mean of 141.19. The strongest depleted cross-face pair is W034/W700, with zero exposures and an expected 23.49 under that reference. The full-search maximum and a ten-test Holm family account for selecting pairs. [Frozen outcomes](administrative/run-v1/summary.json), [pair details](administrative/run-v1/rtl-all.json).

A stronger, post-outcome control preserves whether each face slot contains W700. This matters because 103 primary faces contain both W034 and W700, while only one contains W034 without W700. Keeping that face role reduces expected cross-face W034/W700 exposure to about 0.957. Under the new reference, **none of the sign-pair maxima pass the ten-test Holm threshold**; the primary prototype depletion has adjusted p = 0.6306. Particular partner binding beyond the preserved role remains unestablished. [Role-control amendment](administrative/AMENDMENT-2026-10-11-role-control.md), [outcomes](administrative/role-control-v1/summary.json), [independent allocation audit](review/allocation-checks-v1.json).

Repeated bundles remain unusual under the role-preserving reference: the observed 423 collision pairs compare with a mean of 190.94, adjusted p = 0.002. That outcome is consistent with repeated designs and copying. Mould or production copying can supply the repetition without identifying a tablet's social function. [Role-controlled primary output](administrative/role-control-v1/rtl-all.json).

A descriptive field audit adds a useful relation: 17 complete companion-module classes, held at fixed site and exact object type, occur beside more than one W032/W033/W034 tall-stroke value in a W700 module. This supplies observed examples of a stable module accompanying variable tier-shaped graphics. The audit assigns no probability tail or numerical unit to that variation. [Exact field counts](administrative/run-v1/frame-audit.json).

The prototype reference also has a defined limitation. It reallocates the module bag retained after selecting one instance per bundle, subject to uniqueness. Adversarial review proved that this separately specified law need not equal reallocating the original copy-weighted bag and then selecting unique bundles. A finite example gives total variation distance 2/3 between those laws. Prototype tails describe the selected-bag reference; they do not automatically calibrate an unknown manufacture-and-selection process. The frozen endpoint is retained with that interpretation corrected. [Exact counterexample](review/prototype-counterexample.json), [review](review/REVIEW.md).

## A proposed record and token life cycle

The linked theory treats some carriers as portable records of a stable class and a bounded capacity, grade or entitlement. A longer field could identify an issuer, bearer, service or commodity. A short field could set a tier, scope or limit. This schema explains why shared graphic classes and recurring complete designs are useful targets. The present counts establish neither these labels nor numerical units; W032/W033/W034 name distinct tall-stroke graphics under the catalogue convention.

A carrier could be issued, presented, checked or redeemed, and then retained, returned or cancelled. Its inscription might remain constant while its social role changes. Under this model, an authorization before use and a receipt after use can be phases of the same object. This connects Mukhopadhyay's licence proposal with Rao's allocation proposal and adds a testable state variable: the use phase. Repetition alone cannot decide whether copies were issued entitlements, receipts, commodity labels or corporate identities.

The grammar adds a second proposed mechanism. A completion marker could finish an entity or value field, followed by an attachment that states an operation or account category. The observed late-tail constructions make that sequence worth testing. A name/title construction, a mixed notation and a conventional emblem workflow can also produce it. The next comparison must connect the tail to an independently observed use, keeping alternative preceding-field interpretations in the same sample.

| Proposed mechanism | Observation to obtain independently | Prediction that could distinguish the mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Reusable standing entitlement | Repeated handling/use history, cancellation and access context | Stable modules persist across uses; changes in validity appear in physical use marks or a defined operator field |
| Receipt produced by a completed transaction | Secure event association and measured delivery or allocation | Event differences predict quantities or categories; the completed-use state is observable |
| Issuer or bearer identity | Several objects independently linked to an issuing group or bearer | The identity module persists across different goods and operations |
| Commodity or capacity record | Identified contents and independently measured amount or capacity | Contents predict the class, and measured variation tests a fixed tier or amount proposal |
| Sealed-package or branded-group record | Physical attachment/impression joins and manufacturing context | Stable labels follow the associated package or production group, with relevant attachment traces |

Surface wear, cancellation and attachment must be coded without knowledge of the proposed graphic category, with preservation and material matched. Blinded replica trials can compare repeated presentation, one-use surrender, manufacture and post-deposition abrasion before archaeological features are classified. A manufacturing family must come from independent joins, tooling or excavation information; catalogue adjacency is insufficient. The [pre-result hypothesis note](HYPOTHESIS-NOTE.md) preserves the initial schema, and the administrative package develops the operational alternatives in its [hypothesis sheet](administrative/HYPOTHESES.md) and [machine-readable schema](administrative/administrative-grammar.json).

The exact [anchor calculation](ANCHORS.md) clarifies what such evidence must add. Four invented interpretations have identical graphic probabilities. Under the demonstration's known 10% symmetric errors, one hypothetical contextual partition supplies 0.531 bits of expected information; two distinct independent partitions supply 1.062. Repeating a copy of one witness adds none. These are mathematical planning examples, with no estimated archaeological error rate or ranking of acquisition routes.

## What the review established and what is frozen for later work

Independent checks reconstruct the grammar probabilities, fit alphabets, grouped splits, weight selections, allocation margins, full-search tails and adjusted tests. The reviewer also supplies the prototype-law counterexample. The [review response](REVIEW-RESPONSE.md) records how these findings changed the claims. Mechanical checks establish the stated computations and preserved witnesses; the theory needs the contextual observations in the table above.

The new-text test carries forward fixed class and positional mixtures and groups new objects against the entire earlier register. The administrative test carries forward face-role controls, explicit source joins and rival life-cycle predictions. Both require observations that were not used to choose the present refinements. No further rerun of the existing outcomes can turn this exploratory evidence into that test.

Code, protocols, aggregate outputs, source URLs and hashes are retained in this dated package. Bulk transcriptions, fitted arrays, per-object derivatives and downloaded source witnesses remain local-only.
