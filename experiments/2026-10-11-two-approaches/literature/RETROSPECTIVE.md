# Source selection and development retrospective

Status: **Exploratory**, 11 October 2026. Ownership was limited to this literature directory and its corresponding local raw-source directory. The coordinator and two other contributors owned the selected empirical investigations.

The work began with the project overview, experiment registry, writing and redistribution rules, literature retrospective and the reviewed October 6–7 recommendations catalogue. The earlier catalogue's source-level distinction between a prescription, a reported position and our implementation was reused. Source summaries were rebuilt from selected passages, rather than adding the earlier 56 rows to this package wholesale.

The first recommendation sent to the coordinator selected Rao's grammar-induction prescription and Mukhopadhyay's semantic-scope prescription, with Frenez's branding test as a rival contextual interpretation. The coordinator assigned the empirical work to two parallel contributors. The literature contributor sent them precise source locators, prior-method overlaps and semantic ambiguities as those were checked.

Reading recent sources changed the comparison. Raghavendra and Nair were initially described as newly found leads; the existing literature matrix showed that they were already indexed, and that description was corrected before integration. Full reading supplied specific rival mechanisms and limits. Khanna–Merriam and Merriam supplied direct precedents for context expansion, pair exclusivity and lexical constraint searches. A primary-source check of Tiwari supplied a categorical HMM precedent, so the bounded census replaced the redundant standalone Mahadevan 1986 entry with Tiwari. The historical OCR witness remains local, and Mahadevan remains represented through the Ganesan and Rao/Yadav sources.

The downloaded Khanna–Merriam title page corrected the authors to Ruhan Khanna and Louie Merriam. The earlier follow-up uses Louis L. Merriam for his March 2026 preprint, matching that document's title page; the two source-specific forms are retained. The RMRL grammar route was taken from the existing acquisition manifest rather than a reconstructed URL.

Sharman's author page led to a working 107,551-byte, 17-page v1.0 PDF. The paper's methods qualified the expansion corpus as semi-independent and sharing ICIT lineage. Its specification uses initial/final labels opposite this repository's normalized RTL convention; this was sent to both contributors as a direction-transport issue. The page and PDF remain local. Claims about standard quantities, offices and catalogue clusters are attributed source interpretations whose code and results were not reproduced in this package.

Direct source access and indexed reading had different limits. The direct ResearchGate Merriam request returned 403; indexed author text supplied the substantive sections and recommendation passage. Fuls was inspected as indexed author-PDF text without the original figure pixels. Tool-indexed witnesses were retained separately from downloaded PDFs. The tool supplied no exact fetch timestamp for these indexed readings, so their capture metadata describes that limit. An initial anchor check lacked Merriam's concluding section; a separately preserved conclusion witness repaired coverage and all 22 short anchors now match inspected text.

Earlier local witnesses avoided downloading large PDFs again. Selected page extracts were made with PyMuPDF, and HTML text with BeautifulSoup. A Windows console encoding error occurred while printing the newly downloaded Sharman text; setting the output stream to UTF-8 repaired display. No witness was removed, and no frozen experiment endpoint was edited.

The final census has 20 source records, 22 recommendations and 11 connected author groups within the selected sample. Source payloads, extraction records and access failures remain local. The tracked package contains our prose, source summaries, short anchors, scripts and hashes. The 47 checks cover witness hashes and anchor presence. They do not establish the semantic claims in those sources or publication priority for this project's extensions.
