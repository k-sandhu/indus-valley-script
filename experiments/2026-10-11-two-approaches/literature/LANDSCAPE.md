# Two extensions selected from other Indus research

Status: **Exploratory literature selection**, 11 October 2026. This package selects two connected investigations: inferred sign-class grammar, following Rao and colleagues, and competing administrative roles, following Mukhopadhyay's semantic-scope recommendation. The source census contains **20 records and 22 explicit recommendations**. It is a bounded reading of the approaches below. [Sources](sources.json), [recommendations](recommendations.json), [mechanical checks](validation.json).

The source record distinguishes a published proposal, its actual recommendation, and the experiment we propose in response. Most sources were already in this repository. Reading their relevant passages again establishes the prescription and its limits; it does not make them newly discovered precedents. Sharman's July 2026 paper was recovered through the PDF link on his author page and adds a directly relevant administrative-grammar comparison. Its numerical results have not been reproduced here.

## Recent work changes the comparison

Khanna and Merriam's December 2025 paper already uses singleton seeds, context expansion and substitution-resistant pairs. Its final page asks for narrower semantic categories, site/artifact/temporal restrictions and continuous exclusivity scores. Merriam's March 2026 preprint uses weighted preceding/following context distances and a Proto-Dravidian lexical search, then proposes external and repeated-sign constraints. His reported numeral variation weakens an exclusive 705/706–033 interpretation. These are hypothesis-generating methods whose semantic premises require a separate test. [Khanna and Merriam](https://www.ijcaonline.org/archives/volume187/number64/khanna-2025-ijca-926075.pdf), [Merriam](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/401666259_Identifying_Candidate_Syllabic_Sign_Pairs_in_the_Indus_Script).

Tiwari's July 2026 ACL paper fits a categorical HMM, selects four states and reports boundary-state preferences. It also probes a BiLSTM with concatenation, core exchange and terminal substitution. An HMM with positional classes therefore has a recent direct precedent. The useful extension here is the specified evaluation, object dependence control and rival comparison. Two printed comparisons need reproduction: Table 2 includes rigidity above one under `R = 1 − H_position/H_unigram`, and Table 4's synthetic perplexity means are below its real-text mean while the discussion describes lower probabilities for concatenations and core swaps. [Paper, pp. 316–319](https://aclanthology.org/2026.nlp4dh-1.28.pdf).

Nair's April 2026 scorecard compares heraldic and administrative generators with selected attested systems. Raghavendra's August 2026 SIGIL construction supplies nonphonetic compositional meanings, optional blocks, local category constraints and ligatures. Raghavendra's six exact outcomes come from three dependent method families; his larger battery includes reconstructions. SIGIL is a constructed compatibility example and provides no historical odds for the Indus system. Both sources were already indexed here. They require us to test the proposed grammar against flexible structured rivals and to distinguish candidate coverage from meaning recovery. [Nair](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.17828v1), [Raghavendra, §§4–7 and Limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.02999v1).

Sharman's *The Indus Operating System* reports a `[HEADER][CONTENT][TERMINAL]` grammar, held-out parse rates and separate icon/text identity and content channels. His PDF §2.2 calls the Lipi expansion semi-independent because it shares the ICIT lineage. Table 2 calls G740/G520 headers and G861/G817/G820 terminals, opposite this repository's normalized RTL positional labels. The direction convention must be reconciled before assigning equivalent roles. Catalogue proximity also needs publication-order controls before it can identify a production batch. The paper's semantic and numerical outcomes remain reported claims in this survey. [Author page and paper link](https://www.souldriver.com.au/paper/the-indus-operating-system), local source record SHARMAN-2026.

## Why these two investigations were selected

The grammar investigation can produce a reusable model with available sequences and object identities. Its question is whether a learned class organization predicts unseen strings better than declared alternatives. The administrative investigation can take that organization and ask which source-observed relations it explains: the medium carrying a text, its motif, repeated manufacturing forms and correspondence between physical faces. This combination permits a concrete failure: a class model may predict sign order yet fail every physical-context prediction.

This is a changed-method investigation following existing structural results. It does not rerun the failed unknown-gap estimator or restate positive Markov dependence. The prior context work already tests site, motif, size and chronology, while paired-face and numerical studies expose their source ambiguities. New competing role constraints and cross-face binding tests can use those preserved observations without converting the old assumptions into facts. Relevant memory entries are ROOT-02, ROOT-09–14, SIGN-CONTEXT-SEALS-01, CHRONOLOGY-REGION-01, ARCH-01 and PHYS-01 in the [registry](../../../knowledge/experiments/registry.json).

Shared assumptions belong in the two packages' methods: consistent reading order and inventory; physical-family/text grouping across all faces; training-only class selection and tuning; explicit unread/damaged positions; adequate overlap in conditioned samples; and equal search budgets for rival interpretations. Numerical strokes are graphic families until a physical quantity calibrates them. Observed classes can be named by position or context; names such as merchant, tax, ration, deity and permit remain candidate functions.

## Further hypotheses with discriminating tests

The following are this project's proposals. They are not prescriptions attributed to the sources and have no empirical outcome in this literature package.

1. **A terminal can mark the kind of record being validated.** If the terminal selects a permitted combination of preceding fields, a learned boundary class should improve new-field recombination predictions after a position model is fitted. An ownership label can also produce that pattern, so a second test should ask whether the same terminal follows different labels with stable physical carrier use. No literal checksum arithmetic is assumed.
2. **Opposing faces may bind a scope to a level.** A fixed activity or commodity scope on one face with a small discrete quantity set on another is compatible with entitlement or fee slabs. Freely varying transaction amounts predict a broader conditional quantity range. A name-plus-rank system supplies a third competitor. Independently observed contents, fees or use would be needed to choose among them.
3. **Production copies may be issued categories rather than repeated transactions.** Compare how stable a text remains within an independently observed mould or carving family, and which modules vary among neighbouring manufacture groups. Copies must be grouped before evaluation; museum accession order is an acquisition history variable.
4. **A modifier may restrict a shared base category.** Fresh image-adjudicated base/modifier pairs can predict whether the modified sign preserves most base contexts while excluding a declared subset. Related syllabic spellings and stylistic variants can be fitted with equal complexity. Similar preceding/following vectors alone cannot decide the relation.
5. **Different media may expose different parts of one record system.** Fit sequence modules on one carrier class and predict the modules omitted or added on another, using held-out physical groups and common-site/length support. Any cross-face or cross-medium advantage must be compared with a workflow whose symbolic meanings are known from generation.

## Access and source handling

Seventeen selected records use earlier local source witnesses. Fuls and Merriam have newly inspected indexed primary text; a direct Merriam HTTP request returned 403, and Fuls's author figures were not inspected. Sharman's author page and 17-page PDF were downloaded through an observed link. All payloads and selected-page extracts remain in the local raw directory; this package tracks our summaries, code, source URLs and hashes. Rights and edition limits stay attached to each [source record](sources.json). [Redistribution rule](../../../knowledge/provenance/REDISTRIBUTION.md).

The manual source selection and proposed tests are exploratory. The [47 passing mechanical checks](validation.json) verify retained witnesses and short anchors, not the truth of source interpretations or the results of the two selected empirical packages.
