# Copied carriers, face roles and administrative capacity

Complete face modules recur together more often than an independent allocation of the same modules predicts. The strongest sign-level result concerns where a quantity-shaped face is placed: W034 and W700 never occur across different faces in the selected objects, although the whole-module reference predicts such pairings. Preserving which face slots contain W700 removes the corrected sign-pair result. These observations support a working record architecture with copied bundles and distinct face roles. They leave the choice among licences, identity credentials and canonical transaction tokens open. [Frozen results](run-v1/summary.json), [face-role control](role-control-v1/summary.json).

The theoretical extension is a standing-capacity token. A carrier binds a reusable administrative class to a service or commodity tier. The carrier can authorize an action before use and serve as a transaction token after redemption without changing its inscription. That possible life cycle connects Mukhopadhyay's licensing proposal and Rao's canonical ration proposal. [Operational hypotheses](HYPOTHESES.md) and the [machine-readable grammar](administrative-grammar.json) specify the fields, alternatives and observations that would distinguish them.

## Recommendations and prior work

Mukhopadhyay recommends narrowing semantic scope before assigning individual signs meanings in her 2023 article, p.2. She proposes that opposite tablet faces can record activities and standardized licence slabs, and p.29 asks for a study of stamped pointed-base goblets as commercial vessels. Her 2019 paper already develops functional sign classes and document composition. This run follows the scope recommendation by testing the structure of whole carriers before proposing field names. [2019 article](https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-019-0274-1), [2023 article](https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-023-02320-7).

Rao proposes repetitive economic transactions involving canonical amounts of goods or labour and asks for additional analysis and archaeology, printed p.525. Canonical transactions are a serious rival to licences because both can use a small stock of repeated forms. Frenez proposes formal socio-economic identities and asks for further tests of branding assumptions, printed p.186 note 11. These are rival operational models, rather than labels that the present metadata already identifies. [Rao](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.00049), [Frenez](https://www.harappa.com/sites/default/files/pdf/private-person.pdf).

Recent work also overlaps this direction. Sharman's July 2026 author publication proposes an administrative grammar and identity/content/quantity fields; its semantics and claimed archaeological associations remain his interpretation. Raghavendra's August 2026 SIGIL construction supplies a nonphonetic compositional system with licensing-like fields that reproduces several structural signatures. Administrative composition and such counterexamples are therefore precedents. The present contribution is the measured multiface allocation contrast, its prototype reference, and the distinction between face-role occupancy and partner-specific association. [Sharman](https://www.souldriver.com.au/paper/the-indus-operating-system), [Raghavendra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02999), [source review](../literature/LANDSCAPE.md).

The registry's ROOT-10 paired-face analysis tested dimensions against stroke counts. HARP-SHORT-01 audited the repeated three-face Harappa cohort, and ARCH-03 exposed uncertain physical prism orientation. ROOT-09 and later motif studies lacked independent function labels. Their endpoints are preserved. The new [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) allocates intact face modules within exact site, object type, face count and face-length profiles; it does not repeat the old geometry or motif tests.

Another 174 components contain a mixture of eligible and ineligible rows and cannot supply a complete observed bundle for this test. [Input accounting](run-v1/input-summary.json).

The primary set has 384 two-face and 33 three-face objects. Harappa supplies 363 tablet objects, so this is chiefly a Harappa tablet result. There are 105 removed copy excesses, and the largest exact class contains 20 objects. Of the 312 prototypes, 275 belong to cells that allow allocation exchanges and 37 remain fixed. The face-sharing graph has 66 components, but 222 of the 312 prototypes occupy one component. These are counts of retained graphic units, with residual dependence among them. A row-wise held-out classifier would leak many exact face strings across training and test data.

The broader sensitivity includes every recorded row that is complete, known, nonblank and single-line, allowing any direction label and making each face module invariant to end reversal. It gives 543 objects, 1,124 faces, 403 matched prototypes and 398 distinct global bundles. This recovers mirror-invariant content membership for more objects; it supplies no physical turning convention for an exceptional direction.

Five primary objects and eight broader objects have declared side counts inconsistent with their numbers of recorded rows. The two-face tall-stroke/W700 audit is unchanged when these objects are excluded. Every-recorded-row eligibility remains a catalogue criterion. It does not establish that every physical surface was recorded or that each completeness flag has been independently checked against a photograph.

## What binds, and what the control removes

The frozen all-object reference permutes whole face strings among slots of equal length within a matched cell. It preserves each face's internal co-occurrences and the module bag, then reconstructs all unordered bundles and the complete sign-pair search. The sign search requires at least five face-presence occurrences for each code. An independent 1,999-allocation pilot estimates expected counts; 9,999 tail allocations evaluate the maximum enrichment and maximum depletion across the full eligible pair set. Ten tests across the primary and broader, all-object and prototype representations receive Holm correction.

W034/W700 is the selected depletion in every representation. Its observed cross-face exposure is zero. Expected exposure is 23.485 in the primary all-object set, 8.510 in its prototype set, and 7.440 in the broader prototype set. W034 denotes a four-tall-stroke graphic class under the source convention; W700 is the open U form. In the primary population, 103 faces contain both codes and one contains W034 without W700. Allocating complete modules freely can place another W700-bearing face opposite that module. The avoidance is therefore intelligible as a constraint on the number or role of such faces on a carrier. Preserving W700 slot roles reduces expected primary exposure from 23.485 to 0.957. The [dated amendment](AMENDMENT-2026-10-11-role-control.md) was written after these outcomes. It adds W700 presence to each length bucket and reruns the full maximum search with 1,999 pilot and 4,999 tail allocations. The primary objects have 109 carriers with no W700-bearing face, 305 with one, and three with two. The controlled allocation preserves those observed slots and counts. No corrected sign-pair maximum remains below .05. Prototype depletion changes from raw p=.0001 to p=.5816, Holm p=.6306; prototype enrichment has Holm p=.0804. Copied bundles still exceed the controlled allocation: 423 collisions versus 190.940 expected, Holm p=.002. [Control results](role-control-v1/summary.json).

The measured structure is consequently copying plus face-role occupancy. Evidence for particular sign choices binding to particular opposite-face choices is weaker under the control. Failure of the controlled test does not identify its null as the historical production process, and the W700 partition was selected after the original result. This contrast is a diagnostic for the source of the observed statistic.

The prototype reference is a separately constructed law conditional on the selected prototype module bag and within-cell uniqueness. It is not the distribution induced by permuting the original copy-weighted bag and then deduplicating it. Independent review demonstrates the distinction exactly: the invented population AB, AB, CD yields only AB/CD under the original-bag process conditioned on two prototypes and selected bag ABCD, while direct permutation of ABCD supports three pairings equally. Their total variation distance is 2/3. [Executable counterexample](../review/prototype_counterexample.py), [exact enumeration](../review/prototype-counterexample.json). This limits the prototype result to its stated conditional law.

## Variable tiers and a usable record model

Among the 384 primary two-face objects, 238 have exactly one of the source-qualified W032/W033/W034 tall-stroke groups beside W700 and a companion face. The graphic stroke counts are two on 66 objects, three on 95 and four on 77. The corresponding prototype counts are 58, 70 and 56. Holding the complete companion module, site and exact object type fixed leaves 17 anchor classes with more than one tall-stroke value. Three such prototype classes have three objects and fourteen have two. [Field audit](run-v1/frame-audit.json).

This gives a practical representation: a complete class-like module can accompany more than one tier-shaped module. A parser can preserve the relation without naming the class or interpreting the tier as a weight. The 22 three-face Harappa objects are outside this two-face audit; removing them changes none of these numbers. Their earlier source audit independently retains a repeated profile and unresolved exceptional counts. Short and tall stroke groups stay separate, and the three-face modules are not added as though their graphic counts shared a known unit.

The proposed grammar has a carrier binding layer, a categorical descriptor layer and a tier layer. A standing entitlement design reads the descriptor as a permitted service or commodity class and the tier as its authorization level. An issuer design attaches an issuing office or formal affiliation to the bundle. An identity design attaches a bearer class or name. A canonical transaction design attaches a packet or labour category to a repeated transfer token. The measured graphic architecture can instantiate all of these designs. [Concrete alternatives and their physical predictions](HYPOTHESES.md).

The life-cycle proposal develops a further connection. An object can be issued in a stock of standard tiers, checked at a workshop or gate, surrendered during an exchange, and retained or cancelled afterwards. The same inscription can therefore precede and follow a transfer. Under this hypothesis, the phase of an object at deposition explains why a permission token and a receipt can have identical graphic forms. Identified wear, cancellation and attachment observations would test the phase mechanism. This is a proposed model; the present analysis supplies its structural constraints rather than an observed ancient sequence of actions.

## Exact identification limit and verification

The synthetic certificate enumerates 12 invented visible bundles. Four field interpretations have exactly the same probability for every bundle: bearer entitlement, issuer/service licence, commodity grade and canonical transaction receipt. All six pairwise total variation distances are zero. With equal priors, the best possible classification from those observations is .25 and their mutual information about the interpretation is zero. Further samples from the same visible distribution preserve that equivalence. A separate, explicitly assumed reuse-versus-consumption example shows how an independently observed use event can break it. [Finite distributions](run-v1/identifiability.json).

The independent verifier reconstructs every cross-face matrix by sparse sign-incidence multiplication, using different counting code from the analysis. It checks all 75,984 saved pilot and tail allocations, module margins, slot-role margins, prototype uniqueness, every maximum score and collision statistic, all tail counts, Holm arithmetic and input accounting. All 85 checks pass. A separate adversarial reader checks the declared reference law and interpretation; all 104 allocation-audit checks pass.
