# What an external anchor would have to add

The two research approaches can constrain sign order and the binding between faces while leaving several meanings compatible. A contextual observation becomes useful when those meanings predict different outcomes for it. A more detailed graphic observation can resolve a sign or boundary, but it cannot separate hypotheses that already assign the same probability to every graphic observation.

The [exact calculation](anchors-v1/results.json) constructs four equally weighted interpretations with identical graphic probabilities. Two hypothetical external anchors each divide those interpretations into two groups, and together identify all four when observed without error. The partitions are assumptions of the demonstration. No archaeological objects, bearer labels, quantities or historical probabilities are supplied.

With a known symmetric observation error of 10%, one such anchor supplies 0.531 bits of expected information. Two distinct anchors with independent errors supply 1.062 bits. Two independent observations of the first anchor can improve confidence in its answer, but cannot identify the unresolved second partition. Repeating a copy of the first witness supplies no further information under the specified shared-error model.

The copied-witness example also shows a calibration error. If two identical answers from one witness are wrongly treated as independent observations, the nominal wrong-answer probability falls to 1.22%. The actual wrong-answer probability remains 10%. This is a constructed source-dependence example, not an estimate of corpus annotation errors. The repository's earlier [source-dependence study](../2026-10-02-six-hour/null-calibration/shared-quality/README.md) and [correlated-source study](../2026-10-02-six-hour/null-calibration/noisy-faces/README.md) already examine related measurement problems.

For the proposed record schema, an independently measured delivered quantity, an identified commodity, a bearer connected with several uses, or a documented access operation could supply a useful partition. The broader licence, receipt, identity and commodity models allow overlapping behaviours. Their names do not establish the required partition. A concrete version of each model must state its prediction for the particular observation before that observation can be called a discriminating anchor.

This calculation therefore supplies a planning rule: seek evidence that changes the relative predictions of the competing models, and count repeated witnesses according to their source dependence. It does not numerically rank archaeological acquisition routes. Run `python -B experiments/2026-10-11-two-approaches/contextual_anchors.py --out <new-directory>` to preserve another exact endpoint. [Checks](anchors-v1/verification.json) verify normalization and the stated information identities.
