# Package L: a pilot object ledger of 200 Indus objects

Owned directory: `experiments/2026-10-07-object-ledger/`. Local raw directory: `data/raw/2026-10-07-object-ledger/`.

## Why this package

The October 6–7 census found that no complete index of Indus inscribed evidence exists and measured the gaps (`experiments/2026-10-06-evidence-census/synthesis/REPORT.md`): 166 of 493 inspected CISI 1 identifiers are absent from the local corpus export, at least 68 of 114 online museum records join to corpus records, and the export carries no photograph, dating-sample or rights links. The researcher survey ranks an object, source, date, site and type ledger first (`experiments/2026-10-06-field-recommendations/REPORT.md`), and the chronological and regional sub-corpora that Dennys Frenez recommends depend on it. This package builds and tests that ledger on a 200-object pilot, following the stage 2 plan in `experiments/2026-10-02-dataset-landscape/DATASET_PLAN.md`.

## Inputs

Schema and plan: `experiments/2026-10-02-dataset-landscape/SCHEMA.md` and `DATASET_PLAN.md`, and `experiments/2026-10-06-evidence-census/synthesis/SCHEMA-AMENDMENTS.md`. Census registers and joins: `experiments/2026-10-06-evidence-census/` (site batches in `sites-deep/`, the collection census with its museum joins, the synthesis `coverage.json`, `join-summary.json`, `catalogue-coverage.csv` and `batch-corrections.json`). Local corpus: `data/normalized/lipi_observed_faces.jsonl` (5,680 faces) and `data/normalized/mayig_faces.jsonl`, with the coordinator's `experiments/2026-10-06-research-program/local-site-coverage.json`. Period mapping for Harappa: `experiments/2026-10-06-chronology-region/` (its protocol and period tables). Source frames: `experiments/2026-10-02-six-hour/harappa-object-frame/`, `experiments/2026-10-02-six-hour/lothal-physical-use/`, `experiments/2026-10-02-six-hour/kanmer-physical-use/`, the Susa and Oxus audits under `experiments/2026-10-02-source-recovery/`. The local CISI 1 scan is `research/literature/sources/joshi_parpola_1987_cisi1.pdf` (and an alternate copy beside it). Read `knowledge/provenance/REDISTRIBUTION.md` and `knowledge/provenance/catalogue-caveats.md`.

## Steps

1. **Freeze the design.** In `PROTOCOL.md`, before building rows, write the entity model and the selection rule. Entities: physical object; surface or face; transcription record (one per source, carrying its sign inventory and version); image witness (a pointer with source, page or URL and rights, never a copied image); source edition; identifier or alias (CISI, excavation, field, museum accession and published catalogue numbers, each with the source that asserts it and a status of confirmed or candidate); context (site, area, room or grid, stratum or phase, period label, calendar interval with its dating basis); measurement (dimension, unit, method, source); assertion provenance and confidence; rights record per asset; and a missingness reason for every empty field. Use the census definitions of "face", "local corpus" and "absent key".
2. **Make the schema checkable.** Write it as JSON Schema (or an equivalent machine-checkable definition) with a validator that also checks referential integrity, unit and interval rules, and that no tracked file holds sign sequences from unlicensed sources.
3. **Select 200 objects by the frozen rule.** Stratify across regions, sites, object types, text lengths, damage and multi-face objects, and include: at least 20 of the museum-joined objects, so museum and corpus fields can be compared; Harappa objects with period labels across Periods 3A to 3C; Lothal sealings from the frame; the 22 Harappa short-record tablets; external finds from the Gulf, Mesopotamia, Iran and Central Asia; and at least 15 CISI 1 entries among the absent identifiers, to show how a catalogue-only object is represented.
4. **Populate from local sources only.** Fill each record from the files above, recording the source and page for every value and a reason for every gap. Keep candidate joins separate from confirmed ones. Apply the census review's batch corrections where they affect a selected object.
5. **Measure.** Report field completeness for the pilot against the corpus export, the number of confirmed and candidate joins, the disagreements between sources (by field), and the cost per object in source lookups. Build a review queue of the records an epigrapher or archaeologist should check, with the specific question for each.
6. **Prepare the join to openly licensed transcriptions.** Package M is digitizing the CC BY 1979 Helsinki corpus, which numbers texts by its own scheme. Define how a 1979 text number joins to a ledger object (which concordance is needed, for example the 1980 *Documentation and Duplicates* volume or CISI cross-references), and implement the import interface so its output can be added as a transcription layer.

## Outputs and rights

Keep the full register, including any sign sequences from the Lipi or Mayig derivatives, in the local raw directory. Track in the owned directory the schema, validator, builder, a public-safe layer (identifiers, contexts, measurements, citations and missingness reasons, with no copied third-party prose and no sign sequences from unlicensed sources), the completeness and join reports, and the review queue. Disk space is very low (about 2 GB free): keep outputs small and do not render page images unless a specific value requires it.
