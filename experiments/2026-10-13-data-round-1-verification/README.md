# Data round 1 verification and follow-up

13 October 2026. Two data packages from the morning's data round, the [museum open-access harvest](../2026-10-13-museum-open-access/README.md) and the [CISI 2 basic-data pilot](../2026-10-13-cisi2-basic-data-pilot/README.md), were left unreviewed when their coordinating session stopped at 02:29. This directory holds their independent verification and what followed from it.

## Results

Both packages reproduce. An independent Codex verifier (GPT-6.1-Sol requested; [REPORT.md](REPORT.md), [findings.json](findings.json)) reran every offline step and got byte-identical outputs, recomputed the agreement, coverage and comparison counts from the local readings, and audited every file due to be tracked. It found 19 issues: nine places where tracked files quoted material that must stay local (museum record prose in the join evidence, CISI 2 values in examples and group endpoints), six wrong counts or flags, and four minor points. The coordinator then reran the CISI 2 final builder with its parser step, which regenerated `final.jsonl` byte for byte.

The museum joins hold. A separate reader checked all 26 joins against the printed rows and museum records ([independent check](../2026-10-13-museum-open-access/verification/independent-check.md)): 26 supported, none wrong, a one-sided 95% lower bound of 89% on precision. It found one join the package had missed, Indian Museum 10377/A7252/C.2803 as M-1809.

The corrections are applied and listed in each package: [museum REVIEW-CHANGES.md](../2026-10-13-museum-open-access/REVIEW-CHANGES.md), applied by Codex from [its instructions](CODEX-PROMPT-MUSEUM-FIXES.md) and summarized in [MUSEUM-FIXES.md](MUSEUM-FIXES.md); [CISI 2 REVIEW-CHANGES.md](../2026-10-13-cisi2-basic-data-pilot/REVIEW-CHANGES.md), applied by the coordinator. After them, 542 of the 4,695 local objects (11.5%) have a museum number from some source, against 274 (5.8%) before the round; 519 (11.1%) if a CISI 2 cell with an uncertain digit is not counted ([combined-coverage-post-fix.json](combined-coverage-post-fix.json); the pre-fix figures are in [combined-coverage.json](combined-coverage.json)).

The CISI basic data and the excavators' tables share a numbering. For Mohenjo-daro the FC concordance number that CISI prints for each object is 1000 plus Marshall's 1931 catalogue number or 2000 plus Mackay's 1938 number. Among the objects joined both in the CISI 2 pilot and in the [Mackay and Marshall join](../2026-10-13-mackay-marshall-tables/README.md), 216 of 219 with a legible FC number follow the rule ([fc-crosswalk.json](fc-crosswalk.json), from [fc_crosswalk_check.py](fc_crosswalk_check.py)). The same catalogue numbers are the digits that Lipi appends to its registration strings.

The three exceptions are CISI 2 errors. They belong to two pairs of seals (M-653 with M-655, M-656 with M-707) whose excavation records CISI 2 and Lipi assign the other way round. Measured on the CISI 2 plates, which print seals at 2:1, each of the four seals is within 0.8 mm of the size printed in the excavator's row that Lipi assigns and misses the row CISI 2 assigns by 3.1 to 4.8 mm, against control seals on the same pages that fit within 0.7 mm, one within 2.2 mm ([swap-check.md](swap-check.md), [swap-check.csv](swap-check.csv)). CISI 2's basic data has both pairs crossed in its FC and excavation columns, and the Mackay and Marshall join, which follows Lipi, stands.

## Open items

Ten unjoined museum records have identifier hits in the recall search and need their printed rows read ([recall-candidates.csv](../2026-10-13-museum-open-access/joins/recall-candidates.csv)). Two Lothal families carry another object's excavation number (2067 has L-100's, 2084 has L-166's), so fields Lipi attached through those numbers need checking. The verdict on the crossed pairs rests on size alone; the excavators' natural-size plates of Mackay Nos. 98, 231 and 491 and Marshall No. 57 would confirm it from the designs. The CISI 2 values stay local until the rights in CISI are decided.
