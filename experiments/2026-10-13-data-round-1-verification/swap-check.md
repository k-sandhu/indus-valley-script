# Swap check: M-653, M-655, M-656 and M-707

Lipi's pairing is right for both pairs. Measured on the CISI 2 plates, each seal comes within 0.8 mm of the size printed in the row Lipi gives it and misses the row CISI 2 gives it by 3.1 to 4.8 mm.

CISI 2 prints inscribed sides at 2:1 unless a page heading says otherwise (pp. xxv–xxvi); plates 27, 28 and 43 do not. I took each photograph's extent along the page axes at the outer edge of the seal face, excluding background and shadow, from an Otsu outline on a 600 dpi render checked by eye (about ±0.3 mm). The PDF's declared page size is not the printed size (taken at face value, the controls measure 67% of their printed sizes), so five seals on these pages that both sources pair alike set the scale: 31.7 px per natural mm, implying a 200 x 288 mm page. At that scale M-654, M-657, M-706 and M-708 come within 0.7 mm of their printed sizes, also with leave-one-out scales; M-658 is 2.2 mm small. Impressions differ from their seal photographs by up to 2.3 mm, so the verdicts use the seal photographs.
