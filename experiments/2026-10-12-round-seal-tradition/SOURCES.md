## Catalogues and figures

Used: Table 1 (PDF pp. 5–8; seal numbers, references, BBM numbers), the text of pp. 98–99, 105, 109–112, and Figs. 1, 8, 9 and 10 rendered at 400 dpi with pdftoppm. Figs. 8 and 9 are impression drawings at 1:1 made "after" the publications cited in their captions; Fig. 1 adds photographs of impressions of seals nos. 10, 11 and 56. Laursen's Table 1 cites Gadd's nos. 15, 16 and 17 as plate I; in the scan they are on plate III, as Laursen's Fig. 9 caption states.

## Sign references

Lipi's Wells-coded font (`data/lipi/src/assets/fonts/indus-font.woff2`, SHA-256 `b2758032…1356bf`) maps code n to U+E000 + n. Decoding WOFF2 needs the Brotli extension, which is not installed, so the TTF decoded from the same file by the 2026-10-08 Bonta-extension package (`data/raw/2026-10-08-bonta-extension/source-2026-10-09/lipi-font-local.ttf`, SHA-256 `78516394…128897`) renders the code strings and the 712-code chart used to name alternative codes.

## Statements used from the sources

Laursen p. 98 describes seal no. 11 as "clearly inscribed with a string of pseudo-script", with a "relatively clear 'twin' sign" read first from the right, and seal no. 56 as using two V signs "as fill". Laursen p. 105 describes no. 13 (Failaka, Kjærum 1983 no. 279) as technically of Dilmun Type with what appears to be a "(pseudo-?)inscription". The Fig. 10 caption calls the Kalba seal and the Ra's al-Jinz seal "possible pseudo-'twins'" signs and names the Mohenjo-daro cylinder fragment M-1370. Laursen p. 109 cites the photograph of seal no. 3 as Shah and Parpola 1991: 179, M-1369 A. Laursen's footnote 2 reports Parpola's tentative reading of no. 10, which starts from the right of the impression. Gadd describes no. 2 as bearing "an inscription of five characters", no. 16 as having four characters, and the first sign on the left of no. 15's impression as not found elsewhere.
