# Retrospective: physical numerical anchors and Lothal attachment geometry

Date: 2026-11-23  
Package: `experiments/2026-11-23-physical-anchors-lothal/`

## Approach

We divided Issue #21 into two tightly coupled tracks governed by a single frozen [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md) written before inspecting any Lothal sign-to-attachment cross-tabulation. In Part A we traced `H-904` (`Q-0011`) and `M-1383` (`Q-0012`) directly to high-resolution plate and basic-data renders from our local CISI 2 scan, comparing them against Lipi records, Vats 1940, Mackay 1938, and Vidale's 1987 and 1990 stoneware studies. In Part B we audited `L-206` (`2077`) across CISI 1, Rao 1985, and Frenez and Tosi 2005, coded all 93 Lothal sealings strictly from Table 1 and reverse-trace prose without looking at signs, and then joined the frozen physical table to `lipi_observed_faces.jsonl` for die-grouped permutation and Leave-One-Component-Out cross-validation tests.

## Challenges and fixes

1. **Rao 1985 OCR gaps on `2077` (`L-206`).** Searching the bulk OCR file `rao1985lothal-part2.txt` returned zero hits for `2077` and for the other `G01` excavation numbers because the bulk scan OCR degraded on the sealings chapter. Checking the targeted OCR extracts under `data/raw/2026-10-24-office-seal-evidence/` and rendering the original pages of `rao1985lothal-part2.pdf` directly recovered `2077` on printed pp. 319, 321, and 326 and located its two original photographs on Plate CLXII D (PDF p. 492).
2. **Runtime of unvectorized permutation cross-validation.** Our initial `run_analysis.py` run called `sklearn.metrics` inside every permutation iteration and evaluated 2,000 logistic regression permutation rounds across 36 Leave-One-Component-Out folds, causing multi-minute runtimes. We canceled that run, replaced the Naive Bayes per-fold refit with an exact O(K) sufficient-statistic subtraction checked to agree with the naive per-fold implementation to `1e-10` on every observed dataset, replaced `sklearn.metrics` overhead with direct weighted metric loops, and set logistic regression permutations to `B = 1,000` (while keeping `B = 10,000` for Naive Bayes, association tests, and dispersion tests) as prespecified in [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md).
3. **Header mismatch in independent verification.** The first run of [verify.py](verify.py) failed with `KeyError: 'object_id'` because `independent-first-pass.csv` uses column names `source_cisi` and `context` whereas `reviewed-pass.csv` uses `object_id` and `context_code`. Updating `verify.py` to map the exact headers resolved the error, and all 10 verification checks passed (`10/10`).
4. **Summary table programmatic check while drafting.** While drafting the 93-sealing summary table in [REPORT.md](REPORT.md), our initial hand counts for the Joined-68, Pop B, and Pop A columns differed by one to three items from the exact script outputs. We queried [lothal-test-v1.json](lothal-test-v1.json) and `lipi_observed_faces.jsonl` directly to populate every cell (`Pop B`: 32 wood, 14 pottery, 7 reed, 1 leather, 1 rope; `Pop A`: 19 wood, 2 pottery, 4 reed, 1 leather) before finalizing the report.
5. **Prose review.** Running `python tools/review_prose.py --path experiments/2026-11-23-physical-anchors-lothal --no-write` returned zero flags on [REPORT.md](REPORT.md), [DATA-UPDATE.md](DATA-UPDATE.md), and [RETROSPECTIVE.md](RETROSPECTIVE.md). Twelve flags (10 slash compounds and 2 em dashes) appeared solely in the hash-frozen [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md), which we left unchanged to preserve its pre-run SHA-256 commitment (`4b73b5d4...`).

## Outcomes

- **Part A (`Q-0011` and `Q-0012`):** CISI 2 resolved two long-standing empirical uncertainties: the short strokes on `H-904 A` (`002-840-032-740`) and Fuls's `W032` slip for `W033` on `H-904 B`, and the physical nature of `M-1383` as a unicorn-stamped clay sealing directly on a stoneware-bangle saggar (`MN 82a`, CISI 2 p. 182). Both audits simultaneously sharpened the evidentiary deficits of `F-0006`: `H-904` carries four unmodelled signs with no equal-quantity proof, while `M-1383`'s 82-fragment and 15 to 20 bangle count remains absent from all held primary sources and Vidale 1990's 10 to 18 saggar capacity fails to isolate base 10.
- **Part B (`Q-0019`):** All three primary visual sources confirm `L-206`'s deep smooth curved recess, CISI 1 adds sharp reverse views for `L-124 B` and `L-190 B` and `L-190 F`, and the frozen component-grouped tests establish that inscription features at Lothal do not predict container or fastening class beyond chance, while cleanly quantifying the extreme dispersion contrast between generalist seal `G01` (`p = 0.6963`) and specialist wooden-box seal `G02` (`p = 0.0001`).
