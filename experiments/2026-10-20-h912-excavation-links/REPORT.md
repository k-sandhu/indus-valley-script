# H-912 belongs to a broad 8650 recovery series

Vats's original report places registration **8650c**, the source reference behind H-912, among finds recovered in two squares of Mound F over **4–7 feet below surface**. The collection required three months of knife-and-sieve recovery. It includes tablets, beads, pendants and weights. This establishes a source-defined assemblage; it supplies no tablet attached to a container, documented fish contents or measured allotment. [Source observations](source-observations-v1.json), [complete dispositions](run-v1/dispositions.csv), [summary](run-v1/summary.json).

The source audit also restores fourteen registered objects on the plan of Belcher's fish-study area. Each joins one export family. Their locations and descriptive labels now form a source-linked comparison frame, with the plan's spatial limits preserved.

It supplies no attachment, contents or numerical relation between those jars and a tablet.

Twelve broader registered associations remain separate. They include the two jars, two nearby tablets under 10928a, and nine later finds from the same area. In particular, 10058 is described as having a straw impression. That surface trace does not identify a specific container closure. ## Catalogue identities remain distinct

CISI 1 printed p. 371, PDF p. 407, gives H-302 **F.C. 3864**, excavation **8650c**, accession **ASI 63.11.94**. We checked the page pixels. The export's `8650c864` therefore combines an excavation registration with a suffix consistent with F.C. 3864 minus 3000. It is not evidence for a Vats plate numbered 864. The H-302 A/B photographs are on printed p. 225, PDF p. 261. We did not change its inscription transcription.

H-912 and H-1191 both carry `8650c448` in the export. Their shared source reference is now explicit; physical identity remains unresolved because the relevant CISI 3.1 view was not available in this audit. We performed no merge. The existing uncertain CISI 2 reading `[10/8]650c` also remains preserved, while Vats independently confirms 8650c for the group and row 448.

Another conflict concerns H-289. CISI 1 prints excavation 8650h, while Vats's p. 59 uses that suffix for a gold bead and tiny boss. The sixteen export families with a Harappa `8650` prefix, on 25 face records, are therefore candidates requiring individual source qualification. The prefix cannot make them a uniform physical lot.

## What this changes for the reading

The actual recovery setting supports comparing tablet functions within a mixed collection containing small valuables, weights and varied inscribed objects. It gives no positive content observation for four vessels of fish. A gemstone or craft-license interpretation has been proposed in a 2025 RMRL bulletin, which supplied a useful Vats citation. Its indexed excerpt was a lead; the linked full PDF returned 404. The workshop and gemstone-sign claims remain interpretations requiring their own tests.

We did not open a real confirmation half, fit a classifier, change a sign or merge identities. The [synthetic shots output](shots-synthetic-v1.json) documents only the prospective workflow. The main study's already-frozen tablet comparison remains separate from this source audit.

The main study records its allocation-category proposal in [C-0012](../../knowledge/conjectures/C-0012-fish-category-four-unit-records.md). That is a working interpretation built after the outcomes were seen. This audit supplies its source constraints and comparison frame.

The strongest supported physical interpretation is that H-912's registration belongs to a repeated-tablet series recovered with a broad assemblage of small objects. An allocation indexed by a category is a candidate to test within that series. Literal fish contents require a new registered association: a tablet physically attached to a container or documented allotment, with its contents and quantity determined independently of the inscription.
