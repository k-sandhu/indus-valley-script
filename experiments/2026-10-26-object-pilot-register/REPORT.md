# A source-linked register for twenty targets

We consolidated 539 metadata assertions into twenty records and a local browsable catalogue. The records cover ten Dholavira accession targets, five Harappa targets and five Lothal targets. The [aggregate](aggregate-v4.json), [coverage](coverage-v4.json) and [interface](INTERFACE.md) identify the exact outputs. This contribution combines held evidence and adds no acquisition, empirical field observation or physical merge.

The Dholavira records preserve all 62 candidate corpus families and 274 candidate edges. Their ten accession-labelled photograph panels and seven measurement rows remain linked to Bisht's report. Dimensions retain their published strings, units and uncertainty. No candidate inherits another family's measurements or identity. The source-target photograph therefore remains useful even while its corpus bridge is unresolved.

The Harappa records preserve the registration and lot evidence from the three earlier context passes. H-1657 retains the Room 591 floor caption and full field key. H-1688 retains the earlier lower-street drawing and field registration. H-2587 retains the 7158/7458 disagreement and the earlier figure supporting 7458 within the same excavation lineage. Its older illustration and H-1688's drawing are linked as whole source pages. H-1657 has a source-qualified web photograph pointer without local image bytes. The other two targets retain exact narrative page links and their missing field records.

The Lothal records retain the corrected relation ledger, group-level qualifications and held view inventory. L-164 preserves the incompatible CLXI F references: Rao's p. 319 associates the plate with 1864, while p. 325 associates it with 1830. The page image stays visible with its unresolved binding. Published support reconstructions retain their tentative status and their dependence on the same objects and source lineage.

Across denominator twenty, fifteen targets have a material assertion, sixteen have some dimensions, fourteen have a findspot assertion and fifteen have a stratum or phase assertion. Four have a museum-accession assertion, two have an explicit lot field and one has a calendar-interval assertion. No target gains a mass or a dating-sample link. Eighteen have image or image-page pointers: seventeen include local files and one has a retained web pointer. These counts include qualified source assertions with unresolved fields; no complete all-face inventory or accepted physical identity is inferred from them. Every target carries missing fields, and the local queue has 138 rows.

Sixteen author checks pass. A second implementation by the same author passes seventeen structural, hash, link and HTML checks, including all 1,239 generated local file links. The [validation](author-crosscheck-v4.json) states its scope: it inspects structure and bytes without reviewing browser layout or source pixels. This is author verification; the separate contributor review belongs to the integration package. The first preserved endpoint contains a failed sequence-field guard because an unanchored expression matched `context_code`; the guard was corrected without changing that metadata. Versions 2 and 3 preserve the later additions. Whole inscription strings and proposed meanings are excluded. Bench scoring does not apply to this metadata projection.

Amendment 1 closes two catalogue-only omissions in version 4. H-2587 gains the published maximum diameter and steatite material, with both contrary field keys explicit. L-174 gains its basic-data cells and the earlier payload-free pilot assertions, including the one-versus-two impression disagreement. Its unknown museum cell and corrigendum notice remain visible. The [version delta](delta-v3-to-v4.json) records 27 additional held assertions: identity-key presence rises from 18 to 20 targets, material from 13 to 15, and some dimensions from 15 to 16. The queue falls from 143 to 138 rows. Neither target receives a Lipi family or physical merge. No primary pixels were inspected for this addition; material and catalogue readings remain for the separate reviewer.

The strongest supported conclusion is that these twenty target dossiers can be inspected together without resolving their disputed identities by implication. The decisive evidence is a source-qualified accession-to-CISI concordance or matching carrier photograph for Dholavira, the Harappa field item and stratigraphy records, and a corrected photograph register linking Lothal CLXI F to its excavation number.
