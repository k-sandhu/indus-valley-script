A separate distinction preserves positive ranges without manufacturing scalar measurements.

The first interface draft used present and uncertain as value statuses. Parent review identified the repository's canonical vocabulary, and the successful builds use catalogued, inferred, unknown and conflicting, with uncertainty recorded separately. Zero dimensions keep their raw strings while their normalized missing-value entries are unknown.
