# Readable seal controls: source-extension protocol

Frozen 24 October 2026 before candidate adjudication. We extend the source ledger for Q-0037 without computing a statistic or opening an Indus confirmation cohort. The prior corpus has 11 eligible Akkadian sign-level legends and 23 Tamil-Brahmi legends with coins included (16 seals and rings). The original threshold and kept-statistic rules remain unchanged.

We search primary excavation and institutional publications for Tell Mozan/Urkesh, Tell Brak and Karur, then revised Early Tamil Epigraphy and later rings. Bounds are six targeted web searches and at most eight acquired institutional pages or editions, ending after one coherent candidate block has been adjudicated. Search results identify sources but never establish a reading. Acquisition failures and unavailable pages are outcomes.

Every candidate needs a physical carrier identifier, findsite, edition text, language basis, sign readings, orthographic words, compound members and line-division basis. Source-level dates and dates of objects remain distinct. We accept only complete or editorially restored legends with at least two signs, certain language and traceable physical identity. A die reconstructed from several impressions is linked to its physical witnesses and counted once. Existing aliases and exact sign sequences join the old corpus; an additional rolling of an old die adds no legend. Generic pottery, coins newly substituted for seal gaps, uncertain Tamil identifications, Hurrian names alone and Sumerian logograms alone supply no Akkadian language evidence. Old Assyrian or Old Babylonian finds are a proposed separate period extension and cannot silently enter akk-sargonic.

The versioned local-only ledger records both admissions and exclusions, including unavailable fields as null. Its aggregate validator checks record identifiers, source and object links, duplicate joins and missing admission fields. Detailed data and source HTML/PDF/text stay physically on D:. We retain source URL, retrieval timestamp, bytes, SHA-256, rights status and logical and physical paths. Our package contains acquisition and checking code, prose and aggregate results. Author checks are submitted evidence; no independent verification is claimed.
