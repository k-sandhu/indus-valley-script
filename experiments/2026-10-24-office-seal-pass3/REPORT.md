# Rao's two plate references identify different excavation numbers

We recovered a conflict within the original Lothal report. Rao 1985 printed p. 319 / PDF 70 attaches Plate CLXI F to excavation object 1864. The plate entry on printed p. 325 / PDF 76 attaches the same panel to 1830. CISI1 printed p. 373 / PDF 409 distinguishes these objects as L-164 and L-161. This prevents us from treating the plate as a securely registered photograph of L-164 through either number alone.

The exact passages, page images and coordinates are preserved locally in the observations and artifact records named in [source-manifest-v1.json](source-manifest-v1.json). The earlier pass2 report followed p. 319's reference without recording p. 325's conflicting entry. We preserve that report and document the correction here. We have not established which number belongs to the photographed object.

The bounded reinspection covered Rao printed pp. 317–325 and the CISI identity page. Figure 38B panel 6 remains an unregistered candidate for L-164: neither the nearby text nor the selected catalogue row supplies an explicit drawing-number key. The two drawings of elephant sealings cannot establish specimen identity from resemblance. The plate entry for 1830 also records phase II–III, SRG 3, GX 10, warehouse layer 2 and a depth of two feet. We retain those fields under that source assertion, pending resolution of the panel identity.
