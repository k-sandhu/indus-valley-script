# Dholavira catalogue identities

We checked 28 Dholavira candidate rows against the held primary report and recovered 28 consistent cross-references between its context table and individual seal catalogue. Every registration in the frozen block appears under the expected serial number in both tables. The 28 registrations are distinct within the target block. The source check produces 28 register-ready catalogue identity assertions and finds no registration conflict in this subset. It adds no supported link to a provisional corpus family.

We froze PCF-DHV-005 through PCF-DHV-032 in [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md). We then inspected the serial and accession columns on printed pages 247–249, PDF pages 264–266, of *Dholavira Report (preliminary, Jan 2015)*, held as `bisht-2015-held`. We checked the corresponding individual catalogue entries on 22 pages, listed in [results-v1.json](results-v1.json). The source hash matches the inherited acquisition record. [source-manifest-v3.json](source-manifest-v3.json) records its original URL, retrieval time, bytes, physical storage and derivative hashes.

The detailed assertions are local at `data/raw/2026-10-24-object-identity-extension/identity-assertions-v1.json`, physically on D:. Each assertion carries the inventory row, accession, both table serials, exact PDF pages and unchanged candidate-family relationship. The observations support an internal identity within this publication. They do not supply an accession-to-corpus crosswalk. Consequently all 28 corpus relationships remain candidates, and the overall inherited queue remains 306 candidate-only rows, 28 unlinked rows and 24 previously supported links. These overlapping source checks do not increase the physical-object census.

Our extraction selected only the identity strips. During page-location work a command displayed the first 150 text characters on PDF pages 268–300, which included some motif descriptions and inscription counts. An initially wide strip on PDF page 277 also displayed partial remarks. The local access log records these exceptions, participant and page coverage. No sign sequence or inscription image was displayed, and this work certifies no confirmation eligibility. The catalogue scan mechanically processed identity strips on PDF pages 277–318, retaining strips only where a target serial occurred; the model saw the resulting target accession pairs.

The author check used word coordinates and row intervals, separately from the text-stream extraction, to verify all 56 table observations. [validation-v1.json](validation-v1.json) records the aggregate checks. A first coordinate check excluded words crossing the crop boundary, then a second used too high a top margin for a context page. Correcting those extraction assumptions produced 56 passing comparisons. These are author checks, not independent review.

The strongest supported reading is that this 28-row Dholavira block has coherent published accession identities across two independently arranged tables. The deciding next evidence is an accession concordance from the excavation or museum record to the corpus catalogue; that would settle the surviving family candidates.
