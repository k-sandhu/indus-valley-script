# Amendment 2, 13 October 2026: the ordinal side rule is withdrawn

Written after the verification samples were read and before the index was reported, as the protocol requires when the targeted sample finds a wrong side assignment.

**What failed.** The protocol placed face `n` of an object with several faces on the `n`-th side letter found in that object's file names. Of 20 links drawn to test this rule (sample S2), 15 held, 3 were wrong and 2 could not be decided; across all three samples 6 links under the rule were wrong. The failures have two causes that the file names cannot reveal. On some Harappa bas-relief tablets the export lists the short text first although the catalogue's side A carries the long one: H-779, H-821 and H-823 each show about five signs on A and two on B, while their first face has two signs. On seals and sealings with more faces than photographed sides, several faces share one side: M-326 has seven faces on six sides and its side B carries two texts, and L-190's faces are the impressions numbered 1 to 3 on side A.

**Revised rule.** A face of an object with one face row is still presumed to be on side A; all 52 such links in the samples held. For objects with several face rows the index no longer assigns a side by rule. Their links carry the relation `side-unassigned`, which records that the file shows the object and leaves open which face it shows. The ordinal guess is kept in the column `ordinal_hint` for readers who want a starting point; it was right in 15 of 18 decided S2 links. Where the samples settled the assignment by viewing both texts against the faces' sign counts, `verified-sides.tsv` records the side for 43 faces of 21 objects, and those links carry the rule `verified-by-viewing`.

**Effect on the outputs.** The index of files, keys, kinds, volumes and pages is unchanged. The face-side counts now cover single-face objects and the 21 verified objects; for the other objects with several faces the coverage table reports whether their files show at least as many distinct sides or numbered inscriptions as the object has faces. The run built under the withdrawn rule is kept as `run-2026-10-13/images-v1-ordinal-rule/`, because the verification samples were drawn from it.
