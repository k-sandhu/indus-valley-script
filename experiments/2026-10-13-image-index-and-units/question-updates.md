# Proposed question-card updates

## Q-1004 Build a face-level image index from the 8,722 local CISI crops

Status: `done` for files, pages and single-face sides; propose a follow-on card for the sides of faces on objects with several faces.

Outcome note, 13 October 2026: Every one of the 8,722 crops has a record with hash, parsed name, kind and CISI volume; 3,664 catalogue keys are placed on their plate pages (CISI 1 PDF = printed + 36, CISI 2 = printed + 35), giving a page for 97% of the 8,444 face–image links. The printed caption inside each crop was checked against its name by OCR and viewing: 23 names point at the wrong object and are corrected, and 231 of the 299 volume/page files were assigned by reading their caption. Objects with any image rise from 53.4% to 54.6%, and objects with a photograph of the inscribed side from 17.6% (Lipi index files only) to 46.0%; seals from 10.5% to 68.3%. A random sample of 60 links had 60 correct identities and kinds (Wilson 95% interval 94.0–100%). The ordinal rule for placing the faces of multi-face objects on sides was right in 34 of 40 decided links and was withdrawn; 881 objects with several faces have their files linked without a side, and 43 faces of 21 objects are verified. Evidence: `experiments/2026-10-13-image-index-and-units/README.md`, `run-2026-10-13/images/`, `verification-results.json`, `AMENDMENT-2.md`.

Follow-on card (proposed): "Assign the faces of the 881 multi-face objects to their sides by viewing both sides of each object", data access local-only, compute hours, difficulty standard, with `verified-sides.tsv` as the output format and the sign count of each face as the check.

## Q-1020 Record the unit, precision and source of every dimension value

Status: `done`, with the sample check against excavation tables deferred to Q-1003.

Outcome note, 13 October 2026: Each of the 17,040 face-axis size values has a source, unit of record, precision, conversion fit, website relation and flags in `run-2026-10-13/dimensions/dimension-table.csv`. The Lipi fields `h`, `v`, `th` hold the value as recorded: 5,643 values in inches (2,295 to 0.1 in, 3,298 to 0.01 in), 389 in millimetres and 102 in centimetres, mostly without a unit in the string, plus 4,250 millimetre-only values (2,501 whole, 1,749 to 0.1 mm) and 6,656 empty or zero. The inventory's 228 inch–millimetre mismatches are 180 millimetre and 42 centimetre records and 6 true mismatches; 15 values disagree with their recorded value in all. The 206 cross-export disagreements are 199 Lipi thickness ranges, 3 zero-against-value cases and 4 different values; axis by axis, 9 objects disagree. Two-decimal inch values are not fractions of an inch and millimetre-only values are not lost inch conversions (grid tests at chance). In Shot A's size population, 135 of 590 discovery seals and 146 of 582 confirmation seals have both face sizes rounded to 0.1 in, and 129 and 122 are millimetre records without a stated unit. CISI 1 and 2 print no sizes, so the 30-value check against an independent source waits for the Mackay and Marshall tables (Q-1003). Evidence: `experiments/2026-10-13-image-index-and-units/README.md`, `run-2026-10-13/dimensions/summary.json`, `split-counts.json`.
