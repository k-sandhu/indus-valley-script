## How to work

Work through the towns in the order the rules give. For each town, render the composite at a few scales and in the brightness, RGB, NIR and edge-enhanced layers, and look at the renders with the Read tool, which displays PNG images. Find the mound and its excavated areas, and use the plans to decide which visible features are Harappan architecture; measure in the imagery only features you can tie to a plan or a description. Read endpoint pixel coordinates from renders with a labelled grid, zooming in on each end, refine the lines with `measure_lines.py`, and check each fitted line on a fresh render drawn over the image. Then measure the plans: render the plan page, measure the north arrow from tail to tip and the scale bar, measure the lines, and record what the arrow means with the exact words of its evidence and the survey year. Register a plan to the imagery when both show the same features. Group lines into systems with `systems.py`, which picks the main system per source.

Keep every picks file and render, so that anyone can redraw your lines, and keep `measure-B/LOG.md` as you go: what you looked at, what you could and could not identify, and every judgement call. Write it in plain connected prose. Maximize reader comprehension; explain the idea directly in connected prose; keep necessary shared assumptions together; avoid repeated corrective comparisons or warnings that interrupt the argument.
