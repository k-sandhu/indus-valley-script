# How this package became a verification

On 21 October 2026 at about 01:45 UTC this session was briefed to run study 4 of the [direction note of 20 October](../../../knowledge/directions/parpola-forward-2026-10-20.md) in a package of its own. On checking the repository at 01:51 UTC we found the same study already under way in [experiments/2026-10-21-town-axes/](../../2026-10-21-town-axes/), begun a few minutes earlier by another session: it had created Q-1032 for the settlement table, frozen and hashed its protocol, and launched four blind measurers and a dating agent. Its design matched our brief closely, with the same discovery towns and the same kill rule. We wrote nothing to the repository and asked the session that briefed us how to proceed. It answered that a second frozen test of the same claim on the same towns would add cost but not evidence, and that an independent measurement made before the first study's results were read was the project's standard for verification. The package was renamed accordingly and owns no conjecture or question card.

Before stopping we had read Wanzke's paper in full. Its tables are in gon, 400 to the circle, with clockwise deviation positive (footnote 2, printed p. 38). The main street system of Mohenjo-daro, which he calls system 1, lies about 8 gon (7.2°) clockwise of the cardinal directions, and a second system in the Moneer and DK-C areas is cardinal; his text on printed p. 37 instead gives 1° to 2° clockwise, and his match with Aldebaran rests on that figure. His p. 34 table, taken from Jansen (1979) with an unknown north reference, gives Harappa citadel 0 and streets −11 to +6 gon, Surkotada town wall −16, Lothal "dock" wall +5.5 and Street 1 −4.4, Sutkagen-dor −2, Balakot +3.3 and −1, and Kalibangan surrounding wall 0, exterior wall −2 and main street −13 gon. The other package's protocol records the same reading. On this scan printed page = PDF page − 3, counting PDF pages from 1. These values are given to no measurer of this package.
