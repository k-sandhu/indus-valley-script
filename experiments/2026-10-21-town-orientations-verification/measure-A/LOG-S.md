The read-only Git status check was repeated with a command-local safe.directory option. Other contributors' changes remain outside this run's inventory. Our created-path inventory and audit resolve all output paths within the two owned roots. No Git mutation, external publication, commercial imagery, new imagery acquisition or OSM geometry was used. No other measurer's contents, earlier town-axes contents or prohibited package material was consulted. Files were retained under new names, including rejected and revised picks; the ongoing log and tool-written per-town summary were appended. More than 11GB remained free at the final verification, well above the1GB reserve.

The present records make that test concrete through retained endpoints, source locators and errors.
