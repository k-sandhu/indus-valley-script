The reservoir and acropolis arrangement matches Fig. 3.1 on printed p. 46 of the unattributed HARAPPAN CIVILIZATION excerpt. The plan credits ASI; its author and publication year remain unresolved. The basin's water-to-rim interfaces are visible for the two long brick-lined edges. Vegetation and mixed 10 m pixels limit the identification of the precise brick face. We do not infer a true-north status from the upright page arrow.
