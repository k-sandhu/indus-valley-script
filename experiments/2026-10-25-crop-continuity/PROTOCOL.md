3. Recalculate the reported crop proportions from counts. Compare the numerator and denominator used for cereals, legumes, winter crops, summer crops and millet. Show differences caused by including fruits, weeds or unidentified remains. Counts of recovered remains measure an assemblage; they do not directly measure acreage, food energy or production.

## Decision rules and stopping conditions

An arithmetic replication passes when source counts reproduce the claimed percentage under an explicit denominator and rounding rule. A discrepancy remains visible with the alternative calculation. Unsupported identity or chronology joins remain unresolved.

Presence of familiar crops in post-1900 BCE dated deposits supports survival of elements of the crop repertoire. A claim about an unchanged farming system requires comparable samples before and after the transition, context association, recovery controls and evidence about management operations. Co-occurrence of summer and winter crops by itself cannot identify same-field annual rotation. A local crop sequence alone cannot identify population ancestry or the causal effect of drought.

We will not run a regional significance test unless independent site-phase tables provide comparable counts, recovery metadata and dates. If those conditions fail, the completed endpoint is the Jognakhera audit, a comparison-data register and a specific acquisition/design plan. Missing evidence is a result of the feasibility audit, not an inferred zero count. The first run stops after the focal supplement and a bounded set of up to six candidate comparison publications have been assessed. New acquisitions or repairs receive separate output versions.
