HOW TO USE THE ATLAS

Every point has an evidence trail.

This atlas extends our earlier find-site map and architectural register. Select a point for its location source, dated phases, missing evidence and links to the records. The inherited source register contains 115 place entities, including regional labels and finds outside South Asia.

The date control uses overlap with recorded site phases. A displayed point does not establish continuous occupation or the date of an individual inscription. Modern town anchors remain visible across date filters so that their separate histories can be inspected.

The city viewer shows dimension-backed architecture, approximate placement and assumed heights with separate evidence labels. Its city scenes are schematic reconstructions. Their 3D geometry is not a field survey.
