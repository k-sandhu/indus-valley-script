# INDUS-CITY-3D-2026-10-26: inspectable architectural models

Recorded 26 October 2026 before generating geometry or evaluating the interface. We use the repository's orient, start-study, add-data and write-retrospective workflows. Authors: GPT-6 family through the Codex desktop collaboration harness; the exact serving variant is unavailable.

We will build an offline 3D explorer of Mohenjo-daro and Dholavira city contexts, with separate detailed models of the Great Bath, Dholavira's eastern reservoir and a castle gateway, and Lothal's large basin. The target is architectural presentation. Published dimensions and descriptions define individual observations. The models add explicit display assumptions wherever a surveyed plan, height or phase is missing.

## Inputs and prior work

We start from the town-axis visual atlas, the Q-1032 town-axis register and the shared-infrastructure plan study of 24 October 2026. They retain published plans, orientation conflicts, dimensions and phase limitations. Their source images remain local. The new work adds procedural metric geometry, interactive inspection and export; it does not rerun their archaeological comparisons. We will verify factual building dimensions against excavator publications and official archaeological or UNESCO records. New source witnesses go under physical `D:/projects/indus_valley_script/data/raw/2026-10-26-indus-living-atlas/reconstruction/`, reachable at logical `data/raw/2026-10-26-indus-living-atlas/reconstruction/`. We have checked the D: mount and data/raw junction before acquisition.

The relevant gap in `knowledge/DATA.md` is architectural context: plans do not yet supply a complete, phase-keyed, georeferenced city mesh or joined object findspots. Published site outlines also mix structural episodes. We will not fabricate that missing survey. Every feature will record a source, placement status, dimensional status and height assumption. City contexts are spatial diagrams, with orientation and approximate positions stated. No invented house count will be counted as archaeological coverage.

## Method fixed before outcome

We will generate original meshes from a compact parameter file, using metres and a right-handed export coordinate system. The interface will work without external libraries, map tiles or network requests. It will provide model selection, orbit, zoom, a plan view, reset, feature picking, source notes and export. Water levels, roofs and heights will be named display assumptions. City diagrams will emphasize named districts and known monuments instead of filling unexcavated land with houses.

The source record will distinguish published approximate dimensions, plan-based or schematic placement, and illustrative heights. A top-down view will expose the plan. An adjustable height factor will demonstrate the effect of uncertain superstructures; published basin depth will remain fixed. We will provide at least one useful local OBJ export for each model and a machine-readable scene specification with sources and units.

Success requires all promised models, nonempty finite geometry, source references for every selectable feature, parameter checks for the published dimensions, no external asset dependency, and recorded browser inspection of model switching, orbit, plan, zoom and selection. We will inspect mobile layout and keyboard controls. Failures and repaired endpoints will be recorded separately. We stop when these requirements pass; a visual model is not a fresh archaeological evaluation.

## Evaluation scope

We read `bench/README.md`. Its five archaeological task scorers concern script keys, meanings, sign restoration, segmentation and frozen conjecture shots. This work submits none of those. No matching bench task evaluates an architectural display, and no confirmation material will be read. Deterministic mesh and interaction checks will be recorded as software verification, with no statistical or archaeological confirmation claim.

## Rights and deliverables

Third-party plans, photographs, full text, OCR and precise traced geometry with unresolved rights stay local. Tracked outputs contain our prose, code and original schematic geometry made from cited dimensions. Source titles, locators, URLs, paths, hashes and rights observations will be retained. We will add a report, data update, retrospective, validation output and one owned registry file. Shared indexes, the manifest and the old studies remain unchanged.

The strongest possible reading is that several Indus water structures can be compared in metric space while the missing geometry stays visible. A phase-keyed survey with registered plans, sections and elevations would decide which schematic components can be replaced by observed architecture.
