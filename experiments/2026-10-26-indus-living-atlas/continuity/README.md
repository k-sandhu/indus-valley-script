# Continuity after the urban Indus

The [report](REPORT.md) reviews the theory that people and practices continued while the material features used to identify Indus urban society changed. Twelve primary sources support sixteen evidence rows, with regional differences, occupation breaks and preservation mechanisms retained. The [test design](TEST-DESIGN.md) defines a future comparison of domestic activity, urban markers and recovery.

The strongest regional qualification is Dholavira Stage VI: the preliminary excavation report describes inscribed seals and cubical weights in a reduced settlement assigned about 1950–1800 BCE. That phase spans 1900 BCE, and exact last-use dates require the original contexts and absolute dates. The Alamgirpur report preserves a disagreement between apparent stratigraphic continuity and unresolved calendar dates.
