# Measuring continuation, marker change and visibility

26 October 2026. Prospective design, not run. No confirmation population has been acquired or opened for this design.

We will ask whether the loss of recognizable urban Indus objects occurs in deposits that retain ordinary household and production activity. A second comparison will measure whether later cover and recovery methods affect which parts of that sequence can be observed. Dholavira, Harappa, Chanhu-daro, Jognakhera, Bahola, Alamgirpur, Burj and the Masudpur sites discussed in this report are discovery material. They cannot become fresh confirmation sites by giving them a new random seed.

## What is observed

For a marker to enter the record we observe, people must use or make it, discard or deposit it, the deposit must survive, and our method must recover and classify it. We write the probability chain as:

`P(recorded marker) = P(occupation) × P(marker use | occupation) × P(deposition | use) × P(survival | deposition) × P(recovery | survival) × P(correct recognition | recovery)`.

This is a conditional accounting identity, with every condition also carrying the regional and chronological context. It assumes no numerical rates and no independence between the terms. A zero count constrains their product. Additional observations are needed to decide whether people left, the marker stopped being used, deposits were removed, or our method missed them. Treating an unknown term as zero would choose a history before testing it.

## Acquisition and sampling frame

The first archive task is a deposit-level inventory for four regional strata: Punjab and Haryana, Sindh, Gujarat, and the upper Ganges connection. Each deposit needs coordinates or a restricted local locator, trench and layer identifiers, parent excavation, phase, laboratory sample identities, calibrated date distributions, material, residuality assessment, exposed or sampled volume, recovery procedure, depth reached and preservation observations. Record all eligible deposits, including empty, damaged, undated and inaccessible cases. Precise unprotected site locations remain local.

We will define household activity from floors, hearths, rebuilding, food remains and production waste. Individual indicators can have other origins, so interpretation requires associations and context integrity. Diagnostic urban markers will be coded independently as inscribed seals, other inscribed objects, weights with measurable form and mass, and specified architectural features. Pottery fabrics, forms and manufacturing operations receive their own fields. An excavator's period label cannot be the only evidence both for a date and for the disappearance being tested.

Sites will enter the comparison only after their report coverage and recovery methods are documented. A confirmed occupation break remains an eligible outcome. Excavations that stopped above the target horizon remain unresolved. A date on a residual object or long-lived wood cannot alone date the deposition event. AMS dates will be recalibrated together only when laboratory determinations, materials and uncertainties can be obtained; source calendar ranges remain unchanged in this review.

## Independent comparison and confirmation

Entire sites, publication families and known artifact aliases will remain together. Multiple articles using the same excavation samples count as one field lineage. Soil, plant and lipid measurements may provide different observations at one site while sharing its chronological errors. Discovery work will assess chronological resolution and preservation before selecting the number of independent sites required for a stated effect and error rate. This design sets no unsupported numerical sample target.

Before a new confirmation set opens, we will freeze its eligible-site list, codebook, missing-data rules, date model, comparison and pass rule. If the study submits a historical conjecture, it will use the bench shots workflow, check `bench/spent.json`, register every participant, and record the frozen specification and source hashes. Confirmation will open once. No sign-level reading is part of this design.

The primary comparison will estimate marker occurrence per comparable recovered deposit or volume among deposits with independently established domestic activity, allowing explicit date uncertainty. A separate occupancy measure will summarize the extent and continuity of domestic evidence. We will specify an equivalence margin if claiming stable practice; failure to reject a difference will not supply that margin. Any migration claim will require linked old and new settlement catchments with compatible dates and separate mobility evidence.

## Calibration and distinguishing results

Known-positive deposits within the same landform, depth and recovery setting will assess whether the method detects the target evidence. Archive and field comparisons will retain survey footprints and effort; repeated observations of the same parcel will be joined. Surface, coring, geophysics and excavation have different detection limits. A method that cannot reach the relevant depth cannot support an absence conclusion there.

| History | Result favoring it | Result against it |
|---|---|---|
| Local occupation persists while urban markers decline | Domestic deposits continue across the dated transition while diagnostic marker occurrence falls under comparable recovery | A resolved occupation break in domestic evidence and markers alike |
| People relocate within a region | Dated local abandonment coincides with independently dated occupation elsewhere in the searched catchment | New settlement predates the proposed movement, or chronology cannot resolve the relationship |
| Buried activity is missed on the surface | Target-age deposits occur beneath later cover in independently sampled blocks | Adequate depth-resolved samples repeatedly establish erosion or a target-age nonoccupation surface |
| Ordinary practices continue through a gap in urban administration | The same diagnostic operational sequence is locally reproduced in dated intermediate production contexts | Later objects use different operations or are imports, heirlooms or residual finds |
| Writing shifts to a perishable medium | Securely dated writing implements, ink residues, impressions or surviving text reveal the proposed medium | Appropriate preservation and recovery repeatedly recover comparable perishable records but no proposed tradition; with poor preservation the result stays unresolved |

The disappearance of a narrow marker class cannot settle whether language changed. That requires its own evidence and independent comparison.

## Decisions that could kill the proposal

For a specified local catchment and horizon, the practical-continuity explanation fails if the relevant ordinary occupations show an independently dated break and adequate survey also excludes the proposed local replacement settlements. If regional occupation persists but a diagnostic craft operation disappears from adequately recovered production contexts, we will reject continuity of that operation. A broad claim of institutional continuity fails where its necessary administrative relationships disappear and the later system uses different ones.

The test stops without a historical verdict when dating, preservation or recovery cannot resolve these alternatives. Its output must show the adequately observed denominator alongside every negative result. We will revisit a failed inference when new direct dates, production contexts, raw recovery records or calibrated survey blocks change the observation problem.

The strongest prospective claim is a measurable difference between persistence of ordinary work and persistence of urban administration. Dated deposits that preserve both sides of the comparison, under a method shown to recover them, would decide it.
