# A deciding study of rebuilding and documentary practice

The source review supports a study of common routes and local changes. To decide how those arrangements worked, we need primary contexts joined to independent construction histories. This design extends the earlier corporate and collective-action accounts by making their architectural and documentary predictions compete on the same newly coded observations. It is an acquisition and pilot design. It has not opened confirmation data and is not represented as a completed preregistration.

## Competing explanations

| Explanation | Architectural prediction | Documentary prediction | Discriminating evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common rules maintained through local negotiation | Locally different components and asynchronous interventions meet usable shared routes | Authorisation and repeated formulae connect otherwise distinct production or residential groups | Repairs joined across a boundary, independent timing, material-defined groups and access maintained for more than one group |
| Settlement-wide supervision | Repair and access changes follow the authority's district or settlement programme; local flexibility is permitted where its model specifies it | Documentary operations follow the same administrative hierarchy | Independently identified supervision, synchronized interventions, repeated restricted facilities or a consistent redistribution hierarchy |
| Engineering and resource convergence | Similar gradients, rainfall, terrain and materials produce similar arrangements | No additional text-context relationship after those constraints and recovery are controlled | Hydraulic performance and local resource models explain the architectural and documentary pattern |
| Household property and production | Changes follow household needs and workshop space | Stamps, tags and tablets concentrate at production, storage or household disposal | Primary manufacturing or storage evidence accounts for the proposed handover relation |
| Recovery and redeposition | Excavation and preservation assemble apparently related structures | Secondary dumps create apparent institutional clusters | Relationships disappear within securely primary, phase-matched contexts |

Flexible supervision can resemble negotiation. We will specify the supervision model's allowed timing, component diversity and access arrangements before testing it. If available evidence cannot distinguish those predictions, we will retain the architectural conclusion and leave the political mechanism undecided.

## First field and archive targets

Banawali's IC clearance and later gate-drain rebuilding, Kanmer's IIa open space and IIb occupation, and Farmana's named lane additions offer complementary construction sequences. Harappa's boundary expansion, courtyard production and wall-side dump provide a documentary comparison with different relations to use. Dholavira's north gate and Lower Town offer a display and access comparison. Lothal's passage deposit offers fastening evidence but first requires reconciliation of its objects and layers. Selection is based on those source joins, rather than on which town gives the preferred pattern.

The first archive pass obtains phase-keyed plan sheets, sections, context registers, original sample descriptions, restoration records and retained-object lists. Survey then places the same named wall faces, street surfaces, drains and entrances in a common coordinate system. A wall's true bearing retains its north reference, error and original endpoints. Plans copied from another survey remain dependent sources. Earlier Q-1032 measurements and modern horizons stay linked to their original records.

Construction dates need material actually related to construction or resurfacing. Prefer short-lived specimens securely sealed by the relevant event, with laboratory identifiers, pretreatment, sample material, stratigraphic relation and potential residuality recorded. Model alternate sequences and outliers, retain all dates and report agreement and convergence diagnostics. A phase inferred from a connected drain cannot supply the target date for a test of whether connected drains were repaired together. We follow the sample-selection logic in [Bronk Ramsey 2009](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/bookmarks/uuid%3Abbcb620d-af5c-479c-97e3-9d0c6bdb6e48) and [Historic England's Bayesian process](https://historicengland.org.uk/images-books/publications/radiocarbon-dating-chronological-modelling/the-bayesian-process/). No old BC arithmetic in the present review is recalibrated or silently replaced.

The material programme records component volumes, brick dimensions and bonds, stone species, mortar and plaster, recycled bricks and evidence for construction episodes. Petrography or geochemistry can test material-defined production groups where the source geology and preservation justify it. A common recipe, quarry or mould supports a production relationship; it does not identify an institution by itself. Occupation floors, residues, tools, unfired blanks, firing equipment and waste are recorded separately from debris later dumped in a room.

## Independent coding and joins

Architectural coders receive plans, sections and construction observations without inscription sequences or artifact-count overlays. They code the extent and confidence of access control, use of shared infrastructure, repair, change and preservation. Handover settings must be identified from access, storage or wear evidence while coders remain blind to whether sealings were found there. An enclosure gateway alone is not an independently observed transfer of custody.

Object coders receive individual witnesses and source registrations without the architectural outcome category. They record material, manufacture, dimensions, complete faces, impressions of a common die, fastening traces, wear, residues and disturbance. Text coding uses a fixed graphic inventory, retaining damaged and disputed cases. No proposed gloss supplies a context label.

An identity reviewer then builds object-to-context links from excavation and accession records. Same-object aliases, impressions of one stamp, mould or cutting batches and copied text components receive distinct relation types. Those groups remain together during data division. The final dataset records the quantity of excavated or processed material, screening and retention policy, phase, preservation and primary or secondary deposition. Unjoined finds stay in the register with a reason. A match through the town name alone is ineligible for the deciding subset.

## Pilot, power and freezing

The current corpus has already been examined for site, object type, size, motif and several text families. A new seed does not create a new population. The pilot may use those records to establish extraction reliability, disagreement rates, plausible effect size, context discordance, clustering and source access. It cannot become the confirmation test of a candidate chosen from it.

The prospective confirmation block must contain new independently observed information: newly excavated or newly recovered primary contexts, externally withheld records, or other material whose observation and identity history is audited. New metadata on old objects can add information, but reuse of their text or previously measured attributes remains exposed. The exact endpoint, its exposure history and the permissible inference must be reviewed before a confirmation claim.

The minimum town and context counts on C-0017 to C-0019 are entry gates. They are not a declaration of adequate power. Our [exact synthetic planning calculation](synthetic-v1/results.json) illustrates the difference. For an independent, non-tied sign test at one-sided alpha 0.025, if a useful effect makes 70% of pairs favour the prediction, 20 pairs give power 0.416, 40 give 0.703 and 80 give 0.941. This sign probability is not the odds ratio of a conditional count model. Shared towns, related contexts and ties can reduce effective information. The pilot must therefore simulate the actual planned inference, including town heterogeneity and batch dependence, and achieve at least 0.80 power at the prespecified useful effect while checking false-positive calibration.

When the pilot passes, register every participant sharing confirmation, freeze the observation specification, mappings, scoring code, comparisons, exclusions, effect thresholds, influence rules and joint credence rule. Use `python -m bench shots` and the shared loader. Dates and material classes are fixed before the scorer opens architectural or text outcomes it was meant to predict. The current [synthetic shots run](bench-shots-synthetic-v1.json) checks these guards on generated records. The actual future round requires a new manifest, audited source population and its own versioned score outputs.

## Deciding endpoints

The architectural endpoint compares persistence of shared routes with persistence of internal partitions through independent episodes, matched for material, foundation depth, robbing and exposure. It reports the episode as the unit and the distribution among towns. A connection-based repair model competes with distance, terrain and specified supervision models. A gain in predictive score supports the contrast that was frozen; it does not automatically establish negotiation.

The handover endpoint compares matched primary contexts. Its model adjusts for phase, sampled material, recovery and preservation and reports a conditional effect with a method calibrated for the actual number of independent clusters. It retains direction after omitting each town and production group. An odds ratio of at least 2 and an interval excluding 1 is the proposed useful pass threshold; the pilot may make the design larger but cannot change that threshold after confirmation. The text endpoint tests added information over a context-and-manufacturing-style baseline, with a reciprocal style ablation and frequency- and group-preserving rearrangements.

The labour-obligation endpoint has a further gate. A provisioning association fits goods allocation and general rationing as well. An independent material or documentary observation must distinguish obligations for shared work from those alternatives before that specific reading gains credence. [Rao 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.00049), pp. 518-525, is the direct precedent for labour and ration tokens; our proposed extension is the phase-linked shared-infrastructure referent. If the distinguishing observation cannot be specified from a blinded pilot, the specific labour test remains ineligible.

The three conjecture cards carry one joint rule for overlapping observations. Failed predictions, null findings and exclusions remain in the result. The strongest deciding result would join a newly dated alteration of a shared route to a changed distribution of independently identified documentary operations, with text conventions persisting across the change and the effect surviving manufacturing, household and recovery controls. The same protocol can kill that model if primary contexts and adequate power show no useful relation.
