# Presentation retrospective, 24 October 2026

We used the reviewed run-v3 register as the archaeological input and assembled a versioned snapshot of the three modules, source-use records, the Mohenjo-daro phase series, the Dholavira context-table summary, earlier results, methods, revised conjectures and prospective test design. We preserved the distinction between a registered assertion, a source-use record, a catalogue row, a structure and an independent archaeological context. The deck therefore carries several different denominators rather than one combined measure of town complexity.

The initial 26-slide deck passed package checks and rendered in Microsoft PowerPoint. Native rendering showed that the default line exporter smoothed connections between phase estimates, adding an unsupported dip between two equal Gini estimates. We changed all series to straight segments. We also assigned explicit series fills so markers follow their series colours, added an interval key to the earlier city-prediction result and disclosed the single III A context row's uninscribed status directly on the slide.

The coordinator then supplied six enclosure-dimension ratios derived from the held source observations. We replaced the synthetic power chart with a native chart of those six architectural parts and retained the power gate in the prospective test sequence. The chart preserves the explicit Kalibangan source conflict in its notes and the lack of any dimension error model on the slide. The six ratios do not supply six independent towns or evidence for an intended canonical proportion.

We wrote a second complete deck instead of replacing the first endpoint. The finalizer's structural and first-party import checks passed. Thirteen chart workbooks make the displayed values editable in PowerPoint. A dedicated comparison verified 71 numeric cache entries against the frozen input snapshot and stated derivations. A private copy was used to edit and resize three charts; the changes survived saving and reopening. The final delivered file's hash remained unchanged.

The layout checker produced five estimates of table overflow. PowerPoint's actual renders showed that these tables fit. We inspected every final slide at its full 1280 × 720 export size, including the source captions and multi-line cells. The final deck contains no clipped or overlapping text observed in that review. Its source images, PDFs and excavation plans remain on D and are not embedded. The presentation adds a visual account of existing evidence and acquires no new archaeological data.

The final reading is architectural persistence amid repeated local rebuilding. The deck leaves coordination, negotiation, handover and labour obligations as separate propositions. A deciding study needs independently dated routes and plots, primary-use documentary deposits, blinded coding and a recovery model strong enough to distinguish these propositions from rival causes before confirmation is opened.
