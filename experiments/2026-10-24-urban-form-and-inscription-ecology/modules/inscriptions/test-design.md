# A prospective test of urban coordination through access and documents

This is a proposed design written after source review. It has no fresh confirmation population and no computed archaeological test statistic. The parent study supplies the versioned bench attachment. Existing object, site, motif, size, graphic-variant and numeral results retain their recorded exposure in `bench/spent.json`.

## Claim and competitors

**Rebuilding shared routes and gateways redirected documentary operations while the common stock of written formulae persisted.** We assign this extension a subjective credence of 0.35. The claim concerns a change within a named town across a named construction episode. It predicts a relation between access and how objects were used, while permitting households and production groups to retain distinct practices.

The main competing account is that the distribution comes from local production and rubbish movement. A workshop can send its waste into a street or wall-side dump without any transaction taking place there. Fire can preserve sealings and manufacture failures where ordinary closures disappear elsewhere. Another competitor is a single city-wide issuing monopoly. Its evidence would be restricted production, a common distribution of independently identified issuer groups and exclusive control of transfers across many neighbourhoods. Orthogonal streets, standard brick proportions and shared sign forms do not identify that controller by themselves.

| Coordination scale | Independent architectural or material observation | Documentary prediction that could distinguish it |
|---|---|---|
| Household | Controlled entry to a dwelling; repeated floors; a household assemblage isolated from communal rubbish | Production or use groups concentrated within a household across repeated occupations, after household recovery exposure is controlled |
| District | Several dwellings share a drain, approach or access restriction; adjoining households remain separately accessible | Documentary operations align with district access after household and production group are included; nearby houses on different access systems differ |
| Corporate group | Production materials and methods connect separated workplaces, with membership inferred independently of the script | Shared dies or formulae follow the independently defined production or use group across nonadjacent buildings and, where dates match, across towns |
| City-wide authority | Controlled city access combined with restricted production and an independently documented distribution hierarchy | One issuer network spans household and district boundaries and predicts primary closure-use contexts better than local production and neighbourhood access |

These are competing measurements of organizational reach. A die or mould identifies a tool; a consistent cutting hand identifies a manufacturing attribution. Neither identifies an individual owner or office without further evidence. The same legend on different dies is a formula relation, not proof that one person moved.

## Acquire the observations before reading the outcome

We need a phase-keyed building graph for at least two periods on either side of a named access alteration. Its nodes are rooms or exterior working spaces, and its edges are demonstrable contemporary passages. We record doors as open, blocked or uncertain, with wall construction, rebuilding, drains and street surfaces entered separately. A plan assembled from several phases cannot enter as one lived town. Coordinates, plan scale, north reference and restoration history accompany every geometry. Route centrality measures must include uncertainty from unexcavated connections, alternative doorway readings and excavation boundaries.

Every object needs registration, aliases, carrier and manufacturing class, all physical faces, recovery lot, stratum, deposit kind, phase argument and the source of its relation to the building. We preserve dimensions and measurement units, material identification method, evidence of firing, reverse contacts, residue, wear and accidental die details. A catalogue period derived from seal style cannot test that style's chronological change. A date for a wall's later fill cannot date its original construction. Source-qualified intervals should enter the chronology model directly, with separate variables for carving, use and burial.

Recovery exposure must be quantified. We need excavated area or sediment volume by context, preservation condition, screening method, fraction excavated and the number of eligible contexts searched. Sealings are vulnerable when unfired. A burned building can inflate the recovered ratio of tags to stamps without an ancient increase in sealing. We therefore record burning and conservation alongside the denominator. Structural counts distinguish constructed units, surviving examples, excavated examples and catalogue rows. A building's absence from an unexcavated area remains missing.

The first focused targets are Harappa Trench 54's courtyard sections and object catalogue; the wall 296 debris lot and clump boundary; Lothal's individual passage layers and underside views; and the original field record for Dholavira's panel and narrowed North Gate. Each can resolve a different relation. We should acquire these through existing publications and authorized archives before widening the corpus. HARP or museum correspondence requires separate authorization to message the institution.

## Define independent groups

The source table is not the analysis unit. We first close aliases of the same physical object, fragments of one object and repeated photographs. Impression identity is then grouped separately from object identity. Clay pieces carrying the same die remain different objects but share a tool; multiple impressions on one piece share a depositional history. Tablets made from one mould or repeated complete text can also be dependent. Each connected group receives one split assignment, preserving the full group in discovery or confirmation.

An excavation deposit is a higher grouping level. Sixteen tablets found as one clump cannot supply sixteen independent locations. A layer containing redeposited material receives a different likelihood from an intact working floor. Multiple papers and catalogue entries describing the same find contribute source qualification, not new sample size. District and town comparisons require several independent deposits, not one exceptional cache.

We classify material-defined production groups without viewing inscription classes or architectural predictions. Use petrography, trace elements where appropriate, firing characteristics, manufacturing marks and blind 3D die matching. A second observer checks a reserved subset. Disagreements remain alternative classifications for sensitivity analysis. Only after this step do we join independently coded sign formulae, source-qualified structural contexts and material groups.

## Freeze and score a joint prediction

Discovery selects a small number of measurable operations, such as pre-firing vessel marking, separate clay fastening, moulded tablet production and direct-reading tablet use where physically established. Unknown use cannot be converted to one of these labels merely from sign sequence. Discovery also chooses one access metric and one minimum useful prediction gain, with the metric checked against alternative excavation masks.

The primary prospective comparison predicts operational class in a newly qualified primary context. Its baseline includes town and phase, carrier preservation, burning, recovery effort, household and independently measured production group. The alternative adds the prespecified change in access caused by rebuilding. We compare their predictive log scores on held-out deposition groups. A permutation comparison moves operational labels only among exchangeable contemporary contexts with the same recovery and preservation conditions; it reruns model selection for each permutation when selection is part of the procedure. A separate model checks whether formula identity remains shared across those changed operations.

For the formula comparison, discovery must freeze the formula definition, minimum support and permitted graphic variation. The test uses independently identified formulas, with no inferred office or commodity labels. A common-stock model conditions on carrier and material-defined production group; its competitor adds the independently dated before-or-after phase. Persistence passes only if the upper 95% interval of the added-phase gain is below a prespecified equivalence margin, proposed here as 0.03 bits per independent text group. This requires a narrow interval, rather than treating a nonsignificant phase effect as evidence of sameness. The conclusion applies to the frozen supported formulas, not every sign in the script.

We propose a minimum useful gain of 0.10 bits per independent deposition group, a component-bootstrap 95% interval above zero, and a lower one-sided permutation probability of 0.05 for the primary access comparison. This numerical threshold is a design proposal, not a frozen pass rule or an observed effect. Before registration, a simulation based on independently collected context counts must establish at least 80% power for 0.10 bits and report power for half that effect. If those counts do not provide it, we retain an acquisition study rather than weaken the primary rule after seeing the inscription outcomes.

A regional claim also requires the direction to survive exclusion of Mohenjo-daro and each single town, as well as each major cache or production component. The particular cross-city graphic test already has historical exposure, so a new seed on its objects cannot confirm this extension. A two-town result supports those two towns; a broad civilization-wide claim needs independent comparable cases and a frozen regional rule.

Before any confirmation read, we register all participating models through the shared shots protocol, hash the prediction file, freeze the exact independent groups and record prior access. The model receives no catalogue prefix, registration formatting or object order that reveals a withheld town or cohort. A material or context cohort already used to invent the prediction is discovery data even if a new file hash is produced. The confirmation loader logs the first authorized read.

## Joint credence and failure

The access result and shared-formula result involve overlapping objects. They therefore receive one joint update. A proposed rule is to raise credence from 0.35 to 0.55 only if both prespecified comparisons pass and the result survives the cache, city and preservation influence checks. One pass with one failure leaves the composite claim unsupported and lowers credence to 0.20. Failure of the powered access comparison, or demonstration that the entire relation is explained by secondary manufacture waste or burning, lowers it to 0.05 and kills the present extension. A source or identity failure before scoring makes the test ineligible; it does not count as a scientific failure of the hypothesis.

The specific institutional interpretation follows the winning comparison. Household concentration supports household reach. Access-linked distributions across several households support district reach. Material or die networks connecting separated contemporary workplaces support coordination at the scale of that network. Restricted production and a city-spanning primary-use hierarchy would support the city-level competitor. The model should name the measured reach without assigning a monarchy, language, title or commodity that the observations cannot identify.

The deciding evidence is a new sequence of primary deposits whose access changed, whose documentary objects can be identified individually, and whose production groups and dates were established before their inscriptions were used to select an explanation. That sequence would turn the present source bridge into a test of how Indus towns organized work through urban change.
