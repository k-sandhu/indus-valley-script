# Eastern town coverage

The module adds 118 source-qualified architectural, contextual and coverage assertions for Kalibangan, Banawali, Rakhigarhi, Surkotada, Kanmer, Shikarpur and Farmana. These records summarize ten previously acquired excavation-author/project reports. They do not add ten new publications, newly excavated structures, new radiocarbon determinations or new inscriptions. Earlier source observations remain separately identifiable in their original packages.

`sources.json` records each reused PDF's logical path, physical D: path, SHA-256, byte count, bibliographic identity, original URL, inspected pages and local-only handling. `observations.json` gives one assertion per record. `quantitative-table.json` selects the records with machine-readable values, units and their local denominator or scope. Values include excavation operations, building/room counts, outlined versus reconstructed areas, feature dimensions, rebuilding counts and source-specific dating claims. No missing field is replaced with zero.

Preservation and exposure records explain why complete totals cannot yet be produced. Farmana's perimeter was levelled; Shikarpur's first renewed season had nine small trenches and did not reach the enclosure base; early Surkotada houses are incomplete; Rakhigarhi's draft combines inconsistent phase schemes and area statements. The register retains those reasons beside the relevant comparisons.

The source audit preserves four conflicts: Kalibangan's original scan says 180 m for Period I east–west extent where the current Penn HTML says 160 m; Kanmer's printed interior area does not equal the product of its reported spans; Rakhigarhi's 300 ha statement does not match its 3 km-radius statement; Farmana's early chronology and later laboratory/editor discussion disagree. Farmana PLD-12422 also needs a sample/deposition join because the sample table uses a Harappan room label while its result table assigns a Gupta date.

No new source asset was downloaded. New text extracts and rendered inspection pages are local-only under `D:\projects\indus_valley_script\data\raw\2026-10-24-urban-form-and-inscription-ecology\eastern\`. Their file hashes and paths are in `derivative-manifest.json` in that directory. Source PDFs were reused in place. No imagery or plan is tracked with this module, and no inscription benchmark or spending record was changed.
