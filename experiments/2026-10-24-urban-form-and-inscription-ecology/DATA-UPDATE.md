# Source and coverage changes for integration

The package adds a comparative architectural and documentary assertion register for twelve core towns. Its 287 assertions comprise 126 western, 118 eastern and 43 documentary records. One assertion also links Harappa with the hinterland site Lahoma Lal Tibba. These are source-qualified uses and new structured joins, not 287 new discoveries or independent archaeological events.

The merged register contains 42 source-use records. Thirty-eight path/hash pairs passed checks; these contain 36 distinct held hashes. Repeated editions and source routes are retained. Seven newly acquired methods PDFs are in the local `methods/` directory; the western source work newly acquired Jansen 1989, Kenoyer's 2021-22 synthesis and Green and colleagues 2026. Eastern and documentary modules reused held sources. Download failures, including HTTP 403 and a publisher endpoint returning non-PDF content, remain in acquisition records. The successful alternate route for Singh 2017 is recorded separately from the failed one.

New fields include named architectural part, source phase, ordered alteration, component material, reported quantity, measurement or count scope, source uncertainty, original chronological convention, source access status and missing-reason arrays. The quantity CSV has 134 rows, including dimensions and counts in distinct units. The source index supplies 72 selected town-field profiles and the complete JSON retains all assertions. Source completeness is not inferred from the reference index.

The documentary addition extracts all 225 printed rows of the preliminary Dholavira seal-context table, pp. 247-259. Detailed accession, grid, stratum, depth, area, stage, motif and inscription labels remain local-only. The aggregate preserves 162 unqualified stage rows and 63 missing, mixed or qualified labels, with 139 explicit inscription flags. The earliest listed III A row is uninscribed. No physical alias has been adjudicated by these totals. The prior 28-row audit overlaps this catalogue and cannot be added to it.

The register retains thirteen unknown phases with field-level reasons, conflicting locations, areas and chronology, a missing plate volume and preliminary/draft source status. Sixty readiness rows record five comparison prerequisites not assembled for each town. They are not assertions of archaeological absence. Six ratios of reported dimension pairs add a scoped arithmetic comparison with unspecified uncertainty; no new field measurements are added.

The global inscribed-object census, catalogue concordance, number of faces, calendar construction dates, radiocarbon determinations, true-north bearings and DEM horizons are unchanged. Sixteen earlier exact outputs are pinned in the documentary crosswalk. Prior exposure is recorded and no real confirmation data were opened.

The logical raw root is `data/raw/2026-10-24-urban-form-and-inscription-ecology/`; the physical root is `D:\projects\indus_valley_script\data\raw\2026-10-24-urban-form-and-inscription-ecology\`. All acquired PDFs, OCR, source text, plans, imagery, detailed derivatives and rendered products stay there. Source manifests retain original paths and hashes. Tracked candidates are our protocols, code, authored source summaries, aggregates, reviews and proposed cards. Maintainers can integrate these coverage records into `knowledge/DATA.md` and `data/MANIFEST.json`; the generated map and registry remain unchanged.
