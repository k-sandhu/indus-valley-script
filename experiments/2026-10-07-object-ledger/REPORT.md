# Object-ledger pilot findings

The ledger represents **200 reported physical carriers**, with **180 joined to the pinned local corpus and 20 represented from the published Lothal sealing frame alone**. It adds source-backed metadata and makes missing fields queryable. Physical identity and source-surface alignment still require specialist review. The sample is purposive: 69 records are labelled Harappa and 65 Lothal, so its percentages describe this pilot. [Selection](selection-2026-10-07-v1/selection.json), `objects` and `primary_counts`; [summary](analysis-2026-10-07-v3/summary.json), `sites`.

## Selection and entities

The frozen rule selected all 22 Harappa tablets H2218–H2239, one explicit Period 3A carrier, eight 3B and eight 3C carriers, 24 distinct museum-joined carriers, 25 additional corpus-linked Lothal sealings, 20 absent CISI 1 sealing keys and 18 external carriers. It filled the remaining 74 slots by region/site/type/length/damage/face-count strata. Other source memberships can overlap these primary allocations. The finished population has 34 sites in eight export regions, 63 carriers with maximum face length one or two, 78 with three to five, 35 with six to nine, four with ten or more and 20 with unavailable length. Forty-seven corpus families have several normalized face records; 88 carriers meet the declared damage proxy. These are export metadata categories whose physical boundaries have not been independently recounted. [Protocol](PROTOCOL.md), selection rule; [summary](analysis-2026-10-07-v3/summary.json), `primary_selection`, `regions`, `length_bins`, `multi_face_objects_from_corpus` and `damage_status`.

Separate tables contain 200 objects, 200 contexts, 259 source-specific surfaces/transcriptions, 6,490 assertions, 430 dimension assertions and 356 asset rights records. The transcription count comprises 256 Lipi records and three Mayig records. A surface here preserves a source record boundary; a second annotation does not establish another excavated face. Identifier spelling, source edition, source inventory/version, order and boundary assumptions remain attributed. Every requested null has a missingness reason. The twenty catalogue-only entries all fall in CISI 1's L124–L216 sealing section and the inspected 93-row Frenez–Tosi Table 1 frame. Their physical carrier class and catalogue identity are supported by that frame, while individual inscription eligibility, face extent, damage and photograph page remain review questions. Their absent keys do not establish twenty newly excavated objects. ## Joins and source differences

There are 51 confirmed operational source relationships: 22 Harappa caption joins, 26 museum joins and three Mayig annotation links. The museum set contains 16 published CISI concordances, five shared field/plate joins, three Marshall composite joins, one normalized excavation join and one normalized full-excavation/concordance join. The 34 candidate edges connect nine unjoined museum records to ten selected carriers using shared site and coarse type, with at most five selected candidates per museum row. These search buckets never transfer museum fields or merge carriers. [Join report](analysis-2026-10-07-v3/join-report.json), `confirmed_relationships_by_method`, `confirmed_museum_records`, `candidate_relationships`, `candidate_museum_records` and `candidate_objects`.

Twenty-one selected museum pairs have inherited field adjudications. Five additional v6 pairs lack those comparisons. On the 21-pair denominator, site agrees in all 21; material has two disagreements; dimensions have two outside-tolerance comparisons; and inscription counts have two disagreements. Nine dimension comparisons are incomplete and eight museum records lack dimensions. Sixteen inscription comparisons lack comparable numerical counts. The source audit's dimension tolerance and sign-count judgments are retained as inherited classifications. Our parsed museum axes remain unlabelled dimensions and are not aligned by position to horizontal, vertical or thickness. ## Lookup cost and review

Population required 1,095 distinct file/record lookups across 200 carriers: mean 5.475, median four and range three to twelve. This counts extraction queries and source-specific rights rows. It excludes a human time estimate and records zero newly read primary pages for population. Reusing the census and earlier source frames made the automated pilot inexpensive, while transferring their unresolved primary-review work. [Source lookups](run-2026-10-07-v4/source-lookups.json), `lookup_count` and `locators`; [summary](analysis-2026-10-07-v3/summary.json), `source_lookup_cost`.

The 134 review questions cover 82 carriers. They identify catalogue-only inscription eligibility, missing external registrations, tablet face alignment, stamp-based W1 context, date-label compatibility, candidate museum concordances and inherited field disagreements. The other 118 carriers have no targeted issue in this rule-based queue; they have not received expert identity approval. [Review queue](analysis-2026-10-07-v3/review-queue.json), `object_id`, `question`, `reviewer_role` and `evidence`.
