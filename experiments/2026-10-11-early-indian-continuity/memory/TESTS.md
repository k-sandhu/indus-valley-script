# Tests of practical inheritance and changing origins

These are prospective study specifications. No recensional stability count, excavation match or reset-frequency result has been measured here. The current [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) froze the documentary audit and constructive example; a future empirical comparison needs its own dated corpus, coding rules and endpoint before outcomes. The positive proposition is that some practiced participant/action/object relations can outlast their historical attribution, and that an origin account can reorganize relations acquired at different times.

## A textual test with independent branches

Begin with complete, source-qualified episode sets: the Manu/Iḍā material in both Śatapatha recensions and relevant ritual descriptions, attested parallels of DN27, and origin and particular-event accounts from the same transmission branches. Exclude partial quotations unless their missing coverage is explicitly coded. Translation editions derived from one source are controls on interpretation, rather than separate transmission branches. A documented shared ancestor or later contact must remain visible in the branch graph.

Two coders should independently mark relations before assigning a proposed historical route. The operational unit is a participant/action/object triple plus any explicit precedence constraint: for example, a portion is handed to a specified participant before an utterance or consumption. The attribution unit is a named actor, place, narrated time or causal origin claim. Each aligned unit receives preserved, changed or unresolved status, retaining the original locus. Differences produced by a translator, a variant reading and a missing witness are separate categories. Agreement should be reported before reconciliation, and uncertainty should remain unresolved when the text cannot choose a reading.

The primary comparison asks whether comparable procedural relations remain stable more often than origin details within matched episodes. Match for genre, repeated wording, granularity and whether the same clause carries both units. Report results by branch group and episode before pooling; branches that inherit the same wording are dependent. A larger number of clauses in one unit class must not become evidence of better retention. A failure to find greater procedural stability rejects this sharpened component for the selected corpus. Stability in both classes would support text preservation but would not show when the underlying practice began.

This narrative-reset hypothesis was formulated after the documentary audit. First define resets without reference to the desired result: destruction leaving a common survivor, cosmic renewal or an explicitly new population. Code the stated institutional explanation separately, including who receives rights, who performs duties and who can sanction breaches. Compare complete origin accounts with matched particular-event accounts from the same branches. The hypothesis is weakened if resets show no association with institutional explanations, or if explanatory order fails to track the account's stated legitimating purpose. A genuine historical catastrophe and a legitimating use can coexist; an independently dated catastrophe requires a separate identification test.

## A material test of a small procedure

Choose a jointly constrained procedure before searching for a match. For the Iḍā case, possible variables include ingredient combinations, portion distribution, sequence and the relation of recipient locations. Number alone is insufficient. The archaeological variables must be ones that an assemblage can actually preserve; a recited formula or a priest's title cannot be inferred from a vessel count.

A blinded modern performance/replica study could first establish which residue, distribution and deposition signatures survive for the chosen procedure. It should include ordinary shared meals, alternative division rules, reuse and post-depositional disturbance. If blinded observers cannot distinguish the procedure from these controls, its proposed archaeological signature is unsuitable for a continuity claim. The replica study tests recoverability, not antiquity.

Only then compare independently dated assemblages in the proposed region and intermediate periods. Record excavation context, deposit formation, ingredient identification and chronological uncertainty before assigning a procedural match. A local-inheritance route needs a documented intermediate carrier or transfer, rather than endpoints separated by an unobserved interval. It is weakened if the required relation or material first appears later than the proposed chain, or if adequate dated intermediate coverage shows a different practice. Survival through poorly sampled periods remains a coverage question, and should not be converted into an asserted gap or connection.

## A river configuration test

Specify the claim narrowly: which named river denotes which reach, which reading of RV7.95.2 is assumed, which physical property is predicted, and whether the description is claimed to be contemporary, inherited or composite. Then compare the published samples and horizons at that reach. Large glacier-fed flow, monsoon-fed perennial flow and episodic flow predict different observations. A provenance signal by itself must not become a discharge history, and a river identity must not supply a hymn date.

The contemporaneous-description variant can be tested only when composition constraints and geographical identification have independent support. A reach proven unable to support its required configuration in that independently bounded period would reject that variant. A later memory of earlier flow or a reused name is a different proposal with different required carriers. Iranian cognate-name and local name-reuse controls should be acquired before deciding that the named configuration is specifically Harappan. Each new source's composition, manuscript and narrated time must remain separate.

## Competing routes and stopping conditions

Local inheritance predicts an older, geographically connected relational bundle. Diffusion predicts a supported source and direction of transfer. Reconstruction from current conditions predicts that comparable communities can generate the broad account from ordinary institutions without the proposed chain. Doctrinal construction predicts that actors, social labels and explanatory order follow the account's ethical or political purpose. These mechanisms may combine. The comparison should report which relation each observation distinguishes, rather than forcing a single origin for the whole text.

Stop a proposed narrow route when its required material, direction or chronology conflicts with independently dated evidence. Stop using an archaeological signature when the recovery controls cannot distinguish it from ordinary practice. Stop inferring deep transmission when the only retained feature is a generic ecological or coordination relation. A successful future endpoint would retain the source-qualified procedure, dated intermediate evidence, competing-route controls and the changed origin account together.
