# Practical transmission and narrative origin

Frozen documentary and constructive design, 11 October 2026. Repository memory and source availability were inspected before this protocol. Initial search snippets exposed candidate titles and familiar story summaries. Detailed feature coding, chronology joins and model outputs have not been produced. This is a source audit with declared tests, not a blinded experiment or a calibrated historical classifier.

The question is whether the earliest recoverable Vedic and non-Vedic passages preserve details that discriminate Harappan inheritance from inherited Indo-Iranian motifs, generic ecological experience, diffusion, later retrojection and doctrinal social-origin construction. A second question asks why a text can present coherent social complexity without providing an equally deep dated history. The proposed mechanism is that practiced institutional relations can persist while redaction reorganizes their origin into a causal narrative.

## Source set and exact observations

The principal cases are the Manu flood in the Mādhyandina Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa 1.8.1, the Sarasvatī passages at Ṛgveda 7.95.1–2 and the river list at 10.75.5–6, and the rice/property/theft/elected-authority sequence in Dīgha Nikāya 27. The last case must retain its cosmic and anti-hereditary-status frame. Avestan flood/winter or river-name material is a comparative control if a primary edition can be inspected. Near-event royal names or the Sudās battle can serve as a contrast, without upgrading the hymns to an independently dated annal.

For each case, record source edition/translator, exact loci, inspected witness, source language where accessible, our paraphrased observations, translation disagreement and absent details. Distinguish an explicit absence in the inspected passage from failure to search the entire literature. No sentence about an authenticated Harappan memory will be inferred from story similarity alone.

River comparisons must inspect the primary methods and results of Singh and colleagues 2017, Giosan and colleagues 2012, and Chatterjee and colleagues 2019, with a competing channel/provenance result when accessible. Preserve each paper's sample locations, dating methods, physical units and scope. Different channels, reaches or sediment sources can produce different results. No geological age is assigned to a hymn through an assumed river-name identity.

Professional transmission claims require inspected scholarship on recitation, schools, textual assembly or canon formation. Exact word preservation, retention of an operation, preservation of a referent and preservation of an event date are separate claims. Oral precision demonstrated for an established corpus cannot establish the unobserved history of a story before its fixation.

## Typed chronology and evidence graph

Each observation has an evidence type: textual content, philological/textual stratum, manuscript or publication witness, physical geological measurement, archaeological association, comparative motif, or proposed mechanism. Dates retain their type and source. Narrated time, composition/redaction, attested textual witness, field sampling and estimated physical process time occupy separate fields. Relative strata may be recorded without choosing an absolute calendar interval.

The graph records supported links and proposed links separately. A proposed river identification, a similar motif, a narrated chronology or doctrinal genealogy cannot transmit a calendar bound into a textual composition node. Every proposed Harappan route must name the intermediate carriers, operation and independent observations that would test it. Coding will supply no arbitrary weighted score or historical posterior.

Each case is evaluated with the same questions: What is actually asserted? Which rare or operationally constrained relation might survive? Which alternatives generate it? What independent observation would separate them? What would falsify the sharpened mechanism? A case can remain unidentifiable even when its narrative is detailed.

## Constructive model and tests

Build an explicitly synthetic model in which an operation-dependency graph can be retained, recombined or acquired while event chronology is discarded. Distinct invented histories, including a recent construction and long transmission, must generate the same coherent origin account under the stated transformation. Enumerate the complete finite input space chosen by the code, report its size and the many-to-one mapping, and verify it independently. The example establishes logical compatibility and information loss; its inputs have no estimated historical frequencies.

The prospective study must compare procedural relations and origin narratives using independently dated sources or observations. It must include generic agriculture/coordination controls, related Indo-Iranian names or motifs where inspected, and doctrinal redaction alternatives. Specify observations that could refute a narrow local-transmission route. New fits or added cases after outcomes receive a dated amendment or are marked exploratory.

## Preservation and review

All new files stay within the owned memory package and its local raw directory. Acquired texts, PDFs, extracts and source payloads remain local-only; tracked files contain our observations, methods, bibliographic pointers, hashes and invented examples. Fresh output directories refuse overwrite. Preserve failures and source-access limitations.

Deliver a report, machine-readable observations and evidence graph, sources and source checks, model code/output and independent checks if code is used, reproduction instructions, retrospective and changed paths. An independent reviewer should challenge chronology propagation, alternative explanations and the proposed mechanism. Maximize reader comprehension; explain ideas directly in connected prose; keep necessary shared assumptions together; avoid repeated corrective comparisons or warnings that interrupt the argument. Review prose against the repository writing rules.
