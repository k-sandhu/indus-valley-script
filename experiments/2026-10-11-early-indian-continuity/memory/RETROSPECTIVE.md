# Retrospective, 11 October 2026

The assigned problem was how mature early narratives might preserve practical knowledge without supplying a reliably deep dated history, and whether selected Vedic/non-Vedic memories could distinguish Harappan inheritance from competing routes. Ownership was confined to this memory package and its local raw directory. The earlier administrative experiment, global registry and navigation were left untouched.

Repository memory was read before research: README, INDEX, the relevant registry record, writing rules, redistribution policy and the corrected backward-chronology retrospective. The source inventory's changing calendar coverage had already shown why a dated gap estimate could not become an archaeological continuity claim. The new protocol focused on actual passage details, typed dates and discriminating intermediate carriers, rather than rerunning that calendar-gap inference.

The declared source audit combined the Manu/Iḍā chapter, two river hymns, DN27, an Iranian winter/rescue control, primary river research and scholarship on professional transmission. Detailed observations and model outcomes followed the protocol. We inspected original passages where available, distinguished translations from source-language controls, and recorded the exact scope of abstract-only or indexed access. Source extraction aided navigation; the selected Larios and Thomson PDF pages were rendered and visually inspected. A later narrative-reset interpretation was labelled as post-audit synthesis and received prospective tests.

Changing the retained censure/delegation relation distinguishes the control. The result demonstrates a logical many-to-one mapping under stipulated erasure, without estimating historical frequencies or explaining how the input chain arose.

The first verification invocation encountered a bracket typo before it could run or write an endpoint. It was corrected in the verification code; the subsequent independent hash, locus, graph and enumeration checks all passed, producing the frozen 96-check record. No analysis outcome was changed to improve a result. The independent reviewer reconstructed the finite space and graph separately and examined the source interpretation.

The main hypothesis now has specific targets: participant/action/object relations, their order, and the historical attribution attached to them. The report connects the Manu origin story to its Iḍā instructions and DN27's moral account to boundaries, appropriation and compensated authority. The prospective work compares both kinds of information across attested branches, tests whether proposed material signatures can actually be recovered, and requires matched river reaches and independent identities. It also specifies observations that would reject narrow inheritance or transfer routes. These are proposed studies; no recensional stability rate or material continuity match was measured.

The remaining limitation is the absent diagnostic intermediate chain. The inspected cases permit combinations of inheritance, acquisition and reconstruction. A next endpoint must add independently dated practical relations and carriers, or reject an unsuitable signature using the stated controls. Raw acquired witnesses remain local-only, and this work did not commit, push or publish them.
