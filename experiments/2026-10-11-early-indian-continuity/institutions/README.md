# Institutions and component histories

[REPORT.md](REPORT.md) develops the claim that recurring skilled work, patronage and trained specialists can support sophisticated institutions through settlement change. It uses agriculture, crafting, priestly duties, patrons and assemblies to specify what could persist and what would distinguish inheritance from competing origins.

The package contains 14 source works or editions, 30 curated observations and 7 linked claims. [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md) precedes their assembly. [METHODS.md](METHODS.md) describes the coding and reproducible checks; [TESTS.md](TESTS.md) gives four prospective discriminating studies. [sources.json](sources.json), [observations.json](observations.json) and [claims.json](claims.json) preserve the selected locators and scope.

The corrected categorical endpoint is [run-v2/audit.json](run-v2/audit.json). [AMENDMENT-eligibility.md](AMENDMENT-eligibility.md) explains the filtering error in run-v1, which remains available. The rice classification calculation is identical across the two versions. Source-table bounds are not a probability of cultural continuity.

[source-checks.json](source-checks.json) records witness formats, hashes, acquisition exclusions, transport exceptions and rights. All downloaded text, PDFs, OCR and rendered pages remain under the local-only raw directory. The archive contains no ancient manuscript facsimile or new excavation evidence. [RETROSPECTIVE.md](RETROSPECTIVE.md) records the observable research process and corrections.
