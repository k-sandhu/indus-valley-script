# Independent review of early Indian continuity proposals

The inspected calculations reproduce, and the developed proposals remain suitable for exploratory research. They identify particular carriers and observations to seek. The current evidence does not establish a diagnostic transmission route from Harappan practice to a Vedic or Buddhist institution. The main contribution is a set of source-grounded comparisons and discriminating study designs, together with executable checks that prevent several tempting chronological inferences.

This review covers the institutional ledgers and corrected endpoint, the memory ledgers and first endpoint, the coordinator's literary control and retention model, the developed proposals, and the integrated report and manuscript. Its independent chronology work is in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md), [sources.json](sources.json) and [chronology.json](chronology.json). The source inventories are purposive collections. Repeated editions and modern transcriptions check readings, while sharing the ancestry of the text they reproduce.

## Chronology and source interpretation

The archaeological date of a deposit, the composition of a passage, its collection or redaction, the surviving manuscript and the time narrated by a story are different quantities. The reviewed packages keep them separate. Jamison and Brereton's approximately 1400–1000 BCE estimate remains an attributed, contentious assessment of Rigvedic hymn composition. The early family-book collection does not supply a date for each verse. Witzel's addition list explicitly includes RV 4.57.4–8 at proof PDF p. 113/internal p. 197; independently reading that locus confirms the qualification used by the institutional package. His political reconstruction is retained as an interpretation rather than an excavated event. [J&B introduction, printed p. 5](https://vedisk.sites.ku.dk/files/2021/09/Jamison-Brereton-s.-2-15.pdf), [printed pp. 14–18](https://vedisk.sites.ku.dk/files/2021/09/13-22-Stephanie_W_Jamison_Joel_P_Brereton_The.pdf), [Witzel author proof](https://michaelwitzel.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/Erdosy1995.pdf).

The selected textual institutions are represented at the level their wording permits. RV 2.1.2 predicates differentiated duties of Agni; it supports an intelligible repertoire, while leaving human staffing unresolved. The craft analogy and assembly praise likewise leave production scale and meeting membership open. DN 5 places its livelihood advice within a legendary royal narrative. DN 27's property and authority sequence belongs to a cosmic and ethical explanation of social order. These observations support questions about accumulated institutional reasoning without independently dating its beginnings. A shared Indo-Iranian repertoire, regional acquisition and later reorganization remain substantive competing sources.

The broader source control also separates early canon layers from later physical copies and traditional councils. Allon's article is *Early Buddhist Texts: Their Composition and Transmission*, published in 2022; its 2021 acceptance, copyright and DOI string explain the older local filename. His developed Gāndhārī Sāmaññaphala example concerns a variant of another discourse, not a manuscript of DN 27 or every word of received Pali DN 2. Wiles's audit shows why later accounts and ambiguous traditional council years cannot silently date the composition of an entire Jain canon. The present review read the selected chapters and article passages recorded in its source ledger, rather than their every underlying critical edition. [Allon 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8789374/), [Wiles 2006, printed pp. 61–68, 74–76](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/gdc/gdcebookspublic/20/19/66/78/06/2019667806/2019667806.pdf).

The river comparisons retain different physical questions and reaches. Singh's main paper reports 52 OSL burial ages from seven cores and distinguishes its Kalibangan transect from regional later activity. Chatterjee's renewed Himalayan-supply interpretation uses a roughly 300 km basin synthesis and multiple dating and provenance measurements. Neither source makes a sampled deposit the observed composition date of a hymn. Their shared river-name hypothesis needs an independent geographical and textual argument. The memory package's explicit translation branch is appropriate; this review did not adjudicate *samudrāt* or the identification of every named river. [Singh et al. 2017](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-017-01643-9), [Chatterjee et al. 2019](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-019-53489-4).

The coordinator's control is strongest at the direct literary relation: Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa 11.5.1 places quoted dialogue in prose explanation and proceeds to a fire-production prescription. Jamison and Brereton's judgment about distortion or misunderstanding is attributed to them. An older unrecorded backstory remains possible. The reviewer inspected RV 10.95.1–5 in the university teaching witness and ŚB 11.5.1.1–17 in the Sanskrit and historical translation; this is not a claim to have read the complete modern Rigveda translation. The separate GRETIL check of ŚB 1.8.1.6 confirms the intended Manu-descent reference against the web transcription glitch, without locating a modern mountain. [Teaching witness](https://vedisk.sites.ku.dk/rv-10-95-1-pururavas-og-urvasi/), [ŚB 11 Sanskrit](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/1_sanskr/1_veda/2_bra/satapath/sb_11_u.htm), [ŚB 1 Sanskrit](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/1_sanskr/1_veda/2_bra/satapath/sb_01_u.htm).

## Findings and repairs

The author identified a filtering implementation error before final review: the first institutional endpoint allowed hypotheses to survive removal of every textual locus. The correction requires a surviving cited textual observation. Both scripts and endpoints are preserved, and the independent v2 recount confirms the repair. [Amendment](../institutions/AMENDMENT-eligibility.md).

Review requested clearer labeling of the intermediate-only eligibility row because a source-reported preliminary object comparison can otherwise read like a completed transmission bridge. The report now identifies archaeological candidates and describes the missing manufacturing and operation evidence. The frozen numerical endpoint remains unchanged.

The coordinator's empirical-feedback argument also needed a precise target. A practical success test may validate several workable variants, whereas the invented repair law returns its reference procedure. The revised prose records that distinction. For the fire example, replica success means producing fire; becoming a Gandharva remains a claim of the narrative. This confines the proposed empirical mechanism to observable performance.

The memory report now explicitly distinguishes its stipulated erasure mapping from complexity generation, and its Gāndhārī manuscript control from received Pali wording and DN 27. The literary control also preserves author attribution and the possibility of an older oral background. These reporting repairs resolve the identified interpretation risks without changing frozen source observations or results.

The final source-checker's first attempt recorded one false failure: its literal probe expected “multiple workable variants,” while the correct prose used “several workable variants.” The attempt script and output remain preserved. The revised checker accepts either wording and all 59 checks pass. No author prose or endpoint was changed to satisfy that probe.

## Scope, selection and future falsification

The cases were chosen for inspectable and informative content, and the protocols disclose initial familiarity and source inspection. The developed cross-case proposals remain exploratory. The additional narrative-reset proposal was formulated after the documentary audit and needs the prospective comparison described in the memory test plan. No significance tests or calibrated historical posteriors are claimed. A discovery case cannot also supply its own independent confirmation; the prospective designs appropriately separate discovery and validation, blind material and textual coding, preserve parallel recensions and compare convergence and inherited Indo-Iranian alternatives.

The future tests still need to distinguish textual copying from actual practice, finished-object circulation from local manufacture, and practical success from an exact inherited recipe. A shared unusual operation is more informative when independently dated intermediate production shows it and when material, ecology and preservation controls cannot readily generate it. Joint rarity also needs an observed denominator. Merely adding familiar motifs, generic roles or further editions would enlarge the catalogue without identifying a route. No broad priority claim is supported or needed for these familiar research questions.

The narrative-reset comparison needs genre, topic and episode-length controls. An association between resets and institutional explanation would support that textual pattern; variant or external evidence would still be needed to show that particular historical acquisitions were actually omitted. EI-C11 correctly leaves both the association and its proposed function unmeasured.

Source access remains visible. Witzel's 1997 chapter is metadata-only in the review's independent census; the memory package separately records an indexed primary excerpt, not a full PDF reading. The 1995 author copies used by institutions were fetched with disabled TLS verification and explicitly record that exception. Their hashes fix the inspected bytes; they do not retroactively authenticate the transport. Overlapping J&B excerpts count as one work. Raw PDFs, HTML, OCR, search payloads and rendered pages remain local-only, including sources with unresolved rights or temporary reuse notices.

The check counts measure arithmetic, file identity, selected locators and declared-rule consistency. They do not measure historical truth. [review-checks.json](review-checks.json) records the reviewed file hashes, the independent check capsules and the status of each finding. Reproduction requires the local raw witnesses named by the source manifests; the check scripts refuse to overwrite their existing outputs.
