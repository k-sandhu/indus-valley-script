# Complex knowledge and cultural inheritance in early Indian textual traditions

Research manuscript, version 1, 11 October 2026. This is an exploratory source synthesis and development of testable mechanisms. Its calculations audit selected observations and invented examples; they do not estimate the probability of a Harappan origin for a text.

## Abstract

Early Indian texts already present skilled work, differentiated duties, patronage, collective deliberation and developed accounts of social origins. We investigate two explanations for that maturity: selective transmission of practical and institutional knowledge across settlement change, and preservation of relationships within stories whose historical attribution can change. The institutional study assembles 30 observations from 14 works or editions; the memory study assembles 17 observations in an 18-record source inventory. An independent audit separates archaeological, compositional, redactional, manuscript and narrated time. Farming and craft evidence identifies specific component histories and losses, while Vedic and Buddhist loci show specialist knowledge and institutional reasoning. No selected route yet supplies a diagnostic join from Harappan practice through an intermediate community to a particular textual institution. Exact synthetic constructions show how coherent structure can coexist with different origins or weaker attribution, under declared assumptions. The resulting research program targets linked operations, intermediate production evidence, parallel recensions and independently dated observations.

## 1. The problem begins with a mature surviving tradition

The earliest surviving Sanskrit collection already belongs to a learned poetic world. Jamison and Brereton describe the Rigveda as the product of named poets, bardic traditions, patronage and collection. They also locate inherited conventions in older Indo-Iranian and Indo-European praise poetry. These are positive mechanisms for literary maturity: specialists learn an existing repertoire, adapt it, compose demanding works and train successors. The age of a collection, the age of a convention and the origins of its represented institutions are separate historical questions. [Introduction, printed pp. 4–5, 9–10, 14–15](https://vedisk.sites.ku.dk/files/2021/09/Jamison-Brereton-s.-2-15.pdf), [coordinator observations C01–C03](coordinator/observations.json).

The user's observation therefore leads to a productive research question: which bodies of knowledge had already accumulated, through which carriers, before each surviving text took its present form? A tradition can inherit skills from several sources and reorganize them. Its sophisticated account of the past can combine remembered particulars with explanations built for contemporary audiences.

Harappan archaeology supplies an earlier complex material world, but a broad chronological overlap or regional proximity supplies only part of a transmission argument. Kenoyer's Harappa phase scheme places urban occupation at 2600–1900 BCE and broad Late Harappan occupation at 1900–1300 BCE. Jamison and Brereton offer approximately 1400–1000 BCE, or the second half of the second millennium, as an attributed estimate for surviving Rigvedic hymn composition. These are different kinds of assessment, applied to different units. The [independent chronology ledger](review/chronology.json) keeps their scope visible rather than converting them into a continuous institutional genealogy. [Kenoyer 2006, printed pp. 10–13](https://www.harappa.com/sites/default/files/pdf/Kenoyer2006_The%20Origin%2C%20Context%20and%20Function%20of%20the%20Indus%20Sc.pdf).

## 2. Methods: follow components and keep dates attached to their objects

The program uses two purposive source collections. It selected concrete cases with inspectable loci and substantive rival explanations. Source inventories distinguish full-paper reading, selected pages, source-language loci, historical translations, abstract access and inaccessible targets. Multiple editions, overlapping excerpts and copied files remain dependent witnesses. The counts describe these collections rather than all surviving traditions. [Protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [institutional methods](institutions/METHODS.md), [memory methods](memory/METHODS.md), [independent source account](review/SOURCES.md).

Each proposed continuity link names a component, a carrier and the observations needed between endpoints. A crop, object shape or role name can generate a question. A stronger test asks whether unusual linked choices recur: materials, sequence, division of responsibilities, handling of failure and production context. A link remains unresolved when the intermediate community is merely plausible or the matching feature is common to several mechanisms.

The date audit separates the time of an archaeological deposit or physical process, estimates of text composition, collection or redaction, physical manuscript witnesses and the time narrated by a story. Relative textual strata remain relative when an absolute date is unsupported. The earlier repository chronology of marked objects is retained as a methodological precedent; its coverage gaps are not used as gaps in farming, craft or Indian culture. [Corrected earlier retrospective](../2026-10-06-backward-chronology/amendment-2026-10-07-review-corrections/RETROSPECTIVE.md).

The main alternatives are local transmission, inherited Indo-Iranian knowledge, regional contact, convergence, genre selection and later institutional development. These mechanisms may coexist. The study supplies no subjective evidence score or historical posterior. Its code checks the declared rules, source joins and calculations; substantive interpretation remains subject to source review.

## 3. Institutions carried by recurring work

The selected textual loci show several kinds of differentiated knowledge. RV 2.1.2 applies distinct ritual duties to Agni; family-book passages represent gifts, crafted objects and gathered speech. The divine enumeration reveals a recognizable repertoire of responsibilities. It does not specify the staffing of a human ceremony. Later royal-consecration prose connects specialist households, offerings and allegiance. The institutional report retains the original terms and disputed glosses rather than imposing a modern administrative structure. [Observations T01–T04 and B01–B02](institutions/observations.json), [report](institutions/REPORT.md).

The Buddhist comparison makes institutional reasoning particularly visible. DN 2 lists occupations and services; DN 5 places livelihood-support advice in a legendary royal story. DN 16 presents meeting principles for the Vajjis and then applies similar principles to mendicants. That transfer is an observed use of a coordination idea across settings. It gives a concrete example of intellectual work that can produce mature concepts: people identify a relationship between recurring actions and collective outcomes, then apply it in another institution. The historical age of the rule still needs an independent textual argument. [Exact Pali and translation segment locators N01–N04](institutions/observations.json), [SuttaCentral source repository](https://github.com/suttacentral/bilara-data).

The material record offers components with different trajectories. Rice occurrence extends across source-assigned Harappan and PGW contexts in the inspected study, while the quantified spikelet-base analysis excludes the four PGW contexts because of low recovery. The paper's earlier and later contexts also differ in direct dating support. Occurrence, cultivation, domestication and a particular management system therefore remain distinct propositions. [Bates, Petrie and Singh 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jas.2016.04.018), [A01–A08](institutions/observations.json).

Our classification sensitivity retains the published rounded percentages and moves only uncertain spikelet bases between endpoint categories. Masudpur VII's Mature context has 9.55% domesticated bases; its Late context spans 28.57–57.14% under that reassignment. The ordering survives this particular uncertainty, but there is one retained context per phase. Masudpur I's 9.99–56.02% and Bahola's 33.15–93.08% overlap. These are bounded comparisons of selected assemblages, not a continuous regional domestication curve. [Exact corrected output](institutions/run-v2/audit.json), `rice_classification_sensitivity`.

The craft comparison gives a complementary pattern. Kenoyer's account distinguishes dispersed production from procurement-dependent activities and reports technological losses after urban reorganization. A surviving finished bead can be an heirloom or import; local manufacturing waste and drill evidence provide a different kind of observation. His modern pottery comparison also supplies a convergence control: similar materials and efficient techniques can produce similar results. [Craft observations C01–C05](institutions/observations.json), [craft study](https://www.harappa.com/sites/default/files/pdf/Kenoyer1998_Craft%20Traditions%20of%20the%20Indus%20Civilization%20and%20t.pdf), [bead comparison](https://www.harappa.com/sites/default/files/pdf/Kenoyer%202007%20Intl%20beads%20conf.pdf).

The developed mechanism follows learners, materials, recurring use and sponsorship. Local operations with frequent practical checks can remain useful through settlement change. A technique dependent on scarce supplies or extensive coordination can be lost while other skills continue. Inherited poetic specialists and local practical knowledge can then meet in a later ritual or political arrangement. The combination itself can generate new forms of reasoning about obligation, competence and collective order. This is a working historical mechanism, with selective continuity and recombination as its predictions. [Developed proposals](coordinator/DEVELOPED-PROPOSALS.md), [institutional mechanism and tests](institutions/REPORT.md).

The audit narrows the current candidate links. Of five institutional hypotheses, farming and craft retain a source-reported intermediate archaeological candidate under the declared rule. The craft candidate includes Kenoyer's preliminary Sanauli finished-bead comparison; local manufacture and continuity of operations remain unestablished. None yet has the diagnostic feature required to connect that observation to the particular textual institution. The stricter family-core policy also removes the selected loci lacking independent authentication of their within-book position. These are results about the curated evidence and eligibility rules. They do not establish that a practice was absent from an earlier society. [Corrected sensitivity endpoint](institutions/run-v2/audit.json), `sensitivity`.

## 4. Narratives can retain different parts of an older world

The memory study compares the Manu flood account, Rigvedic river passages and the Aggañña social-origin account. Each presents a different possible memory object: a motif and ritual sequence, named geography, or a causal account of institutional formation. Evaluating those objects separately yields sharper questions than assigning an age to the story as a whole. [Case observations](memory/observations.json), [memory report](memory/REPORT.md).

In Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa 1.8.1, fish rescue and survival lead into the Iḍā account and ritual distinctions. The text's technical and prescriptive ending makes performance knowledge a candidate carrier. A proposed old flood event would additionally need independent dating, location and transmission evidence. In DN 27, storage, boundaries, theft and the selection of an authority paid in rice occur inside a cosmic and ethical explanation of society. The sequence can reveal sophisticated reasoning about institutional dependence while leaving its historical origin open. [Memory loci and source checks](memory/source-checks.json).

A further theory developed after the documentary audit concerns these accounts' common starting point. A survivor or cosmic renewal can allow a tradition to explain rights and duties from a shared beginning. Practices acquired at different times can then enter one coherent account without each acquisition's separate history. This would help explain an appearance of deep, unified ancestry alongside selective inheritance. The proposed association between narrative resets and institutional explanation is unmeasured; it requires complete episode sets and comparison with particular-event accounts from the same branches. [Prospective reset test](memory/TESTS.md).

Named rivers can constrain a geographical question more than a generic flood motif. But a geological deposit and a hymn remain different objects. The inspected palaeochannel studies use different samples, reaches and physical inferences. A river identification alone cannot transfer a sediment age to the composition of a hymn; an account can also reuse an older description. The typed graph rejects ten specified unsupported date routes and keeps two attestation controls. That is a safeguard on this analysis, not a finding that the stories are recent or that old memories are absent. [Claim graph](memory/claim-graph.json), [graph audit](memory/run-v1/graph-audit.json).

A directly inspectable literary control strengthens the distinction between preserved wording and changing explanation. SB 11.5.1 embeds older dialogue within a developed prose frame and ends with a fire-production prescription. Jamison and Brereton judge that its background distorts or misunderstands parts of RV 10.95. We retain that attribution and the possibility of an earlier unrecorded backstory. The source relation itself demonstrates why wording, interpretation, operations and historical reference need separate assessments. [Coordinator observations C05–C12](coordinator/observations.json), [control study](coordinator/CONTROL-AND-MODEL.md).

The developed memory mechanism uses differences in feedback. Teachers can check recitation against a learned text. Practitioners can check operational success. A distant attribution may be harder to check and may be adapted to a new explanatory system. Allon's comparative study documents mnemonic features alongside sanctioned change, rather than a single universal mode of oral transmission. Modern learning practices offer mechanisms to investigate, with independent evidence still needed for their ancient application. [Allon 2022](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10781-021-09499-6), [source-layer audit](review/SOURCES.md).

## 5. Exact constructions clarify the limits of structural evidence

The memory package enumerates 10,368 invented histories varying route, duration, actors and time gaps. Under its stipulated rewrite, retaining only the operation sequence gives one output. Keeping a route gives four outputs; keeping time annotations gives 108; retaining all annotations gives 10,368. A changed retained relation produces a different account in the positive control. The construction shows what its specified deletion rule removes. It does not show that real ancient redaction followed that rule. [Complete synthetic construction](memory/run-v1/compression-demo.json).

The coordinator's separate model starts with a correct procedure and correct attribution and gives them different invented transition rules. Its primary scenario retains the reference procedure with probability 0.9891304 after twenty synthetic steps, while retaining the attribution with probability 0.5057646. All 180 declared sensitivity scenarios remain available, including stable attributions and unrepaired procedures. The model assumes that its repair check can return the reference operation. Real success can validate multiple workable variants, so an empirical study must define what its feedback distinguishes. [Protocol](coordinator/MODEL-PROTOCOL.md), [exact rational results](coordinator/run-v1/retention.json).

These examples establish logical compatibility. A coherent structure can have several histories, and preservation of a checkable procedure need not entail preservation of its origin label. Neither example produces literary maturity or estimates how much history a Vedic or Buddhist story retained. Literary sophistication receives its positive explanation from specialist learning, inherited repertoires, adaptation and collection; particular historical lineages require additional observations.

## 6. A discriminating research program

The next material study should select linked operations before comparing contexts. A bead pilot would examine drill-hole microstructure, abrasives, heat treatment, material supply and production waste across dated local phases. A farming pilot would join short-lived dated remains, weed ecologies, processing stages and recovery protocols. These comparisons can test selective practical transmission and convergence using the same observation units. [Four institutional test designs](institutions/TESTS.md).

The textual study should code role–action relations separately in assessed RV strata, later ritual prose and relevant Iranian controls. A parallel-recension study should compare specific Buddhist sequences before mapping them onto archaeological institutions. The memory study should freeze a rare-detail prediction on a discovery set and test it in an independent source or assemblage. A myth that selected the case cannot also count as its own independent validation. [Memory tests](memory/TESTS.md), [joint proposals](coordinator/DEVELOPED-PROPOSALS.md).

Support for a specific Harappan contribution would come from dated intermediates carrying an unusual operation bundle and from evidence that distinguishes alternative routes. Incompatible intermediate operations, late first appearances or an equally explanatory regional control would weaken that proposed lineage. The relevant contribution could be a skill, an organizational relationship or a narrative detail. Each should receive its own verdict.

## 7. Review and research status

Three parallel contributors owned the institutional, memory and independent-review directories. The coordinator integrated their work and developed the separate source control and retention model. Original endpoints and source witnesses remain preserved. The institutional filter correction retains both scripts and outputs; it changes candidate eligibility after textual removal rather than altering source observations or rice inputs. [Amendment](institutions/AMENDMENT-eligibility.md).

The independent review tests source scope, chronology, competing mechanisms and arithmetic. Its mathematical check uses independent recurrences and primitive event enumeration. Passing integrity or locator checks establishes the stated reproducibility scope; it does not authenticate a cultural lineage. The final [review](review/REVIEW.md) and [response](REVIEW-RESPONSE.md) record substantive findings and repairs.

The resulting thesis is selective inheritance combined with intellectual recombination. Early textual maturity can emerge from accumulated skills, specialist teaching, inherited poetic traditions and later institutional work. Some of those contributions may have Harappan or posturban carriers. The current program makes the strongest candidate components and missing transitions explicit, so the next observations can change particular historical claims.
