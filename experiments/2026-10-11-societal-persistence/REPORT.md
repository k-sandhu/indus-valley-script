# Minimum capacities for long societal persistence

11 October 2026. Reviewed exploratory theory, literature survey and Harappan application.

**Working theory:** persistence over a specified horizon requires continued access to the services needed at the stated size and function. Where starting assets and baseline outside supply cannot cover that demand, additional provision through renewal, replacement, acquisition or substitution becomes necessary. The combined operations must fit available resources and time, and have an arrangement through which people can perform them. Those arrangements can be distributed across households, specialists, public bodies and outside suppliers; their particular form needs its own evidence.

The relevant minimum functions are bodily provisioning, renewal of productive and infrastructural capacity, availability of competent participants, and allocation of the combined work. Buffers, substitution or relocation become requirements when the defined disturbances otherwise interrupt the chosen outcome. A city's requirements differ from those of a smaller settlement or a craft tradition. Population continuity, urban scale and continued knowledge should be measured separately.

The survey examines 25 primary works at recorded reading scopes; the separate Harappan application codes 34 observations from 14 works. It finds substantial precedents in economic reproduction, viability theory, maintenance, collective action and archaeological settlement persistence. The proposed contribution is a measurement procedure: specify what persisted, bound usable delivery from starting assets and outside supply, then test the remaining renewal demands together. The [survey](literature/SURVEY.md) and [predecessor assessment](literature/GAP-ASSESSMENT.md) state what the inspected literature already covers and which proposed measurements it does not supply.

## What the new work establishes

- **Present operation can coexist with an unavailable replacement route.** In the invented finite model, aging expertise makes indefinite continuation impossible at a budget that suffices when expertise does not age. Both services still operate at the endpoint before failure.
- **Average affordable work can conceal a deadline conflict.** Budget 2 exceeds the model's mean work demand of 4/3 per period, yet state `(1,1,1)` requires two repairs and training together. The shared period capacity cannot meet that combination.
- **Apparent alternatives can share a prerequisite.** Two providers for each service still have a single failure point when all depend on one input. Separate inputs change the minimum failure set under the stipulated graphs.
- **Longevity can imply additional provision only with independent bounds.** The accounting residual is informative when minimum demand exceeds all bounded baseline delivery. Unknown imports, surviving-asset output or occupation demand can leave it at zero.

The [numerical report](MODEL-RESULTS.md) contains 32 declared worlds, an independent 5,218-check reconstruction and two sensitivity analyses. The [adversarial review](review/REVIEW.md) supplies six counterexamples concerning horizon, shared work, information, participation, access and outside support. The examples have no archaeological calibration.

The review also led to a separate development: repair and training may be one operation. A post-baseline variant gives training zero incremental cost when paired with repair. It removes several replacement deficits under its invented law, with 16 worlds, 1,133 reviewer checks and a second 1,968-check reconstruction. This makes the relationship between ordinary work and learning a measurement target. It does not establish a historical training cost.

## What the Harappan sources add

Harappa's Mound E sequence records a clogged drain replaced by a culvert on a new route. Dholavira's Stage VI follows a reported brief desertion and combines selective wall retention for reservoirs with stone salvage and changed streets and housing. These are observations of renewal and reconfiguration. They give the theory cases to explain without requiring that the same urban form persisted throughout. The [evidence study](evidence/APPLICATION.md) retains exact source loci, dating units, shared field lineages and countercases.

The next empirical step is a source-linked reconstruction of one service sequence, followed by joint work-window tests where dates and resource bounds permit them. The present sources do not support a numerical Harappan renewal budget. [The study design](EMPIRICAL-PROTOCOL.md) states the required measurements, comparison population and stopping conditions.

Long social development can accumulate practical knowledge and obligations across many generations. This supplies a mechanism to investigate alongside the maturity of early Indian literature. A particular Harappan contribution to that literature still requires a traceable historical route; the [earlier continuity studies](../2026-10-11-early-indian-continuity/REPORT.md) preserve that separate question.
