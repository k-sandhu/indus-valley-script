# Survey retrospective

11 October 2026.

The assignment was to survey capacities supporting continued habitation, cities and exchange, and to identify comparisons that could develop a conditional persistence theory. The root protocol, knowledge navigation, registry, writing and redistribution rules, and relevant previous retrospectives supplied the scope. Work stayed within the literature directories. The coordinator and reviewer acquired several direct predecessors; those witnesses were reused with explicit hashes and selected independent readings.

The survey began with resilience, settlement duration, collective action and economic reproduction. It followed close primary references into learning and maintenance. Early reading showed that joint reproduction, present-state versus future viability, interdependent restoration scheduling and institutional adaptation already have substantial predecessors. This changed the contribution assessment: the useful proposal is an archaeological procedure joining independently bounded demand and supply to dated operations, shared resources and participation evidence. A broad claim that persistence or maintenance is neglected would be unsupported by this sample.

The first XML metadata extraction omitted author names because the witnesses inherit author status from their contributor group. A separate v2 extraction corrected that rule. The retained v1 and v2 paragraph lists are identical, and the audit checks their parent hashes and amendment lineage. This was a metadata repair, not a change to the authors' evidence.

The Sraffa scan is image-only. Selected pages were rendered and OCR helped locate the text; visual inspection supplied the edition and Chapter I reading. The title/copyright identify the 1963 Indian edition with copyright 1960. The original filename keeps the work's first-publication year. The derivative OCR remains uncorrected source material, and the ledger does not claim a whole-book reading.

The ledger and prose use the actual 15-author identity while preserving the original witness filename. The official Ostrom lecture's heading is *Beyond Markets and States: Polycentric Governance of Complex Economic Systems*. Its 2009 lecture and related 2010 journal article are distinguished.

## Capture and inspection limits

An attempt to serialize all retained web outputs in one Windows command exceeded the process command-length limit and wrote no file. Splitting the transfer into bounded chunks recovered the seven-response archive, whose JSON was then checked. The later Priß search rendering was saved separately. Search themes are documented, while the query log does not invent a complete exact invocation transcript where one was not serialized.

An early HTML inspection assumed UTF-8 and failed on a non-UTF-8 byte. Reading the original bytes through Beautiful Soup retained the witness encoding and allowed inspection. Some broad page-read outputs were truncated by the tool. Reading scopes therefore follow passages actually visible in bounded inspections; an acquired full document is never counted as fully read merely because extraction succeeded.

Jervis's 2026 conclusion explicitly named Priß's network study as a possible integration partner. This justified one limited primary follow-up. Its abstract, route reconstruction and metric assumptions were inspected, and the ledger states the page scope. No further general acquisitions followed. The count is 25 selected primary works and three limited leads, with 45 attributable observations.

## Outcome and verification

The survey identifies positive mechanisms for provisioning, material replacement, practical learning, participation and adaptation. Six proposed comparisons specify measures, rival explanations and rejection conditions; a seventh record supplies common chronology and observation controls. Author recommendations and our proposed extensions are stored separately.

The retained source audit passes 312 checks of hashes, witness lineage, IDs, scope declarations and ignored raw status. It also verifies that the author-extraction amendment leaves paragraph text intact. The Markdown wording scan had no flags at its recorded run. Verification concerns this package and its source boundaries; it does not validate the primary studies' statistics, prove archaeological continuity or estimate a society's minimum resources.

The inspected settlement-duration passages do not provide the joint output, demand and import bounds needed for the coordinator's minimum-renewal inference. That remains a prospective collection task. No supplementary datasets, ancient schedules or published numerical models were independently rerun in this branch. No field-wide omission or mathematical novelty is claimed.

All acquired third-party material and mechanical derivatives remain local-only. Authored summaries, code and metadata are separated from these witnesses in the manifest. No existing endpoint was overwritten, and this branch did not edit global navigation, publish, commit, push, or create CI/CD or automations.
