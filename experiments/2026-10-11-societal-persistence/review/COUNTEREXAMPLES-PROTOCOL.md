# Frozen laws for reviewer boundary examples

Written before calculations, 11 October 2026. Every quantity below is invented. These examples reject specific unrestricted implications; they do not refute the correctly bounded viability construction or estimate historical societies.

1. A stock state is a positive integer. Its only transition is x′ = x − 1; no renewal action exists. Initial x = 4. Check safety x ≥ 1 at every state for horizons 3 and 4. Finite persistence can be supported entirely by starting stock.
2. Two services each need state xᵢ ≥ 1. Each starts at 1, loses one unit per transition and can receive binary renewal uᵢ. Shared budget u₁ + u₂ ≤ 1. Check each separate service and the jointly safe successor. A budget of 2 is the positive control.
3. Two actions and two shocks have a safe successor precisely when their labels match. Compare ∃ action ∀ shock with ∀ shock ∃ action. This tests the effect of observing the current shock before choosing an action.
4. Two actors can each provide a shared repair. Feasible action profiles are (0,0), (1,0), (0,1). Service is supplied when either repairs. Each receives benefit 1 when service exists; its own repair costs utility 2. Enumerate pure generalized Nash equilibria using deviations that stay within the feasible profile set. A jointly feasible safe action need not be voluntarily implemented.
5. A fixed supply of two units can be allocated (0,2), (1,1) or (2,0). Aggregate service is safe when the sum is at least 2; resident safety requires each share at least 1. Compare the sets without assigning a moral weight or identifying them with all meanings of societal persistence.
6. A local stock starts at 1 and obeys x′ = x − 1 + m, with import m = 0 or 1. Imported service is funded by an external stock initially 3, decreasing by m; external stock must stay nonnegative. Enumerate safe local paths for horizons 3 and 4 with and without imports. A local provision boundary can hide eventual upstream exhaustion.

The script will enumerate the small spaces directly, export witnesses and state the exact implication each example can reject. It refuses an existing output path and records input hashes. The coordinator's model will be reviewed separately with an independent transition reconstruction after its law is available.
