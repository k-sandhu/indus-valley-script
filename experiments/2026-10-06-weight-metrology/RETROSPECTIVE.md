## Results and limits of the revised approaches

The final run gives 13.716 g/CV 1.591% for 90 certain intact weights of 5–100 g, and 14.280 g/CV 5.777% for 147 weights of 1.3–5 g. Two-unit fits improve AIC by 24.270–100.475 but include diffuse or boundary second components. Absolute-error broadening, a positive small-target offset and extra fractional levels also improve the pooled model. These comparisons expose model dependence and test candidate explanations; assigning the excess to manufacture, scale calibration or historical standards needs object measurements. Wear-admitting fits retain the central-band contrast.
