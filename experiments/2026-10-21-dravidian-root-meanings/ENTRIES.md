Three of Parpola's senses have no place in the dictionary as entry-and-gloss pairs: *vaṭam* "banyan", *piḷḷai* "squirrel" and *vēḷ* "Murukan". The first two are coded absent everywhere, as the protocol requires; VELGOD is coded through the Kāma sense of 5528, which the protocol's class includes. Two of the pun pairs fall in different etyma: "fish" (4885) and "star" (4876) are separate entries, as are "earring" (4979) and "youth" (4978), and "spindle" (1195) and "ray of light" (1193).
