## 1. The study in brief

Parpola reads the Indus fish sign through a Dravidian pun: *mīn* "fish" and *mīn* "star". The study asks with what probability each meaning his readings need was present at the root of the Dravidian family tree, and at its North Dravidian node, using the *Dravidian Etymological Dictionary* (DEDR, Burrow and Emeneau 1984) and the dated tree of Kolipakam and colleagues (*Royal Society Open Science* 5: 171504, 2018). Each meaning is coded on the 20 tips of that tree as present (1), absent (0) or missing, and reconstructed by parsimony and by a two-state maximum-likelihood model. Meanings nobody proposed, drawn from the same entries, give the reference.

5. **`recon.py`**: the reconstruction library.
   - Parsimony: most-parsimonious reconstruction sets at every node for a binary trait with missing tips, by Sankoff's algorithm with a downward and an upward pass and unit costs; the score at a node is 1, 0 or 0.5 as the protocol defines.
   - Mk1: likelihood by Felsenstein pruning with scaling, rate q estimated by maximum likelihood on the tree rescaled to root height 1, with log q bounded to [log 1e-6, log 1e3]; search a grid of at least 61 log-spaced values and refine the best interval with `scipy.optimize.minimize_scalar(method="bounded")`. Flat root prior. Marginal posterior state probabilities at every internal node through a downward pass (or exact rerooting). Return q, log-likelihood, a boundary flag, probabilities at all nodes, and the oldest node whose probability of state 1 exceeds 0.5 with its age.
   - Mk2 (sensitivity S2): separate gain and loss rates by maximum likelihood (L-BFGS-B on log rates over the same bounds, several starts), with a flat root prior and, separately, the stationary root prior; same outputs.
   - Missing tips have likelihood 1 for both states. A trait with fewer than two observed tips returns its results with a flag.

6. **`test_recon.py`** writing tracked `run1/selftest.json`. Tests with known answers:

   - brute-force enumeration of parsimony costs against the Sankoff sets on the same trees;
   - closed-form cases: a cherry with both tips 1; a trait with every tip missing (root probability 0.5); invariance of the ML root probability to multiplying all branch lengths by a constant;
   - planted patterns on a 20-tip tree with the topology and branch lengths of the parsed MCC tree and on random Yule trees: a state present only in one young clade (root probability near 0), present in tips of every major group (near 1);
