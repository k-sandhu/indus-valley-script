# Coordinator amendment: order of freezing

Recorded 13 October 2026, after Shot B's confirmation outcome and before Shot A's predictions were reviewed or frozen.

The design let each shot freeze and fire on its own schedule. Shot B froze and read the confirmation half first. Its outcome, including the post-outcome finding that title and determinative signs are more site-concentrated than name signs on confirmation seals, is now known to the coordinator. Shot A's draft includes a related prediction, that opening signs are more specific to site than closing units, on the same confirmation half.

To keep Shot A's test fresh, the coordinator does not pass Shot B's outcome to the Shot A agent and makes no change to Shot A's site prediction or its population, statistic, null or pass rule. Any coordinator change to Shot A's other predictions is recorded with its reason in Shot A's `PREDICTIONS.md` before freezing. Shot A's report states that its coordinator review took place after Shot B's outcome.

For later shots that share a confirmation half, every shot freezes its predictions before any of them reads it.
