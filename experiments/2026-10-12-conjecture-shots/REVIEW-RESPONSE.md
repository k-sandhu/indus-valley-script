## Rules for the next shots

1. Every shot that shares a confirmation half freezes its predictions before any of them reads it.
2. Predictions that share objects carry a joint credence rule.
3. Pass rules for small groups include an influence criterion: a rank statistic or a leave-one-out condition.
4. Before freezing, measure the split-half reliability of each per-sign or per-object measure on the discovery half, and plant effects of plausible size, not only large ones, to state power.
5. Site tests exclude Mohenjo-daro or stratify by site pair, and estimate the variance across re-splits before freezing.
6. Shots that withhold site strip CISI identifiers and record-order fields that track site.
7. The confirmation half of the October 12 split is spent for seal size, site and motif statistics. New tests of those use new material or a new partition of objects not yet used for them.
