# Publication note

13 October 2026. When this package was first published, the files that give precise locations of candidate anomalies, reconnaissance prospects and review targets were kept out of the public repository, so that the repository does not point to possible unrecorded archaeological remains. The withheld paths are listed in `maintenance/2026-10-11-public-release/public-omissions.json` and in `.gitignore`; their original bytes remain in the local working tree. Published locations of known sites, the Natural Earth basemaps and the satellite scene footprints remain tracked. The atlas viewer therefore runs only in a local checkout that holds the withheld data files. Requests for the precise geometry for research or heritage protection can be raised through the repository's issues.
