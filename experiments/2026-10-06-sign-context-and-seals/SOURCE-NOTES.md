# Source and measurement notes

Status: Exploratory source review, 7 October 2026. These notes were added after the frozen analysis. They leave [site-table.json](site-table.json), [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md) and the first run unchanged.

The frozen Chanhu-daro row locates Vidale's section as “Chanhu Daro.” The actual heading in the acquired Treccani page is **Chanu daro**, followed by Massimo Vidale's name. Its first paragraph describes a visible surface of approximately 200 by 150 metres and says lower architecture may extend farther. The rectangle's product is 3 hectares; it measures a visible bounding rectangle rather than the occupied settlement area. Kenoyer's settlement-hierarchy paragraph elsewhere on that page gives 6 hectares, the frozen reference. Both definitions should accompany any later revision. Vidale's section describes concentrated bead, shell and seal production; its urban description also shows that the package's single functional category simplifies a mixed settlement. Source K2005, these two exact sections, is recorded in [sources.json](sources.json).

Kenoyer's 1997 printed Table 4.2 gives open lower bounds for Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Rakhigarhi and Ganweriwala. The numerical anchors used in the model therefore have no demonstrated upper limits. Mohenjo-daro's published numerical anchors in this compilation span 100 to 250 hectares, with 200 in adjacent Kenoyer prose and the 2005 hierarchy. Suryanarayan's Table 4.1 footnote questions the calculation criteria for Rakhigarhi and Farmana. This compilation has not verified a contemporary occupied area or every later published estimate. Exact locations and qualifiers are in `site-table.json.sites[].sources`.
