Frozen before the new source inspection and consolidation on 26 October 2026. This is metadata consolidation and source review. It targets physical identity, source coverage and archaeological context; it submits no reading, meaning map, segmentation or new functional conjecture. Bench scoring is inapplicable. Earlier exposures remain exposures.

These are twenty target records, not twenty newly identified physical objects. Candidate identities remain unmerged until a source-qualified bridge is demonstrated.

Three gpt-6.1-sol workers in the Codex collaboration harness own separate packages: `2026-10-26-object-pilot-register`, `2026-10-26-image-recovery`, and `2026-10-26-source-conflicts`. It retains missing fields, source dependence and contrary values. The image worker inventories the 284 anonymous crops and the broader unmapped set, prioritises the fixed frame and missing-face queue, and checks a bounded set of candidates against source pages. Its own protocol must freeze the inspection bound before reading pixels. The conflict worker checks the Lothal CLXI F identity and Harappa 7458/7158 discrepancy, preserving both witnesses and specifying the record needed to decide any unresolved case. New searches require a changed route and bounded acquisition recorded before outcomes.

Code, protocols, summaries, aggregate counts and manifests may stay in the checkout. Existing files and frozen endpoints are preserved. Checks cover fixed membership, provenance and page coordinates, candidate versus confirmed status, explicit missing fields, conflict retention, existing source hashes, D: storage, local link targets and accounting of every claimed gain. An exact file or image duplicate does not by itself identify the physical object. The new image coverage counts must distinguish indexed filenames, verified image-to-catalogue links and newly imaged pilot targets.

Completion requires the twenty-record catalogue, a ranked missing-evidence queue, the three contribution records and a separate review. A zero-gain source search is a valid recorded outcome. Unresolved target identities are not a reason to invent a merge or keep searching without a new premise.
