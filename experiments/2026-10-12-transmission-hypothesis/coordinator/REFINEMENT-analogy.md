# Practical operations as conceptual resources

12 October 2026. Exploratory refinement after reading the new fixed passage frame. It does not expand the candidate families or add a hymn. The original selection and earlier theory draft remain part of the record.

The textual contributor's observation `TX-RV10.71.2` identifies a concrete relation: fashioning speech is compared with sifting meal, and companions recognize affiliation through speech. This gives an observable practical-to-conceptual association at the textual endpoint. The coordinator's initial argument proposed that working knowledge could enter organized speech; this verse supplies a more specific case to develop.

The new distinction is between incorporation into a text and descent from a particular donor. An operation can supply a model for thinking about composition, refinement or competence without being described as a manual. The analogy is directly present in the selected text. Its source community, earlier verbal history and Harappan contribution remain separate historical questions.

The comparison will therefore record the represented operation, the conceptual relation, the surrounding learned-speech context and the competing origins of the image. A possible material comparison must match sifting or separation rather than substitute a generic quern for a sieve. The motivating stanza is discovery evidence; a future test needs an independently fixed passage set and comparative controls.

A transmission interpretation would gain support from a distinctive inherited metaphor or operation bundle with independent intermediate evidence. The simpler incorporation claim concerns what the poem does with a recognizable practical operation in its own setting. It can be supported while the donor route remains unresolved.
