# Source-linked transmission and rival audit

12 October 2026. Frozen before coordinator comparison coding. The October 11 findings and motivating candidates are already known. This is a new exploratory historical audit, not a confirmatory test of a randomly sampled corpus.

## Purpose

The audit asks what the selected observations warrant at each transition. It does not assign ancestry by counting similarities. Its input records join a named feature to source observations, state the proposition being tested and describe what each rival predicts. The recorded outcome can support an endpoint or an intermediate practice while leaving its contribution to literature unresolved.

The initial coordinator candidate families are agriculture and water-related operations, hard-stone manufacture, ceramic production, specialist occupations, and poetic or ritual learning. Intermediate material evidence and new textual loci are coded in separate contributor packages. A comparison can conclude that a proposed cross-domain feature is too coarse or mismatched to be tested. New families require a separate post-inspection addition record.

## Inputs and decisions

Each comparison has a stable ID, a candidate family, an explicit proposition, cited observation IDs, chronology and dependency notes, a sequence of proposed transitions, and five rival records. R1–R5 are defined in the root protocol and may contribute to one component together. A rival record contains a predicted observation, the actual inspected observation, its evidential relation and the remaining missing discriminator. The allowed relations are `positive_observation`, `compatible_only`, `counter_observation_under_stated_assumption`, and `unresolved`. These are descriptions of the comparison, not an ordinal score.

Each proposed transition names its source and destination, the particular feature, its evidence type and whether it is directly observed, an attributed inference or unresolved. `material_operation`, `textual_operation`, `comparative_reconstruction`, `learning_relation`, `population_opportunity` and `later_reorganization` are distinct types. Population opportunity alone cannot establish a feature-specific teaching or literary-descent transition. A material technique at both endpoints is compatible with several paths until the operational match and alternatives are assessed.

The coordinator audit will verify that all cited IDs exist, that assertions about an endpoint cite an appropriate observation, and that a claim of traced local literary descent has no unresolved transition and states its rival-discrimination basis. It will separately list missing observations. Unknown is preserved rather than converted to absence. An observation that weakens a necessity claim does not automatically exclude a possible contribution.

The actual historical comparisons use exact source observations. A rule check can flag malformed or unsupported records; passing that check validates joins and declared requirements. It is not an automated historical verdict. Comparative philology remains an inferential method with its own premises; the audit will not treat lack of an excavated teacher as a refutation of a reconstructed Indo-Iranian verbal inheritance.

## Inference controls

Before reading the coordinator result, the reviewer should challenge six possible overclaims:

1. A material practice continues, therefore a later textual institution descends from it.
2. Populations mix, therefore they transmit a specified practice or institution.
3. A later text uses a contact word, therefore its practice came from a named Harappan donor.
4. Iranian or Indo-European comparison is available, therefore every local contribution is excluded.
5. A later explanation reorganizes an earlier repertoire, therefore all its components are late inventions.
6. Many cited versions agree, therefore the number of independent observations increases.

These controls test the proposition's scope. They can be checked by source-qualified contrasts or by expressly hypothetical countercases. They do not add empirical observations to the historical sample.

## Preservation and interpretation

Input ledgers, code and protocol hashes will accompany the first audit output. It will refuse an existing output directory. Any correction changes code and results in a new version, retaining the failed or superseded run and explaining the externally observable defect. A separate reviewer will reconstruct source joins and examine the substantive interpretation.

The report will distinguish positive findings, compatible historical hypotheses and proposed new measurements. Existing comparative, transmission and cultural-learning literature must delimit the contribution. No universal novelty, decipherment or numerical historical likelihood claim will be made.
