# Rival-route review protocol

12 October 2026. Frozen before this study's source acquisition and control results. The earlier continuity and persistence studies are available background; their rice, retention, script and renewal calculations will not be repeated.

The review asks whether mature early Indian knowledge and particular Harappan-to-textual connections distinguish a transmission route. The control frame tests shared Indo-Iranian institutions, chronological and genre selection, later textual synthesis, population ancestry, and convergence. It will retain exact textual strata and distinguish composition, collection or redaction, physical witness, modern edition and narrated time.

## Bounded primary-source frame

Target six authored scholarly works or editions, with selected passages and exact access depth recorded. These are purposive controls, not a sample for estimating how common a feature was. An unavailable target remains recorded; a replacement requires a separately dated amendment.

1. Jamison and Brereton, *The Rigveda* (2014), introduction on inherited poet/priest patronage and the collection. Reuse the exact earlier witness if available.
2. Skjærvø, *Introduction to Old Avestan* or its author-issued Old Avestan teaching edition, for a source-language institutional comparator and its translation. Confirm edition identity and date from the actual witness.
3. Boyce, “HAOMA ii. The Rituals” (2003), for inherited ritual elements and the separation of Old Avestan, later Avestan and modern practices. Reuse the prior witness if available.
4. Narasimhan and colleagues, “The formation of human populations in South and Central Asia” (2019), for what the sampled demographic histories establish and what they do not identify about a particular taught practice.
5. Witzel, “The Development of the Vedic Canon and its Schools: The Social and Political Milieu” (1997), for textual collection, schools and attributed mechanisms of later synthesis.
6. Pollock, “The Theory of Practice and the Practice of Theory in Indian Intellectual History” (1985 or the date verified on the witness), for the relation between prescriptive texts and practice. Its later examples will remain controls on inference rather than direct evidence of the earliest Vedic society.

The review will use only actual inspected primary scholarly passages and text editions for substantive source claims. Public access does not establish redistribution rights. Downloads, full extraction and rendered pages stay in the owned local raw directory, with inherited witnesses referenced at their existing paths and hashes. Source records will retain URL, retrieval time, bytes, hashes, edition metadata, inspected loci, rights status and unsuccessful attempts.

## Review endpoints and decision rules

An observation will record the feature, witness and stratum; the author's explanation; the route alternatives it bears on; and any unresolved dating or identification premise. A generic role, possible contact, demographic ancestry or successful physical operation cannot by itself serve as evidence for the transfer of a specific feature.

For an asserted route, ask whether the carrier, feature-specific transfer and dated intermediate are observed or assumed. An allowed typed path establishes compatibility under its rules. Eliminating every competing path requires an explicit alternative-route and missing-evidence assumption. Presence in two descendants can follow shared inheritance, borrowing, convergence or later synthesis; shared manuscripts and source reports count as one lineage where applicable.

The reviewer will develop exact counterexamples if the coordinator's proposed procedure warrants them. Each new calculation will have a separate protocol and fresh output directory before its own results. No arbitrary evidence scores or posterior probabilities will be assigned. Any hypothetical feature masks, carriers or censoring laws will be marked invented and kept separate from the documentary control frame.

Deliver a source ledger, observation ledger, `REVIEW.md`, `FALSIFIERS.md`, a retrospective, changed-path inventory and source-integrity checks. Once the integrated draft is ready, record a final substantive assessment and hashes for the reviewed files in a new endpoint. Previous frozen protocols, sources and outputs will remain unchanged.
