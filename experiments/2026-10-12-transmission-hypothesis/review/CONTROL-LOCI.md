# Prespecified Old Avestan control loci

12 October 2026. These locators were selected before opening the new Old Avestan witness. They are an institutional and teaching control, not a frequency or comparative-rarity sample.

Inspect Yasna 33.6 for the ritual-priest role; Yasna 45.1 for addressed listeners and instruction; Yasna 31.9–11 for cow, cultivator and choice language; and Yasna 46.14 for Vištāspa and the relation between teaching and support. Confirm the actual wording, translation, paragraph divisions and scholarly interpretation in the selected edition. Keep figurative, divine and normative relations distinct from demonstrated human staffing or agricultural operations.

The comparator asks whether a mature role or conceptual relation in early Indian literature already has a plausible Indo-Iranian antecedent or analog. A cognate name and inherited role can weaken a specifically Harappan source claim; similarities in broad teaching or cultivation language alone cannot identify ancestry. The source's uncertainty in dating the Gāthās will be retained. Later Avestan ritual detail will not be assigned to these Old Avestan passages.
