# Discriminating the component routes

12 October 2026. Prospective tests following the exploratory source reading. These are rejection conditions for particular reconstructions, not a completed comparative sample.

The test unit is one feature or linked relation at a stated textual or production stage. Freeze its description and working alternatives before choosing favorable examples. Preserve context resolution, source lineages and actual recovery. A missing attestation can constrain a route only when the relevant corpus or material recovery makes its absence informative. Words, procedures, participant relations and later explanations have separate histories; several routes can contribute to one result.

| ID | Proposition to test | Observation that could change the conclusion | Required control and feasible first step |
|---|---|---|---|
| F01 | A particular operation continued through local post-Harappan work | Finished objects prove curated or imported; associated working evidence disappears under contextual reassociation | Reconcile accessions, working stage, waste, tools and deposition. Begin with BPR-36 and the separate BPR-208 collision; preserve one record per accession. |
| F02 | A distinct technical sequence was transmitted across successive production contexts | Material/task-matched experiments or independently derived controls repeatedly produce the same alleged signature | Prespecify nonobvious choices and alternative workable solutions. Analyze the whole sequence and its dependent steps, not products of separate rarity estimates. |
| F03 | A population-contact scenario supplied the relevant carrier | Specific practitioners or production contexts show a different immediate source, or the proposed transfer postdates the represented feature | Treat demographic opportunity separately. Seek sourcing, working-context co-occurrence and dated operation changes before attaching a teaching edge. |
| F04 | A contact word identifies a particular technical donor | Earlier recipient use, multiple plausible donor languages, or a term unattached to the proposed operation survives source-language checking | Freeze lemmas, senses and strata. Compare phonological inheritance, loan hypotheses and actual technical referents. A donor society cannot be supplied from a generic substrate label. |
| F05 | An inherited poetic role explains a specified institutional component | The allegedly shared feature differs in function, chronology or distribution once complete primary contexts are compared | Compare Old Avestan and Rigvedic role-action relations under explicit philological premises. Keep inherited formula, office, evaluation and local technical content separate. |
| F06 | Grain-sifting imagery in RV 10.71.2 preserves a particular earlier process | The description recovers only a widely available purpose, or matched traditions use the same conceptual image without the candidate toolchain | The present positive endpoint is a represented analogy. Test a complete episode and controls at the same genre/stratum; do not infer a worker-to-poet apprenticeship from the analogy alone. |
| F07 | Later textual synthesis created a mature combination from received components | The proposed earlier component has no verified earlier locus, or the supposed late organization is already present in an independently supported older stratum | Compare exact verbal relations and recensional organization, distinguishing composition from collection. Reuse of older material and creation of a new frame can occur together. |
| F08 | A route is favored because a relation bundle is unusually rare | A fixed, task- and genre-matched control frame shows the same bundle broadly, or its apparent rarity disappears after shared-source and preservation controls | The current purposive frame cannot estimate rarity. Build a new comparison corpus with complete units, negative cases, source lineages and missingness before any rate or likelihood. |
| F09 | A validated record establishes a historical transmission | A deliberately irrelevant source ID and invented edge tags pass the same declaration validator | The present fixture already demonstrates this limit. Maintain manual source support and rival review as separate requirements from software schema checks. |

The imaginative extension is to test whether a verbal specialist tradition preserved its inherited office while adopting practical relations from another repertoire. That mixed history predicts different comparative alignments for office/formula, operation, terminology and explanation. It is weakened when the proposed distinctive operation is absent from the alleged donor contexts or already belongs to an independently supported recipient tradition. It remains unresolved when only generic work imagery or demographic contact is observed.

A useful first study would code practical comparisons throughout a fixed textual stratum and a matched Iranian or Greek corpus, with whole episodes as units. It would record the depicted operation, the intellectual relation it models, and the passage’s ritual or social setting. Such a study tests conceptual reuse and its distribution. A second, separate study would ask whether an unusual operation signature links to independently dated material intermediates. The new source-based results justify these two questions while keeping their outcomes distinct.
