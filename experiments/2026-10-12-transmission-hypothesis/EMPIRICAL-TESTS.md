# Tests of practical transmission and literary incorporation

12 October 2026. Prospective designs developed after exposure to the motivating cases. Discovery observations in this package cannot also serve as independent validation.

The comparison unit is a named operation or organizing relationship. Define it before collecting a new test set. Record dates, recovery and observation lineage independently of the proposed donor. Separate local manufacture, continuity of an operation and incorporation into a later text; a study may answer one of these without answering all three.

## 1. Follow replacement operations in a production sequence

Select independently dated production contexts spanning urban and later settlements. Before coding the target sequence, use experiments and known-history controls to identify tool preparation, use, replacement and failure traces that separate workable techniques. Record unfinished objects, local waste composition, tools and deposits. Match comparison assemblages for raw material, intended product and recovery conditions.

The local-retention prediction is persistence of distinctive operational choices through successive working contexts. The imported-finished-object prediction permits products without corresponding local production traces; imports of roughouts or scrap require their own alternative. Regional transfer permits local production with an externally supplied technique. Constrained reinvention predicts that repeated choices follow the material and task even in independently established traditions.

Compare the linked choices as a bundle with measured dependence. A drill, abrasive and characteristic hole may all arise from one physical constraint. Their co-occurrence is not three independent observations of transmission. A preserved local production sequence can weaken a narrow finished-import account while leaving the teaching route unresolved.

A curated container requires manufacture and deposition to be dated separately. The test should stop an intermediate-production claim if the chronology rests entirely on the container or if the assemblage demonstrably mixes phases without further manufacturing-context evidence. The present mixed bead-pot case motivates this control and belongs to the discovery set.

## 2. Join agricultural acts to recovery-controlled material contexts

Freeze a textual operation bundle at its exact locus and genre. Distinguish coordinated acts in a hymn from an agricultural manual. Separately select intermediate fields, processing deposits or storage contexts using archaeological criteria. Code processing stage, species identification, weed ecology, sampling volumes and preservation. Directly date selected grains where possible while retaining the relationship between dated grain and the proposed operation.

The local-practice hypothesis predicts a material sequence and its operative requirements before the relevant textual stage. The wider-regional hypothesis predicts an alternative earlier or contemporaneous operational distribution. The convergence hypothesis concerns the available practical solutions under the same environment and inputs. A shared grain name or a crop list cannot select among these routes.

A rotation claim requires evidence about succession in the same fields; a mixed crop deposit could represent separate fields, storage, consumption or accumulated use. Chaff impressions in clay require a control for source sediment and wild plants before they are interpreted as cultivation or intentional temper. These observations should be coded without knowledge of which route the author hopes to support.

The cross-domain join is strengthened by a distinctive combination of operations rather than by modern identification of a general season or a single tool. It remains unresolved when textual or material resolution cannot distinguish the proposed alternatives.

## 3. Test the recoding of practical knowledge

Select independently documented transfers between communities whose working practices and terminology can both be observed. A modern or historical control validates the measurement; it does not date an ancient Indian route. Code operations, terms, teaching relationships and explanatory accounts separately, with assessors unaware of the proposed donor where feasible.

The recoding prediction is that an adopted operation can retain donor-associated choices while acquiring recipient-associated terminology or explanation. Its rivals include a word borrowed without the operation, a technique independently reconstructed under common constraints and complete inherited packages. Specify the distinctive choices and known contact before looking for the expected mismatch.

If neither the operational sequence nor its teaching path aligns with the proposed donor, the recoding claim fails for that control. If several donor traditions supply the same observations, the result supports transfer or compatibility at the available resolution rather than a unique donor. Apply a validated comparison to ancient contexts only when its assumptions are independently justified there.

## 4. Test the relation between working knowledge and specialist speech

Create a fixed textual frame that includes complete hymns or episodes and adverse context. Code demonstrated roles, selection or correction, differentiated contributions and technical actions separately from narrator judgments. Include comparative Indo-Iranian or Indo-European forms, later classifications and ordinary practical constraints. Retain the composition/stratum, collection, physical witness and modern edition as separate dates.

The learning-institution hypothesis predicts repeated relations between competence, membership, performance and differentiated work. A comparative inherited explanation predicts specific shared constructions rather than only similar activities. A later-synthesis explanation predicts an observable reorganization at a later textual stage. A local material contribution requires its own feature-specific join to the verbal repertoire.

A prospective comparison should distinguish content represented by a text from a profession's observed prevalence or a formal school. Normative criticism of an occupation attests that classification in the text; it need not describe all practitioners. Silent technical detail can reflect genre, so absence becomes counterevidence only under a declared coverage premise.

## Decision record

For every test, preserve the motivating observation, selection rule, rival predictions, dates, dependencies, code and first output. Report positive intermediate or endpoint results even when a more ambitious join remains unresolved. A later refinement receives a new protocol and version. None of these designs assumes that all early literature or all post-Harappan communities share one transmission route.
