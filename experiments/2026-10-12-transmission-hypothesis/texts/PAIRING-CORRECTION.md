# Greek translation pairing correction

12 October 2026. The selected control remains Odyssey 17.382–386.

The first extraction selected the first translation card numbered 380 in the entire English TEI. Card numbers repeat across books, so its Greek lines from book 17 were incorrectly paired with a translation from book 15. The source language selection and the earlier manual inspection of the correct book 17 translation were unaffected. The erroneous derivative is excluded from the comparison endpoint.

The successor extractor first restricts the English text to book 17, then selects its card beginning at line 380. The original script and `paired-passages-v1.json` remain. `paired-passages-v2.json` records the correction and its parent hash. A separate audit will require both the book-qualified locator and the occupational words, rather than merely checking that a card exists.
