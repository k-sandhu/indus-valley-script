## Rival coding

The coordinator's five routes can combine across components:

| Route | Evidence that would favor a specific contribution | Observation that is insufficient on its own |
|---|---|---|
| R1: local post-Harappan transmission | An unusual operation/role bundle with dated local intermediate carriers and a textual counterpart | Generic farming, occupations, urban scale or an uncertain substrate word |
| R2: inherited Indo-Iranian/Indo-European repertoire | Source-language cognacy, shared formal construction or a diagnostic inherited ritual/role relation under a comparative argument | A broadly similar useful operation across traditions |
| R3: wider regional contact | A dated contact route and distinctive shared operation or lexical component with geographically discriminating distribution | Contact in general or an unidentified donor language |
| R4: practical convergence or reconstruction | Common task constraints readily producing the observed operations, including available substitute methods | Assuming that a useful practice was independently invented without evidence |
| R5: later specialist synthesis | Layer/genre evidence that older components were coordinated, classified or reinterpreted in the attested text | Late placement alone as a date for the first existence of every component |

Code each stanza first for what it explicitly says, then for author interpretation and the institution inferred from it. Features include named work/objects, ordered or coordinated operations, skill/teaching, authorization/selection, human versus divine attribution, and literal versus ritual/metaphorical function. Record ambiguous cases rather than converting them to negative observations.

For each proposed bundle, report every rival as favored-for-a-component, compatible, conditionally-weakened, unresolved, or excluded-by-specific-evidence. These are documentary categories, not numerical likelihoods. Favoring an inherited lexeme does not classify every practice in its hymn. A missing material carrier leaves the R1 join unresolved rather than refuted. Duplicate editions do not create independent ancient witnesses.
