# Prospective discrimination tests

12 October 2026. These designs follow the exploratory observations. None has been run.

## Practical relations used as conceptual models

Select complete passages from a fixed, genre-matched textual frame before coding the favored mechanism. The present RV 10.71.2 analogy is a motivating case and should be excluded from confirmatory outcomes. Include comparison traditions and source-language checks rather than constructing matches from one English translator's terminology.

Code the practical input, action, retained result and success criterion alongside their conceptual counterparts. Mark explicit comparisons, ambiguous imagery and a commentator's proposed interpretation separately. Ask whether an unusual relation survives in the conceptual use, and whether a documented earlier poetic formula can explain it. Broad sifting, fashioning or measuring alone can arise from common practical experience.

A claim of a specifically local contribution weakens if an independently supported external repertoire already contains its distinctive relation before the proposed contact. A claim that the analogy preserves a particular operation weakens if its supposed relation appears only after translation or later commentary. Better evidence for local incorporation would require an intermediate practice and contact opportunity with sufficient chronological and regional specificity. The wider cognitive mechanism can remain plausible under several donor routes.

## Rare practical bundles across material and text

Begin with a defined operation, not an occupation name. A grain-processing test needs residues, use-wear and waste capable of identifying what was processed, as well as the order and co-use of tools. The Bhagwanpura querns motivate this work but their present context has no recovered grain. Hulas husk inclusions and the Jognakhera mass motivate material-handling studies; they do not presently determine the complete processing sequence.

For the well imagery, declare the proposed physical arrangements and their observable consequences before inspecting candidate installations. Stone-wheel terminology, wooden receptacles and straps do not determine a single mechanism. A meaningful match would combine unusual design and dependency features in an independently dated context. A contradictory combination, a different function or date uncertainty wider than the proposed transition should stop that particular join.

Compare surrounding regions and earlier external repertoires using equivalent preservation and recovery. Rare local manufacture, imports, curated artifacts and independent reconstruction need distinct predictions. A matching mature and intermediate assemblage establishes endpoints; evidence for a carrier or a distinctive retained relation is still needed to link them to the text.

## Vocabulary and practice under language change

Freeze the technical terms and semantic operations separately. The present *bīja* addition is exploratory and cannot become its own confirmation set. Use philological assessments with explicit sound correspondences, donor alternatives and attestation layers. Keep absent terms within the defined frame and record semantic relatives such as pasture-*yavasa* without converting them into grain-*yava*.

Test whether a distinctive operation relation persists while its name changes across independently supported stages. If only a word is shared, investigate borrowing, inherited vocabulary and changed meaning before inferring a technical route. If the operation is generic, lexical contact can remain established without identifying a Harappan donor. A claim to a named donor language should stop when the donor is an inferred unknown substrate rather than independently attested language material.

## Participation, permission and teaching

Distinguish a depicted peer group from a documented institution. Evidence for teaching would include repeated learner/practitioner associations, graded work or explicit instruction linked to a practical setting. Evidence for recognition and permission could include independently attributable admission, performance, support or exclusion relations. The selected hymns supply portrayals; DN 1 supplies a normative list.

Compare knowledge availability with authorized use. The incorporation hypothesis predicts that a recipient group can retain or recognize an art while changing whether its members may practice it. Test this through independently related textual branches or dated settings, retaining source dependence. A moral condemnation alone cannot establish that a technique was performed at a particular population frequency.

All tests need declared time and place, source lineages, alternatives and stopping conditions. A date on a grain, a digital manuscript excerpt or a narrated ancestor belongs to its own object. No numerical likelihood is justified until an empirical reference model and a suitable observation frame have been independently supplied.
