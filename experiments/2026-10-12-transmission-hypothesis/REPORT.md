# What could survive the Indus cities and enter early literature?

12 October 2026. Source-based rival comparisons, developed theory and independent adversarial review.

**The developed hypothesis is selective survival followed by recoding and intellectual combination.** Working communities could retain practical skills; practitioners could transfer them through co-work, households, exchange and teaching; later verbal specialists could represent those skills in a different terminology and explanation. The operation, its organizing relationships, its words and its conceptual use need separate histories.

This continuation adds new intermediate archaeological loci, a complete 27-stanza Rigvedic frame, bounded Iranian/Greek/Pali comparisons and independent controls. It preserves the earlier studies and their endpoints. The [seven manual comparisons](coordinator/comparisons-v1.json) test five compatible routes: local post-Harappan transmission, inherited Indo-Iranian or Indo-European repertoires, regional contact, practical reconstruction and later literary organization.

## Tests that changed the interpretation

The apparent Late Harappan bead-workshop bridge became weaker after the collection description showed that the pot contains objects from several occupation phases. Deposition no longer supplies a manufacture date. Bhagwanpura's unfinished bead is a separate, source-qualified intermediate candidate. Its BPR-208 catalogue conflict is retained as one accession, not two objects.

The grain comparison distinguishes dated specimens from continuous farming. Jognakhera's rice and Vigna ranges establish particular second-millennium endpoints at the source's probability scope. The inspected date table does not supply each specimen's layer join. Crop co-presence is insufficient for rotation in one field. Hulas rice impressions likewise need formation and recovery controls.

The complete textual frame prevents a manual from being extracted from selected poetic lines. RV 10.101 places agricultural and water imagery alongside sacrifice, Soma and a well without digging. RV 9.112's occupations share a broader comparison of desires. These texts can recognize practical relations without specifying the manufacturing sequence needed for a material lineage.

The independent Indo-Iranian controls change the institution question. Shared professional verbal and ritual relationships give some components an available ancestral explanation. They challenge a uniquely local necessity claim while allowing local practical contributions. The most concrete positive incorporation result is the sifting/speech analogy, developed as an explicit post-reading refinement within the fixed hymn frame.

## What this means for early literature's maturity

The strongest plausible practical legacies are locally useful ways of handling materials, crops, tools and recurring work. Their carriers could include households, makers, working groups and people who teach while doing. A later professional repertoire can draw on those contributions and an inherited verbal tradition together. It can also create new comparisons, classifications and explanations.

The sifting example shows one way accumulated practical experience can shape an intellectually mature poem. Its representation is observable without identifying a Harappan donor. The theory therefore advances from a broad continuity proposal to particular transmission objects, transitions and rival tests.
