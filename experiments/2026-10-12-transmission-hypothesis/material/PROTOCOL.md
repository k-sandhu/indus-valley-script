# Practical transmission: material candidate and rival rules

12 October 2026. Frozen before acquiring and coding the new primary witnesses. Earlier packages and discovery snippets exposed broad candidate claims; this is a purposive documentary comparison, not an outcome-blind or statistically preregistered historical test.

The question is which practical operations could continue after Indus urban contraction and supply knowledge to later Indian communities. The material study identifies intermediate production and cultivation evidence before attempting a textual join. It preserves earlier rice, chronology, bead and service-renewal outputs and seeks new source loci or genuinely different observations.

## Fixed candidate families

| ID | Candidate and operation bundle | Material observation needed for a stronger comparison |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Hard-stone bead making: selecting or treating material, roughing, drilling, grinding, tool replacement or reuse | Intermediate contexts with roughouts, unfinished products, manufacturing waste, tools and experimentally or microscopically supported operation order |
| C2 | Ceramic knowledge: preparing clay and temper, forming and joining, finishing, controlling firing | Production contexts or technological observations across Late Harappan and later assemblages, distinct from shared vessel shapes or merely co-deposited wares |
| C3 | Crop-processing and ecological knowledge: cleaning and sorting, storing, managing crop/weed and seasonal relations, carrying out linked agricultural tasks | Contextual processing residues and methods capable of separating cultivation, cleaning, storage, consumption and deposition, with intermediate dates and recovery controls |

Primary leads are Hulas, Bhagwanpura and Sanghol field reports, together with a reported Late Harappan Harappa bead-pot context. A site or publication added later can test a fixed candidate; a newly introduced candidate family must be labeled exploratory. A textual counterpart, if used, must be independently selected by the textual contributor rather than matched to whichever material observation looks favorable.

## Fixed rivals and comparison vocabulary

Each observation receives `source_observation`, `author_interpretation`, `current_inference`, `dating_kind`, `context`, `reading_depth`, `witness_hash` and `lineage`. Excavation observations, modern experiments and source-author explanations remain separate. Finished objects, processing stages, local manufacture, succession of operations and actual teaching are distinct claims. Pottery-period labels identify archaeological classifications; they do not assign language, ethnicity or a named historical population.

The rivals are local practical transmission (L), imported or curated objects/foods (I), regional contact or movement of skilled practitioners (R), and independent reconstruction under similar constraints (N). Regional transfer can produce local manufacture; local manufacture alone therefore does not choose L over R. Imported roughouts or scrap can also occur in a workshop, and old objects can enter later deposits. Manufacturing context, waste composition and dated operation relationships matter to the imported-finished-object account.

Use a qualitative comparison with four permitted statuses: `compatible`, `less_adequate_for_this_observation`, `unresolved`, and `contradicted_at_stated_scope`. The last requires an explicit observation inconsistent with a narrow rival's stated prediction; it cannot be applied to a broad route merely because another route has suggestive evidence. No numerical likelihood, necessity score or historical posterior is assigned. Unknown rarity and missing intermediates remain unknown.

For every candidate report what is established, which narrow rival is weakened, which broad routes still explain the observation, what intermediate or carrier is missing, and the next observation that would change the comparison. A knowledge-transmission claim needs a chronology and an operation bundle capable of discriminating its rivals. An independently dated local working stage can establish intermediate practice without demonstrating an uninterrupted local teaching chain or a Harappan-to-textual origin.

## Chronology, scope and endpoint

Keep excavation season, publication, stratigraphic ordering, cultural-period assignment, radiocarbon/other physical dates, object manufacture and deposition separate. Reoccupation and coarse phase ranges cannot resolve continuous teaching or individual identities. Source copies and later syntheses share observation ancestry. Compare actual technical signatures against other regional solutions before describing a bundle as unusual.

The endpoint is a bounded source and observation ledger, a candidate-specific rival matrix, an operation-level textual-join table, a developed carrier mechanism with counterevidence, and an empirical next test. Freeze coded observations and output each analysis to a fresh dated run. Any post-outcome refinement must preserve the first endpoint. Raw source text, PDFs and images remain local-only within the owned raw directory; authored prose, code, metadata and aggregate comparisons can enter the research package.
