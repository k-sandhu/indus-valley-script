# Transmission after the Indus cities

12 October 2026. Exploratory historical continuation. This protocol precedes the new source coding and synthesis; the team has seen the motivating October 11 studies and their outcomes.

## Question and change from earlier work

Which kinds of practical knowledge and organizing relationships could survive Harappan urban contraction and contribute to early Indian literature? Which inspected observations distinguish a particular local contribution from inherited Indo-Iranian repertoires, wider regional exchange, independent development and later specialist synthesis?

The October 11 continuity study established candidate mechanisms and missing joins. Its corrected institutional eligibility endpoint has no diagnostic material-to-textual join. The persistence study further showed why current service and replacement knowledge need separate observations, and why working and teaching may share an operation. This continuation collects new scoped intermediate material contexts and new early textual loci. It does not repeat the rice reassignment, prior filter, memory-erasure construction or retention model. Their original endpoints remain intact.

The coordinator owns this root package, `coordinator/`, synthesis and current navigation additions. Contributors accommodate shared work and do not revert changes outside their ownership.

The material frame initially covers hard-stone production, ceramic production and crop-processing or ecological practices. The textual frame initially targets new agriculture, occupation and learning passages, retaining book, genre and source strata. Each contributor freezes exact candidate selection and coding rules before its new coding. Additions after inspection remain marked exploratory. The review supplies independently sourced controls and examines both the source claims and inference procedure.

## Rivals and observation units

R1 is transmission through local or post-Harappan practitioners. R2 is inherited Indo-Iranian or Indo-European specialist knowledge. R3 is regional contact or transfer whose immediate route need not pass through an Indus city. R4 is independent reconstruction from ordinary work, recurring ecology or analogous coordination problems. R5 is later intellectual combination, literary organization or redaction. A component can pass through more than one of these routes; they are not exclusive civilizations competing to own an entire text.

A test concerns a named feature or linked operation bundle. Material deposits, textual loci and scholarly comparisons retain separate identities. A deposited object's date does not date a textual proposition. Narrated time, composition or stratum, collection, surviving witness and archaeological context remain distinct. A modern ethnographic or experimental observation tests a mechanism in its observed setting.

For each comparison record: the feature, direct observations, source author's interpretation, predicted discriminating observation, source dependence, chronology, carrier evidence, plausible rival routes, counterevidence and decision. Use statements such as supported at an endpoint, compatible but untraced, weakened under a stated assumption, or unresolved. No numerical plausibility score, posterior probability or evidence-count voting is planned.

## What would discriminate

A local practice becomes a stronger transmission candidate if independently dated intermediate production contexts preserve linked, unusual operational choices. Local manufacture needs its own waste, tools or production-context evidence. Imported and curated finished objects remain separate alternatives. A plausible meeting of populations establishes opportunity; it does not identify what they taught one another.

An Iranian or Indo-European control can show that a repertoire has an available ancestral explanation outside a proposed local route. Its exact shared features and differences must be checked. This can defeat a claim that urban local inheritance was necessary without proving that local contributions were absent. Missing control attestations are not contradictions unless the comparison's recovery and coverage justify that inference.

Textual learning, occupational differentiation or practical reasoning can establish that knowledge was organized in the text's own setting. A specific Harappan ancestry requires feature-specific transitions. Borrowed vocabulary can support contact while leaving the source language, donor society and technical practice's age unresolved. Translated terminology can also hide a practice's earlier origin.

R5 gains direct support where a later text visibly reorganizes a received account or repertoire. Its presence does not determine every component's origin. Early hymns and later non-Vedic explanations are compared at their own strata, rather than pooled as one earliest textual endpoint.

## Coordinator inference audit

The synthesis will keep three questions separate: whether a practice continued, whether a carrier relationship is observed, and whether a literary feature descends from that practice. It will list the least-supported transition in each proposed chain. A compatible route is a hypothesis, not an observed path. Alternative-route exclusion requires stated scope and coverage assumptions.

Source-linked rival comparisons will be performed on the newly inspected observations. A structured audit will check exact joins, claimed transitions, dependence and decision rules, with independent reviewer reconstruction where appropriate. If a hypothetical control is needed to demonstrate an inference failure, it will be labelled as a construction and will not supply historical evidence. No simulated likelihoods will be substituted for historical data.

The developed theory will state testable relationships about selective survival, learning during work, language change and later conceptual organization. It will explain what observation would change each conclusion. No decipherment, authenticated Indus language, ethnic attribution from artifacts or verified priority claim is planned.

Current shared navigation receives its own snapshot immediately before additions. New source witnesses, extraction and images stay local-only under `data/raw/2026-10-12-transmission-hypothesis/`, with reading depth, retrieval route, rights observations and parent hashes recorded.

The package will include an argument, source and observation ledgers, actual comparison results, reusable audit code, claim map, review response, retrospective and final manifest/verification. New findings enter the knowledge index and registry over current bytes. No commit, push, external publication, CI/CD or automation is authorized by this study.

Prose will maximize reader comprehension, explain ideas directly in connected paragraphs and keep shared assumptions together. Source titles remain exact. The manual prose scan receives a separate review of any flags before delivery.
