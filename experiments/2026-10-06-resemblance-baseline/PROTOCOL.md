# Shape resemblance feasibility pilot

Declared 6 October 2026 before descriptor coding or match-rate computation. Status: Proposed. The study asks how often the 42 Tamil Nadu base drawings share declared shape descriptors with a fixed Indus subset and two geographically separate control inventories. It assesses the descriptive value of a shape match under these rules. The authors' exact/near parallels and their reported percentages require a separate source audit because the acquired 2025 witness is an eight-page brochure.

## Descriptor codebook

Trace the centreline of the published drawing, ignoring print thickness, labels, crop borders and dashed editorial guides. A **segment** is a maximal straight portion between corners, or a smooth curved arc between corners. Intersections do not split a straight segment. A circle is one curved segment. A polygon has one segment per side. A U is one smooth curved segment unless the drawing has square corners; a zigzag is split at every turn. Small spirals are one smooth arc each. This operational segment count is not an inferred pen-stroke count.

Record `segments`, `curved` (number of those segments that are curved), `loops` (bounded regions enclosed by the centreline), `L` (degree-two corners), `T` (degree-three contacts with a through-line), `X` (degree-four crossings), `Y` (degree-three contacts without a through-line), `other` (degree above four), and `terminals` (degree-one endpoints). Count loop subdivisions: a bisected circle has two loops, a quartered circle four. A segment endpoint touching an interior point of another segment is a T contact. A sharp open V has one L corner and two terminals; a Y has one Y contact and three terminals. Smooth continuation has no junction. Disconnected marks are included in the same descriptor, with `components` counting connected pieces. A dot is recorded as a zero-length segment with `dots=1`, no terminals. Record `uncertain` and a note for contacts, tangencies, print defects or segment boundaries that need adjudication.

Record approximate reflection symmetry as `none`, `vertical`, `horizontal`, `both`, or `diagonal`. Treat ordinary drawing irregularity as noise; diagonal applies when an oblique reflection exists and no horizontal/vertical reflection exists. Record `orientation` as `vertical`, `horizontal`, `diagonal`, or `mixed`: it describes the predominant segment direction, with mixed for balanced directions or curved closed figures. Uniform scaling and translation are ignored; rotation and reflection are not applied in the strict rule. These are coarse summaries and do not encode adjacency, angles, relative lengths or stroke order.

## Rules, outcomes and agreement

R1 (coarse): exact equality of `(segments, loops)`. R2 (topology counts): exact equality of every integer field, including components, curved segments and dots. R3 (orientation-sensitive): R2 plus exact symmetry and orientation categories. A query matches an inventory when at least one member passes the rule. Report the number and fraction of the 42 queries matched and unmatched. Because these are enumerated drawings, no confidence interval is attached to the census fraction.

A separate agent will code a fixed sample without seeing primary rows or match results: Tamil Nadu IDs 1, 6, 9, 14, 19, 20, 23, 30, 34, 42; atlas selected positions 0, 7, 14, 21, 28, 35; Ogham U+1681, U+168B; Tifinagh U+2D30, U+2D3F. Its ownership is limited to the repeat-coder raw JSON and its own coding notes. Report fieldwise raw agreement and complete-vector agreement, with unadjudicated disagreements retained. This is independent agent-pass reproducibility, not agreement between trained human epigraphers. The primary coding defines the pilot endpoint; do not reconcile toward higher resemblance after seeing outcomes.

## Inventory-size and complexity baseline

For each inventory and rule, sample reference subsets without replacement at n=5, 10, 20 and its full size (omit impossible sizes), with 2,000 replicates and seed 20261007. Compare all Tamil Nadu queries against each subset and report mean match fraction and the central 95% simulation interval. Stratify queries by segment complexity 1–3, 4–6 and 7+. This describes coverage under finite-inventory subsampling, not population uncertainty or archaeological probability.

Generate synthetic random marks at the descriptor level by drawing complete descriptor vectors with replacement from each inventory's empirical joint distribution. Each generated mark inherits a physically observed descriptor; dependencies between segment count, loops, contacts and terminals are preserved. These are surrogate marks, without newly generated drawings. Draw independent sets of 42 synthetic query descriptors and n reference descriptors, for n=5, 10, 20, 42, 100 and 400; repeat 2,000 times. Report the collision rate, central 95% simulation interval, and the exact mean `sum_d p(d) * (1 - (1 - p(d))**n)` for each rule. Also compare the fixed Tamil Nadu queries with these random references. This empirical-support null cannot invent absent descriptor classes; larger n describes saturation of observed support, not an extrapolation to a true 400-sign script. A fuller null must be learned from many independent script families, with held-out drawing checks.

## Interpretation and negative results

A high control match fraction or strong changes across strictness and reference size indicate that this descriptor-level resemblance carries limited information about transmission. A low control rate accompanied by an Indus advantage motivates a fuller test only if the advantage survives balanced size/complexity, adjacency-aware descriptors and independent coding. This pilot has no significance threshold for transmission. It fails as a quantitative archaeological test if drawings cannot be coded reproducibly, if controls lack query-complexity support, or if conclusions depend on this coarse descriptor. Keep all those limitations beside the relevant measurements. Positional structure, composite order, specimen dates, function and contact histories are outside the endpoint.

Do not revise selection or matching rules after observing rates. Preserve the first computation in `run-v1/`. Any later rule or sample is a dated amendment and a separate Exploratory endpoint. Reviewer checks should independently recode source drawings, compare script-size curves and rerun the exact seed and formula checks.
