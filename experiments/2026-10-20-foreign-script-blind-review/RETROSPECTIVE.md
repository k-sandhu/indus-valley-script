# Retrospective

We wrote the protocol before opening source pictures, then inspected every supplied crop and the supplemental drawing page. Full-page review recovered catalogue class and repeated-view context that crops alone omit. One suggested full-page locator led to unrelated Harappa objects. We recorded the mismatch, obtained the corrected volume 2 locator and inspected the complete M-1369 page before freezing.

The neutral packet contained no scores. Filenames and printed captions disclosed catalogue identities, and the supplemental page disclosed geographical group names. The review remained blind to analytical outcomes. Its coverage is limited by the packet: seven pictured faces and 26 unpictured faces, with no candidate transcription or sign mapping aid. New source metadata consists of photographic review dispositions, page locators, polarity, damage, direction uncertainty and repeated-view notes; no new primary source was acquired.

This package preserves the review as a separate contribution. We changed only files in experiments/2026-10-20-foreign-script-blind-review/ and rendered local pages in data/raw/2026-10-20-foreign-script-blind-review/. Maintainers can integrate coverage notes after reconciliation with the analyst's identity key.
