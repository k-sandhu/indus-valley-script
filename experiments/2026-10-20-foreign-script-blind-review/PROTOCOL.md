# Score-blind source review protocol

Recorded 2026-10-20 before opening any plate. Authors: independent GPT-6.1-Sol agent, Codex desktop collaboration harness.

We inspect the supplied neutral packet of 33 opaque faces. Seven faces have indexed crops; a supplemental complete page contains Laursen Figure 11. We retain every face in the disposition table, including faces without pictures. The frame and pictures were selected by the main analyst. This review does not establish independent sampling or geographical identity.

The reviewer has read project entry, writing, reproduction, contribution and redistribution instructions and the verify-study skill. We deliberately exclude the score-containing registry, analytical reports, old objects.csv, FOREIGN-FINDS-SEQUENCE-STATISTICS results, identity key, and all fresh-test-collection records. General orientation requirements yield to this restriction. The packet contains paths, kinds and hashes, with no model scores or outcomes. Filename identities and printed captions may be visible.

For every supplied picture we record photographic versus drawn evidence, apparent seal versus impression polarity, direction evidence, damage, visible sign count when defensible, shape/orientation ambiguity and repeated views. We distinguish the image kind supplied by the packet from what the picture supports. No candidate transcription or sign mapping is supplied, so code-level agreement remains unassessed. We request full published pages for crops and record absence explicitly. We will not force faint marks into codes or infer missing pictures from other objects.

We calculate no semantic or order score and take no confirmation shot. Observations and input hashes are frozen with SHA256 before communicating substantive observations to the main analyst. Raw pictures and sequences stay local. Our public record contains identifiers, source locations, coverage, our observations and hash references. The review ends when all 33 faces have a disposition and every available supplied image has been inspected.
