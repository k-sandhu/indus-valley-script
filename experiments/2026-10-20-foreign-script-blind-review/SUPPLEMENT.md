# Supplemental Figure 11 observations

We inspected the complete supplied Laursen page, printed 116, PDF page 21. Figure 11 contains printed drawings. Its caption states that the inscriptions are shown as seen on the impression and that the numbers refer to Table 1. These drawings are editorial readings of the marks, distinct from photographs. The labels on the page disclose geographical groups; no score blindness depends on hiding those printed labels.

Grey areas interrupt or border the drawings numbered 4, 6, 7, 12, 14, 17, 18 and 19. These areas must remain missing or uncertain evidence. Number 19 preserves only a small portion of its drawn inscription. Number 13 has a visibly complex arrangement over more than one level; a single linear traversal needs further justification. Numbers 10 and 3 include groups of short strokes whose segmentation needs an explicit rule. These observations concern the printed drawings and do not verify the underlying incisions.

We have not joined the figure numbers to opaque packet IDs before freezing. All 26 opaque IDs without indexed images remain unverified at the object level. The page supplies comparative drawing evidence but cannot by itself resolve photographic damage, alternative sign forms or identity aliases for those opaque IDs.
