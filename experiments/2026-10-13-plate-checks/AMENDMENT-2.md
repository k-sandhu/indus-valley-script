# Amendment 2, 13 October 2026: catalogue captions in the blinded crops

Written during Check 2, before any seal of the blinded set was read under the final labels.

The Lipi crops are cut from the CISI pages with the printed caption, so the first blinded composites (`data/raw/2026-10-13-plate-checks/blind/`) showed each seal's CISI number below its photographs. The first composite opened, version-1 label S01, showed the caption "M-629 a", a set seal (pair P23). Those composites were withdrawn. A second version removed captions by a rule based on the white gap above the caption; a low-resolution check of its bottom strips showed that many captions survived, and one of them, "M-393 a" (set seal, pair P07), was legible in the check image, as was a caption of the form "C-2 a" (comparison seal, pair P12). The third version (`blind-v3/`, key in `blind-key-v3/`) cuts each crop above the lowest row that is at least 45% non-white, which removes the caption band; it draws new random labels. A check of its bottom strips at one-tenth resolution showed object edges and no isolated print, and four composites in which one photograph was left uncut are opened knowing that a caption may remain.

Three further identities are known from the blinded sheet itself: the two composites with an impression only are the comparison seals C-2 and Dlv-1 (the only seals whose crops lack an object photograph), and the composite with direction code `-` is the comparison seal M-414. The seals of pairs P07 and P23 (set), and C-2, Dlv-1 and M-414 (comparison), are therefore recorded as read without blinding. Any other caption seen while reading is recorded in `observations.json` against the seal it identifies.
