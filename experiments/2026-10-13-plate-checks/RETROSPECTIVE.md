# Retrospective: plate checks of 13 October 2026

## Approach

The protocol named the objects, image sources, recorded fields and decision rules for both checks, and its hash was written before any image was opened. The Check 2 list came from Shot A's own population code, imported read-only, and reproduced Shot A's 29 set seals (18 discovery, 11 confirmation). A comparison seal was fixed for each set seal before viewing by type, site, length and nearest face area. Check 2 crops were read as composites under random labels, and each reading was logged before the key was opened. Check 1 had a second reader, a separate model instance that received renamed images and no expectations. Both checks were decided by a script that joins the readings to the keys.

## Problems and repairs

Seven set seals carry numbers beyond the ranges of CISI 1 and 2 and have no held photograph. One, C-56, cites Mackay 1943 Plate LI no. 30, and [Amendment 1](AMENDMENT-1.md), written before viewing, added that plate. Its halftone proved too coarse to read.

The Lipi crops include the printed caption with the CISI number. The first blinded composite showed "M-629 a", and a gap-based caption filter in the second version missed many captions. The third version cuts each crop above its last mostly non-white row. [Amendment 2](AMENDMENT-2.md) records which identities were exposed, and the report counts those seals as read unblinded. Four composites in which one crop escaped the filter exposed four more captions, and these were logged as they appeared.

The first reader's initial views of the Check 1 images were not written down before the images left the working context. Those images were viewed again and recorded as they were read. The second reader had been launched in between, and its report arrived after the first reader's record was complete.

The protocol's size rule for the final sign did not anticipate a closing sign that is small by its form. The seals ending in 001 close with a single short stroke, and the rule left them undecided instead of assigning them to either reading. The protocol was not changed after the readings; the report gives the case separately.

CISI 1 and CISI 2 basic-data pages give no dimensions. Scale came from the volumes' 2:1 printing policy, checked against seals on the same or nearby pages. One CISI 1 seal (L-1) disagreed with the others by about 10%, and that spread is carried into the L-161 face width.

## Outcome

C-0004: P1 passed, P2 is unresolved because the readers disagree on M-895's animal, and P3 passed on carved form. The sealings were made by a seal as wide as M-895 whose sign proportions and field differ. C-0001: prospective prediction 1 was met (impressed face 23.4 mm), and prospective prediction 3 passed with 14 of 22 viewable set seals. No seal in either group showed a layout or direction pattern. All seven viewable seals ending in 820 support the grade reading.
