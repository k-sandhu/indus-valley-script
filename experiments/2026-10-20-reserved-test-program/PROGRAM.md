# Recover a usable part of the Indus writing system

Our aim is a complete, testable reading of one class of record. We want to take an inscription we have never analysed, identify its units, predict what those units refer to, and check that prediction against evidence recorded independently of the writing. A successful result could identify an issuer, a commodity or an allocation without yet supplying its ancient pronunciation. Phonetic readings remain a further target.

The immediate program supplies two missing ingredients: reserved objects for prospective tests and source-checked records of how the script was used outside South Asia. The existing [working model](../../knowledge/WORKING-MODEL.md) supplies competing accounts. The [fish program](../2026-10-20-fish-context/PROGRAM-REPORT.md) identifies the physical observations that would distinguish persistent designations from contents and quantities. The following objectives extend that work; they are research targets, not new measured findings or submitted sign meanings.

## Identify what a recurring designation names

We should follow a designation through production, movement and use. Define workshops from clay fabric, chemical composition, carving and tool marks without using the text. Define contents and use from residues and registered archaeological associations. Then ask what remains constant when a legend recurs.

A producer designation should follow a manufacturing group even when its products move. A commodity designation should follow independently identified contents across producers. An institution can commission several producers, so its designation should recur across manufacturing groups while retaining an independently observed pattern of use or distribution. A person's name needs evidence that separates an individual from an office or inherited title.

The deliverable is a prediction table for recurring units: what each predicts, the strongest competing explanation, which observation distinguishes them, and the failures. A semantic claim must transfer to a source block excluded from fitting. A result that disappears when workshop, period or object form is controlled cannot establish the proposed referent.

We can start the source joins and blind coding now. Direct residue, composition or use-wear measurements require published object-level data or archaeological collaboration. A photograph alone cannot supply them.

## Recover a numerical expression with a physical answer

The H-912 reading gives a concrete target: a quantity, a vessel unit and an item. The current tablet and excavation studies own that case. We should seek a series in which quantity varies, the descriptor remains stable, and an independent measurement changes in the predicted proportion. The full series, all faces, manufacturing relationships and contradictory cases belong in the register.

One closed assemblage could be more informative than thousands of loosely described seals. A meaningful result predicts an independently measured capacity, inventory or allocation on a held-out object. Standard denominations and entitlement tokens remain explicit alternatives to delivered quantities. If each numerical value can be freely relabelled to fit each object, the proposed reading has not supplied a measurement rule.

## Determine what travelled with the script

The Gulf study can test whether regional differences survive source correction and matching by seal form. A stronger continuation would track a dated transition: which sign forms persist, which ordering rules change, and whether the change follows manufacture or use. The target is an explanation of adaptation with predictions for additional objects.

Three accounts deserve comparison: continued use of the same conventions, local adaptation of a functioning writing system, and imitation of prestigious signs. Their predictions must first be tested on readable scripts and documented imitation traditions at the size and damage level of our sample. Unusual order by itself cannot identify a spoken language. An account that cannot distinguish these controls should not be used to classify the Indus examples.

## Make a serious attempt at sounds

The ambitious linguistic route combines structural units with external anchors. Assemble contemporaneous names and terms explicitly identified in their primary sources with Meluhha, keeping the source's identification separate from modern etymology. Explore whether a small, fixed set of correspondences explains several independent items. The [Parpola-forward plan](../../knowledge/directions/parpola-forward-2026-10-20.md) already proposes the acquisition and comparison work.

This is a long shot because a named person or word in cuneiform has no automatic match to an Indus legend. The decisive observation would be an independently justified link, followed by correct predictions for further forms under the same sound rules. Flexible word resemblance cannot supply the link. The project should preserve unsuccessful candidate correspondences so that a later dictionary search does not present them as new evidence.

## Learn what a successful decipherment method can recover

The earlier [synthetic key study](../../knowledge/registry/attempts/KEY-CALIBRATION-POWER-SYNTHETIC-2026-10-12.json) used homophonic encodings of running text. Its stated retry condition permits formulaic and logographic generators. Build a small artificial society whose independently generated people, offices, goods and transactions produce seal legends and tablets. Hide its key and some archaeological observations from the decoder. Include formulaic linguistic and nonlinguistic alternatives with matched corpus sizes and repetition.

The target is to recover the generating relationships and meanings on withheld records, with a measured rate of confident mistakes. This tests a method under specified assumptions. It also identifies which additional observations make otherwise indistinguishable explanations separable. The simulator must include adversarial alternatives and be authored independently of the decoding procedure.

## Commit the reserve to decisions

We will use the old corpus for unrestricted exploration and develop new claims there. Before opening the reserve, competing models must state what they predict differently on the same eligible objects. Existing prospective seal-structure predictions keep their original outcomes; a new model receives its own declared test and a joint rule when evidence overlaps.

The present acquisition creates a finite resource. Its complete object count, independence and exposure history determine which tests are possible. We will retain the collection if it is too small for a useful decision and extend it by the same neutral acquisition rules. A small pool can still contribute images, aliases and context without supporting a semantic conclusion.

The strongest ambition is a reading that works outside the examples used to invent it. Our next collection and the Gulf source audit make that ambition more concrete: one supplies unseen cases, and the other checks whether our proposed units remain useful when the setting changes.
