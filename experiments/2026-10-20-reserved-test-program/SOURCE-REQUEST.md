# Source access needed for the next collection

This is an unsent request draft. No publisher, author or institution has been contacted.

## Khirsara article

Subject: Research access to Nath and Kumaran, Itihas Darpan 17(2), 2012, pp. 235-242

We are compiling a source-linked research register of Indus inscribed objects and would like to consult Jitendra Nath and R. N. Kumaran's "Inscribed materials from Khirsara, Dist. Kachchh, Gujarat", published in Itihas Darpan 17(2), 2012, pp. 235-242. This is the title in the publisher's contents index; a later ASI citation uses the singular "Material".

The publisher's old-editions page links the issue, but downloading is disabled and we could reach only its cover through the available viewer controls. Could you provide a permitted research copy of the article, including its plates and captions, or an accessible viewing route?

Our immediate work concerns object registrations, dimensions, find contexts, photographs and the crosswalk to earlier catalogues. Please also indicate any applicable terms for retaining a local research copy and reproducing individual photographs. Public source access and permission to redistribute images will be recorded separately.

## Farmana companion CD

Subject: Object registers accompanying Excavations at Farmana, 2006-2008

We are consulting Vasant Shinde, Toshiki Osada and Manmohan Kumar, editors, Excavations at Farmana, District Rohtak, Haryana, India, 2006-2008 (2011). We would like access to the accompanying CD's individual object records for the marked-pottery section, particularly excavation registrations, dimensions and the crosswalk to illustrated specimen labels.

The printed account gives 29 marked sherds, while the combined narrative and photographic labels yield 30 candidate identifiers. We have retained the conflict and would appreciate the source register or a correction that resolves the identities. Please indicate the terms for using the records in a local research register and citing individual source observations.
