# Reserved acquisition and the overseas comparison

We recovered a Farmana source block containing fifteen pottery records absent from the pinned export under the recorded-findspot assumptions. The source images and metadata are reserved, but the collection supplies **zero approved objects for the frozen seal tests**. A separate audit of inscriptions outside South Asia corrected the comparison's object types and confirmed that the available statistical contrast remains inconclusive. Both results establish concrete source work for the next stage.

## What changed in the overseas comparison

The [foreign-script audit](../2026-10-20-foreign-script-audit/REPORT.md) reviewed 33 circular-object face records with 32 export-family prefixes. Those prefixes are not 32 distinct physical objects. Seven faces have 28 indexed images, and the remaining 26 have none. A separate reader checked the complete catalogue pages for the pictured faces and all 29 entries in Laursen's Figure 11 before receiving model scores. The analyst had already seen the legacy scores; this exposure is disclosed and the revised analysis remains exploratory.

M-500 is an incised tablet. Removing cylinders from the old comparison reduces the western-minus-South-Asian mean rank difference from 0.1654 to 0.0788; removing the tablet then raises it to 0.0985. The latter bootstrap interval is −0.1077 to 0.3490. Every reported interval includes zero, including the original comparison. The independent [numerical verification](../2026-10-20-foreign-script-verification/REPORT.md) reproduced counts, means, family deletion results and seeded bootstrap intervals within 1e-12. The rank values are unchanged from the October 12 analysis.

The audit joined eight face rows to seven Laursen objects, but no foreign object has a complete photograph-to-Wells sign-code concordance. We therefore have an eligibility correction, not a new transcription-based test. A dated and independently coded comparison is still needed to distinguish continued conventions, local adaptation and imitation. Gulf adaptation is a working interpretation supported by the source discussion; the current rank differences do not decide it.
