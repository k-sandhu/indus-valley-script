# Recovery after the shared disk filled

The shared disk filled while the first outer-fold extra-text ensemble array was being compressed. The original log records `OSError: [Errno 28] No space left on device`; the user identified Windows error 112 and reported that the coordinator had freed space. The stopped continuation had completed all ten selections for each of MKN, GRU, transformer and metadata, and one ensemble selection. Curves, final summaries, verification and freezing had not started. [Interruption audit](interruption-audit.json), `completed_selection_records`; local `finish.log`.

The recovery audit parsed 1,048 JSON records and 48 JSONL files, and checked archive integrity for 990 probability arrays and 102 checkpoints. One 13,353-byte array, `ensemble-f0-extra1-outer-c2.npz`, failed its archive check. Its bytes were moved to the local `interruption-witness/ensemble-f0-extra1-outer-c2.partial`. No selection record referred to that incomplete file. Resuming the ensemble stage rebuilt the probability array from the completed classical and neural predictions. Existing selection endpoints were reused. [Interruption audit](interruption-audit.json), `checked`, `invalid_files`; local `resume-after-interruption.log`.

Individual inner-seed probability arrays were no longer needed once their three-seed means and seed validation records were complete. After checking that both existed, the recovery removed 450 such arrays, freeing 2,039,403,915 bytes. All individual fit histories, averaged inner arrays, outer predictions and outer checkpoints were retained. [Cleanup record](storage-cleanup.json), `removed_files`, `removed_bytes` and `files`. The PowerShell cleanup record initially had a UTF-8 byte-order mark; the original bytes were preserved locally and the owned JSON was converted to UTF-8 without a mark.

The grid, masks, folds, selection rules, floating-point arrays and fitting code were unchanged. The continuation uses the original compressed-array writer. All remaining fits and plots use the assigned directories; no further literature downloads are needed. Deleted intermediate seed arrays can be regenerated in a fresh complete run. Completed selections make them unnecessary for resuming this run.
