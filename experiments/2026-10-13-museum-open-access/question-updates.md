# Proposed question-card updates

## Q-1018 Harvest open museum records and images for Indus inscribed objects

Proposed status: `done`.

Outcome note, 13 October 2026: Seven institutions have a dated harvest record ([package](../../experiments/2026-10-13-museum-open-access/README.md)). Inscribed Indus originals found: Metropolitan 5 and Cleveland 3 (CC0, with photographs, no findspot or number), Penn 5 (CC BY 4.0 data; plus 4 uninscribed seal-amulets and 38 casts or impressions), V&A 2 inscribed originals among 4 reviewed attributed records (plus a modern impression and figurine head IS.9-1951 outside inscription scope), British Museum 7 with a stated inscription among 27 listed Indus stamp-seals, Art Institute of Chicago 0, Smithsonian 0 (20 casts in 539,615 bulk records). Every record has a row with its data and image licence in `harvest/` and `joins/join-table.csv`. Joins beside the census's 68: 27 exact or confirmed records in this package, 19 new, so 87 museum records are joined. Casts and impressions are recorded and not counted as originals. Left open: the British Museum's object pages are behind a Cloudflare challenge, so 20 of its Indus seals lack a reachable description and five cannot yet be joined; reading their Miscellaneous numbers in a browser would settle them. Raw V&A responses must be deleted by 10 November 2026 under the V&A API terms.

## Q-1019 Resolve the 46 museum records that have no checked join to a local object

Proposed status: `done`.

Outcome note, 13 October 2026: Each of the 46 records has a decision with its basis ([q1019-decisions.csv](../../experiments/2026-10-13-museum-open-access/joins/q1019-decisions.csv)). Sixteen are confirmed through CISI 1 basic-data rows read by eye (Lothal LTH SRG numbers, NMI and IM numbers, Banawali and Chanhu-daro rows), the Marshall 1931 and Mackay 1938 seal tables, and numbers written on the objects in CISI photographs (L-29 B "SRG 989", L-122 B "2845"). Twenty-one have candidate sets (the Met seals, seven Indian Museum seals without a confirmed identifier join, the Louvre seals from Susa and Girsu, two British Museum seals and three National Museum records), eight are unmatched and one is uninscribed. The confirmed lower bound rises from 68 to 84 for the census's 114 records. Five unmatched records are candidate additions to the corpus: CISI H-224 (NMI 1296), Banawali 3714 and Dholavira DHR-22198, DHR-6032 and DHR-7860. The National Museum record DK12995 shares its number with CISI H-13, a Harappa unicorn seal, and is not that object. The independent reviewer added Indian Museum 10377/A7252/C.2803 as family 2194 (M-1809), through Marshall 1931 vol. II p. 403 row 199. The recall search leaves 10 non-joined records with hits, four confined to one family; printed-page checks are pending. Retry when CISI 3.1 or the British Museum object pages are available, or with better copies of the Mackay 1943 plates.
