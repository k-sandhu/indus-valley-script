# Fish-context study record

We started from the existing fish-adjacency and numeral-fixity results, searched the registry and checked the source and data gaps. The study combines a full fish-object register with three comparisons and an astronomical long shot. We used the shared shots loader, recorded prior exposure and froze one participant before opening confirmation. The observable result is an ordered field of fish forms, a numeral gain largely explained by two-stroke constructions, a failed carrier-preference test and a failed narrow astronomical prediction. All outputs remain at separate versioned paths.

## Approaches and repairs

The first discovery run recognized only the literal type SEAL and omitted subtypes such as SEAL:S. Its five eligible seal texts exposed the error. We preserved that run and its code snapshot, corrected the prefix rule and wrote discovery v2 before confirmation. Method checks now cover all seal subtypes used in the study. The pre-freeze source clarification also restricted the astronomical model to short-stroke groups, preserving the distinction from Parpola's long-stroke compounds.

The shared meaning-map power helper used alpha 0.05 internally despite a stricter enclosing CLI alpha. We retained its output, wrote a vectorized arithmetic check that reproduces its contrast, reference mean and SD, deletion influence and four power counts, and applied the actual study threshold. This was a discovery diagnostic; no shared harness file was changed.

New downloads acquired Parpola's 2010 PDF and Belcher's Harappa chapter. Both publisher routes for the 2023 economic paper returned 3,038-byte HTML responses with HTTP 200. We retained the failed responses and located the already held 38-page publisher PDF, checking its title, author, DOI and licence notice. The bibliographic record distinguishes a new acquisition from reuse of an existing witness.

The object register initially called bracketed catalogue transcriptions conflicting values. Version 2 keeps their catalogue provenance and marks their uncertainty separately. Its field counter then counted the placeholder - - as a depth. Version 3 applies the canonical inventory rules and gives 748 objects with a usable depth string. No statistical score uses that counter; the frozen comparisons were unaffected.

The first source packet could not find H-902 in the fish-bearing register. Checking its photographs and the cited CISI page showed why: Parpola's 2010 Figure 6 depicts H-912 B, while H-902 has different signs. We preserved H-902 as a control, added the correct source relation and left the source corpus unchanged. The same inspection established that M-478 A is a vessel-shape comparison outside the fish-bearing denominator. The packet retains cases lacking a photograph and distinguishes an original object from its impression.

After the frozen numerical success we tested pooled-fish, preceding-context and constant-two references, and deleted value-two events. The preceding-context model predicted better overall, and deleting two removed L's advantage over Q. An independent-position reference and a Mohenjo-daro exclusion left the order result positive. These are labelled post-outcome in their amendment and outputs; they do not replace the frozen result.

## Decision

Repeating fish adjacency or a broad numeral-versus-item comparison on this export will not resolve meaning. A useful retry needs new source-checked seal texts for the frozen order, or an explicit tablet-to-container, content, lot or measured-allotment relation. The strongest working reading is an ordered compound-designation field; H-912 B supplies the more concrete candidate four vessel-units of fish. Their deciding tests are new compound sequences and independent physical unit-and-content observations respectively.
