# Fish signs form ordered combinations; their numeral pattern needs a preceding-context control

We assembled the fish-bearing objects, linked their available source records and tested four competing predictions. A discovery-learned order of fish signs predicts the reserved seal texts: 43 dependency groups favour it, eight oppose it and two tie. Fish-specific stroke values also beat a general quantity reference, but that gain disappears when value-two cases are removed. A reference using the sign before the strokes predicts slightly better overall. The evidence supports a structured field of fish combinations while leaving its personal, institutional, commodity and celestial meanings open.

A source check also changes which object carries a familiar working reading. Figure 6 in the acquired version of Asko Parpola's 2010 *A Dravidian solution to the Indus script problem* depicts H-912 B, as shown in CISI 2 page 339. The figure caption and prose call it H-902. H-902 is a separate tablet on the preceding page. [Source observations S2–S3](source-observations-v1.json) and [source note](SOURCE-NOTES.md).

## Frozen comparisons

The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [predictions](PREDICTIONS.json), learned order, probability models, code and bands were frozen before the single logged confirmation read. Discovery supplies 645 distinct complete seal texts and confirmation 667. All predictions belonged to one registered participant. The three statistical comparisons use a threshold of 0.05/3. The astronomical long shot has its own sample-size, coverage and predictive-score rule.

The corpus had already been used for fish adjacency, numeral fixity and site, carrier, motif and size studies. The harness records `fresh_claim_allowed: false`. Results are frozen evaluations on previously examined material. A source join to a catalogue value that Lipi already carries improves traceability without creating an independent excavation observation. The [freeze record](freeze-v1.json), [access log](round.access.jsonl) and [result](confirmation-v1/results.json) preserve that scope.

The [carrier bench output](confirmation-v1/meaning-map.json) uses 9,999 draws. The independent check enumerates all 120 distinct fish-label allocations and gives p = 0.48333, consistent with the Monte Carlo result. Discovery effects have moderate reliability between subhalves, but planted-effect power is limited. The shared helper's power calculation uses alpha 0.05 internally; the [power audit](discovery-v2/power-audit.json) reproduces its arithmetic and applies the study's stricter threshold. Twelve trials per effect give imprecise power estimates. A carrier-test failure therefore supplies no positive evidence for a tablet or commodity meaning.

## What survives stronger comparisons

The [post-outcome comparisons](adversarial-v1/results.json) were specified in [Amendment 2](AMENDMENT-2.md) after the frozen result. They are exploratory checks of its interpretation.

The ordering result survives a model of independent fish frequencies by text length and position. The frozen order gains 0.343 bits per dependency group over that reference; 41 groups favour it and 12 oppose it. Removing Mohenjo-daro leaves 13 favourable groups, two opposed and one tie. The relation is therefore present beyond the dominant site. These checks support sequential constraints among fish forms. They do not require the whole seven-sign order to hold without exception: the common pair W235 then W240 occurs 14 times against two reverse occurrences, while other pairs are less settled.

The numeral result narrows more sharply:

| Stronger reference or restriction | L's mean gain, bits per group | Consequence |
|---|---:|---|
| Pooled fish-family value distribution | +0.0910 | Wins in 50 groups and loses in 51; no consistent code-specific advantage |
| Sign before the strokes, learned on discovery | −0.0468 | The preceding-context reference predicts slightly better |
| Delete every value-two case | −0.0747 | The frozen advantage over Q disappears in the remaining 25 cases |
| Long-stroke cases only, against preceding context | +0.2449 | A limited family-specific contrast remains in 31 cases |

The 102 reserved numeral events contain 77 value-two cases. Both short- and long-stroke constructions matter, and Parpola treats the two long strokes as a separate compound in his rebus arguments. We should therefore treat a recurring pair of strokes as a construction requiring explanation before counting it as two things. Standard quantities, grammatical links and fixed words remain competing accounts. The general Q reference never represented every possible economic system; standard quotas can produce fixed expressions too.

The narrow astronomical model was deliberately stronger than Parpola's general proposal. Only 17 qualifying seal groups with short strokes before plain fish were available. Three, six and seven cover 11, but their model predicts the rest poorly. Some excluded values could belong to connective constructions rather than genuine numerals. The test therefore fails to support an asterism-only account of this population and leaves particular celestial readings unresolved. It does not measure the probability that Dravidian was spoken.

## The corrected vessel-and-fish case

H-912 B contains a stroke group, a V-shaped sign and a fish-like sign. Parpola reads the intervening sign as a vessel, supported by the pictorial comparison on M-478 A. The candidate functional reading is four vessel-units of fish. Its number is not immediately adjacent to fish, so the frozen adjacent-stroke comparison does not test that construction. H-912 is already in the pinned corpus; the source check changes a bibliographic attribution, not any frozen sequence or result.

Belcher's 1991 *Fish Resources in an Early Urban Context at Harappa, Pakistan* provides independent fish-resource evidence by room, street, pit and deposit. The chapter separates initial use, later truncation and dumped debris around Street NS2355 on Mound E. No object-to-lot join currently connects the studied fish-sign tablets to those deposits. The chapter gives a concrete acquisition target for testing literal contents, rather than a label we can attach through site alone.

## Working readings and next tests

The registered rule gives the compound-label hypothesis a credence of 0.55 after A and B pass. Our judgement after the stronger references is **0.45**: the ordered field is supported, while the numeral interpretation has a stronger syntactic rival. The [compound-designation card](../../knowledge/conjectures/C-0007-fish-compound-designations.md) keeps both estimates. The [narrow astronomical card](../../knowledge/conjectures/C-0008-fish-numerals-asterism-coverage.md) records its failure and current credence 0.05. The [vessel-and-fish card](../../knowledge/conjectures/C-0009-h912-vessels-of-fish.md) states the corrected source-based reading at 0.35 and leaves its physical test prospective. These credences are working judgements, not probabilities calculated from p-values.

The boldest supported reading is that **fish combinations form an ordered designation field**, with successive forms carrying different parts of a compound label. A new source-checked series of seal texts can test the frozen order, especially W235 before W240. Our most concrete content reading is **four vessel-units of fish on H-912 B**. A linked series of tablets and containers with measured capacity, identifiable contents or documented allotments would decide whether its V-shaped sign names a vessel unit and whether fish names the contents.
