# Fish signs, marking operations and a shared written convention

Three studies now constrain the next reading. Fish forms occupy ordered combinations in seal texts. Harappa goblet stamps were selected during vessel manufacture. In a separate sample of fine graphic variants, adding city did not improve prediction once the recorded context was known. Together these results make a shared system of persistent designations a useful working model. Its personal, institutional and commodity categories still need independent physical labels.

These samples overlap in the underlying corpus but answer different questions. The goblet study does not identify its legends as fish texts, and the variation score contains no fish target. The numerical scores therefore cannot be combined into a probability for one semantic model. The fish and variation splits use previously examined inscriptions; their frozen procedures do not supply fresh archaeological populations. All three contributions remain at the submitted verification tier.

## What we recovered

The fish register contains 1,246 provisional objects and all 1,520 recorded faces belonging to them. It links 846 objects to CISI basic data, 409 to excavation-table rows and 838 to indexed images. A targeted thirteen-case source audit retains two cases without photographs. The acquired Parpola 2010 Figure 6 reproduces **H-912 B**, although its prose and caption say H-902. H-912's four strokes, V-shaped sign and fish support the inherited candidate reading **four vessel-units of fish**. The frozen numerical test uses strokes immediately beside fish, so it does not test that intervening unit sign. ## What the comparisons ruled out of our current argument

The fish-specific stroke model beats the general quantity reference, but 77 of its 102 reserved events are value-two constructions. Deleting value two removes its advantage, and a model using preceding context predicts slightly better overall. Fixed words, connectors and standard allotments remain rival explanations. The narrow celestial model, restricted to short strokes before plain fish and favouring three, six and seven, fails its declared coverage, score and minimum-size rules. Seventeen qualifying groups are available. The seal-preference test also fails after its calibrated comparison. Particular celestial uses and Parpola's proposed Dravidian sound value need their own evidence. [Primary results](confirmation-v1/results.json), [stronger comparisons](adversarial-v1/results.json) and [carrier calibration](confirmation-v1/meaning-map.json).

City also failed to improve the tested fine-variant model. This is compatible with multilingual users sharing conventions, one language with shared conventions, or institutions standardizing a common repertoire. The present observations cannot choose among those mechanisms. Identifying institutions requires independent evidence of workshops, production, distribution or repeated use. Identifying languages requires systematic correspondences whose predictions differ across linguistic models.

## A test that connects the fields

The next informative design follows an inscription through manufacture, distribution and use. First identify stamped wasters and manufacturing groups from fabric, chemical composition and tooling. Then code contents or use from residues and excavated associations, keeping those labels separate from the inscription. Record aliases, all faces and the physical mark-making sequence before fitting a semantic mapping. A commissioning institution predicts the same designation across several manufacturing groups; a producer predicts concentration within a manufacturing group even after distribution; a content category predicts recurrence with the same independently identified contents across producers.

For fish specifically, acquire the registration and lot needed to connect the H-912 type of tablet to a container, measured allotment or content observation. Belcher's Street NS2355 deposits identify an available source path, but the inscription-to-lot join is still absent. Freeze any claimed vessel unit, quantity tolerance and contents mapping on discovery objects before reading the confirmation group. The case becomes a physical reading when it predicts a separately measured relation.

The boldest combined model is a written system of stable, ordered designations portable across cities and potentially across spoken languages. The strongest specific content reading remains **four vessel-units of fish on H-912 B**. Registered objects linking a legend to both production and independently measured contents would decide how those designations worked.
