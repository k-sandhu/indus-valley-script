# Source inspection after scoring

We inspect the four published anchors H-9, H-902, M-410 and M-478. We also choose four fish-bearing objects with excavation-table joins by a fixed SHA256 ordering, two examples of reversal of the learned fish order, and two plain-fish seals whose preceding short strokes fall outside 3, 6 and 7. These twelve object selections are for source checking and explanation. The cases selected by outcome do not estimate an error rate.

The packet gives available photographs with their printed captions, source page and kind. It preserves unknown face assignments. The audit records visible strokes and fish forms, damage and caption identity; a transcribed code remains source-dependent when the image cannot decide it. H-902 B's misleading file name is resolved through the October 13 image index and its printed caption. Views and impressions of one object count once as physical evidence.
