# Retrospective: Harappa sealing and vessel settings

We selected the 47-object gap in ARCH-01 after reading the context-data plan and earlier Harappa, Lothal and administrative studies. The active Mohenjo-daro map and stratigraphy work and the main fish-sign statistics were outside this package. The new contribution is object-to-source linkage and recovery of individual excavation keys.

The protocol preceded new acquisition and observation coding. A bounded search used twenty queries. It did not locate the 1998–1999 full excavation report, the complete CISI 3.1 plates or a registered view of the unbaked stopper. We inspected the held 2010 source passage, located Figure 12 in the held 1997 excavation chapter and rendered both complete pages. The 1997 caption supplied two target joins and three unjoined stamped-vessel records. The 2001 online excavation summary, held 2005 chapter and held 2006 context chapter supplied no additional registered target figure in this search.

Five author-hosted HTML pages were acquired. Two image requests without a descriptive User-Agent returned HTTP 403. Ordinary requests with the same descriptive User-Agent used for the HTML pages succeeded. Both failed and successful acquisition endpoints remain in the package. The photographs were inspected in their entirety. Their lack of an individual CISI join or an attachment reverse was retained in the result.

Two diagnostic commands failed before producing artifacts: a source-list command had an unmatched parenthesis, and printing a retrospective encountered the Windows cp1252 encoding limit. The corrected commands listed the sources and displayed the text with UTF-8 output. An early lookup used the absent `cisi` field rather than `source_cisi`; the corrected lookup produced the target metadata. These console errors did not change a frozen endpoint. Poppler reported missing display fonts while rendering the 2010 page; the inspected output retained readable text and complete margins.

The source caption identifies H-1734 as registration H95-2526, lot 4450, item 31. The export stores the year, lot and item without the registration. The builder checks uniqueness across every export face before accepting that join. It also searches every excavation string for the three unmatched figure keys and preserves their zero-match dispositions. The mismatch between H-1741's derivative TAG label and the primary vessel-stamp range is kept as a conflict.

The seven-object sealing class failed the protocol's minimum of ten independent groups before any alias or text grouping. We therefore made no context-prediction run. The shots synthetic run passed; it has no empirical bearing on a sign meaning. The shared ledger was inspected and copied locally, with no merge addition proposed. All source-derived corpus rows and acquired witnesses remain local. The public package contains identifiers, our observations, source pointers, code and aggregates.

The strongest result is the separation of three events: manufacture of a vessel, application of its pre-firing stamp, and later recovery of the broken vessel in a fill deposit. Two individual source joins now preserve that sequence. A stamped kiln waster and independent clay-fabric and residue labels would make the next functional comparison possible.
