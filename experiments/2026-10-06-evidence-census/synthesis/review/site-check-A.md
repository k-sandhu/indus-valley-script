# Site check A: Rappwala Ther and Chanhu-daro

Reviewer notes on two rows of `site-rows.json` in the batch A folder (Pakistan and Afghanistan, as_of 2026-10-07): `Rappwala Ther`, drawn at random with six reported counts, and `Chanhu-daro`, chosen for size with 74 local faces. Checked on 2026-10-07. The review was read-only, and this is the only file written.

## Summary

Chanhu-daro: 19 assertions checked. 10 confirmed, 8 qualified, 1 discrepancy, 0 unverifiable.
Rappwala Ther: 15 assertions checked. 7 confirmed, 6 qualified, 0 discrepancy, 2 unverifiable.

The seven Chanhu-daro counts all sit on the Mahadevan 1977 pages the row cites (printed pp.6 and 7), and the 66 objects reappear as 66 text numbers under Chanhudaro in the Texts section. The CISI 1 range C-1 to C-50 on pp.327-343 is exact and unbroken. The one discrepancy is `post_cisi_assessment.latest_cisi_coverage_year` = 1991, because CISI 2 has no Chanhu-daro catalogue. The Rappwala Ther row reports no numbers, and none of the sources I could open gives one. Its region and table-of-contents statements match the batch's stored capture of Fuls 2022. Its page-level statements about Fuls 2023 p.498 and Mughal 1989 p.113 could not be opened.

## Findings

**Chanhu-daro counts and CISI range.** The seven Mahadevan figures are on the cited pages with the stated unit and year, and two tallies from the Texts section (66 text numbers in all, 58 in the seal series) agree with the tables. The row words the scope correctly: it calls the figures M77-included objects and says blank cells are zeros only within that corpus. The CISI 1 range is exactly C-1 to C-50 on pp.327-343, with data rows on pp.374-375.

**Main points in the Chanhu-daro row to correct or qualify.** (1) `latest_cisi_coverage_year` is 1991, but CISI 2 has no Chanhu-daro section. The verified coverage year is 1987, and the 36 local identifiers from C-51 to C-94 come from a source the row does not identify; its own search record points to CISI 3.2. (2) The 2018 season belongs to the span that Didier 2017 reports on p.969, and the partner named there is the national DOAM. (3) The caution about bracketed numerals cites CISI 2 p.434, which governs that volume's data table. Chanhu-daro entries follow the scheme on CISI 1 p.xxiv. (4) `final_report.full_text_inspected: true` rests on the title page, preface and contents of an image-only scan.
