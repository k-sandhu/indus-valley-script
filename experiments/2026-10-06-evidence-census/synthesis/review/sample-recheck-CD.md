# Sample recheck C and D

Independent re-adjudication of ten of the twenty rows in `source-sample-audit.json`, and a check of seven entries in `sources.json`, for the B3 synthesis of the 2026-10-06 evidence census. Written 2026-10-07 by a read-only review agent.

## Method

Each verdict was formed from the source pages before `source-sample-audit.json` was opened. Labels and `B3_observation` text were compared afterwards. A verdict says whether the source shows the assertion, using whichever pages the check needs. Locator and evidence-text defects go in the note column, so a row can be supported and still carry a wrong page number.

Page references read printed page / PDF page. Offsets: CISI1 PDF = printed + 36 in the first copy (the file holds the 431-page volume twice; PDF 793 repeats printed 326); IAR2013 +5; Bhagwanpura +38; Nippur +38; CISI33 excerpt, roman pages, PDF = printed + 2; Salut PDF = printed - 100; Gonur one PDF page per two-page spread. Scanned CISI1 and Bhagwanpura pages were read from images. Pages with a text layer were read from the text and checked against the image. For all seven sources the SHA-256 recorded in `sources.json` matches the local PDF.

Files read: the page witnesses in `reviewer-sources-2026-10-07-rerun` (read only) and the original PDFs under `data/raw/2026-10-06-evidence-census/sites-deep/` for the verdicts. `source-sample-audit.json` was opened after the verdicts were fixed. `sources.json`, the rows for these sites in `coverage.csv` and the upstream source records in `sites-deep/c-north-india/sources.json` and `sites-deep/d-finds-abroad/sources.json` were read afterwards, to check the entries, explain label differences and find locator mismatches.

## 3. Findings

**Counts.** On the ten assertions I record ten supported, with none qualified, contradicted or unavailable. The package recorded seven supported and three qualified for the same rows. The labels differ on Kalibangan, Banawali and Shortughai, and in each case the package is the more cautious.

**Label differences.** The Kalibangan and Banawali qualifiers state that B3 checked one endpoint page. CISI1 gives no reason to qualify: the contents list K-1 to 122 at pp. 298-326 and B-1 to 37 at pp. 344-349 and 364, and the running heads form unbroken series ending at K122 and B37. The upstream batch record shows that the contents and pp. 298-326 and 344-351 had already been inspected, so the stronger label was available. The Shortughai qualifier concerns whether both seals carry script. Neither cited page says so and the excerpt has no catalogue pages, so that limitation stands, and the census row already says only one seal is verified as inscribed. The audit row's tested assertion omits the script claim, so the row should either name it or be labelled supported.

**Locator problems.**

- Nippur: the audit locator "p. 81 catalogue / PDF 151" combines two pages. PDF 151 is printed p. 113 (Catalogue of Registered Objects); printed p. 81 is PDF 119, the chapter 4 text that the census row cites as pp. 81-82.
- Karanpura: p. 116 holds the graffiti figure only. The seal text is on pp. 114 and 117 (pp. 115-116 are plate and figure pages), and the plates are on pp. 118-119.
- Tepe Yahya: pp. vi and xi show the range and TY-106. The other-scripts element is on pp. xii, xv and xx-xxi. B3's witness list includes PDF 17 (p. xv), so the evidence was in hand.
- Banawali: the contents give pp. 344-349 and 364, and the package range omits one addendum photograph, B-26 a.
- In `coverage.csv` (census rows for four of these sites, not audit rows) four locators are off by one page: Kalibangan K1-65 ends at p. 311 (row: 312); Banawali B1-22 ends at p. 346 (row: 347); the Shortughai preface p. xv is PDF 17 (row: PDF16); Gonur Fig. 115 is on p. 258 (row: p. 259).

**Misattributed figure.** Bhagwanpura Fig. 39 (p. 140) draws one tablet, A-1, in three views. The audit observation says the descriptions and the figure show two specimens. The second tablet, A-2, is in the text on p. 139 and in Pl. XLIII B. The counts of two tablets and nineteen sherds rest on the p. 139 opening and on entries A-1, A-2 and B-1 to B-19.

**Generosity.** No supported label is too generous for the assertion as worded. Three supported rows carry a citation defect that a reader would meet: Nippur (wrong printed page), Bhagwanpura (figure) and Tepe Yahya (incomplete locator). In the Salut row, "two impressed fragments are described" fits the first fragment, and the second receives one sentence.

**Bibliographic entries.** Errors found: the Salut title omits "recent discoveries and"; the IAR2013 title reads "2013-2014" where the volume reads "2013-14", and its author field is corporate although PDF 2 names a chief editor and an editor; the CISI33 URL leads to the author postprint of one essay; the Gonur imprint note is still open although PDF 6 gives the imprint. The CISI33 title page names Parpola and Koskikallio as the editors and names no one else; a 2010 work by Parpola, Pande and Koskikallio is cited on Salut p. 118 and is a different publication. All seven opened flags are backed by saved page images or text, and the recorded hashes match the local files. Two entries describe less than the flag implies: CISI33 was opened as a 40-page excerpt without the catalogue, and the Nippur audit locator cites a page missing from its witness list.

**Process.** One verdict changed during the comparison. Before opening the audit file I had entered qualified for Tepe Yahya because pp. vi and xi do not show the other-scripts element. For Kalibangan I had used pages outside the cited one, so the two rows followed different rules. I then applied the source-wide rule to all ten, which gives supported for Tepe Yahya, and recorded the page gap as a locator defect. Under a page-bound rule, with only the cited pages counting, Kalibangan, Banawali and Tepe Yahya would read qualified, and the label differences would be Shortughai (package more cautious) and Tepe Yahya (package more generous).
