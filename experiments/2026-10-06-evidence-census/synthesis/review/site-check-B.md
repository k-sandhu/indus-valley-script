# Site check B: Pabumath and Daimabad

Independent read-only check of two rows of `experiments/2026-10-06-evidence-census/sites-deep/b-gujarat-kutch-maharashtra/site-rows.json`, made on 2026-10-07. Row pointers read `[name=...] > field`. Page numbers are printed pages unless marked PDF. "Lipi" is `data/normalized/lipi_observed_faces.jsonl`. Verdicts use the brief's four terms; items I did not look at are listed separately under "Not checked".

## Page offsets and local copies

CISI 1. `CISI1.pdf` has 862 PDF pages because it holds one 431-page scan twice: PDF page i and PDF page i+431 carry identical embedded images for all 431 pairs. `sites-deep/c-north-india/cisi1.pdf` is a byte copy of it (same SHA-256, `47a4da42...`, also 862 pages), so the repository holds no separate 431-page file. Each PDF page is one printed page. In the first copy PDF page = printed page + 36 for the Arabic-numbered body; I checked printed 352-355, 357-359, 365, 366 and 375, which are PDF 388-391, 393-395, 401, 402 and 411. Front matter runs PDF page = roman numeral + 4 (title page PDF 5, p.III PDF 7, p.IV PDF 8). In the second copy add 431 (printed 352 = PDF 819). The archive.org text `CISI1OCR.txt` also holds the book twice. The saved renders `CISI1-p388`, `-p394`, `-p411` and `-p8` match PDF pages 388, 394, 411 and 8.

Other offsets. Mahadevan 1977 (`MAHADEVAN1977.pdf`): PDF = printed + 11. Sali 1986 (ASI scan `data/raw/2026-10-06-backward-chronology/asi-daimabad49.pdf`, 763 PDF pages): PDF = printed + 16. Uesugi 2018 (`GAZETTEER2018.pdf`): PDF = printed + 5. IAR 1977-78 printed 21 = PDF 28; IAR 1978-79 printed 67-68 = PDF 73-74; IAR 1980-81 printed 14 = PDF 25.

## Findings

Every number that either row attaches to a source matches that source. Pabumath: one inscribed seal in IAR 1980-81 (printed 14) and one catalogued object in CISI 1 (Pbm-1, printed 358). Daimabad: two sign-bearing seals, four Phase II sherds and one Phase III sherd in Sali (printed 504-505); four seals, one incised tablet and four graffiti sherds in CISI 1 (printed 352-354); Fuls's site figures of 5 and 2 legible signs; and the local-corpus counts of 1/1/1 and 2/2/2. The recomputed Lipi figures are Pabumath 1 face, 1 family, 1 identifier (Pbm-1) and Daimabad 2 faces, 2 families, 2 identifiers (Dmd-1, Dmd-8). The errors are in descriptive fields and locators.

CISI 1 numbering. Pabumath fills the lower third of p.358; Daimabad fills pp.352-354 (three objects, two, four); both are listed in the data table on p.375. A base number identifies one object. Capital letters name sides (A to E occur in these sections), a lower-case letter names a plasticine impression, a numeral after a side letter would name a separate inscription, and bis or ter would name a repeated photograph; these two sections use only side letters and impression letters. Counting base numbers 1..N therefore gives the number of catalogued objects, 1 and 9, and neither inflates nor deflates it. The Daimabad nine include two objects without Indus script, and Sali's tally of script-bearing objects is seven.

Daimabad gap. Only Dmd-1 (round seal) and Dmd-8 (sherd graffito) are in Lipi. The seven absent identifiers are three seals (Dmd-2, 3, 4), one incised tablet (Dmd-5) and three pottery graffiti (Dmd-6, 7, 9). Dmd-4 and Dmd-5 carry no Indus script, so five objects that Sali reports with Indus signs (Dmd-2, 3, 6, 7, 9) are missing from the local corpus. Fuls's total of two legible signs for the site equals the legible signs in the two Lipi texts, so the same gap exists in Fuls's ICIT-based list and does not look like a loss in the local export.

Discrepancies: P13 (KC read as Kutch Museum), P14 (PBM034 for PBM 024, called a registration), D20 (ABD 80/78 for 807A). Qualifications that change how a field reads: D4 (locators), D16 (Daimabad point about 14 km from two independent listings), D22 (dimensions and registration numbers not on the cited pages), P15 and P16 (steatite and the 1978-79 seal come from a volume the row does not cite), P7 (dimension count depends on an unseen definition), D6 and D13 (wording and label). Ledger issues: the DAIMABAD1986 and CISI32 witness labels, the CISI1 render range, the Gazetteer page range, and the FULS2022 entry (marked an opened preview with an empty `opened_in`, while the CISI 3.1 evidence is a search snippet).

Not checked: the "unresolved" fields (`post_cisi_finds`, `final_report`, sealings, tablets, metal and other objects), the search ids, the rights statements, the inside of CISI 2, 3.1 and 3.2, IAR 1979-80 (no local copy), the Fuls book itself (saved transcripts only), the Scribd page itself, and the upstream definition of `faces_with_dimensions`. Not found: any Pbm-2 or Dmd-10 in the CISI 1 section, contents or data list; any Pabumath or Daimabad entry in the Mahadevan 1977 site table; any Dmd or Pbm identifier in Lipi other than Pbm-1, Dmd-1 and Dmd-8.
