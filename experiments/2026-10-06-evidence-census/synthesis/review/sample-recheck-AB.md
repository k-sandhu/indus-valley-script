# Sample recheck A and B: ten assertions from the B3 source-sample audit

Independent read-only review, 2026-10-07. Rows rechecked: Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Balakot, Sarai Khola, Rehman Dheri, Lothal, Dholavira, Rangpur, Desalpar and Nagwada. Nothing in the package was changed.

## Method and conventions

I formed each verdict from the source pages before opening `source-sample-audit.json`, then compared labels and observation text. Pages came from the saved witnesses in `data/raw/2026-10-06-evidence-census/synthesis/reviewer-sources-2026-10-07-rerun/` and, for neighbouring pages, from the original PDFs named in its `source-page-manifest.json`. Two further sources were read on archive.org page scans: the Dholavira report and Rao 1985, vol. 2 (findings 2 and 3). Near each cited page the PDF page differs from the printed page by a fixed offset. IM77: printed + 11. CISI2: roman numeral + 3 in the front matter (PDF18 = xv, PDF21 = xviii, PDF28 = xxv) and printed + 35 in the plates (PDF413 = 378, PDF445–446 = 410–411). LOTHAL2005: printed − 62, because the PDF holds only the article (pp. 65–103). RANGPUR1963: printed + 39. IAR 1963–64: printed + 9. IAR 1987–88: printed + 8. Dholavira has no page images in the package; its numerals come from a text mirror (finding 3). "Not found" means I looked and the item is absent. "Not checked" means I did not look.

## 3. Findings

**1. Counts and disagreements.** Eight assertions are supported (Harappa, Mohenjo-daro, Balakot, Sarai Khola, Rehman Dheri, Rangpur, Desalpar, Nagwada) and two are qualified (Lothal, Dholavira). None is contradicted or unavailable. For the same ten rows the package has seven supported and three qualified, so the labels agree on seven. The package qualifies Balakot and Sarai Khola because Dales 1974 and Halim 1972 were not opened. Both assertions are worded as what CISI2 reports (28 drawings not located, 68 sherds attributed to a 1972 report) and the pages show exactly that, so supported fits. The package's sentence about the unopened articles is an accurate scope note and can stay beside the label. Lothal runs the other way. On the seven agreed rows the observation text is accurate apart from the wording and locator points in the Note column.

**2. Lothal: the 93 and the 91.** The article prints 93 on pp. 67 and 68. It also says "Rao found 93 terracotta sealings" (p. 70), counts 46 of them with a precise provenience (p. 71), and uses 93 as the site total on pp. 73 and 82. The saved pages never mention 91. The 91 is in Rao 1985, vol. 2 (Memoirs of the ASI 78), p. 305, in the introduction to the chapter on seals and sealings: a passage about baking says that 71 of 91 sealings from Lothal came from the warehouse passages and that ten more were baked in the township area. I read that page on an archive.org scan (item `masi78lothalaharappanporttownraos.r.part2_531_D`, leaf n55). Frenez and Tosi cite the same page for the 71 (their note 18, which also records that Rao's warehouse subtotal varies across his publications: 65, 69, 71). The package did not see this page. Its saved copy of Rao 1985 is a 41-character challenge page, the 91 reached batch B through web-excerpt logs (O01, O09, O16), and the synthesis `sources.json` has no entry for Rao 1985. REPORT.md's sentence reads as two studies in disagreement, while the article gives 93 as the excavator's total; neither text explains the difference of two. A faithful sentence would say that Rao p. 305 gives 91, that Frenez and Tosi give 93 for Rao's finds, and that the inventories need an excavation-number crosswalk. I did not check Rao's plate lists (pp. 325–328), which Frenez and Tosi say underlie their provenience count.

**3. Dholavira: figures, pagination, provenance.** All three figure sets occur in the mirror and in page images. The 225 seals and 60 sealings are on p. 233 (the package cites p. 232, which holds only the graffiti photographs, Fig. 8.10). The 194 and 139 are on p. 242 and the 61 sealings on p. 308, as cited. Batch B's Dholavira row (`sites-deep/b-gujarat-kutch-maharashtra/site-rows.json`) also places the graffiti paragraph on p. 232; by the mirror's numerals it is on p. 231. The mirror prints each page numeral after that page's text. That holds for 25 of the 30 chapter-8 figure captions against the report's own list of figures, and in none of the 30 does the preceding numeral equal the listed page. I also read the printed numerals on nine archive.org page images (item `dholavirareportpreliminaryjan2015_860_K`, creator field Dilip Balakrishnan, 938 scanned pages, no title page; leaf = printed page + 16): pp. 232, 233, 234, 238, 240, 241, 242, 243 and 308 match the mirror's text and numerals. In the mirror the p. 232 cite equals the numeral printed before the passage, while the pp. 242 and 308 cites equal the numerals printed after theirs. Applying one convention throughout gives pp. 233, 242 and 308.

The figures are consistent as nested populations of seals but not fully explained: 225 recovered (a few at the National Museum not analysed, p. 238), 204 in the locality analysis (p. 243), 194 in the inscription analysis, 185 in the period analysis and 136 in the motif table against 130 in the prose beside it (pp. 240–241). The sealing figures conflict: 60 on p. 233, 61 on p. 308, "remaining 59" after four undated (61 − 4 = 57), and a pie chart that sums to 58. The prose gives 23% for missing inscriptions where the table gives 11.9%. `conflicts.json` records the 60/61, the 59 and the 23%, and batch B's row notes that the stage subtotals are inconsistent. Neither gives the 58, the 130/136 or the 204/185 populations.

The mirror's provenance is documented only in part. The pdfcoffee page names an uploader and an 87 MB file. The Scribd page for the same title names the same uploader and 940 pages. Neither page says where the PDF came from, and the mirror text starts at the contents page, with no title page or imprint. The archive.org PDF is a second upload, titled "preliminary, Jan 2015", with the same pagination on the nine pages compared. Page numbers can therefore be cited as the report's pagination under the numeral convention above. Whether either file equals the printed ASI volume is undocumented.

**4. Other locators and wording.** Nagwada: the sealing is on p. 20 and the site name on p. 19. Mohenjo-daro: the page says the 3,455 are objects in the photographic card catalogue. REPORT.md's row for IM77 cites pp. 5–7 while the witness list holds p. 6 only. The other locators sit on the printed pages cited: IM77 p. 6, CISI2 pp. xv, xviii, 378, 410–411, Lothal pp. 67–68, Rangpur p. 130, IAR1963 p. 10.

**5. Source entries.** The DHOLAVIRA2015 title and access label are discussed in the table. The LOTHAL2005 rights note says the Zenodo page gave no readable licence. The Zenodo API record now lists `cc-by-4.0` and open access. The depositor sets that field, so a redistribution decision should still go through `knowledge/provenance/REDISTRIBUTION.md`, but the note is out of date. Rao 1985 is absent from `sources.json` although REPORT.md uses its figure.
