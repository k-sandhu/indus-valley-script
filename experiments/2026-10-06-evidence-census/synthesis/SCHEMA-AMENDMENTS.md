# Proposed amendments to the October 2 dataset specification

The census shows that coverage requires explicit source inventories, counting units and archaeological identity layers. The [October 2 schema](../../2026-10-02-dataset-landscape/SCHEMA.md) already separates objects, surfaces, inscriptions, images, alternative transcriptions, assertions and rights. Retain those entities and their missingness rules. The additions below make incomplete inventories, compound carriers and provenance conflicts queryable. Status: Proposed; no schema or earlier plan was edited.

|---|---|---|

[sample audit](source-sample-audit.json), `Nippur`; [priority gaps](priority-gaps.json), ranks 4 and 6. |

| Rights scope extensions to `rights_record` and `asset` | Separate record-data/image/database/derived-annotation scopes; policy version; applicable item flag; notice locator; permission evidence; expiry/review date | The normalized derivatives have no item-level rights links. Each count assertion must specify its counting unit before entering a coverage denominator. A catalogue entry can refer to a carrier, a fragment, several faces or a comparison object. Multiple entries can refer to one carrier. The system should preserve these source assertions and calculate physical-object totals only on accepted identity links. Missing inventories need null counts with an access result, not zero counts.

Add a coverage view keyed by `source_inventory_id`, version and eligibility rule. Its columns should include nominated entries, inspected entries, accepted object matches, unresolved candidates, new represented objects, excluded entries and unavailable entries. Those dispositions must exhaust the acquired source frame. A second view should measure each metadata field on explicitly eligible objects, faces or views, with missingness reasons and independent-review status. Whole-population percentages remain null where the inventory is unavailable.

## Revised local pilot

The [October 2 plan](../../2026-10-02-dataset-landscape/DATASET_PLAN.md) proposes a 200-object local pilot. Keep that size and freeze the actual eligible object IDs before reviewing quality. Use the following acquisition strata to exercise the gaps found here:

| Primary acquisition stratum | Proposed physical objects | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Harappa and Mohenjo-daro, 30 each | 60 | Recover source-backed context and multiple faces in the largest local population. |
| Dholavira and Kalibangan, 20 each | 40 | Test later-edition cutoffs, sealing/seal classes and registration concordances. |
| Lothal | 30 | Pair sealing front/reverse views and archaeological contexts with catalogue entries. |
| Other Gujarat/north-India CISI 1 gaps | 30 | Resolve missing identifiers, including Banawali and Rohira; record excluded comparison objects. |
| Foreign and borderland script-bearing carriers | 20 | Exercise findspot/production roles, exceptional contexts and fragment relationships. |
| Currently unjoined museum records | 20 | Resolve registrations and preserve conflicting dimensions/material descriptions. |
| Total | **200** | Count accepted physical carriers once. |

Assign each accepted object to one primary acquisition stratum; retain its other source memberships. Within each stratum, specify object class, short/damaged text and multiple-face quotas after the eligible inventory has been assembled and before quality outcomes. Reserve uncertain joins in a candidate pool until their identity is reviewed. If a stratum cannot supply its allocation, report the shortfall and amend the selection before freezing the pilot. A catalogue number or a newly downloaded image cannot by itself fill a physical-object slot.

Create a separate companion frame of **50 comparative mark observations**, drawn from Rehman Dheri, Sarai Khola, Balakot, Rangpur, Karanpura and Bhagwanpura where accessible. Its purpose is to test mark classification, phase and firing observations. Its observation count stays separate from the 200-object pilot. Exact eligibility and sampling rules remain Proposed because the full inventories and identities have not been obtained.

For the frozen pilot, require a source locator on every identity and metadata assertion, an explicit rights disposition on every asset, and an inventory disposition on every nominated source row. Review every accepted merge and retain conflicting fields. Measure image availability, findspot, phase, numerical date attribution and independent dating association separately. The October 2 requirement for independent expert review remains; this AI-authored census does not fulfill it. Access and permission requests can be prepared from the gap register, with sending dependent on the user's instruction. This proposal creates no requests or publication artifacts.
