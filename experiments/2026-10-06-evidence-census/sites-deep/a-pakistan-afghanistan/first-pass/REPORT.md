# Findings: Pakistan and Afghanistan site census

The census has 33 site rows from Pakistan and Afghanistan, supported by 90 source-ledger records and 126 distinct web queries dated 2026-10-06 or 2026-10-07. Every row links at least three queries. Five local-corpus labels were left out because the source trail places them in India; the locations and evidence are recorded in `scope-exclusions.json`. Karanpura was found as an additional source-attested Inter-Riverine site in Rajasthan, but it falls outside this batch's national scope.

The bibliography fields identify a site-level final or comprehensive excavation publication for 14 sites: Allahdino, Amri, Balakot, Chanhu-daro, Harappa, Kot Diji, Mehrgarh, Mohenjo-daro, Mundigak, Nausharo, Nindowari, Pirak, Rehman Dheri and Shortughai. This is a bibliography-level identification, not a claim that every volume or catalogue was fully inspected. The 19 remaining rows have no final report verified in this pass; some have earlier excavation notices, survey reports, catalogue entries or incomplete bibliographic leads. See each row's `publications` and `open_problems` fields.

Seven sites have at least one numeric published-count record: Balakot, Chanhu-daro, Harappa, Lakhanjo-daro, Mehrgarh, Mohenjo-daro and Nausharo. The records include two physical seals and one inscribed bangle reported at Balakot; 22 Harappa tablets and 66 illustrated faces of those same tablets; 850 pottery marks at Mehrgarh; 66 Chanhu-daro objects in a secondary reproduction of Mahadevan's table; and corpus summaries of 70 Chanhu-daro, 1,585 Mohenjo-daro and 37 Nausharo unique texts. Lakhanjo-daro's three unfinished seals were not established as inscribed. The 66 faces are not additional objects, and the text totals are deduplicated corpus values rather than physical counts. Source IDs, dates, bases and locators are in `aggregate.json` and `site-count-comparison.json`.

The local snapshot joins 27 rows and reports 4,832 face rows, 3,903 object-family records and 3,634 distinct CISI identifiers. Only seven sites have any numeric count claim in this source set, and even those numbers cannot be directly reconciled with the local data without object-level joins. Highest CISI sequence numbers are not completeness measures: numbering may have gaps, include different object classes or reflect cataloguing conventions. Local totals are available by site in the comparison file.

The batch has 27 rows with a post-CISI note or lead. That total includes leads and later corpus/catalogue references, not only securely excavated new objects. Each row distinguishes later excavation claims from catalogue updates, news and incompletely documented reports. It should be read alongside `post_cisi_finds`, source access state and the exact searches in `searches.json`.

The largest unresolved gaps are full CISI page-range checks for several sites; reconciled object counts by class; inspection of excavation catalogues for context, dimensions, material and registration numbers; confirmation of current artifact custody; and documented digital reuse terms. The opened-source/page level varies by row. Null count fields mean the recorded query set did not recover a class total, not that no objects exist. `sources.json` records whether a source was opened or encountered only as a search lead/snippet. The project protocol and row fields preserve the limits of each claim.
