## Focused pass, 7 October 2026

The protocol was amended before the new acquisition. Museums of India routes initially returned HTTP 404 because the museum parameter was missing or empty. The portal's own JavaScript supplied museumId=all and an internal JSON fetch route. Seven complete paginated searches then returned 4,007 unique record IDs. All 158 candidate object pages returned HTTP 200. Institution/accession deduplication left 154 candidates, including 94 confirmed originals and ten plaster copies. [Portal counts](portal-candidate-counts.csv), [queries](focused-query-log.csv).

The first automatic classification treated the word cast in ancient copper-tablet descriptions as a modern reproduction and admitted descriptions mentioning another script or denying script. A dated protocol clarification and second extraction corrected those rules. The initial extraction remains in raw portal-record-review.json; the revised review is separate. Forty-four candidates still lack a confirmed inscription in the inspected description.

Met's website supplied records despite the API failure. The page=2 parameter repeated its first 42 results; offset=42 supplied the remaining 34. Five seals were inspected, including three small image witnesses. Penn's inscription filter enumerated 34 records, separating five original seals from 29 plaster impressions. Its field numbers and published plate references supported five joins. The five originals also carry MFA numbers, so they were counted once at Penn.

The existing complete CISI Volume 1 scan supplied a published museum/excavation concordance. OCR located candidate rows; page images were checked before recording 42 rows. The official PDF URL previously labelled Volume 3 actually identifies Volume 1, and the current source register corrects that attribution. A new stream timed out after 12,058,624 bytes; the partial file was preserved and excluded from joins. The full earlier scan was reused.

The corpus's undelimited strings were already present in the source CSV. Exact/normalized matching, CISI concordances and field/plate references yielded 61 matched museum records and 67 faces. A normalized DKI838 hit selected another corpus object; CISI's museum registration row supplied M-543 and the rejected collision was retained. Marshall's Volume II table, printed p. 404, explicitly paired HR 5635 with plate number 345, explaining HR 5635345. [Audit](paired-full-audit.csv), raw paired-comparisons.json and marshall-table-055.png.

Direct BM and MFA routes remained HTTP 403, Louvre search returned 429 while object JSON succeeded, NMMA requests timed out, and Karachi's focused request failed during SSL transfer. Browser alternatives also failed to provide valid counts. These attempts remain recorded; their totals remain unmeasured. Indian portal records reserve rights, Met eligible assets use CC0, Penn's downloadable data use CC BY 4.0, and Louvre record data use Etalab Open Licence 2.0. Image conditions were reviewed separately.
