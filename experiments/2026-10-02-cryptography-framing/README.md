# CRYPTO-01: verified synthetic label symmetry

The [protocol](protocol.md) records the exact demonstration. [Results](results.json) are generated by [the reusable script](../../src/exploratory/permutation_identifiability.py).

Enumerating all **720** relabellings of a six-symbol synthetic corpus preserves its consistently fitted Markov score, unigram entropy and transition-count multiset. Fixing two symbol identities leaves **24** maps. Reproduce with `python src/exploratory/permutation_identifiability.py`.

This illustrates why good structural prediction alone cannot select arbitrary meanings. It suggests measuring which independent contextual constraints actually shrink the mapping ambiguity. It is a mathematical simulation, not evidence that the Indus system is encrypted, not a proof that all meanings are unrecoverable, and not a claim of a new theorem.
