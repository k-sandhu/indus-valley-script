# Harappa short records: source-linked analysis

This exploratory study uses the 22 incised steatite tablets H2218–H2239, shown as specimens 2–23 in Meadow and Kenoyer's 2000 Figure 4. It asks whether the graphic family and separate stroke count on one face predict those on the other two faces, and identifies object pairs whose inscriptions differ only in count or arrangement. The earlier object frame and representation audit supply registration joins and unresolved drawing conventions. The new work adds a complete short-record corpus join, renewed image inspection and finite ambiguity bounds. No prediction experiment or generated corpus is rerun.

## Inputs and units

The physical tablet is the primary unit. Its source registration, excavation unit and item, CISI key and published microscopic engraver attribution remain separate fields. Top, middle and bottom identify displayed views. A, D and C denote the previously coded angle-like, diamond-like and complex graphic families. Exact derivative codes are preserved alongside those observations in a local-only joined table. A code's numerical value is left unspecified.

The source is the preserved author manuscript of *The “Tiny Steatite Seals” (Incised Steatite Tablets) of Harappa: Some Observations on Their Context and Dating*, Richard H. Meadow and Jonathan Mark Kenoyer, 2000. Figure 4 is PDF page 15, displayed author page 14; its caption is PDF page 16, displayed page 15. The earlier book-page mappings are retained in source provenance. The native figure has a 0–3 cm scale bar. Nearby source-defined objects and the same-context seal are comparison records with separately stated inclusion and join status.

## Observation and analysis order

1. Freeze this protocol before renewed inspection. Preserve all earlier annotations and new disagreements.
2. Reconstruct the 22 source registrations and join normalized Lipi rows by CISI plus the existing registration mapping. Preserve all three rows without the length filter. Inventory image-index routes and witness labels. Derivative A/B/C suffixes are not assigned to displayed faces until their graphic correspondence is checked.
3. Inspect the native figure and the available image routes for H2238, H2230 and H2234. Record each visible graphic family, its displayed position, separate stroke count or candidate set, damage and witness type. Review the other specimens sufficiently to build count candidate sets. Unreadable faces retain unknown count; the published three-or-four description supplies a conditional domain distinct from a directly read count.
4. Calculate within-tablet family coverage, count profiles, exact or bounded count agreement and arrangement classes. Report results by physical tablet and by shared deposit and published engraver attribution. These dependencies limit a general frequency claim.
5. Enumerate candidate completions for small unresolved domains to bound relations. Analyze fixed displayed views, exchangeable lower faces, common end reversal and independently oriented drawings. Count relations which ignore order need no face-order convention.
6. Find inscription-equivalent object pairs and pairs differing in one stroke group or one module under each declared representation. Retain exact derivative-code comparisons separately from source-observed comparisons. A pair confirmed only by an imported code is a reinspection target.
7. Specify an additional physical observation that could separate a fixed template, identity-bearing record and counted account: readable count changes, common physical orientation, cutting sequence or an independently linked counted referent. State the predicted observable outcome for each bounded test.

## Checks and preservation

The helper writes a source-keyed local joined dataset and tracked own observations, aggregates and provenance. Independent scalar checks reconstruct registration coverage, face totals, count bounds and pair counts without calling the analysis routine. Endpoint directories are created once and refuse replacement. All raw image witnesses and full imported inscription rows stay under local-only `data/raw/2026-10-02-connections-and-priority/harappa-short-records/`. Our observations cite parent asset hashes and pixel bounds. Source images, modern transcriptions, published production interpretations and our observations are distinguished in the field schema.

The prior image work supplies only seven directly counted faces and three unreadable lower pairs. New pixel inspections can change those fields in this new branch. Drawing hatching and incomplete physical orientation may still leave several relations bounded. The material currently supplies no independently linked commodity quantity or named person, so the analysis measures graphic/count covariation and sets out a physical discriminating test.
