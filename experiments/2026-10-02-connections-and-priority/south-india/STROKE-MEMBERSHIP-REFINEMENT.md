# Extending the count without a complete component-name map

The first endpoint required every component to have a resolved government identifier and retained 115 of 209 compound keys. It excluded forms when an unrelated component lacked a name. The plain stroke components themselves have a complete, distinct mapping: sixteen RMRL codes map to sign 39 and variants 39.1–39.15.

The refined calculation uses membership in that fixed sixteen-code set. Other component codes remain unnamed. This suffices to count explicit plain stroke components, their positions and their contiguous blocks in the portal representation. Forms whose arrays contain no explicit plain stroke code remain opaque for this question, including composites stored as a single whole-form code. No internal breakdown is invented for them.

This refinement was selected after the first endpoint and a source check. Three printed concordance examples were checked. Two agree with the portal component arrays; form 4.19C has an extra stroke component in the printed table. The refined endpoint therefore describes the frozen portal representation. Its archaeological use requires checking more forms against the printed concordance and specimen photographs.

Preserve `stroke-context-v1/` and write the refined result to `stroke-membership-v2/`. Report the count of opaque forms alongside the resolved forms and keep the representation disagreement in the result.
