# Tamil Nadu specimen connections

This source-acquisition study starts on 2 October 2026 after a search found the Tamil Nadu State Department of Archaeology and Roja Muthiah Research Library graffiti portal. The questions concern data that can connect graphic forms to an independently recorded physical or numerical observation.

Inspect the portal's public catalogue and user guide, the government library's 2025 morphological study and its 2026 *Inscribed Potsherds of Tamil Nadu* volumes. Record source identities, exact retrieval routes and hashes. Downloaded pages, catalogue snapshots, specimen photographs and whole source tables remain local under `data/raw/2026-10-02-connections-and-priority/south-india/`.

The analysis asks which available fields have specimen-level keys, which are aggregate distributions, and whether one can join a graffiti form to a measured vessel capacity, weight, independent count, dating sample or readable Tamili inscription. Preserve the government's sign numbers and the library's internal numbers as separate namespaces. Curated Indus and Brahmi shape parallels are author assertions; record how their rows relate to catalogue entries.

First acquire the public base-sign, sites, filter-options, Indus-comparison and sign-family routes that the deployed client uses. Request each route once in the acquisition run, with a maximum of four concurrent reads. Freeze the resulting bytes. Inspect the catalogue volumes for numerical marks, counts, pre-/post-firing marks and shared Brahmi/graffiti specimens. Identify exact pages and specimen registrations for any useful bridge. Do not infer joint specimen associations from separate marginal arrays.

Report the observed coverage, missing keys and the best bounded next test. Any association chosen after this inspection is exploratory. Keep failed retrieval routes and corrected routes in the acquisition log; future acquisition should start from the working route.
