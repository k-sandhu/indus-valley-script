This continuation answers the request for an independent assessment of our contributions and for parallel investigation of numerical links through Mesopotamia, Oxus, Central Asia and South India. It also takes the strongest source-based paths from the decipherment readiness review into new analyses of Harappa and Lothal.

The [plain-language account](../../knowledge/directions/regional-numerical-connections-2026-10-02.md) brings the results together. Five branches retain their own protocols, source coordinates, calculations, verification and retrospectives:

The independent novelty review assessed the existing work before these new regional calculations were integrated. Its report remains unchanged as the review endpoint. The new source analyses are exploratory. Published masses, marks, archaeological observations and historical proposals retain their authorship; our contribution consists of the joins, bounded calculations, checks and acquisition targets named in each report. No verified Indus numerical value was obtained.
