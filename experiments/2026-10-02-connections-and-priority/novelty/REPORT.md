# Which data connections contribute new research?

Independent review, 2 October 2026. This review read the four manuscripts, their indexed outcomes, the novelty matrix, mapping precedents, readiness reports and relevant retrospectives. It searched primary literature for counterprecedents and inspected several original papers directly. The search record is [primary-source-search.json](primary-source-search.json); the checked numerical extracts and input hashes are [evidence-check.json](evidence-check.json). No predictor, simulation or frozen endpoint was rerun.

Within the four-paper baseline, the project contributes original measurements, a useful source-linked evidence collection and several controlled examples of inference failure. It connected physical aliases to held-out prediction, repeated inscriptions to fastening traces, source inclusion to permutation laws, numerical operations to inventory completeness, surviving numerical alternatives to informative observations, and reference records to latent forms and symbol assignment. The strongest contribution is measuring how those connections change an evaluation. The inspected literature supplies the general methods: duplicate analysis, Markov restoration, contact-trace interpretation, additive accounts, constrained fitting, permutation blocks, latent inference and parameter identification.

An original measured result is a new calculation on a specified population, even when its method is established. An application transfers an existing method to this problem. Evidence curation joins and checks previously published observations. A genuinely new method would need a distinct algorithm or theoretical result and a comparison showing what it adds beyond the relevant predecessors. The rows below can contain more than one of these contributions.

## Importance and publication decisions

The source links and computational links have different consequences. Lothal, Harappa and catalogue reconciliation improve which archaeological observations can be compared. The simulation branches improve which model, sampling law or new measurement should be used once the needed observations exist. Their practical importance follows from the specific errors they expose: a hidden alias crossing a split, a copied object generating excess rejections, a complete inventory repairing local membership, a wrong scale passing every additive relation, or a fitted objective preferring the wrong correspondence.

| Candidate | Precise contribution that merits a manuscript or note now | Missing validation that changes its standing |
|---|---|---|
| **Paper 001: first empirical priority.** | A paper on predictive constraints in a declared transcription population, with aliases, alternate candidate events, common-mask Markov comparisons and the failed gap-count result. The 0.848-bit gain and accuracy/loss tradeoff are publishable questions. | Independent epigraphic review, a source-access/reuse route, a fresh frozen population, and a faithful common-protocol comparison with the earlier Markov smoothing implementation. | **Paper 002: strongest methods consolidation.** | A focused methods paper or technical case study on explicit conditional spaces, catalogue selection and fitting feasibility. ## Evidence acquired during the present review

The new Susa source work opens the most concrete numerical research path in this turn. Ascalone's [*Bronze Age Weights from Mesopotamia, Iran & Greater Indus Valley*](https://airus.unisalento.it/retrieve/c39309c8-8dae-4dba-b990-86171612edf6/E.%20Ascalone%2C%20Bronze%20Age%20Weights%20from%20Mesopotamia%2C%20Iran%20and%20Greater%20Indus%20Valley_compressed.pdf), printed p.49, catalogue 224, identifies AS2615/SB13677 as a 334.25 g hematite ovoid bearing four circles. The [Louvre record](https://collections.louvre.fr/ark:/53355/cl010186254) independently joins the two identifiers and describes four points. Catalogue 698, printed p.63, records the 27.25 g jasper cuboid AS5724/SB17774; pp.69–70 propose an Indus import and weight-system correspondences, including an equivalence interpretation for catalogue 224's marks. The present contribution is recovering exact object identities and making the mass, marks, provenance and prior interpretation available together for a reproducible compatibility test. The [regional acquisition manifest](../regional-west/source-acquisition-v1.json) preserves source hashes and local-only rights status. Fresh mass measurement, mark inspection, competing unit models and independent dating/context would make this a stronger test than another supplied additive simulation.

The newly acquired [2026 Volume I](https://tamildigitallibrary.in/assets/docs/uploads/catalogue_article_file/BOK/upload/2026/03/TVA_TVA_BOK_062969/upload_primary_20260302113351638_20260302111421.pdf) and [Volume II](https://tamildigitallibrary.in/assets/docs/uploads/catalogue_article_file/BOK/upload/2026/03/TVA_TVA_BOK_062968/upload_primary_20260302120759232_20260302110011.pdf) of *Inscribed Potsherds of Tamil Nadu: Graffiti and Tamiḻi* provide 740 and 712 pages respectively, confirmed from their title pages and saved PDFs. The [Volume I manifest](../south-india/inscribed-potsherds-vol1-2026.pdf.acquisition.json) and [Volume II manifest](../south-india/volume2-acquisition.json) document the acquisitions. Their value is the prospect of joining graffiti, readable Tamiḻi and archaeological context at a named specimen. At this review's endpoint, the source acquisition is complete and that specimen-level numerical bridge is still being investigated. A joined, audited observation would be a new project result; the catalogue authors retain credit for the underlying observations.

The remaining originality is precise: new measured evaluations, finite examples of source-conditioned sampling laws, parameter-specific recovery and identification results, and checked joins among published witnesses. This bounded search covered the assigned connection families and close primary counterprecedents; broader first-use priority remains an open bibliographic question. The next publication priority is the empirical prediction paper, followed by a focused conditional-law paper and a physical-source data note. The new Susa object joins deserve immediate evidence work because they connect actual measured mass to actual marks and known catalogue identities.
