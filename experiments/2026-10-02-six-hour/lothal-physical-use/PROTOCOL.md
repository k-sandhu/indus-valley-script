# A source-first table of sealing use

ARCH-02. Acquisition and observation protocol, 2 October 2026.

Read Frenez and Tosi's 2005 original Lothal chapter and inspect its illustrations. The source was located through the authors' Zenodo deposit. Record publication/deposit dates separately, preserve the acquired file and hash, and keep source PDF, extracted text and rendered images local.

Build an observation ledger for every individually identified object or illustrated sealing relevant to reverse-side attachment traces. Record the chapter's object identifier, exact page/figure/table coordinate, impression side, visible trace and the authors' physical-use interpretation. Keep the observable trace separate from that interpretation. Retain uncertain and unillustrated cases, repeated illustrations, compound impressions and missing identifiers.

First assess whether the source offers a join between seal impression/text identity and reverse-side use. If it does, measure coverage and variation: repeated stamp across attachment categories, multiple stamps on one support, and repeated attachment patterns across stamp identities. Use the object as the observation unit and distinguish extra views from extra objects. Avoid choosing objects by an interesting inscription.

The initial result is a source-coverage and hypothesis table. Any association calculation requires a separate protocol after checking independent identities, label counts and the nature of the published typology. Proposed follow-ups should state which physical observation distinguishes ownership, access control, movement or quantity hypotheses. Link each assertion to inspected source coordinates and retain failed acquisition or joining routes.
