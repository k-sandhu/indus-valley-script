# Fields for a physical-use comparison

A sealing preserves the shape of the surface against which soft clay was pressed, the route of a fastening, the stamp on its exposed face and later damage. These traces can help reconstruct the writer or seal user's task. The source's categories combine several of those dimensions: `woven_straw` describes contact texture, `wooden_box` proposes a support, and `single_rope` describes an attachment. Record each dimension separately so that a comparison follows the surviving physical evidence.

Use one object record, one record per photographed view and one record per asserted trace. Keep the author's surface terms where available. Give an unlabelled photograph a view identifier and leave its physical side unresolved until the views can be matched.

| Field | Values or recording rule | Source example |
|---|---|---|
| Contact geometry | flat, concave, convex, corner, channel; several may occur | L206's deep concavity; L163's folded edge |
| Contact texture | parallel ribs, crossing fibres, pleats, smooth region, rough region, unreadable | L124's crossing ribbed marks; L143's parallel bands |
| Fastening trace | cord channel, knot recess, wrap around edge, none visible, unresolved | L163's channel and edge geometry |
| Exposed stamp surface | view coordinate, frame visibility, overlap, stamp-count assertion | L174's differing prose and table counts |
| Clay manipulation marks | parallel curved cuts, finger ridges, smearing; count and count basis | Figure 6's unnumbered inset |
| Preservation | broken edge, missing surface, concretion, low image contrast | L198's reported concretions |
| Proposed support | source author's vessel, peg, wood, cane, locker or alternatives | L124's proposed vessel cover |
| Proposed action | closing, wrapping, pressing, removing; author and source location | L208's proposed rope supervision |
| Context basis | documented findspot, regional association, stamp-derived inference, unresolved | W, W2 and W1 have distinct bases |
| Assertion origin | image observation, source table, source prose, corpus metadata, new hypothesis | Every assertion carries this field |

Store missingness as `not_visible_in_available_view`, `unreadable_image`, `source_not_reported` or `surface_missing`, with a brief reason. A broken-away region and an intact region with no visible cut provide different evidence. The chapter notes this explicitly for L165 and L170, where fingernail marks could have occupied lost clay.

Keep the chapter's overall object category as a source field. Additional fields preserve detail within and across its categories. L124 can carry `contact_texture=crossing_ribbed_marks`, a source assertion of woven straw and a possible vessel-cover interpretation. L198 can retain the table's structural category while recording the prose's locker alternative and concretion problems.
