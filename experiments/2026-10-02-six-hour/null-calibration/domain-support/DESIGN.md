# Defining a fitting statistic over its conditional space

The [saved witness](main-v2/results.json) contains twenty objects whose two-face inscriptions form a chain of shared whole strings. The physical/text grouping joins all forty records into one component. Fitting the preserved five-fold routine raises `ZeroDivisionError`. One ordered-vector shuffle produces twenty components and a finite score, while preserving the same vector margins, expanded record margins and metadata. Both tables have positive probability under the declared noisy-face generator. The first witness attempt called an unavailable helper; [its failed source and report](main/) remain saved. The corrected witness uses the frozen `group_records` function.

The conditional table-law proof establishes uniform sampling after the appropriate margins are fixed. It applies to this space, including its infeasible table. The rank proof additionally needs a real-valued statistic for every table that the reference can sample. The preserved fitting function does not supply one for the connected witness.

One design can extend the statistic with a stated score for tables that have too few fitting components. For example, a separate protocol can assign zero gain to a reference table with fewer than five components and abstain whenever the observed corpus has that problem. This defines a total reference statistic over the declared finite design while making the observed test's abstention visible. The constant and guard rule are part of the procedure and must be recorded before its outcomes. A mathematical rank bound then concerns that extended statistic, with the same extension used when ranking every table.

A second design can condition the observed/reference law on fitting feasibility. It samples uniformly from the feasible subset of the original conditional space. Rejection from the original uniform law provides that sampler when feasibility is checked for every proposal. The interpretation then includes the observation-selection event, or explicitly conditions inference on the observed corpus being feasible. Its conditional probability and acceptance rate belong in the sampling argument.

The current main runs retain their original procedure and fixed proposals. Their completed simulation rejection counts measure that procedure on the sampled populations and reference tables. The fully enumerated unique-record example has six distinct components in every table, so its complete nested-score rank calculation also retains its stated finite-space interpretation. The undefined-support witness limits a universal exact guarantee for the unextended noisy-face fitting procedure. A future use should declare either the total statistic and abstention rule or the feasibility-conditioned observation/reference law together.
