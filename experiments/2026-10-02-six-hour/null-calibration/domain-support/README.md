# Fitting feasibility changes the reference question

The same ordered face-vector margins can support both a finite fit and a fitting failure. In the saved twenty-object witness, consecutive objects share a complete inscription. The physical/text grouping joins them into one component, and the preserved five-fold routine raises `ZeroDivisionError`. A single within-stratum vector shuffle creates twenty components and a finite fit. Both tables have positive probability under the declared noisy-face generator. [Witness and failed fit](main-v2/results.json), [design interpretation](DESIGN.md), [independent verification](verification.json).

A separate exact experiment compares two declared responses to that domain limit. It uses the original 8,000 balanced binary tables. Of these, 5,760 have at least five components; 2,240 cause observed abstention. The extended statistic assigns zero gain to an infeasible reference table. The other reference conditions its law on the feasible subset. Their observed rejection fractions are:

| Nominal threshold | Extended law, all 8,000 observations | Extended law, given a feasible observation | Feasible reference, all 5,760 feasible observations | Feasible reference, all 8,000 observations with abstention |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1% | 0.3250% | 0.4514% | 0.4514% | 0.3250% |
| 5% | 5.0000% | 6.9444% | 4.7917% | 3.4500% |
| 10% | 9.8750% | 13.7153% | 9.9653% | 7.1750% |

At a 5% threshold, the extended law rejects 400 of all 8,000 tables. Those rejections occupy 400 of the 5,760 feasible tables, or 6.9444%. Conditioning the reference on feasibility changes the tail denominator and rejects 276 of 5,760 tables, or 4.7917%. The sampling population and abstention rule therefore belong alongside a reported rejection rate. [Exact counts and hashes](guard-exact/results.json), [all scores and tails](guard-exact/scores_and_tails.json), [pre-outcome guard protocol](GUARD_PROTOCOL.md).

The experiment reused all 2,880 frozen unique-table scores and fitted the 2,880 five-component tables once. It took 20.21 seconds. An independent graph traversal checked every component count, score-frequency counting checked every full and feasible tail, and six selected scores were refitted. The witness's shuffle and its fitting exception were also replayed. All checks passed.

The initial witness source used an unavailable helper name, `rebuild_groups`. Its [failed source and report](main/) are preserved. The corrected source calls the existing `group_records` function and writes a new endpoint. The main calibration runs and original scoring algorithms were left unchanged.

The conditional sampler's uniform law and the score's domain are separate parts of an exact test. This experiment supplies a finite example of a stated extension and a stated selection law. The current manuscript can report the main measured calibration outcomes together with the connected-table domain limit; a future implementation should declare its feasibility rule before fitting a new corpus.

## A component guarantee under the copy premise

When every physical connected block contains one complete string value, physical/text closure gives one component per distinct string. The number of used codes at any fixed slot is then a lower bound on components. Singleton-record shuffling preserves this premise. Shuffling literal-copy object columns preserves it as well, because the two expanded records remain identical. The original completed populations have bounds of eight for independent and unique records, and six to eight for matched copy references. The uniqueness-conditioned reference has exactly forty components throughout its conditional support.

The [support audit](support-bound/results.json) checks all 3,000 observed margins and 396,000 saved reference groupings against the completed full audit. It separately regenerates 198,000 physical-copy reference tables. Every matched object reference preserves the copy premise; all 99,000 ordinary copied-object record references violate it. Their sampled component counts are nine to twenty, but the slot-inventory guarantee requires the copy premise. The nonidentical-face witness has all eight codes in a slot and only one component, showing where that premise matters.

Five positive-weight components suffice for the preserved allocator. Its first five greedy assignments fill the five outer folds, leaving at least four components in each outer training set. Three inner folds then each receive a component, leaving at least two in every inner training set. All records here have three slots, so their training and held-out noninitial counts are positive. Positive smoothing and normalization keep these bounded simulation scores finite. The audit exercises the actual allocation functions in 29,169 cases, including a thirty-six-record component beside four singletons. Every one of 145,845 outer and 437,535 inner splits satisfies those minima. It refits no scores.
