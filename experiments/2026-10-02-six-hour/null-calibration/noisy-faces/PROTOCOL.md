# Known-null calibration for correlated, nonidentical faces

Written before the pilot and main outcomes, October 2, 2026. Each synthetic population has twenty independent latent objects, ten in each of two site/type strata. Each object has three independent slots with the eight-code probabilities used in the existing calibration study. Generate two observed faces per object. Independently at every face/slot, keep the latent sign with probability .9 or replace it uniformly with one of the other seven codes with probability .1. The two faces share their latent signs and may differ. Their slotwise equality probability is `.9² + .1²/7 = .8114285714`.

Keep every latent string, replacement indicator and observed face. Fits and references receive only observed rows and their metadata. Physical aliases connect the two faces. Retain the immutable original method snapshots, five outer folds, three training-only inner folds, grid 5/20/100/500/2000 and fitting seed 20261001. Every observed and reference corpus rebuilds artifact/text groups and reruns the complete procedure.

Compare ordinary record-column shuffling with a face-vector reference. For each length/slot/site/type cell, the latter permutes ordered two-face sign vectors among objects, then restores the two signs to the destination object's fixed face labels. It preserves the multiset of joint face vectors, expanded record margins and fixed physical aliases. This conditional law follows the generator: ordered face vectors are independent across objects within a stratum and across slots, with a shared probability distribution in each cell. Their probability product is constant when the vector-count multiset is fixed. A uniform permutation samples that conditional space, with equal multiplicity for duplicated vectors.

First run a timing pilot with two populations and nine fitted draws per reference. Save its source, data and endpoints separately. Use its timing to confirm a main budget of 1,000 independently generated populations and 99 draws per reference, giving 198,000 fitted reference corpora plus 1,000 observed fits. The main probability is `(1 + reference scores ≥ observed score)/(1 + 99)`. Report all rejection counts at .01, .05 and .10 with 95% Wilson intervals. The references share each observed population but use disjoint seed namespaces; report paired disagreement counts and a 20,000-replicate paired-population bootstrap for their rejection-rate difference, seed 2026100204.

Use generator seeds 40261002+1009i for main population i. Reference seeds are 2,000,000,000+1,000,000i+j for record shuffling and 4,000,000,000+1,000,000i+j for vector shuffling, j=0,...,98. Pilot generator/reference seeds use separate bases 50261002, 6,000,000,000 and 8,000,000,000. These namespaces are disjoint within and between this study and the earlier calibration runs.

Before the main run, verify latent/observed generation, margin and alias preservation, exclusion of latent truth from fits, vector-count preservation and score counting. Enumerate four-object binary column spaces with four distinct vectors and with two repeated vectors. Derive exact generating permutation multiplicities. Sample 100,000 three-column tables for each vector-count case with seed bases 2026100205 and 2026100206, incrementing by 100 per draw, preserving every table count. This finite calculation checks the new conditional sampler; its tiny space has no nested-fitting endpoint.

The final audit regenerates every observed population and every reference from its recorded seed, independently rebuilds all groups and fold loads, checks all p-values and aggregates, and replays selected complete fits. Record how often the ordinary reference changes within-object vector counts, plus observed face equality and complete-string equality. Preserve interrupted/failed trials and completed results.
