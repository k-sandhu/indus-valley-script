# Shared-quality work record

The earlier imperfect-face study used one replacement rate for every object. This follow-up supplies an observed low-error or high-error class that remains fixed across an object's slots. The earlier main, channel and domain outcomes were known when the protocol was written. The new populations and outcomes were generated after its parameters, references, score extension and abstention rule were frozen.

The sampler extends the ordered-vector profile with quality for one reference and retains the broad site/type profile for the other. Quality remains a stored metadata field; the preserved fitting model continues to read its existing site/type stratum. A source inspection and quality-relabeling fits verify that separation. Every vector proposal preserves physical aliases, all supplied metadata and broad scalar/vector margins. The quality reference additionally preserves quality-specific margins.

The two-population pilot completed thirty-eight fits in .6738 seconds. Its elapsed time supported retaining the proposed 300-population budget. Main throughput was slower than that pilot extrapolation: 59,700 observed/reference fits took 1,599.17 seconds rather than about eighteen minutes. The count, seeds and parameters remained fixed, and the allocated time allowed completion. No fitting exception or guarded state occurred in the main endpoint.

The nested comparison gives a one-point paired difference at 5%, interval −1 to 3 points. Both mean p-values are close to .51. The exact table calculation still finds unequal probabilities under broad vector counts and a uniform restricted law after quality-specific conditioning. It enumerates six broad columns and four quality columns, and independently replays all 20,000 sampler draws. Those two results address different measurements: a reference's stated law and this statistic's observed sensitivity to it.

An analytic calculation was declared during the run, after 100 populations had completed and before their fitted main scores or p-values were inspected here. It computes every single-face covariance and ordered-vector event covariance under the supplied quality mixture. Rational product calculations check 256 and 16,384 entries. Independent latent-sign-pair sums replay all single-face entries and four vector maximum witnesses. The main protocol and its fitted results are untouched.

The final calibration audit reconstructed all 300 populations and 59,400 proposals, all fixed quality/physical relations, every vector/scalar margin, independent graph/fold allocations and every inclusive rank. It repeated sixteen fits and the 20,000 paired bootstrap draws. Both generated replacement flags and agreement counts match their supplied class correspondence. All checks passed. The generator, sampler, statistic snapshots, protocol, audit sources and input hashes remain available in this tree.

The next experiment uses fresh populations and a simple integer count of adjacent face-agreement events. Its protocol is explicitly post-outcome. It can determine whether the same known reference mismatch becomes visible when the statistic reads the paired observation mechanism directly. Any result belongs alongside the small nested comparison already reported.
