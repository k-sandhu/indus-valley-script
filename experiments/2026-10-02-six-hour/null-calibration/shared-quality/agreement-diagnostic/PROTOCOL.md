# Agreement events as a score for the shared-quality mechanism

Written October 2, 2026, after the completed shared-quality nested endpoint and full audit. Its 5% rejection rates are 4% for broad vectors and 3% for quality-conditioned vectors, with a paired interval of −1 to 3 percentage points. That outcome motivated this new statistic. The nested score, populations and outputs remain frozen. The present diagnostic uses fresh populations and is explicitly post-outcome method development.

Reuse the immutable shared-quality generator and vector samplers. Generate 1,000 fresh twenty-object populations with two faces and three slots, the same two sites, eight categories, .025/.60 fixed error classes and latent probabilities. For object o, let A(o,i) equal one when its two observed faces agree at slot i, and zero otherwise. Define the integer statistic `S=Σ_o[A(o,0)A(o,1)+A(o,1)A(o,2)]`. It reads observed signs and fixed object pairs. Latent signs and quality labels never enter the score. Its range is zero to forty and it is defined on every observed table.

Compare broad and quality-conditioned ordered-vector references with 99 draws each on the same populations. Preserve all rows/truth, observed scores, reference scores, seeds and margin checks. Use `(1 + count(reference S ≥ observed S))/100`, including ties. Report rejection fractions at .01, .05 and .10 with Wilson intervals, all paired disagreements, and 20,000 paired population-bootstrap intervals, seed 2026100211. No statistic, threshold or sample count changes after generation.

Generator seeds are 80,261,002 + 1,009i. Reference bases are 160 and 180 billion + 1,000,000i + draw index. They are disjoint from the completed main reference streams and the fixed 1,000-generator list will be checked against previous population seeds. No nested model is refitted.

For a reference cell with n objects and agreement counts a0,a1,a2, independent column permutations give conditional mean `(a0*a1+a1*a2)/n`. Sum it over the two broad cells or four quality cells. The supplied generator has mean score `20*(H_low²+H_high²)`, where H is each class's face-agreement probability. The mean broad-reference expectation over generated populations is `40*((H_low+H_high)/2)²`. Their difference is `10*(H_low−H_high)²`.

Compute exact conditional score distributions as an additional fixed endpoint. Ordered-vector permutations induce uniform binary agreement arrangements given their counts. Enumerate their integer multiplicities by a row-state dynamic program for all column-count triples at n=5 and n=10. Verify totals against products of binomial coefficients and means against the expression above. Convolve those cell distributions for every observed population and report exact inclusive tails alongside the 99-draw tails. A separate hypergeometric-intersection calculation will check every cell distribution without using the row-state recurrence.

The final audit independently generates every fresh population, computes S from observed pairs, replays all 198,000 vector shuffles and margins, checks every Monte Carlo and exact tail, verifies all cell distributions and conditional means, and repeats the paired bootstrap. Preserve failures, sources and hashes in this endpoint. The result measures sensitivity of a declared observation statistic to the known measurement-quality mechanism.
