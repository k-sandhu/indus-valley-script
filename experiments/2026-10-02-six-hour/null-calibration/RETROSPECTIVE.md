# Work record for conditional-reference calibration

## Question and preserved methods

The manuscript uses fitted conditional references that rerun grouping, fold allocation and model selection. This study checks their sampling and rank calculation on synthetic populations with known laws. The original four method files are copied into [sources](sources/) with hashes. The new study calls those snapshots and preserves the five outer folds, three inner folds and grid 5/20/100/500/2000. It leaves all original archaeological fits and endpoints intact.

The [timing pilot](pilot/results.json) fitted nine observed datasets and 108 reference corpora in 2.90 seconds. It supplied the runtime estimate for the [main protocol](MAIN_PROTOCOL.md): 1,000 independent datasets in each of three families, with 99 fitted reference draws per reference. The physical-copy family has both record and object references. Output was made compact after the pilot, and zero/all-success Wilson endpoints were set to exact 0/1. The pilot source and results remain saved.

## Conditional-space construction

The first design step was to state the observation process before fitting a statistic. Independent categorical slots become uniform after their cell margins are fixed. Conditioning that process on whole-string uniqueness produces a uniform restricted space. Copying one latent object twice produces a different supported space in which its two fixed records stay identical. These arguments are written in [MATHEMATICS.md](MATHEMATICS.md), and the study keeps all observed rows, proposal seeds, accepted seeds, grouped fold loads and selected models.

The exact binary example has 8,000 ordinary tables and 2,880 unique tables. A labeled permutation gives every ordinary table 46,656 generating representations, so repeated values have equal multiplicity. Complete enumeration verifies the .36 unique acceptance probability. The saved rejection sampler accepted 100,000 draws from 278,408 proposals, with observed acceptance .359185. All 2,880 unique tables were fitted with the preserved nested routine.

The ordinary tiny space exposed an algorithm-domain limit: 2,240 of its tables have fewer than five components. The integer statistic is evaluated over all 8,000 tables, and every table's probability remains counted. Nested scores are calculated over the complete unique subset, where six distinct groups support the actual outer/inner procedure. Assigning a default score to infeasible ordinary tables would have changed the statistic's question, so that route was not used.

## Additional finite observation-process checks

[Copy-tail counting](copy-tail-counts/README.md) isolates the effect of copying on a simple adjacency statistic. The 216 latent tables are enumerated, larger ordinary reference spaces are counted with binomial coefficients, and an independent row-state dynamic program verifies those distributions. Three copies per latent object give a 25% rejection probability at a 5% ordinary-record threshold, compared with 2.78% under the matched object reference. This supplies an exact example of the exchangeability requirement that the main nested study measures separately.

[Unequal-copy sampling](unequal-copy-counts/README.md) addresses artifacts with different record multiplicities. For weights `[1,1,1,2,2,3]`, six column assignments preserve both three latent ones and five expanded-record ones. A direct sampler counts legal continuations with integers and selects branches proportionally. Every path has exact probability 1/6. Its 100,000 three-slot draws and 100,000 rejected-permutation draws are saved. The direct sampler's uniformity diagnostic is p=.01336 at the declared seed; the diagnostic and full independent stream replay are retained. The endpoint was not regenerated to obtain a different diagnostic. Permuting only within multiplicity classes was also counted: it fixes extra margins and narrows the table space from 216 to 27.

Unrestricted object shuffling was insufficient for unequal copies: fourteen of its twenty distinct columns changed the expanded record count. Holding that weighted count by rejection or continuation counting fixes the problem. Holding only the weighted count creates a different issue, since compatible columns have different latent sign totals. The exact known-probability calculation records that difference, and the direct uniform sampler conditions on both totals.

[Deduplicated selection](dedup-selection/README.md) distinguishes another known observation process. Seven independent records, collapsed to six distinct strings and conditioned on balanced retained columns, give unequal masses to the four allowed supports. The frozen 2,880 nested scores were reused with those exact masses. An independent seven-step seen-set probability recurrence verifies the support law. The uniform reference's exact 5% rejection rate becomes 5.1537%; tails matched to that selected law give 4.9407%. No model was refitted for this calculation.

## Sources and next measurements

The [noisy-face run](noisy-faces/README.md) completed 1,000 populations and 198,000 reference fits in 5,577.93 seconds. Scalar record shuffling rejected 110/1,000 populations at 5%, while the ordered-vector law rejected 50/1,000. The paired difference is six percentage points, interval 4.3–7.7. The final audit checked every population, proposal, margin, physical alias, independent graph/fold calculation and p-value, with fifteen complete fit replays. It found no fitting-domain failure among the sampled tables. The separately preserved connected-table witness shows why the exact proof still needs a domain rule.

The first noisy-face seed schedule used bases two and four billion. A pre-main namespace check found potential overlap with a finite sampler stream. The main bases were moved to twenty and forty billion before any main outcomes. The timing pilot and finite sampler snapshots retain their original seeds and hashes. The final audit verifies 198,000 distinct main reference seeds.

[The domain-support witness](domain-support/README.md) extends the earlier tiny-space check to nonidentical physical faces. Twenty objects form one component through a chain of shared complete strings. The frozen fit raises a division-by-zero exception; a table with the same ordered-vector margins has twenty components and a finite score. The first witness source called an assumed helper name and failed before fitting. Its source and exception are retained, and the corrected source uses `group_records` in a new endpoint.

The subsequent guard protocol fixes a zero reference score below five components and observed abstention. Its exact 8,000-table calculation reused 2,880 old scores and fitted 2,880 additional feasible tables. At a 5% threshold, the extended law rejects 400/8,000 tables; among the feasible observations that is 400/5,760. Conditioning the reference on feasibility gives 276/5,760. Every table identity, graph count and tail was independently verified. This makes the denominator and observation-selection event explicit while retaining the original running endpoints.

The follow-up support audit establishes a constructive guarantee when every physical block contains one complete string value. A fixed slot's used-code count bounds distinct strings and hence components under that premise. It applies throughout the original singleton and matched identical-copy conditional spaces, with observed-margin bounds eight and six to eight. The ordinary record comparator can separate a copy pair and connect different strings through its fixed artifact alias. The audit regenerated all 198,000 copy-reference tables: all 99,000 ordinary draws broke the copy premise, and all matched draws retained it. The nonidentical-face witness has eight codes but one component. The protocol and result therefore keep the premise alongside the bound.

The actual greedy allocator fills all five outer folds whenever at least five positive-weight components are supplied. Removing a fold leaves at least four components for three inner folds; each inner fit retains at least two. A finite allocator audit covers 29,169 size/seed cases and 583,380 split checks, including a thirty-six-record component beside four singletons. All counts are positive and all declared component minima hold. This audit uses the completed main replay certificate and makes no new score fit.

The rank arguments are checked against [Phipson and Smyth](https://gksmyth.github.io/pubs/PermPValuesPreprint.pdf) and [Hemerik and Goeman](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6405018/). The finite laws, exact integer/rational counts, source hashes and preserved implementation supply the experiment-specific evidence.

[Related work](RELATED-WORK.md) connects the physical-reference design to repeated-measurement block permutation in neuroimaging and pseudoreplication in ecology. The literature search returned primary indexed text and bibliographic records for Winkler et al. (2015) and Hurlbert (1984). Opening the PMC full page encountered a browser-check screen. A later Human Brain Mapping DOI, 10.1002/hbm.70474, returned HTTP 403; no claims were added from that inaccessible article.

The next archaeological measurement concerns how the source population creates records: which faces are literal copies, how many records each physical unit supplies, and how selection/deduplication weights them. A proposed block reference should follow that measured process and its metadata cells. A larger calibration population could then retain the observed length distribution, inventory size and sparse metadata cells. The current study fixes three slots, eight codes and two strata so that its conditional laws and implementation checks remain explicit.

## Object quality and statistic sensitivity

The [shared-quality follow-up](shared-quality/README.md) holds one observed low/high quality class across all three slots and two faces of an object. Latent slots remain independent within site/type, and the two supplied replacement rates are .025 and .60. Broad vector shuffling changes the class-specific observation margins, while quality-conditioned vector shuffling preserves them. The nested statistic retains its original inputs and does not read the quality label. Its 300-population study required 59,400 reference fits and 1,599.17 seconds, longer than the eighteen-minute timing projection. Both processes and all checkpoint records were retained until completion.

The nested score showed a small measured separation: 4% versus 3% rejection at 5%, paired difference one percentage point with an interval of −1 to 3. The full audit checked every population and proposal, independent grouping/folds, p-value counts, sixteen complete fits and all paired intervals. Exact finite sampling and supplied-kernel moment calculations establish that the two reference laws still differ. Those calculations suggested a score that directly observes the mechanism.

The [agreement diagnostic](shared-quality/agreement-diagnostic/README.md) was designed after the completed nested result and generated 1,000 fresh populations. It sums adjacent products of the two-face agreement indicators, using observed signs and object identity. The statistic contains no latent sign or quality label. At 5%, broad and quality-conditioned Monte Carlo references reject 94.2% and 1.0%; their exact conditional tails reject 95.0% and .8%. A binary row-state enumeration and an independent hypergeometric derivation agree on all 1,547 cell score distributions. All 198,000 full vector proposals, 2,000 exact tails and paired intervals were audited. The diagnostic isolates the observed agreement dependence that the unchanged nested predictor revealed only weakly in this population.
