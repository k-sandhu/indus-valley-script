# Experiment map for the calibration manuscript

This note gives source paths and claim boundaries for paper 002. The original and noisy-face main runs and their full independent audits are complete and passed. Every corpus in this tree is a labeled simulation or a finite combinatorial table.

## Shared fitting procedure and rank calculation

[MAIN_PROTOCOL.md](MAIN_PROTOCOL.md) defines the original study. [MATHEMATICS.md](MATHEMATICS.md) gives conditional probabilities, permutation multiplicities, rejection uniformity, copy support and the inclusive rank calculation. The statistic is the dependent model's held-out log-score gain over the selected independent baseline, in bits per noninitial sign. Every reference reruns physical/text grouping, five outer folds, three inner folds and smoothing selection over 5/20/100/500/2000. The four immutable method snapshots and hashes are in [sources](sources/); the study wrapper is preserved in [main/code_snapshot.py](main/code_snapshot.py). The original archaeological endpoints remain separate.

With 99 reference draws, the declared Monte Carlo p-value is `(1 + number of reference scores ≥ observed score)/100`. Exact finite enumeration uses the inclusive tail mass in its full stated space. A rank proof requires a defined statistic on that support; the domain-support experiment below supplies the missing boundary case for nonidentical faces.

## Main simulation endpoints

The original [main directory](main/) contains 1,000 populations each for independent records, whole-string-unique records and identical physical copies. Every population has forty records, three slots, eight categories and two metadata strata. The physical-copy family compares record-column and latent-object-column references. There are 396,000 reference fits and 3,000 observed fits. The final endpoint is [main/results.json](main/results.json); its independent audit is `verification_main.json`. At 5%, rejection is 50/1,000 for independent records, 59/1,000 for uniqueness-conditioned records, and 159/1,000 versus 54/1,000 for copied records under scalar versus object references. The original physical references share streams across 500 linked pairs of trials. [The pre-outcome paired amendment](PAIRED_UNCERTAINTY_AMENDMENT.md) fixes the 500-cluster bootstrap. [Its completed audit](paired_seed_audit.json) verifies 49,500 shared reference seeds and a paired 5% rate difference of .105, interval [.085,.126].

The [noisy-face protocol](noisy-faces/PROTOCOL.md), [pre-main timing/seed amendment](noisy-faces/MAIN_BUDGET.md) and [law](noisy-faces/METHODS.md) define twenty latent objects, two observed faces, three slots, eight categories and ε=.1. The within-slot face errors are independent conditional on the latent sign. Whole ordered face vectors move by slot within matching strata. The study compares that matched law with scalar record-column shuffling on 1,000 populations and 99 references per case: 198,000 reference fits and 1,000 observed fits. The final endpoint is `noisy-faces/main/results.json`; its independent audit is `noisy-faces/verification_main.json`. Its seed namespaces are disjoint.

The final audits reconstruct populations, proposal streams, margins, physical aliases, independent graph components, fold loads, p-values and aggregates, and replay selected fits. The original audit checks 928,424 proposals and 396,000 accepted groupings with 33 complete fit replays. The face audit checks 198,000 references with fifteen replays. [RETROSPECTIVE.md](RETROSPECTIVE.md) preserves sampler-design choices, reporting repairs, seed overlap, failed designs, sources and follow-up measurements. [The manuscript figure](plots/main/calibration.svg) retains eighteen points, [input hashes](plots/main/figure.json) and a [numeric point table](plots/main/points.csv).

## Prior methods and sources

[Phipson and Smyth (2010), *Permutation P-values Should Never Be Zero: Calculating Exact P-values When Permutations Are Randomly Drawn*](https://gksmyth.github.io/pubs/PermPValuesPreprint.pdf), Statistical Applications in Genetics and Molecular Biology 9(1), article 39, DOI 10.2202/1544-6115.1585, supplies the Monte Carlo rank reference. The source states its February 2011 correction. [Hemerik and Goeman (2018), *Exact testing with random permutations*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11749-017-0571-1), TEST 27(4), 811–825, DOI 10.1007/s11749-017-0571-1, supplies a further random-permutation treatment.

[Winkler et al. (2015), *Multi-level block permutation*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4644991/), NeuroImage 123, 253–268, DOI 10.1016/j.neuroimage.2015.05.092, supplies the related-observation/block principle. [Hurlbert (1984), *Pseudoreplication and the Design of Ecological Field Experiments*](https://esajournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2307/1942661), Ecological Monographs 54(2), 187–211, supplies the experimental-design parallel. [RELATED-WORK.md](RELATED-WORK.md) states how those existing principles relate to this fitting study and distinguishes physical blocks from held-out components.

The bounded [noisy-channel follow-up](noisy-channel/PROTOCOL.md) reuses frozen simulated faces to study a known symmetric error model. Its exact absence and agreement checks and identification limits are under [noisy-channel](noisy-channel/). Observer-error fitting references are [Dawid and Skene (1979)](https://doi.org/10.2307/2346806), [Dempster, Laird and Rubin (1977)](https://www.maths.usyd.edu.au/u/jchan/GLM/Dempster1977EM.pdf), and [Allman, Matias and Rhodes (2009)](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.5032). This branch supplies an observation-model extension and uses no archaeological error-rate estimate.
