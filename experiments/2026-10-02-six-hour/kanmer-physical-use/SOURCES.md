# Sources used in this frame

Agrawal, D. P., J. S. Kharakwal, Y. S. Rawat, T. Osada and Pankaj Goyal. 2010. **Redefining the Harappan hinterland.** Antiquity 84 (323), March, pp. 1–5. [Original publisher record](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/antiquity-project-gallery/article/redefining-the-harappan-hinterland/11D7D7DE4161A3DDB82755A7856D5521). The three-object photographs are Figures 3a and 3b, PDF page 3. The current online date is recorded separately.

Lawler, Andrew. 2010. **The Coastal Indus Looks West.** Science 328 (5982), May 28, pp. 1100–1101. DOI 10.1126/science.328.5982.1100. [Author-hosted text](https://andrewlawler.com/the-coastal-indus-looks-west/), [NLM citation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20508107/). The text attributes the passport suggestion to Osada. The author's page retains a misleading January date and errors later corrected in the journal.

Kharakwal, J. S., Y. S. Rawat and Toshiki Osada. 2011. **Annual report of excavation at Kanmer 2007-08 and 2008-09.** In Linguistics, Archaeology and the Human Past, Occasional Paper 10, pp. 71–104, edited by Osada and Akinori Uesugi. RIHN, January 2011. ISBN 9784902325614. [Public preview](https://www.scribd.com/document/336418894/OP10-1). Printed page 90, preview page 98, gives Figure 10 and the reported stamp, impression and hole dimensions.

Kharakwal and colleagues. **Kanmer: A Multicultural Site in Kachchh, Gujarat, India.** Printed pp. 355–376. [Institutional PDF](https://nichibun.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/1259/files/symp_016__357__355_376__357_378.pdf). PDF pages 15–16, printed 369–370, supply the three-object description and Figures 12–13. The 2011 date follows the author bibliography; the acquired article PDF lacks a dated volume cover.

Seth, Hansmukh, J. S. Kharakwal, R. Menaria and H. Bunker. **Chapter 8: Seals, Sealing and other Script Material / Harappan Script Material from Kanmer.** Public draft/offprint, pp. 1–13. [Public preview](https://www.scribd.com/document/212393968/Kanmer-seals-sealing-and-other-script-material-Chapter-8). Draft pages 4–6 contain the registrations and measurements. The final 2012 volume's Chapter 8 pages remain unacquired; source publication-date and page references are retained in the disagreement ledger.

Teramura, Hirofumi, Yasuhisa Kondo and Takao Uno. 2012. **Archaeology with GIS at Kanmer.** In Excavation at Kanmer: 2005-06–2008-09, pp. 165–196. RIHN, March 2012. [Institutional metadata and file link](https://t2r2.star.titech.ac.jp/cgi-bin/publicationinfo.cgi?q_publication_content_number=CTT100634320). The repository uses the alternative title **Archaeology with GIS in the Indus Project**. The acquired author copy contains the entire Chapter 5 through printed page 217. PDF page 25, printed 187, gives Figures 5.1.27–5.1.34. Its scanner method is on PDF page 23, printed 185.

Parpola, Asko, and Juha Janhunen. 2011. **On the Asiatic wild asses and their vernacular names.** In Linguistics, Archaeology and the Human Past, Occasional Paper 12, pp. 59–124, edited by Osada and Hitoshi Endo. RIHN. [Author/institutional PDF](https://tuhat.helsinki.fi/ws/portalfiles/portal/127256217/Parpola_A_2011_J_Janhunen._Asiatic_wild_asses_their_vernacular_names.pdf). PDF pages 13–15, printed 70–72, give the Kanmer seal and reverse-mark discussion with Figures 15–16.

Seth, Hansmukh, and J. S. Kharakwal. 2023. **Harappan Script Material from Kanmer, Gujarat.** In Research on Indus Civilization in the Wake of Hundred Years of Excavation at Harappa, pp. 342–348, edited by Rajesh S. V., Abhayan G. S. and Preeta Nayar. Department of Archaeology, University of Kerala. ISBN 9789356272675 (print), 9789356028869 (e-book). [Author-hosted chapter](https://www.academia.edu/110107957/Indus_Script_Material_from_Kanmer_Gujarat). Figures 3 and 4 show four fronts and four numbered reverses; printed pages 344–346 contain the physical and functional discussion.
