# Work record for the latent-generator condition

The completed same-length mapping experiment left a useful theoretical question: does its exact observation law determine the hidden product forms when their number is supplied? The primary identification paper gives a condition that can be checked directly on the stored synthetic parameters. The protocol fixed the three adjacent slot pairs and all thirty-six profiles before computing ranks. Earlier finite-sample mapping outcomes were known and are recorded as the reason for this follow-up.

The source generator supplies one form draw and six separate slot draws. Its common-length probability vectors have small binary64 row-sum deviations. The theorem uses stochastic conditional matrices, so the protocol declared exact rational normalization. It also declared a second population that follows the actual rounded cumulative thresholds and final-symbol clamp with independent continuous uniforms. This made the numerical convention part of the model definition before outcomes. The positivity check included both unshifted and rotated mixtures because both populations occur in the earlier experiment.

The exact minors establish ranks 3/3/3 for every main profile under both conventions. Grouped marginalization recovers each original slot distribution, giving the bridge from the theorem's three composite variables back to six ordered slots. The alpha 1 endpoint exposes the boundary directly: all components coincide, rank falls to one and changing positive mixing weights leaves the population law unchanged. This control strengthens the interpretation of the condition by preserving a concrete failure case beside the successful profiles.

The first independent review used an incorrect support-count assumption. It expected five positive symbols at zero alpha in both conventions. A rounded cumulative sum below one can give the final-code clamp a small positive remainder, so the threshold convention can have six. The failed source and assertion remain in the first review folder. The revised verifier reconstructed the same saved probabilities, retained this difference and passed in a fresh endpoint. No rank calculation, dataset, main output or protocol was changed.

The primary source is Elizabeth S. Allman, Catherine Matias and John A. Rhodes, *Identifiability of parameters in latent structure models with many observed variables*, The Annals of Statistics 37(6A), 3099–3132, DOI 10.1214/09-AOS689. The versioned author PDF is [arXiv:0809.5032v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0809.5032v2). Its reprint pages 11/12 state Theorem 1 and Corollary 2; page 21 proves the corollary, and pages 22/24 give the grouping and marginal-recovery lemmas. These reprint page numbers differ from journal pagination. Source coordinates, download size and the PDF hash are saved in [the source record](sources/primary-source.json).

Web text access supplied the named theorem, corollary and proofs. Attempts to obtain screenshots of PDF indices 10/11 failed because screenshots were unavailable in that tool. The versioned PDF was then downloaded and retained locally with its SHA256 hash. The report uses the accessed text and named coordinates; it contains no screenshot-based observation.
