The tracked [acquisition ledger](acquisition.json) gives retrieval times, ordinary public routes, byte counts and SHA-256 hashes. Downloaded sources, extracted text and figure crops remain local under ignored `local-witness/`. The source assertions below identify the part of the model each witness informs.

The mechanics sources support measuring material response before predicting forces. The local independent-column groove-transfer rule, 0.08 mm visibility height, 80% stroke threshold, invented graphic classes and grid weights are experiment assumptions recorded in [the protocol](PROTOCOL.md). They are available for testing in the prospective physical experiment.
