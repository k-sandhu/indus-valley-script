# Selection by visible length

This extension was written after inspecting the frozen main geometry results and before computing a length-conditioned endpoint. The main run keeps rows with any visible graphic. The project's principal corpus population requires at least three observed signs, so the same synthetic visibility grid needs that selection rule examined.

Reuse every frozen primary configuration and its slot/class visibility matrix. Change no geometry, groove, retention, damage or recognition threshold. For each configuration calculate the exact probability of at least three visible graphics, expected visible length conditional on that event, first/last-visible class distributions and the class distribution among visible graphics. Keep configurations with zero probability in the accounting. Reproduce the main equal-weight pools, weighting endpoint distributions by the selected-row probability.

Each slot still independently selects one of four equiprobable invented graphics. A polynomial product of Bernoulli slot visibility probabilities gives the visible-count distribution. The first-visible mass at a slot is its class visibility probability, multiplied by the probability that all earlier slots are invisible and that at least two later graphics survive. The last-visible calculation reverses those conditions. The probability that a particular graphic contributes to the selected visible-class count uses at least two survivors among the other slots.

Check these probabilities against exhaustive enumeration of all 64 three-slot rows for selected and empty cases. Also enumerate all 65,536 eight-slot rows in two named configurations: the untilted, undamaged 24×8 mm matrix at radius 25 mm and displacement 0.4 mm, and the same matrix at displacement 0.8 mm with two degrees of row tilt and left-quarter loss. Check that the minimum-one calculation reproduces the saved main endpoint probabilities for every configuration. Keep the frozen primary endpoint and write the extension to `length-selection/` with its own code snapshot and hashes.
