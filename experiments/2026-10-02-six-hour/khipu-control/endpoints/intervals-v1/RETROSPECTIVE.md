# Recorded interval follow-up

The KHI-01 audit found zero in every individual pendant attachment-position field, while recorded cluster start/end intervals remained available. This follow-up used that coarser geometry to test grouping. Its protocol fixed the two source ranges, group-count priors, largest-gap rule, control sets, tie handling and independent interval-width sensitivity before scores were calculated.

The tested input excluded numerical quantities and reference partitions. The analyst had already seen both published partitions and the recorded geometry, so the endpoint is a reference-controlled test. Raw inputs, evaluator labels, detailed candidate sets and exact certificates remain local; their hashes are recorded in the inference and input manifests.

Both recorded gap patterns selected one correct partition. The equal-cord-count baseline also selected one correct partition in each case. Keeping that result beside the geometry score preserves a competing explanation for success on this small set. Exact metric permutations matched the reference in 20 of 420 UR063 cases and 2 of 156 UR068 cases.

Missing and tied measurements retained complete alternative sets. Widened gaps first lost unique selection at radius 0.25 source units, producing 10 and 12 admissible partitions. Larger widths preserved the source partition while admitting more alternatives. The uncertainty model treats derived gap bounds independently; it does not estimate historical measurement error.

Independent verification checked every rational ranking witness and exclusion certificate. Accepted and excluded cut sets together cover all combinatorially possible candidates. It also verified source extraction, permutation multiplicities, information-loss counts, the equal-size baseline and origin, scale and orientation invariance. No parsing or arithmetic repair was required in this run.

A future source test needs accounts whose intervals and group labels have been independently documented, with object selection fixed before interval review. Cases where the largest-gap and equal-size rules disagree would distinguish their information requirements. The present endpoint preserves the source cases, shared priors and all ambiguity instead of selecting a preferred grouping model.
