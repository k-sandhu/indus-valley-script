# Collective observation availability

This supplement precedes generation. The collective inventory and all visible mark features are available before the first local-membership prefix. Prefixes add individual associations. The collective row always uses the whole observed inventory, including parent records whose individual associations have not yet been inspected.

The baseline deliberately ignores this additional observation. The comparison measures the effect of that supplied constraint. It assigns no acquisition cost to the collective inventory and makes no claim about a gain under equal inspection budgets. Counted and unmeasured views share the same geometry; only the counted view receives its object's measured scale.
