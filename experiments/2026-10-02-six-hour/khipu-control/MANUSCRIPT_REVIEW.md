# Read-only review of the physical-observation manuscript

The sample sizes, principal recovery counts, source alignment and exact examples in paper 003 agree with the saved PHYS-01, PHYS-02 and KHI-01 outputs. The points below concern specific premises and wording. The review used existing records and short residual calculations; it did not rerun the experiments or edit the manuscript.

## Factual corrections

1. In section 2.4, the counted object's feature vector is `[1,0,0]` and its observed value is one. This directly fixes the first positional value. Replace "all three positional values concealed" with an explanation that the counted unit fixes the first value and account relations constrain the remaining two. Complete and incomplete worlds generate parent sums; ownership worlds draw parent quantities independently. State those two generator cases together before describing their common representation.

2. In section 3.1, "All five fail the eight withheld relations" can imply that all eight residuals are nonzero in each case. The actual numbers are 6, 5, 6, 6 and 8 failures out of eight. "Each fails at least one of the eight withheld relations" matches the result. The [saved review residuals](manuscript-review-facts.json) identify the five collections and retain every residual.

3. In section 3.2, the normalized width change from `47/192` to `1765/10368` compares the original information/rank policy with the overlap policy on the same 24 fresh worlds. Name that comparison explicitly. The counts of six newly identified vectors, fifteen narrower ranges and nine ties are correct.

4. In section 3.4, the scalar result is a difference of sixteen, not an agreement. Every allocation preserves that difference. A direct replacement is: "Every allocation preserves the scalar total difference of sixteen, so that score cannot distinguish coordinate correspondences."

5. In section 2.2, public action descriptors also disclose completeness and the bounded child-count menu. Keeping those assumptions with costs and availability makes the acquisition problem easier to interpret. The selector acquires relations and counts under this supplied observation menu.

The repeated-carry example in section 3.5 is exact. A [compact witness](illustrative-carry-ambiguity.json) extracts the eight equations and saved positive family from the frozen endpoint and checks `(1,2,28)` and `(1,10,100)` separately. It is a post-outcome illustration of seed 2026100442, rather than another experiment.

## Reader-facing edits

The abstract promises an observation table, and section 4 says that the table follows, but the current manuscript describes its fields only in prose. Either insert a compact table with each observed field and its constraint role, or call this an observation design in both places.

The abstract's phrase "inaccurate exact counts" describes the explicit failure example. "Counts with understated uncertainty" also covers the full undercovered-count condition used in the experiment.

The Susa paragraph already explains the accession, the balanced mixture and the proposed extra measurement. Its final contrast about adding equations from a proposed translation can be removed. A direct ending is: "The accession and its documented contents provide a specific object for collecting those additional measurements."

The claim-evidence map ends with another statement that an Indus value assignment is absent. Its scope column and the manuscript's methods already locate the claims. The final paragraph can simply state that the contribution is measured recovery and acquisition performance, exact failure examples and a source-linked observation design.
