The fresh evaluation and expert/source review are the largest remaining evidence gaps for generalization claims. The current exploratory measurements can be described in a preprint or technical report with their existing status visible. Submission readiness will depend on the selected venue and claim scope.

## Result-specific checks

The gap-count section needs a newly specified predictor or additional physical gap evidence before a rerun becomes informative. Its current matched-law result already answers the artificial-design question. If geometry is added, first define what a gap width measures, how sign spacing varies by material/layout, and how known originals are sampled. Keep count recovery and conditional glyph ranking separate.

The second-annotator evaluation should preserve five unmapped and seven wholly unsupported exclusions, the 20 partially unsupported faces, the 54 fully supported subset, and candidate-set widths. A crosswalk review can change the scoring events; such a change needs a new endpoint rather than rewriting the frozen score.

Known-slot performance should include micro and equal-text results, OOV coverage, per-type/frequency summaries and calibration. The current headline accuracy cannot by itself support a production tool for eroded inscriptions. A practical tool evaluation would need source-verified eroded specimens, a count/segmentation protocol, and reference uncertainty.

## Separate publication candidates

The numerical equality audit is a possible short source-critical note after resolving the historical tablet identifier and M1383 context/range. Its full solution set and parity correction are ready to inspect, but the proposed equations and selected values are prior. Its most valuable extension would be an independently measured quantity prediction under frozen premises.

The chart atlas is a resource candidate with uncertainty labels and provenance. Before a recognition paper, obtain an independent reference, freeze source geometry before scoring, and sample a new block after the post-reference geometry repair. Preserve the failed fresh20 endpoint. The present geometry diagnostic is useful development evidence.

Substitution examples belong in the current discussion and retrospective. A separate class paper would require source-certified graphic identity, physical-independence checks, priority review and pair-level selection-adjusted evaluation. Aggregate transfer and a visually attractive individual pair answer different questions.

## Proposed submission package

Keep [manuscript.md](manuscript.md), [methods.md](methods.md), [claim-evidence-map.md](claim-evidence-map.md), a frozen numeric review and a permitted data/code manifest together. Selected figures should show the predictive contrast, known-slot versus unknown-count restoration, and calibration or rare-sign coverage. Use the original report for additional explorations rather than expanding this paper into a catalogue of every attempted reading.

All further experiments should be registered in `knowledge/experiments/registry.json` with a dated output directory and a reason that distinguishes them from an already completed path. No workflow, deployment service or recurring run is required for this submission plan.
