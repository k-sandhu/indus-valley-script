# Figure 4 scale and source pagination

A pixel reread of the 2000 author manuscript found a 0–3 cm scale bar at the bottom of Figure 4. The first observation had recorded that no numeric scale was attached. The [correction record](correction.json) preserves that field and records the visible bar, its native source bounds and the source hash. The source image is local-only; [the crop manifest](crop-manifest.json) identifies the unchanged witness and extraction.

Figure 4 is on PDF page 15, which displays the author's internal page number 14. Its caption and the discussion of turning the tablets are on PDF page 16, displaying page 15. The frozen ledger's 334/335 labels are mapped book pagination. Future locators should retain PDF page, displayed author page and mapped book label as separate fields. The correction lists the affected JSON fields without changing them.

The text describes variation in inscription sequence and orientation as a tablet is turned between its faces. It also reports microscopic examination of cutting direction and sequence. The inspected figure, caption and paragraph supply no explicit clockwise turning convention, common physical end or map from drawing rows to oriented prism faces. Our coded `top`, `middle` and `bottom` labels name display positions. The lower faces record A/D form and displayed left/right position relative to the stroke group; the top complex form was not coded as a physical orientation anchor. A representation audit can state its face-exchange and whole-object reversal assumptions alongside these observations.

All original annotations and frozen endpoints remain unchanged. This record supports later calibrated drawing measurements and clearer source references.
