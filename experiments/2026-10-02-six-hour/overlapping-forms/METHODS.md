# Methods and scope

This post-outcome stress test addresses paper 004's declared common-length and overlapping-role extension. Its main seed range begins at 1010261002, separate from the preceding graph, slot and selector grids. The pilot uses 2010261002. Before main fitting, the executable copied itself and every imported graph, slot and selector helper into the endpoint and recorded their hashes in [execution-freeze.json](main-v1/execution-freeze.json).

The original administrative generator supplies three independent-slot forms, four disjoint five-symbol pools and a Dirichlet form mixture. Existing slots retain their probabilities. Added slots use new within-pool draws, and the common background uses a twenty-symbol Dirichlet draw. Alpha .35 or .70 is a probability-mixture weight. The added slots make the forms equally long, so the separated/common-length comparison combines a length change with added observations. The supplied-form comparison uses identical records and isolates the additional conditioning field.

Each record uses one form uniform and six position uniforms, sampled before truncation. Variant generation reuses those uniforms, encoding and stream seeds. Reference, training and held-out streams are separate. The .35 process change rotates part of the form mixture while preserving all slot distributions. [Parameters](main-v1/parameters.json) and [worlds](main-v1/worlds.json) preserve the full specification and observed records.

The ordinary slot model fits `q(r | length, position)` with one pseudocount per symbol. Its mapping weight sums encoded cell counts times reference log probabilities. The stronger-input diagnostic fits `q(r | supplied form, position)` and uses the same linear assignment procedure. Form labels enter that diagnostic and evaluation. The primary selector call accepts reference rows, inventory size and fold seed; its inputs contain no form labels, generator parameters or mapping answer.

The graph fit preserves the existing two deterministic FAQ starts, ten randomized starts, 2-opt refinements and frequency candidate, with fixed start/end matches. Conditional graph likelihood is its whole-record likelihood divided by `start × transition^(length−1) × end`. Three-fold reference prediction chooses between this score and the product of ordinary slot probabilities; ties choose graph. Main source and helper snapshots reproduce the exact implementation and budget. Both conditional held-out scores use the same observed token denominator. The diagnostic's held-out likelihood additionally conditions on its supplied form, so its conditioning field stays explicit in the output.

The independent review reconstructs all generator draws and sampled inputs, then uses Counters, scalar logarithms and a state-vector recursion to recalculate every fold, choice and score. Independent row/column-potential dual programs bound all 384 linear optima. For each selected code, a separate assignment forbids its chosen match; alternatives within 1e-7 are checked by exact prime-exponent cancellation of the rational likelihood ratio. Objective comparisons use 1e-7 and per-token comparisons use 1e-9. The maximum observed pooled-score discrepancy is 2.19 × 10⁻¹¹ bits. Dual and equality certificates concern linear scores; the twenty-symbol graph optimum is uncertified. [Review protocol](REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md), [checks](review-v1/checks.json), [dual bounds](review-v1/dual-certificates.json).

The twelve systems are the resampling units. Each system's two reference sizes and two mixture conditions remain together in paired comparisons. Twenty thousand bootstrap draws use seed 1210261002. The post-outcome diagnostics compare supplied-label recovery, classify truth-score gaps and retain exact truth-equality checks, without changing any fit or selection.
