For a two-state history h with training count N(h), the preserved second-order factor is `q2(y|h)=[C(h,y)+α*q1(y|last(h))]/[N(h)+α]`. For N>0 the finite mixture is equivalent to ordinary smoothing with `α_eff(h)=α+κ+α*κ/N(h)`. The added weight therefore applies extra shrinkage to rare histories.
