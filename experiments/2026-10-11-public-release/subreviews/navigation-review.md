## What a public checkout can omit

The 53 genuine local targets comprise ten normalized corpus or metadata files, twenty raw witness/review paths, four explicitly ignored Kanmer observations, one detailed Markov prediction audit, seven generated Parquet release tables, nine downloaded PDF witnesses, the acquired Sproat manifest, and the quarantined mixed-corpus comparison file. Their exact paths, source roles, ignore rules and referring documents are recorded in the JSON. The generated Parquet tables have reviewed licences; their exclusion is a packaging choice. Downloaded source PDFs remain local even when an edition permits selected attributed derivatives.

Registry availability adds twelve raw witnesses under EVIDENCE-CENSUS-COLLECTIONS-01. Two already appear in the candidate list, leaving ten additional exact paths. The combined public omission register therefore has 63 targets. A public checker should report these as unavailable local witnesses or build products, while checking that the registry's public summaries and methods are available. It should fail for any other missing target until that target is inspected and given a documented role.

Use the Git-selected tree or a fresh checkout as the availability boundary. The present knowledge checker uses workspace existence for registry evidence and Markdown links, which accepts ignored local files and will fail in a public clone that correctly omits them. Its document-context hash checks can be retained. Keep local-input reproduction checks separate from public-package structure checks, and require current landing pages and viewer resources to exist publicly. An ignore rule alone is not a reason to excuse a missing navigation target.
