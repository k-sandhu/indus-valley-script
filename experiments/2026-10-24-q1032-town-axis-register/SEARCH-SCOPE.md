# Town and source search

We start with the required eleven towns in [Q-1032](../../knowledge/questions/Q-1032-town-axis-table.md). The first dated package's [town list](../2026-10-21-town-axes/towns-v1.json), its measurement and dating records, and the independent verification's 33-town frame supply the further candidates. We read both studies' reports and retrospectives and the plan-registration report before combining their records. Source-specific unsuccessful searches remain in [absence-records.jsonl](run-v4/absence-records.jsonl) and the original dating evidence records, whose paths and hashes are retained.

The combined frame has 34 towns. Its additional candidates are Allahdino, Balakot, Bhirrana, Desalpur, Farmana, Ganweriwala, Gola Dhoro, Juni Kuran, Karanpura, Khirsara, Kot Diji, Kotada Bhadli, Kunal, Kuntasi, Lakhanjo-daro, Mitathal, Nausharo, Navinal, Pabumath, Rangpur, Rojdi, Sutkagen-dor and Tarkhanewala-Dera. Inclusion records a search target; it does not establish that every site has a measurable orthogonal Harappan plan.

Tarkhanewala-Dera enters through the verification measurer B's [record](../2026-10-21-town-orientations-verification/measure-B/towns/tarkhanewala-dera.json), which cites P. K. Trivedi, *Excavations at Tarkhanewala-Dera and Chak 86 (2003-2004)*, MASI 99 (2009). That record identifies the held scan, its hash, the plan figures and the printed report coordinate, and records the lack of prepared imagery. We carry its plan evidence into the new register and leave the horizon and direct imagery fields missing with reasons.

The new bounded web check located the primary journal record for *Tata Institute Radiocarbon Date List X* and the official Copernicus documentation. A government-hosted alternative scan at `https://ignca.gov.in/Asi_data/57533.pdf` could not be acquired through the web reader or Requests; the failures are recorded in [source-checks.json](source-checks.json). Held primary scans supplied the page checks. We found no matching Q-1032 issue through the connected GitHub issue search on 24 October, and made no issue claim or external post.

This continuation does not provide a new systematic census of every excavation report. A further acquisition should search the report catalogues for straight Harappan architecture outside this frame, add each candidate with its publication locator, and retain exclusions for unexcavated mounds, later architecture, schematic plans and unresolved site identities. Repeating the same backfilled-site image searches adds no independent bearing.
