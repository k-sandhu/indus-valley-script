# Data and coverage changes proposed for integration

The current endpoint is [run-v4](run-v5/town-axis-table.json). It combines the 29-town town-axes table with the 33-town independent verification and the plan-registration study into a 34-town index. Counts describe source assertions and source uses. They are not new independent measurements or new inscribed objects.

We add two exploratory image lines at the corrected Surkotada location, 35 primary published orientation rows from Wanzke 1987, and two corrected-location modern DEM profiles. This raises the number of towns with a direct image line from five to six in the combined frame, and the number with a retained usable modern-location profile from eighteen to twenty after excluding the mislocated Gola Dhoro profile. The new lines have digitizing errors only; a complete one-sigma error is missing. No radiocarbon determination or inscribed object was added.

The register preserves north reference, exact arrow label, measurement part, endpoints, metric length, reported error, original phase, source locator and source hash through the measurement-to-source join and the original record pointer. The dating records retain laboratory codes, original age and error, half-life, context and published calibration. The horizon records retain coordinate, DSM and observer assumptions, complete profile references and tile acquisition records. Coordinate quality, source dependence, calibration warnings, failed registration validation and reasons for missing fields are explicit.

All 46 acquired DEM tiles and every viewed image or plan remain local-only. The new tiles total 349,539,705 bytes. The source product is Copernicus DEM under its source-specific licence, with the attribution carried in the repair output. The publication scans were already held. No new archaeological publication was downloaded successfully. Source-use records number 539, including tile records and repeated uses of the same publication. The machine-readable [manifest additions](manifest-additions.json) distinguish new files from reused sources and project outputs.

Do not transfer the old profiles at Surkotada and Farmana to their corrected coordinates. Keep the Gola Dhoro location unresolved until the archaeological site is distinguished from the town of Bagasra. Do not replace the two Lothal imagery values with a single pooled value. Keep the earlier registered bearings exploratory because their validation failed. Q-1032 remains open; the exact missingness ledger is [missing-fields.csv](run-v5/missing-fields.csv).
