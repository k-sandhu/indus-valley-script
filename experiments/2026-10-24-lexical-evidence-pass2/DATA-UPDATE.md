# Proposed lexical source update

This package adds a frozen sixteen-case lexical review for Q-0039. Its selected evidence contains 35 Tamil Lexicon sense references and 26 case references to 23 DEDR entries. It records seven contextual increment supports, one printed-boundary support, one source-context contradiction, six homograph separations and one nominal-label conflict. The exact categories and source-qualified judgments are in the versioned results and local review.

Newly acquired grammar sources are E. Annamalai's institutional “13. Sandhi” HTML and Eva Wilden's Grammar of Old Tamil for Students, first edition, as a mirrored PDF with a December 2016 preface. Rendered PDF pp.27–28 supply the published m-assimilation discussion. The Tamil Digital Library candidate grammar exceeds the frozen size limit and remains unacquired. Neither downloaded source has a verified open reuse licence in this package.

All copies, extracts, source packets, full lexical rows, annotations and rendered pages are physically under `D:\projects\indus_valley_script\data\raw\2026-10-24-lexical-evidence-pass2\`, reached through logical `data/raw/2026-10-24-lexical-evidence-pass2/`. The manifest records both locations, source editions, query pages, entry numbers, URLs, retrieval times, bytes and hashes. Existing dictionary copies remain at their earlier D: source paths.

The prior extraction, exact candidate subset, selected members and scorer eligibility remain unchanged. Source-specific sense evidence does not supply a dictionary-wide classification of native and borrowed vocabulary or specialist adjudication. Remaining work includes the full increment distribution, the dry-material noun policy, competing origin accounts, botanical identity and dates of the cited texts. No object or archaeological metadata coverage changes in this package. Maintainer integration should register the source review beside the frozen extraction and retain the original unresolved fields.
