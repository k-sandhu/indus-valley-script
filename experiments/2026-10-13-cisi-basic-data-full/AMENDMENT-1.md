# Amendment 1: tail strips for blocks cut short at the page foot

14 October 2026, about 00:20 UTC, written while the CISI 2 readers were still at work and before any tail strip was read or any pass was compared.

A pass 2 reader reported that the left half-column of CISI 2 p. 438 ended at M-1195 although the page heading runs to M-1277 and the pilot's procedure puts M-1196 and M-1197 at the foot of that column, and that the last line of the right half-column was cut by the bottom of its final strip. The cause is in [make_strips.py](make_strips.py): it ends each block at the last text row it detects with a darkness threshold of 128, and the lines at the foot of several CISI 2 pages are fainter than that. Measured again with a threshold of 170, the text of 17 CISI 2 blocks runs below their last strip, by between 5 and 703 pixels (up to about fifteen lines, on the right half-column of p. 446, and the remaining corrigenda on p. 447). No CISI 1 block is affected; the text above the first strip of several blocks in both volumes is the page heading, which is not transcribed.

[make_tail_strips.py](make_tail_strips.py) cuts tail strips for those blocks, listed in [tails.json](tails.json): `t01` repeats the block's last strip for context, and `t02` onward run to the last line of text. Two further Claude instances, one for each pass, read every tail under the same brief and isolation rules as the main readers. Each transcribes, for each tail, the last entry that begins in `t01` in full and every entry after it, together with the remaining corrigenda of CISI 2. When a block is assembled, the entries of its main pass file from the first CISI number that the tail file also contains onward are replaced by the tail file's entries. Everything else in the protocol is unchanged, and `make_strips.py` and the main strips stay as frozen.
