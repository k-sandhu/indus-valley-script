# One complex sign and two display characters

The first crosswalk displayed Sumerian *geme₂*, “female worker,” with SAL and KUR as two canonical components and called it a multi-sign word. The pinned ORACC sign list assigns that value to the complex sign `|SAL.KUR|`. Its display still uses two Unicode characters. The second endpoint records one complex sign and keeps the identical rendered characters. No lexical meaning, comparison family or selected source word changed.

The second endpoint also attaches the sign-role references for Egyptian A9 and A119 explicitly to their dated word examples. The A119 reference drawing is from a 2023 proposal table; the Old Kingdom/First Intermediate Period use comes from AES. Those are separate observations.

Loading the complete Ur III glossary and catalogue together used about 1.9 GB and took several minutes in the first builder.
