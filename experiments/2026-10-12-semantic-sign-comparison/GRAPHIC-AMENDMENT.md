# Source-chart crop correction

The first contact sheet exposed clipped or incorrect Linear B pictures. The source tables have unequal column widths, while the initial cropper divided the width evenly. Eight of the fifteen selected labels also had incorrect row pointers, partly because sex-qualified animal and compound-sign rows were miscounted.

The source charts were inspected again, including enlarged columns with both the drawings and their printed labels. The revised row pointers select B107, B109, B121, B145, B159, B176, B200, B202 and B243 correctly. This is nine corrected row pointers. The revised cropper locates the actual ruling lines in the images and uses those coordinates. All fifteen resulting details require visual comparison with their source labels.

Run-v1 and run-v2 retain the incorrect original locators and crop manifests. Run-v3 is the current endpoint. The chosen sign IDs, English meanings and family retrieval links are unchanged. Their pictures and source-cell pointers are repaired. No source table, Indus hypothesis or semantic accuracy figure is modified.
