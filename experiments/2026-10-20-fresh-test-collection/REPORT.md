# Farmana source acquisition

We recovered 15 marked-pottery objects absent from the pinned export by their recorded excavation contexts. This conclusion assumes that the source and export findspots are correct. Another 16 candidate identifiers need alias or inscription review, and five objects match existing corpus families. The register preserves 36 identifier rows, rather than asserting 36 independent objects. All have a source image or drawing. Sixteen pottery rows have a photograph. The source reports 29 marked sherds, while its narrative and photograph labels produce 30 candidate identifiers. We preserve the conflicting labels for review.

The source is Shinde, Osada and Kumar, editors, *Excavations at Farmana, District Rohtak, Haryana, India, 2006-2008* (2011), acquired from Internet Archive item `excavationsatfarmana200608vshindeetal_212_v`. Its 107,158,489 bytes have SHA256 `ef5e71456067fbf099492cc116d51cea2f0d58ff572c93c562194a60a2a32215`. The title and copyright pages identify the Indus Project at the Research Institute for Humanity and Nature. The uploader's CC0 tag does not establish permission to redistribute the volume. The report, page images and object register remain local.

We selected Farmana first because the manifest listed its final report as not held and supplied a public acquisition route. Rakhigarhi had an unresolved held-source identity and a previously discussed photograph. Kanmer had extensive earlier object work and unresolved final-report gaps. Shikarpur's report was already held. Identity checks then showed that all four sites are represented in the export. We read the complete Farmana marked-pottery and seal-object sections and retained every candidate, including known objects. Selection used source coverage and identity, with no prediction or scoring.

Objects whose contexts do not resolve that comparison remain uncertain. The local register records each decision and all source coordinates.
