# Review retrospective

We reviewed the frozen collection independently in a new package and preserved the acquisition files. Mechanical hashing established byte identity before source inspection. A separate parser then checked each identifier and page coordinate, and complete photographic plates supplied bounded pixel checks.

The main identity challenge was the corpus's incomplete registration crosswalk. Family 154 contains two different locus cues, while family 155 needs an I-to-1 normalization. Keeping those premises together allowed the fifteen conditional absence claims to be assessed without comparing inscription content. The source's 29-versus-30 conflict remains visible, and available dimensions in the seal-section tables are recorded as a proposed enrichment.

Two extraction issues were repaired only within this review. Printed page numbers include punctuation, and two captions begin with spaces. The revised checks recognize both. The first corpus metadata projection also included incidental count fields; the reviewer encountered them, did not use them, and recorded the exposure in the new access log. Later outputs contain only the permitted identity and coverage fields. No original frozen log, payload or shared registry was edited.

The result is a source and identity review with a recoverable local audit trail. Further identity resolution requires the missing registration evidence; test eligibility remains a separate decision.
