# Tests of the cultural-transmission model

These are proposed empirical tests. The present run constructs theories and audits source scope. Its synthetic shots result tests generated procedural guards and does not score any historical prediction.

## Keep the observations together with their sources

Use five linked ledgers: archaeological plant and technology observations; local chronology and depositional context; lexical forms and etymologies; textual loci and layers; and source dependence. Preserve uncertain dates and classifications. A copied specimen, a repeated dictionary entry and a later paper drawing on the same excavation retain one dependence identity. The exposed 11 October study and 25 October crop summaries remain discovery material.

For each new claim, record what would distinguish it from ecological convergence, exchange of finished products, inherited Indo-Iranian tradition and later borrowing. No common crop, isolated word resemblance or generic ritual gesture can alone identify a transmission route.

## C-0020: practical carriers

Define operation bundles and their reliance on local materials, distant supply and large coordinating networks before examining reserved contexts. Use independently dated local phase pairs; match crop or craft category, material, deposit type and recovery. Record production or management evidence for each proposed retained operation. Estimate the difference in retention between dependence classes using a model or resampling procedure that keeps sites and related deposits together.

The pass rule requires an interval excluding zero and stability when each catchment is omitted. Select the precision target and demonstrate sensitivity to a practically meaningful difference using a discovery pilot before confirmation. A null result from a small or poorly recovered sample is unresolved. Source review of intermediate production and learning contexts supplies a separate check on the proposed carrier.

## C-0021: transmission across languages

Start with a bounded, stratified textual corpus and a complete list of its agricultural terms. Register the lexicons, meanings, botanical assignments and frequency-matched basic-vocabulary controls. Include inherited agricultural terms rather than selecting only suspected loans. Have etymological coding reviewed independently of the preferred archaeological route. The categories are secure inheritance, supported borrowing, internal innovation and unresolved. Unexplained forms cannot be counted as supported loans.

Compare root families rather than treating every inflection or descendant as independent. Reclassify disputed etymologies through their supported alternatives and keep textual-layer uncertainty. A contact signal must survive those checks. Donor identity requires regular correspondences and a dated distribution; a broad contact signal alone cannot select Dravidian, Munda or an unidentified language.

Horsegram is a discovery example. Its word forms and proposed transmission routes are already examined. New confirmation would need reserved lexical evidence and independently dated occurrences that discriminate among those routes. A later word attestation constrains the word's documented history, without dating the first use of the plant or the composition of a different text.

## C-0022: a diagnostic ritual operation

Choose at most two operation bundles from a bounded Vedic corpus in discovery. State exact loci, textual-layer alternatives, materials, action sequence, participants and expected tool, residue or processing traces. Common features such as fire, a plough or a request for rain do not provide sufficient specificity. Freeze the bundles before new archaeological evidence is inspected.

The test requires independently supported links through an Indus-period context, an intermediate sequence and the text-relevant region and time. Include a control for inherited Indo-Iranian features using Iranian comparanda, and an ecological control for independent solutions to the same problem. Before confirmation, specify how to score bundle matches, count the search over alternatives and assess sensitivity to missing traces. A rare match selected after searching many recipes remains exploratory.

## Joint credence rule

The three cards form one connected model. Crop retention shared by all three is counted once. A confirmed practical-carrier result updates C-0020 directly; it leaves C-0021 and C-0022 unchanged unless their separate lexical or operational predictions pass. A supported borrowing route updates C-0021, without automatically establishing continuity of a ritual. Only a diagnostic archaeological and textual chain can raise C-0022.

Within a card, related predictions receive one update under its stated rule. Across cards, store a dependence table before scoring and report a joint interpretation; do not multiply nominal likelihoods or p-values from reused crops, sites, texts or etymological roots. If the same observation satisfies several descriptions, apply the most specific qualified update and describe the others as dependent support.

## Confirmation and stopping

All participants sharing new material must freeze their predictions and artifacts before the first confirmation read. Record every acquisition already read. A new seed or partition over examined assemblages does not establish freshness. The current `bench shots` loader uses the normalized inscribed-face schema; plant samples and lexical histories need an appropriate reviewed adapter and scorer before an empirical round. No sample will be relabelled as an inscription to force a score.

Stop at source review when chronology, identification, recovery or independence cannot support the planned comparison. Preserve every failed prediction and changed interpretation in a new dated endpoint. The present theories remain open and untested; source scarcity does not count as a successful prediction.
