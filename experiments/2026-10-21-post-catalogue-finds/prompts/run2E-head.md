# Codex run 2E: OCR and extract *Indian Archaeology 1996–97 – A Review*

## Your part

Part letter `E`. Owned paths: `experiments/2026-10-21-post-catalogue-finds/extraction/E/` and `data/raw/2026-10-21-post-catalogue-finds/run2E/`. Output files are named with `E` in place of `<part>`.

The other extraction runs (2N, 2G, 2D, 2P) never read the 1996–97 volume of IAR, because the ASI mirror file of that name holds the 1997–98 volume. The coordinator fetched the Internet Archive copy (identifier `indianarchaeology199697areview_534_b`) with `tools/fetch_data.py`: source id `iar-1996-97-archive`, file `data/raw/2026-10-21-post-catalogue-finds/coordinator/sources/iar-1996-97-archive/Indian_Archaeology_1996_97_A_Review.pdf` (337 pages, page images at 100 ppi, SHA256 `3bb42d877df5a6c45367e7aacb9588206a71447dae4c32fde8f2ff9b5bba23c3`; bibliographic record in `acquisition-coordinator/manifest-proposals-coordinator.json`). The archive's own OCR of this item is unusable, so do your own: render each page at 300 dpi in grey to a temporary file, OCR it with Tesseract (`C:\Program Files\Tesseract-OCR\tesseract.exe`, English), save the text as `data/raw/2026-10-21-post-catalogue-finds/run2E/text/iar-1996-97-archive/p<NNNN>.txt`, and delete the render at once. Verify the file's SHA256 first, confirm the volume year from its title or contents page, and record the printed page of each PDF page where the running head or footer shows it.

Then apply the sweep rule of run 1A to the page text (seed sites and aliases in `selection/sites-seed.csv`, inscription keywords seal, sealing, tablet, inscri-, script, sign, character, letter, legend, graffit-, tag, impression; and pages with "Harappan" and seal, inscri- or script under any site heading) and write `candidate-pages-E.csv` in the format of `acquisition/candidate-pages-1a.csv`. Read every candidate page and its neighbours and extract rows for every Indian site, using the site codes in `selection/sites-seed.csv` and the codes the other parts use for added sites (Masudpur MSD, Mitathal MTL, Hulas HUL, Dhalewan DHL, Alamgirpur ALG, Chandigarh CHG, Lohari Ragho LHR, Balathal BLT, Juna Khatiya JNK, Pabumath PBM, Pithad PTH, Nageshwar NGS, Kanewal KNW, Rangpur RNG, Prabhas Patan PRB); for another new site, assign a three-letter code not used there and record why you included it. Kalibangan, Rojdi and Balathal have a CISI 3.2 baseline, so this 1996–97 volume gives count statements only for them.

The 1996–97 season falls within longer campaigns at several sites (Dholavira, Bagasra and Kunal among them). Other parts extract those campaigns from their final reports; your rows for the same objects will be merged by the matching run, so record them fully with their season and locators, and set `possible_duplicate_of` only within your own table.

OCR at 100 ppi garbles small type. Put doubtful characters of registrations in square brackets, and set `extraction_note` to `ocr-doubtful` when a field you record is uncertain because of the scan.
