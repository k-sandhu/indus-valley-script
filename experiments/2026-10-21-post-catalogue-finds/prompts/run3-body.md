## Steps

1. **Merge.** Concatenate the regional tables. Assign final `row_id` values `PCF-<site code>-<nnn>` in site order, then primary publication order, keeping the provisional id in `provisional_row_id`. Flag (do not merge) rows from different parts that share site and registration; list them in `cross-part-duplicates.csv`.
2. **Catalogue containment.** For rows at CISI 1 or 2 baseline sites, compare each row's normalised registration with the excavation numbers of that site's CISI entries. A match sets `catalogue_status = in-baseline-catalogue` and records the CISI number in `cisi_in_baseline`. A near match (one character different, or same digits with a different prefix) is listed in `containment-review.csv` for the coordinator and leaves the status unchanged.
3. **Normalisation.** Normalise registrations by upper-casing, removing spaces, punctuation and a leading site prefix (`RGR`, `DLV`, `DHR`, `FRM`, `KNM`, `KMR`, `SKP`, `BHR`, `KRP` and the like), and stripping leading zeros in numeric parts. The local `excavation-idno` often appends other numbers to the registration (DATA.md section 2) or starts with a hyphen or a project label (`RIHN-Indus project06-1054`); treat a local value as matching when, after normalisation, it equals the row's registration, begins with it followed by non-digit or catalogue digits, or contains it as a maximal digit run of four or more digits with the same leading context. Record which rule matched.
4. **Match proposals.** For each row, compare it with every local family at the same site, using the site aliases in `selection/sites-seed.csv`. Apply the protocol's rules: `matched` (exactly one family by registration or by a cited plate or figure number in the local record, no conflicting field among object type, findspot, depth or layer and period), `candidate` (families of compatible object type at the site that no identifier ties to the row; list them all), `absent` (no family can be the object), `in-baseline-catalogue` (step 2). Object types are compatible when the local `type` code's first segment agrees with the row's `object_type` (`SEAL` with seal, `TAG` with sealing or tag, `TAB` with tablet, `POT` with inscribed-pottery, `IMPL` or `MISC` with inscribed-tool or other or uncertain). A local family matched to one row is not a candidate for another row unless the two rows are flagged duplicates. Record for every row `local_match`, `local_family_ids`, `match_basis` (`registration-exact`, `registration-normalised`, `registration-contained`, `plate-citation`, `context`, `type-only`, `no-local-family`, `all-families-assigned`, `conflict`), and `match_evidence` (the compared field names and whether each agreed, without values from the sign or motif fields).
5. **Counts.** Write `site-counts.csv`: per site, `rows_total, rows_individual, rows_enumerated, rows_uncertain_status, rows_possibly_in_cisi3, rows_in_baseline_catalogue, rows_post_catalogue, matched, candidate, absent, local_families_at_site, local_families_matched, local_candidate_families_unassigned, absent_lower_bound, absent_upper_bound, best_publication, publication_basis`. Count only `post-catalogue` rows with `inscription_status = stated` in the main columns, and give the uncertain-status rows separately. The upper bound is the post-catalogue rows not matched; the lower bound is the absent rows plus the candidate rows in excess of the unassigned local candidate families. Total all sites in a final row.
6. **Local families not in the list.** Write `local-unlisted.csv`: local families at the listed sites that no row matched or named as a candidate, with their identity fields (excluding sign, motif and size fields). These show where the list is incomplete or where the corpus holds objects from sources we did not read.

## Outputs (tracked, in `matching/`)

`objects-merged.csv` (the extraction columns plus `provisional_row_id, cisi_in_baseline, local_match, local_family_ids, match_basis, match_evidence`), `site-counts.csv`, `cross-part-duplicates.csv`, `containment-review.csv`, `local-unlisted.csv`, `match-summary.json` (counts by site and status, input hashes), the scripts, and `selftest-3.json` from a script you write and run. The selftest checks that every extraction row appears exactly once in the merged table; every `local_family_ids` value exists in the corpus at that site; every `matched` row has exactly one family; no family is `matched` by two non-duplicate rows; the site counts recompute from the merged table; the bounds satisfy lower ≤ upper; the corpus file hash is as recorded; and no tracked file contains a field from the excluded list or any of these words (case-insensitive, whole words): unicorn, bull, rhinoceros, elephant, tiger, buffalo, zebu, goat, antelope, motif, animal, "sign sequence", "reads as", or a number followed by mm or cm. CISI basic-data values stay out of tracked files except the CISI numbers.
