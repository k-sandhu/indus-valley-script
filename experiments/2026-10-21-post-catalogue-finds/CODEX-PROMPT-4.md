## Checks

1. **Blind re-extraction.** Draw a random sample of rows with seed 20261021: all rows if there are fewer than 60, otherwise 60 rows stratified by extraction part in proportion to its row count, with at least 5 per part. Write a script that gives you, for each sampled row, only its `row_id`, `site`, `primary_publication`, `pdf_page` and either its `registration` (when not empty) or its position among the rows citing that page (when the registration is empty). Without looking at the other fields of the table, read the page and record your own values for `season`, `object_type`, `findspot`, `page`, `plate_or_figure`, `photo_published`, `drawing_published`, `transcription_published`, `inscription_status`, and whether the registration (when given) appears on the page. Only after all sampled rows are recorded, compare with the table and write `verification/blind-sample.csv` (sampled row, field, table value, your value, agreement) and per-field agreement rates with exact binomial 95% intervals in `verification/blind-summary.json`. Record disagreements; do not resolve them.
2. **Omission check.** Draw 40 candidate pages at random (seed 20261022) from the pages, across all parts, that the extraction runs list in their `pages-read-*.csv` with purpose `candidate`. For each, list the inscribed objects the page reports under the protocol's definition (identity fields only), and compare with the rows that cite that page as primary or other publication. Write `verification/omission-sample.csv` and the count of objects found on the pages but absent from the list, with an exact binomial interval for the share of sampled pages with at least one omission.
3. **Count recomputation.** Recompute every column of `sites.csv` from `objects.csv` and `join-decisions.csv` with your own script, following the protocol's definitions of the bounds; report every cell that differs in `verification/count-check.json`.
4. **Coordinator joins.** Re-derive every coordinator decision whose `match_basis` is `context`, `plate-citation`, `registration-normalised` or `catalogue-containment` from the fields it cites (the row's findspot, depth or layer, period, object type, material and registration against the family's identity fields), and report each one you would not reach under the protocol's rule, with the reason. For Dholavira, also recompute the one-to-one grid-square rule in `review/match_dholavira.py` with your own code and report any difference.
5. **Locators and identifiers.** Check that every `primary_publication` and `other_publications` id exists in a source register; every `pdf_page` exists among the page-text files of that source; every `local_family_ids` value exists in the local corpus at the row's site; every `matched` row names exactly one family and no family is matched by two rows that are not flagged duplicates; and every `in-baseline-catalogue` row names a CISI number. Write `verification/locator-check.json`.
6. **Content restriction.** Scan every tracked file in `experiments/2026-10-21-post-catalogue-finds/` (excluding the protocol and prompt files, which name the restricted categories) for the words unicorn, bull, rhinoceros, elephant, tiger, buffalo, zebu, goat, antelope, motif, animal, "sign sequence", "reads as", and for a number followed by mm or cm, case-insensitively and as whole words; for any field holding sign numbers; and for any run of more than 12 consecutive words copied from a page-text file (sample at least 200 windows per source cited in the list). Write `verification/content-check.json` with every hit by file and line.
7. **Corpus objects the list does not name.** For each row of `local-unnamed.csv` with an `excavation_idno`, search the page text of every source cited for that site in `objects.csv`, and of that site's candidate pages, for the registration (allowing for spaces, hyphens, the DHR prefix and OCR confusions of 0/O and 1/I/l). Record in `verification/unnamed-search.csv` the family, the registration, and every source and page where it occurs, or `not found`; read nothing on those pages beyond the registration's immediate line. This tells the coordinator which unnamed objects the extraction missed and which come from sources it did not read.
