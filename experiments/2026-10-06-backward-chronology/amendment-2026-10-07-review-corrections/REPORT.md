## Pirak's period evidence

Kenoyer's *Indus Seals: An Overview of Iconography and Style* (2009 file; journal 2006–2007), printed p. 10, gives the pooled 1800–1300 BCE estimate. His Fig. 3 caption, printed p. 21/PDF 19, assigns the terracotta button seal to **Period II** and the bronze compartmented seal to **Period IIIA**. The corrected parent `pirak` is non-additive; `pirak-II` and `pirak-IIIA` each record one captioned specimen. [Evidence table](inputs-05/evidence-table.json), those three identifiers, `dating_assessments`, `count_basis`.

The opened primary catalogue, Shah and Parpola's *Corpus of Indus Seals and Inscriptions 2: Collections in Pakistan* (1991), places **Pk-15–17 on p. 381 in Period IB**. Its later pages include II/IIIA material, but this pass did not secure an exact catalogue concordance to the two Fig. 3 specimens. The p. 381 citation cannot authenticate their later period dates. The catalogue was reused from the evidence-census package rather than downloaded again. [Source record](inputs-05/sources.json), `cisi2-1991`, `locations_reviewed`, `local_witness`.

Jarrige and Santoni's *Fouilles de Pirak, Volume I: Texte* (1979) was located bibliographically, but its original chronology was not opened. The publisher preview of Magee and Petrie's 2010 chapter contains preliminaries, not its recalibration table. Their opened 2012 paper gives Period III's probable start as 1460–1150 BCE and its possible end as 1300/1200–760 BCE (pp. 4, 11). These ranges produce a **1460–760 BCE possible envelope for Period III**, selected C/low for `pirak-IIIA`; they do not separately date IIIA or the seal. Period II has no selected absolute interval. Its assumed start/end envelopes remain unselected. [Petrie and Magee 2012](https://ijas.usb.ac.ir/article_1054_7880d0eba8951d2e17e4a19a17a8c13f.pdf); [source access records](inputs-05/sources.json), `jarrige-santoni1979`, `magee-petrie2010`, `petrie-magee2012`.

The 80 scenarios substitute one two-seal parent envelope, disabling the two child rows, and vary its start (1500, 1400, 1300, 1200 BCE), end (1100, 1000, 900, 800, 760 BCE), grade/strength (C/low or hypothetical B/moderate), and Perumbalai strength (low or hypothetical moderate). They preserve all other rows. The range is sensitivity to these choices, not a calendar confidence interval. ## Methods, availability and checks

The inherited overlap, eligibility and gap functions are reused. Each row has at most one selected calendar assessment; alternatives are not extra assemblages. Century bins are half-open, from −3500 through −200. Pooled parents, duplicate subsets, unjoined dated comparators and adjacent contact objects do not enter South Asian additive coverage. No full interval is manufactured from the Kodumanal, Korkai or Sivagalai point labels. The exact-object lower bound counts inventories wholly contained in a century; the upper bound counts inventories possibly overlapping it. It does not date each object.
