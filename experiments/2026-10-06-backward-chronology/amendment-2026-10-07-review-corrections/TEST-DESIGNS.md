# Tests for dated transmission, 7 October 2026

Status: Proposed. No test below was executed. Numerical planning assumptions in [power-targets.json](power-targets.json) are Synthetic and rerunnable with [power_targets.py](power_targets.py). The designs test declared routes and features; a rejection applies to that model and detectable effect.

Each analysis needs independent object registrations, images, deposit/phase, marking stage, orientation and method-specific dates. Deduplicate drawings, impressions and editions of the same object. Freeze features, transformations, route, exclusions and the held-out evaluation before viewing cross-corpus outcomes. Use two blinded annotators and retain disagreements. Preserve shared site/workshop clustering and dating uncertainty in the null. The planning calculations assume independent effective units, fixed baseline rates and one-sided alpha 0.0025, allowing up to twenty Bonferroni-controlled feature/rule tests within each design at family-wise 0.05. Larger searches need a revised threshold before evaluation. For clustered data acquire more raw objects and demonstrate at least 80% simulated power for the stated effect before interpreting a negative evaluation. The binomial powers below are exact under fixed null rates; the two-group powers are normal approximations. Those rates and effect sizes are proposals rather than measured frequencies.

## 1. Rare compounds through dated intermediate deposits

Select in a discovery set a compound with at least four strokes, two fixed junction relationships and a conservative null frequency no greater than 1%. Freeze component order, enclosure, asymmetry and permitted transformations. Acquire at least **300 effective independent objects** across three dated intermediate deposits, with at least 100 per deposit and at least two sites, plus 1,000 independently assembled complexity/medium-matched controls for estimating the rarity bound. Require the diagnostic compound in all three deposits, in temporal order under the date distributions. Pre-firing pottery, post-firing graffiti and seal faces are separate strata.

The fixed-rate planning alternative is 5% occurrence against 1%. For 300 objects, rejecting at ten occurrences has size 0.001023 and power **0.9350**; 180 is the smallest sample meeting 80% in this ideal calculation. The larger acquisition target allows the three-deposit recurrence requirement. Recompute joint power with estimated control frequency, clustering, damage and route uncertainty. If it falls below 80%, acquire more before a rejection. [Targets](power-targets.json), `design1`.

Compare the fixed route with 9,999 permutations matched on stroke/junction count, symmetry, ware, medium, date uncertainty and object class; correct over every searched feature/route/transformation. Support graphic transmission along the declared route if the rare structure recurs in the ordered deposits and exceeds the corrected null on held-out sites. Reject that route/feature prediction if secure dates reverse it or an adequately powered confidence bound excludes the proposed excess and the matched null explains recurrence.

The repository holds Quivron's 1980 mark charts, Durrani's 1988/1995 catalogue, Harappa Ravi/Kot Diji objects and southern drawings. It lacks a registered, dated intermediate route. Object 54 has IA/IB alternatives; a strict order must propagate them. Quivron p. 275 distinguishes seal and pottery repertoires, so transfer must be measured over the eligible population. [Rows](inputs-05/evidence-table.json), early Mehrgarh/Rehman/Harappa identifiers; [sources](inputs-05/sources.json); [October 2 South India work](../../2026-10-02-connections-and-priority/south-india/REPORT-reviewed-v1.md).

## 2. Shape features against complexity-matched inventories

Encode stroke count, endpoints, junctions, loops, enclosure, symmetry, angles and relative component positions. Freeze a graph-edit distance on training data; preserve uncertain orientations. Changizi, Zhang, Ye and Shimojo (2006), *The Structures of Letters and Symbols throughout Human History Are Selected to Match Those Found in Objects in Natural Scenes*, *American Naturalist* 167(5), E117–E139, supplies a shared shape-configuration baseline. Extend its configurations for larger compounds before comparison. [Primary paper](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/full/10.1086/502806); [opened full-text witness record](inputs-05/sources.json), `changizi2006`.

Acquire **600 effective objects per comparison group**, spread over at least three independently sampled inventories. Planning targets a fixed rare-feature or below-threshold distance rate of 30% against 20%, with approximate power **0.8887** at alpha 0.0025. Do not treat six hundred drawings of repeated types as independent evidence. Estimate design effects from object/site clusters and simulate the frozen graph statistic with at least 1,000 null and 1,000 alternative datasets; require at least 80% rejection probability at the proposed ten-percentage-point effect. [Targets](power-targets.json), `design2`.

Compare held-out nearest-neighbour distances, rare-feature enrichment and distributional similarity with 9,999 resamples conditioned on repertoire size, complexity, symmetry and marking medium. Repeat after removing simple symmetric marks. Support graphic sharing beyond the matched convergence baseline if the effect survives correction and replicates in independent dated assemblages. Reject the specified effect if an adequately powered interval excludes it. Transmission additionally requires the dated route in design 1 or chronological predictive result in design 3.

The repository holds images and the prior South India decomposition/membership audit, but no complete blinded graph annotations or matched independent null inventories. Mehrgarh's 147 variants/50 categories and Rehman Dheri's 94 catalogue indices require normalization; their numbering systems cannot supply a shared sign identity. [Rows](inputs-05/evidence-table.json), `mehrgarh-pottery1980`, `rehman-dheri-stratified`; [previous source recovery](../../2026-10-02-source-recovery/south-india/REPORT.md).

## 3. Seriation: copying with mutation versus contextual independence

Acquire **12 dated assemblages with 30 effective independent objects each across at least three sites**, totaling 360. A full object-by-feature-by-deposit matrix must include ware, surface, marking stage, complexity and registration. Resolve the plausible 14/41 Rehman phase transposition by catalogue recount, preserving printed and reconstructed columns. Propagate stratigraphic order and complete date distributions instead of using midpoint order alone. [Audit](inputs-05/dating-details.json), `count_audit`.

Define both models over the same frozen feature states and covariates. The contextual independence model has probabilities `q_context = softmax(beta × covariates)`, including complexity, medium, ware and production context, with hierarchical site effects. Counts have a Dirichlet-multinomial sampling layer. Conditional on those factors, a deposit's repertoire is independent of the preceding deposit. The copying model uses `q_t = (1 − rho) q_context,t + rho M_mu q_previous`, where `q_previous` comes from the prespecified earlier route deposit and `M_mu` is a frozen transition matrix over legal single-feature edits. Both models share covariates, priors and the same dispersion layer. No ancestry is assigned from the observed closest shape match.

The planning alternative fixes **rho = 0.70**, mutation probability **mu = 0.10**, and Dirichlet concentration **50**. Target a held-out log-score advantage of **0.10 bits per object** for copying. Fit nuisance coefficients on training data; hold out whole sites and temporal blocks. Use dates only in stratigraphically admissible permutations. Generate at least **1,000 null and 1,000 alternative simulations**, with the planned exclusions and missingness, and require at least **80% power** at alpha 0.0025. The proposed 360-object target has **no estimated power yet**. Dirichlet overdispersion and within-site dependence can require substantially more raw objects than effective units. [Targets](power-targets.json), `design3`, `power_estimated: false`.

Support this copying model if it predicts held-out repertoires better at the prespecified effect, with corrected significance, and recovers independent chronology under date uncertainty. Reject the rho/mu/effect prediction if adequate power excludes the gain or the model reverses securely dated deposits. A failure to recover twelve eligible assemblages is an acquisition outcome, not a negative transmission evaluation.

Three additive exact-count rows currently have at least thirty objects: Kot Diji 95, Rangpur 222 and Rehman Dheri 190. None supplies twelve normalized, specimen-registered deposits. The matrix may be buildable from the Rehman catalogue; its arithmetic discrepancy alone does not block reconstruction. Modern calibration conventions and comparable post-urban matrices remain missing. [Evidence rows](inputs-05/evidence-table.json), `marked_object_count`, `count_kind`; [regional output](run-2026-10-07-04/summary.json), `regions`.

## 4. Positional and combinatorial rules

Acquire **400 effective multi-mark objects per comparison group/time slice**, with at least three dated intermediate slices on the route. Planning detects a prespecified initial/terminal, adjacency or compound-position rule at 20% against 10%, with approximate power **0.8836** at alpha 0.0025. Simulate the complete multi-slice, cluster-aware statistic to achieve at least 80% joint power before evaluation. [Targets](power-targets.json), `design4`.

Preserve physical order, surface, damage and incision stage. Annotate seal faces and their mirrored impressions explicitly. Freeze candidate mappings and directional rules in discovery data; test held-out sites using 9,999 shuffles preserving object length, sign marginals, site and object class, plus complexity-matched external inventories. Include the Volume I p. 265 reported direction difference between Iron Age graffiti and Indus pot texts in the model. Sanur's permutations are observations to explain, not a fixed reading direction. [Claim records](inputs-05/continuity-claims.json), `tn-morphology`, `mahadevan-pairs`, `rehman-continuity-followup`.

Support positional transfer if a fixed rule recurs beyond the conditioned null in the ordered intermediates. Reject the declared effect if adequate-power bounds exclude it, or if it is explained by vessel/production conventions. The repository holds Indus sequences and southern illustrations, but no complete dated, direction-annotated intermediate multi-mark corpus. Total sherd counts do not establish this sample size.

## 5. Firing technology and mark frequency

Recover marked and unmarked vessels by firing batch/workshop, kiln type, ware, vessel form, breakage and marking stage, with independent dates. Keep Quivron's 142 jars, Miller's about 140/estimated 200 load, and the separately cited 100-vessel/33-marked storage room as distinct reported populations. Contemporary open-air and updraft methods at Mehrgarh/Nausharo are controls. [Rows](inputs-05/evidence-table.json), `mehrgarh-failed-firing`, `mehrgarh-nausharo1997`, `nausharo-kiln-II`.

Freeze the alleged change date before scoring. A binomial mixed model with site/workshop effects and, where available, matched difference-in-differences controls tests a proposed decrease of at least ten percentage points. Determine batch-aware sample size by simulation after reconstructing denominators, then acquire until power is at least 80%. Support the specified mechanism if the decline follows the dated change and survives ware/recovery controls; reject the effect-size prediction if adequate sampling excludes that decrease or if it precedes the change. Batch registrations and method-specific mark frequencies are unavailable here.

Join **H98-3485/8437-01** to its feature, lot, deposit history, carbon sample, lab code and original calibration. Review short-lived material, residual/intrusive carbon and stratigraphic constraints; use multiple samples where available. The 2005 sample table and site-phase dates are leads until their join to this sealing is established. Support a before-2800 context if at least 95% of a defensible joined calendar model is before 2800 BCE; reject that endpoint if at least 95% lies after it. An interval crossing 2800 or a missing specimen/sample association is inconclusive. The repository holds descriptions, figures, the sample table and gallery captions, but not the required field-register join. This dates the context, with its association audited separately.

[Targets](power-targets.json), `design7_graphic`.

For a predictive paired-script mapping, acquire at least **60 independent held-out paired objects**, beyond the discovery set, across at least two provenance groups. A fixed candidate predicting whole blocks correctly at 50% against a 20% null has exact power **0.9863** with rejection at 22 correct objects (size 0.002044). Validate that null with sign-marginal/length-matched shuffles and rerun power if it changes. Correct for all candidate alignments. Support a mapping only through independent held-out replication; reject it with adequate power if it fails. An object with one script fails the paired-object eligibility criterion. [Targets](power-targets.json), `design7_alignment`.
