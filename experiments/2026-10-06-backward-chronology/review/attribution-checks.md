# Attribution of the continuity and origin claims

Reviewer check of the 17 records in `amendment-2026-10-07-frenez/continuity-claims.json` (the first 11 are the first-pass claims). Each claim was read against the cited source at the cited location. Local copies were used where they exist; the Cambridge abstract of Coningham and colleagues (1996) and the journal page for Tripathi (1996) were read on the web. Verdicts: confirmed, discrepancy (the package states something the source does not, or omits something that changes the claim), unverifiable (the cited source was not opened by the package or by the reviewer).

## Lal 1960 and 1962

The cover and title page of *Ancient India* no. 16 read 1960 and the imprint line reads 1962. A postscript on p. 3 says no. 17 (1961) appeared on time and no. 16, due in 1960, was delayed two years. Lal's article is stamped "Received on the 20th October 1960" (p. 24). The package keys the work as `lal1960` and notes that the Tamil bibliography gives 1962. Both dates belong to one work: 1960 is the issue label and the date of composition, and 1962 is the printing date. The note in `sources.json` ("use the original issue date") should say that the issue carries both, and the package should use one convention for nominal and printed years; it uses the printed year for Thapar (1967) and the nominal year for Lal.

## Mahadevan 2001

The venue in `sources.json` ("Studia Orientalia 94, pp. 379–386") is correct; the volume is the Parpola Festschrift, *Vidyārṇavavandanam*, edited by Karttunen and Koskikallio. The journal.fi PDF has the same SHA-256 as the package's local copy.
