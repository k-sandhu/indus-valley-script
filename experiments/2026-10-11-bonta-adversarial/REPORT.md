# Carrier, container and toothed-implement meanings after external review

The revised catalogue leads with seven candidate families covering nine Wells codes. It adds precise Sanskrit comparisons for a carrying pole, harrow and comb, reunites W158 with the qualified carriers, and separates their pictorial, contextual and lexical evidence. These are imaginative working meanings under the comparison vocabulary requested by the user; no probability of historical correctness was estimated.

Open the [visual catalogue](run-v6/catalogue.html). It retains all 713 earlier codes, gives 56 exact dictionary sense pointers and twenty bounded inscription examples. The [earlier catalogue](../2026-10-08-bonta-extension/run-2026-10-09-v5/catalogue.html) and October 11 run-v3 through run-v5 remain unchanged.

## Lexical rivals and repaired Sanskrit joins

The former A/B/C grades used occurrence count and selected role, producing 44 A priorities. The current tiers distinguish semantic candidates, structural roles, pictorial leads and conflicted lexical proposals.

The original literal matcher searched one-to-six-letter SLP1 residuals containing a vowel, under imported full-stem values and chosen word windows. Its 41 bhāra residuals are identical for the W233 and W231 motivating pairs because they depend on the same suffix. The [lexical repair](lexical-diagnostics-v3/INTERPRETATION.md) compares every rival across all patterns and adds the same finite joins for every candidate.

Literal sam is a comparatively good candidate in that restricted model, tied with five others below prati. Sa ties with kara and putra; the finite joins also admit a. None has a full intersection across its nonempty original frame. Their ranks vary with the model and imported values, so the specific heavy-load, collected-provisions and share-product readings remain alternatives rather than selected meanings.

Samvāha and saaṃśa are absent as literal queries, but joined **saṃvāha** and **sāṃśa** are attested at MW 226133 and 240923. The new model handles bounded nasal and vowel joins; it has no complete stem morphology or unrestricted Sanskrit parser. Removing either coder's disjoint imported candidates leaves 38 W233 and 19 W231 patterns, with joined ati rank 7 and sam rank 13. The conservative union removes all eight W204 windows because its target code is itself in the SENA disagreement, making that retained-frame intersection undefined. Both exclusion rules remain published.

The five semantic comparisons bhārin, varga, dvāra, kṛṣi and lāṅgala receive alternative stems, both component orders and 1,000 same-length dictionary controls. Before-anchor literal candidate counts are 4, 56, 69, 0 and 7; limited joins give 4, 58, 69, 0 and 7. Those measurements describe dictionary productivity. There are no asserted new sound windows for these words. [All outcomes and exact entries](lexical-diagnostics-v3/RESULTS.json).

W204's separate graphic conflict remains visible: first-coder B043 SENA maps to W373, second-coder to W204. The catalogue retains this source disagreement and withdraws the earlier confident share-product label.

## Visual reasoning, source images and prior work

The visual reviewers compared regularized carrier/person/tool drawings, licensed chart classes and selected source photographs. W154/W156 carry paired hook-like marks above the crossbar; the public W154/W156/W158 images are the same P004/V145 class drawing and cannot show exact Wells variant strokes. The cards state that limitation beside the picture. W527 is a divided panel rather than a verified granary or doorway. Its spine and bars resemble W400 with an added side; related design is a testable rival.

M-16 shows a person with a toothed horizontal implement. Matya is a useful harrow comparison, while a specific plough is visually unselected. M-81 shows two separate grid outlines with a gap. Mayig encodes two P268 tokens where Lipi encodes W617. Recoding W617 as abstract G617a/G617b preserves 1,859 recoded strings but changes tokenwise positional evidence: one token cannot be last and the other cannot be first by construction. A pair-as-block reference restores the original ordering. [Encoding sensitivity](context-diagnostics-v1/W617-recoding.json). The public schematic illustrates adjacency, not cell count or artifact spacing.

Source photographs remain local, with exact PDF hashes, leaves and crop locators in [sources.json](sources.json). A seal and its impression are views of one object. This is model-assisted visual reasoning across selected families and contexts; no blinded withheld-family semantic benchmark was performed.

Rao's 2018 paper already proposed carrier subtypes and agricultural labour from person composites. Those are precedents for the present meanings. New work here consists of Wells-code context diagnostics, exact sense choices and explicit rival tests. [Rao, *The Indus Script and Economics. A Role for Indus Seals and Tablets in Rationing and Administration of Labor*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.00049).

[Harappa/HARP's tablet photograph](https://www.harappa.com/slide/three-groups-tablets) supplies a public excavation-image lead, also reproduced in Rao's paper. It was not added as a new object group because the excavation/CISI join was incomplete. The [October 8 public-image audit](../2026-10-08-public-image-audit/README.md) preserves visitor photographs, Rakhigarhi reporting and museum leads with their unresolved joins. Public upload dates do not establish newly excavated evidence.

Mahadevan's [arrow/harrow discussion](https://www.harappa.com/content/indus-arrow-and-harrow-sign) supplies a competing interpretation of the comb component. Kenoyer's [*Ancient Textiles of the Indus Valley Region*](https://www.harappa.com/sites/default/files/pdf/Kenoyer2004%20IndusTextilesfinal.pdf) supports the archaeological activity but provides no sign-level bridge selecting W825 as vastra.

Repeated Sanskrit fits to the same selected strings do not replace that observation plan.
