# Carrier, category and compartment hypotheses after adversarial review

The reviewed catalogue retains eight candidate families, covering nine Wells codes. Carrier variants have the closest agreement between their pictures and repeated closing positions. The comb, bounded panel and paired grids support broader closing-category interpretations. The staff-bearing and tool-bearing people supply useful pictorial anchors with weaker positional support. These are ranked exploratory meanings under the Sanskrit comparison the user requested; no posterior on meanings was estimated.

Open the [reviewed visual catalogue](run-v5/catalogue.html). Each card gives the selected Sanskrit dictionary sense, an inscription example, alternatives and the exact structural results. All 713 earlier codes remain searchable. The [earlier catalogue](../2026-10-08-bonta-extension/run-2026-10-09-v5/catalogue.html) is preserved.

## What the structural tests establish

The frame contains 2,930 complete single-line faces, 2,469 object families and 1,859 distinct complete code strings. Within each distinct string, 5,000 permutations preserve its length and sign multiset while randomizing positions. Singleton strings are excluded from position tests. The analysis tests initial, medial and final enrichment for all 596 previously new codes, giving 1,788 comparisons. Forty codes have at least one BH-adjusted q below 0.05. That count describes positional organization.

The stricter sensitivity compares each observed standardized count with the maximum over all 1,788 comparisons in every permutation. This common reference accounts for selecting favourable code/position combinations under the declared ordering null. Neither adjustment controls the selection of pictorial meanings or Sanskrit words.

All exact values are in [position-audit.json](run-v2/position-audit.json). A p of 0.000200 is the resolution floor, 1/5,001. The family sensitivity selects the longest complete face of each family. Distinct families can still share moulds or production traditions. The site sensitivities generally preserve carrier/closing tendencies at Harappa and Mohenjo-daro, but include small samples and exceptions; they were not used as independent confirmations of meanings.

W142 is medial in 27/39 tokens. Its BH-adjusted result is favourable, but maximum-statistic p is 0.478. The staff-bearing picture therefore carries the candidate; a separately established office or grammatical function does not. W130 occurs in eight distinct texts and has no selected positional enrichment.

[Exact substitution templates](run-v2/substitution-audit.json) hold the whole remaining string, length and position fixed. W154 and W156 share four such templates. W151 and W161 share two. W400 substitutes with W151 in two templates and W527 in one. These observations strengthen a common slot/category possibility. They also admit grammatical suffixes, issuer emblems and measures. Many templates are short; a shared slot alone supplies little semantic specificity.

W158 should be kept separate from W154/W156. It is final in 7/19 tokens, compared with 25/32 for W154. The selected final/nonfinal Fisher comparison gives p = 0.006247. This is a descriptive, post-outcome contrast with shared production traditions unresolved, but it is sufficient reason to avoid pooling all visually related carriers as interchangeable signs.

## Lexical matches that were weakened

The earlier A grade used at least ten occurrences, a chosen role label and noninheritance. Its 44 entries were priorities for exploration. This iteration replaces the grade with separate semantic and structural decisions.

Three old post-outcome refinements were examined literally, alongside the old normalized matcher. The literal matcher retains exact SLP1 stems; the normalized matcher also collapses distinctions in the inferred residual. Every search still assumes a sign window corresponds to one dictionary word, permits several imported values, and limits the missing residual to one through six SLP1 characters containing a vowel.

| Earlier proposal | Object families for motivating pair | Literal exact residual alternatives | Selected residual fits | Full literal intersection |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| W233–W151 → ati-bhāra | 1 | 41 | ati in 6/46 searched patterns | 0 |
| W231–W151 → sam-bhāra | 1 | 41 | sam in 7/22 | 0 |
| W204–W540 → sa-bhāga | 1 | 115 | sa in 5/8 | 0 |

Atibhāra is an attested dictionary comparison. Mahābhāra, udakabhāra, aṃsabhāra and other compounds yield competing residuals for the same load anchor. Replacing bhāra with vāha or bhārika changes the candidate set; direct ativāha and atibhārika are absent from this pinned headword index. Controls using 1,000 same-length dictionary stems measure word-ending productivity. They cannot supply a historical probability for a reading or resolve sandhi, language and word boundaries. [All rivals and control outcomes](run-v2/lexical-adversarial.json).

The literal intersection test challenges a universal constant residual under this window model. A failed intersection can also reflect the wrong word boundary, a wrong imported sign value or a logographic context; it is not evidence that every occurrence must obey this word model. The proposed ati, sam and sa meanings lose their selected status here.

W204 also conflicts with the existing blind graphic review. The first coder matched Bonta's B043 SENA drawing to W373; the second matched it to W204. Inspection of manuscript leaf 81 and the local font supports treating W204 as a crosswalk lead. The catalogue exposes that disagreement and withdraws the confident shared-produce label. [Graphic sensitivity](run-v2/graphic-sensitivity.json).

## Visual reasoning, source photographs and prior work

This pass compares carrier compounds, repeated slots and exact substitutions, then checks selected source photographs against the code interpretation. It is a model-assisted review of selected images and transcriptions. It does not constitute a blinded, withheld-family benchmark of visual semantic reasoning. The [earlier visual-reasoning inventory](../2026-10-08-public-image-audit/README.md) records that remaining gap.

In the primary M-81A impression and corresponding seal panel, two adjacent grids are visible. Mayig records two P268 tokens while Lipi uses one W617. This changes token count and potential lexical boundaries, so the paired-compartment meaning carries an explicit segmentation rival. M-16A supplies the visible toothed implement mentioned above. Each photographic witness has a source PDF hash and exact crop/page locator in [sources.json](sources.json); both downloaded photographs remain local. Viewing a seal and its impression adds views of the same object, not independent objects.

Rao's 2018 paper already proposed carrier subtypes and agricultural labour interpretations from composite person signs. It includes HARP tablets with a carrier design and container-like head. Its interpretation is a prior hypothesis for these families. The additions here are specific Wells-code diagnostics, explicit Sanskrit sense choices and alternative tests. [Rao, *The Indus Script and Economics. A Role for Indus Seals and Tablets in Rationing and Administration of Labor*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.00049).

[Harappa/HARP's three-groups tablet page](https://www.harappa.com/slide/three-groups-tablets) supplies a public excavation-image lead and notes scribal variation. The photograph is also reproduced in the prior paper. It is not a newly counted object group in this analysis: a reliable excavation/CISI concordance for that online group was not completed. Public upload dates and venue do not establish object novelty. The [October 8 public-image search](../2026-10-08-public-image-audit/README.md) retains visitor photographs, Rakhigarhi field reporting and museum leads, with unresolved object joins.

Mahadevan's [arrow and harrow discussion](https://www.harappa.com/content/indus-arrow-and-harrow-sign) provides an alternative reading of the comb-like design. It leaves the Sanskrit gaṇa comparison open. Kenoyer's [*Ancient Textiles of the Indus Valley Region*](https://www.harappa.com/sites/default/files/pdf/Kenoyer2004%20IndusTextilesfinal.pdf) makes textile production a plausible archaeological activity; it supplies no sign-level bridge selecting W825 as vastra. The new catalogue retains that rare sign among pictorial leads.

## Tests that would change the shortlist

Carrier meanings should predict consistent associations with labour, transport, containers or rations in independently joined excavated contexts. An issuer/name interpretation predicts continuity with repeated issuer emblems without the commodity restriction. The distinction requires physical/context metadata, rather than another Sanskrit fit to the same strings.

W400 and W527 should be compared against closing suffixes and account categories in withheld families, with token segmentation fixed before scoring. A selected group sense should explain contexts beyond being final. W617 needs image-based segmentation adjudication before any two-sign lexical reading. W130 needs tool comparison and source contexts distinguishing cultivation from other crafts. The user-requested imaginative meanings remain useful candidates for those tests.

## Reproduction and preservation

Run-v1 has partial diagnostics and failed while serializing a NumPy integer. Run-v2 corrected serialization and added literal matching; its successful diagnostics are preserved. Run-v3 is the initial public-viewer draft. Run-v4 added the CLI subscription check. Run-v5 uses enlarged detail crops of the licensed chart cells after display inspection. The [decisions](review-decisions.json) document changes and rejected refinements; [verification](verification.json) records source/output hashes, example validation and endpoint preservation.

For a new diagnostic endpoint, use `python -B experiments/2026-10-11-bonta-adversarial/analyze.py --run <new-name>` with NumPy, Pillow and the pinned local inputs. The maximum-statistic script currently targets run-v2 and refuses an existing output; adapt paths in a new dated copy if repeating it. For a new public viewer, use `python -B experiments/2026-10-11-bonta-adversarial/build_catalogue.py --run <new-name>`; it reads frozen run-v2 diagnostics and requires the local input/sense index to validate the small public excerpt selection. An empty public clone can inspect the saved outputs and open the viewer without those inputs.
