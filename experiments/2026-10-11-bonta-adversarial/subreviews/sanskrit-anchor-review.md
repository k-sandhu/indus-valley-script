# Sanskrit sense review of the shortlisted families

The selected Sanskrit senses support the intended broad comparisons. Two ledger fields need correction before the next catalogue is built: the gloss for *adhikārin* combines different sense records, and the period assigned to *kṛṣaka* is more specific than its selected citation supports. The carrier, group, compartment and cultivation comparisons can remain exploratory candidates, with the lexical distinctions below visible on their cards.

This review compared the seventeen shortlisted headwords in [run-v5/sanskrit-senses.json](../run-v5/sanskrit-senses.json) with their exact records in the pinned Cologne Monier-Williams source. It inspected the report, hypotheses and source index; it did not recompute corpus statistics, inspect all original inscription photographs or adjudicate the language of the inscriptions. The source pin is `sanskrit-lexicon/csl-orig` commit `55e8addbd96e8d9001b8789026b026763f4049c1`; the local `mw.txt` SHA256 is `555ecf5aadaaf21e5965d6b3b419f428fc9ab9237f563627001ceca468d0cff8`. [Pinned source](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-orig/blob/55e8addbd96e8d9001b8789026b026763f4049c1/v02/mw/mw.txt), [official display](https://www.sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/MWScan/2020/web/webtc2/index.php).

The web check identified official scanned-page URLs. The browser tool could not render the requested scans, so the findings below depend on the preserved digitized records rather than a new visual verification of the print. Dictionary citations identify a source used by the lexicographer; they do not establish a word's first occurrence or its Bronze Age use. Rao's carrier and agricultural-worker proposals already supply precedents for these pictorial families. [Rao 2018, *The Indus Script and Economics. A Role for Indus Seals and Tablets in Rationing and Administration of Labor*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.00049).

## Corrections to integrate

For *adhikārin*, record **4197.1**, p. **20,3**, gives the superintendent/governor sense. The authority or eligibility comparison belongs to **4197**; an official or claimant belongs to **4198**. Keep 4197.1 if the card says “superintendent or governor,” or add a separate record for the broader entitled/authorized agent. The selected records supply no textual citation, so “date not established by this selected record” is a more accurate period label than “later institutional sense.” The staff in W142 directly motivates *daṇḍin*; it supplies no additional bridge to the administrative *adhikārin* sense. [Official print page 20](https://sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/MWScan/MWScanpdf/mw0020-adharAk.pdf).

For *kṛṣaka*, record **55086**, p. **306,1**, cites **Cāṇ.**, not an epic. Replace “Epic and later” with “Cāṇ. cited; chronology not established here.” The current farmer/cultivator gloss is suitable. *Kṛṣi* and *lāṅgala* at the same conceptual level have Rigvedic citations, but they refer respectively to the activity and the tool. They provide alternatives for what a person-with-implement sign could encode; they do not make the pictured implement a verified plough. [Official print page 306](https://sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/MWScan/MWScanpdf/mw0306-kRzanAvat.pdf).

## Distinctions worth keeping in the catalogue

| Family | Lexical distinction | Integration decision and distinguishing evidence |
|---|---|---|
| W151, W154, W156, W161 | *Bhāra* 149882 is a load or burden. *Bhārin* 149938 is a laden bearer; *bhārika* 149937 is a porter. The latter records cite Manu and later works, or Rājataraṅgiṇī/Kathāsaritsāgara. The selected human-carrier *vāha* 192710 relies on Wilson; the Rigvedic draught-animal sense is another record, 192708. | Retain both worker and consignment alternatives. Contexts independently identifying counted people, work or payments would favour the worker scenario; commodity quantities and package impressions would favour a load category. Final position does not decide that distinction. |
| W154, W156 | *Kumbha* 52479, p. 293,1, gives a vessel comparison; a grain-capacity unit is another sense, 52482. | Keep the jar/container comparison. A jar-like addition can qualify cargo, carrier or issuer; it does not select a later capacity unit or a wage grade. Compare variants in matched physical tablet groups. |
| W400 | *Gaṇa* 62524 permits a group or class. *Varga* 187198.05 permits a division or set. *Gaṇanā* 62679 refers back to the counting sense of *gaṇana* 62678. | Retain group/class as the preferred semantic family. Counted groups, an act of counting and an arithmetic result are different meanings. A total requires independently recognized quantities and a repeatable arithmetic relation. A group/category predicts substitution among comparable closing classes without that relation. |
| W527, W617 | *Koṣṭha* 56870 specifically supplies a granary/store-room; enclosed area is the separate 56878, p. 314,3. *Dvāra* 98316 supplies the door/entrance comparison. | Add 56878 as a separate sense if “compartment” becomes the selected lexical gloss; otherwise leave it as the pictorial description. Portable containers, store buildings and access points predict different physical sealing contexts. [Official print page 314](https://sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/MWScan/MWScanpdf/mw0314-kolyA.pdf). |
| W617 | *Dvaya* 98138 is the adjective double/twofold, with Rigvedic citation. The two-things/pair sense is 98138.3 with different citations. *Māna* 162838 gives measure or standard; it identifies no particular unit. | Keep “paired/composite” with the segmentation rival. Two grids can represent one composite token or two repeated tokens. Image adjudication and repeatable quantity relationships must precede any value-two or named-unit assignment. |
| W142 | *Daṇḍin* 89766 describes carrying a stick; its ascetic and door-keeper/policeman senses have different records. | Make staff-holder the direct anchor and administrative agent an additional scenario. A matched building/access context would support keeper or gate attendant; ritual assemblages or varied staff-users would keep other roles active. |
| W130 | *Kṛṣaka* denotes a worker, *kṛṣi* an activity and *lāṅgala* a tool. | Keep the tool-bearing person as the graphic observation. Compare implement shape and independently established agricultural contexts before selecting cultivation over another craft. M-16 and M-1981 remain examples to inspect rather than lexical attestations. |

The dictionary labels are headwords. In a phonetic Sanskrit model, *bhārin* and *daṇḍin* can appear as nominative *bhārī* and *daṇḍī*, and other grammatical forms have other endings. A search over headword stems therefore tests its chosen normalization and word-boundary assumptions. In a semantic/logographic model, the same headwords can serve as convenient glosses without specifying pronunciation. The catalogue should keep the model used by each lexical test explicit.

## Three additional comparisons with distinct tests

These additions are proposed after seeing the existing shortlist. None has been tested against a withheld inscription set.

| Anchor and exact MW locator | Candidate role | Distinguishing prediction |
|---|---|---|
| **yuga**, 171838, p. 854,1: yoke/team; its pair sense is separate 171839 | Equipment or a paired service unit, as an alternative to the human worker interpretation of W151 and relatives | A unit/equipment interpretation predicts continuity with otherwise comparable depictions that preserve the crossbar and paired loads while changing or omitting the person. A worker interpretation predicts category distinctions tied to the person and task. The visual concordance would need to be fixed before scoring those distributions. Do not import the age/cycle or symbolic-number senses of *yuga*. [Print page 854](https://sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/MWScan/MWScanpdf/mw0854-yuktikathana.pdf). |
| **kośa**, 56743, p. 314,1: box/chest/container | Portable container, as an alternative to a store-room or doorway for W527/W617 | On independently classified sealings, a portable-container hypothesis predicts associations with container closures or package impressions; a room or doorway hypothesis predicts access/building contexts. Keep the vessel/box sense apart from later treasury, wealth or lexicon senses. [Print page 314](https://sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/MWScan/MWScanpdf/mw0314-kolyA.pdf). |
| **śreṇi**, 223691, p. 1102,2: row/series; guild/company is separate 223693 | An ordered row or series for W400; an occupational association as a second scenario | A series hypothesis predicts regular relationships between graphic repetition, tooth count and patterned contexts after controlling engraving variation. An occupational-company scenario predicts recurrence with multiple independently identified workers from one task group. The guild sense cites Manu/epic sources and is a later cultural comparison. Neither interpretation follows from the final slot alone. [Print page 1102](https://sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/MWScan/MWScanpdf/mw1102-zrutimUla.pdf). |

The most useful immediate change is to correct the two sense fields and preserve the noun/worker/activity distinctions. The three added anchors broaden the competing semantic tests; they should enter the catalogue as alternatives without increasing its evidence ranking.
