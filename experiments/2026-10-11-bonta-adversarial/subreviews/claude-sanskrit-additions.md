# Verification of Claude's direct pictorial Sanskrit additions

Claude's proposed *vivadha*, *matya* and *kaṅkata* comparisons have the stated senses in the pinned Monier-Williams source. They sharpen three visible components: the carrying pole, the toothed implement and the comb. Add them as semantic anchors alongside the earlier worker and category proposals. The weight sense of *bhāra* and grain-capacity sense of *kumbha* are also present, but their particular quantities belong to the later references cited by the dictionary.

I inspected records in the local `mw.txt` and the earlier dictionary index, then used the official web scan index to locate the corresponding printed pages. The web PDF pages provide stable page URLs but were not newly rendered during this check. The pin and SHA256 are recorded in [sources-claude-sanskrit.json](sources-claude-sanskrit.json). The candidate additions came from the completed [Claude review](../claude-review.md); this follow-up verifies its lexical pointers and examines what follows from them. No new inscription analysis or first-attestation study was run here.

For the current builder, the simplest direct anchor is **vivadha**, SLP1 **vivaDa**, paired with **199980**. The variant lookup **vIvaDa** exists in the index under **199980.1** and **203957**. The former points to record 199980; the latter is a cross-reference. A `vīvadha` lookup with entry 199980 would fail the builder's same-key/same-ID assertion even though the variant is attested inside that record. Preserve the canonical headword and its recorded variant explicitly rather than changing the source index.

## Measures as separate rival senses

*Bhāra* **149885**, p. **753,1**, is a weight sense distinct from the general burden/load record **149882**. It cites Harivaṃśa, Pañcatantra and Suśruta and gives a particular equivalence of twenty tulās. *Kumbha* **52482**, p. **293,1**, is distinct from the jar record **52479**. It cites Manu 8.320 and Hemādri for a grain measure; the entry itself reports differing capacity conventions. These are appropriate reminders that a load or jar may function as a measure. Their historical ratios should remain outside the Indus assignments. [Bhāra print page 753](https://sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/MWScan/MWScanpdf/mw0753-bhAma.pdf), [kumbha print page 293](https://sanskrit-lexicon.uni-koeln.de/scans/MWScan/MWScanpdf/mw0293-kumbh.pdf).

A preceding numeral would be compatible with people, discrete loads, containers and measured amounts. To favour a measure interpretation over a counted worker or container, require repeated arithmetic relationships or independently identified quantity/commodity associations across different numerals and object families. Neither a two-loop drawing nor a later dictionary equivalence supplies an Indus unit value.

## Distinguishing tests to retain

For the *vivadha* equipment comparison, classify bearer, suspended contents and crossbar components on source images before checking which combinations recur in counted or named-party contexts. That separates a carrier-class hypothesis from a consignment category more directly than another dictionary compound fit. The reported difference between W151 and jar-qualified carriers should be scripted and sensitivity-tested before it influences the rank.

For *matya*, compare the handheld component with W400's standalone toothed shape using fixed graphic features: spine, projection arrangement, handle/arm connection and any working edge. The dictionary's harrow/roller gloss alone does not resolve the artifact's tool identity. If the same component appears with multiple independently identified tool-users, that would favour a shared implement class over a particular named person.

For *kaṅkata*, compare W400's preceding-sign distribution with matched closing signs after controlling site, text length, formula duplication and numeral context. An object/tool interpretation and a collective-category interpretation make different predictions only once the preceding signs have independently supported classes. The same strings cannot both define those classes and validate the result without a separate evaluation frame.

## Additional corrections confirmed

The exact *gopāla* record **67231**, p. **365,2**, cites VS. 30.11 and Śatapatha Brāhmaṇa IV as well as later sources. Replace its “Later comparison” label with those citation details. The cowherd sense is supported; its selected record cannot be used to date a special Indus occupation. The earlier corrections to *adhikārin* 4197.1 and *kṛṣaka* 55086 remain in [the first Sanskrit subreview](sanskrit-anchor-review.md).

W527's relationship to W400 remains a graphic rival: it shares a spine and parallel bars, then closes their free ends with another upright. This resemblance supports testing a related or modified closing design. It establishes neither a historical derivation nor an interchangeable allograph. The [visual subreview](visual-family-review.md) gives the actual component and public-image differences.
