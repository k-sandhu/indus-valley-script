Where they were

Each circle is one of a civilisation's largest settlements in this period; its area shows the upper published estimate of the settlement's extent. Positions are for location only. Zoom in on the Near East to see the other names; hover a circle for its figures.

How big their largest settlements were

Up to five of the largest settlements in each civilisation, as published ranges of occupied area in hectares (1 ha = 100 × 100 m). A row with several dots of similar size has several cities of the same rank; a row with one dot far ahead has a single dominant centre.

How large their territory was

Published estimates of the area each civilisation's settlements or political control covered, on a logarithmic scale: each gridline is ten times the one before. Definitions differ: the label after each line says whether the figure is the spread of sites, political control or settled land, and only figures of the same kind compare directly.

How many people lived in the largest city

Population estimates for the largest settlement, where a published one exists. Most multiply occupied area by an assumed density of people per hectare, so their ranges are wide.
