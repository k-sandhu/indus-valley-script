# H-912 belongs to a four-unit series; its contents remain unanchored

We recovered the four catalogue entries behind Parpola's vessel-and-fish comparison: H-302, H-912, H-1191 and H-1192. Three have four–V–fish; one has four–fish–V. All four have fish in the longer inscription on their other face. Their best current functional gloss is **four units in the Fish category**, where Fish names an unresolved category. A standard allocation and a record of four vessels containing fish predict the same graphic observations. No registered container, measured allotment or fish-remains assemblage currently distinguishes them.

The source audit also changes the apparent precision of H-912's context. The original excavation records give **4–7 feet below the surface**, across two squares and a collection made over three months. The export's 5.5 feet equals that range's midpoint. We preserve the source range and the derivative point separately. Nearby jars, beads and weights belong in a source-qualified context record; none is an independently established physical referent of the tablet.

## Excavation links and their strength

The [archaeological continuation](../2026-10-20-h912-excavation-links/REPORT.md) inspected the original Vats narrative, catalogue tabulation and relevant drawings. Vats pp. 58–60 place the 8650 collection in squares H12/21 and I12/1. The tiny finds were recovered with knives and sieves continuously over three months. The table on p. 348 assigns 8650c and a 4–7-foot range to catalogue no. 448. A shared square, broad depth interval and registration series supplies a recovery-area association. It supplies no closed container or single-deposition association.

The source narrative mentions jars 10312 and 10360 near a platform. It does not link their contents or attachments to H-912. Beads, precious materials and two small weights motivate an economic-context rival, but some bead records carry a Stratum III label against the Stratum IV overview. These competing assertions prevent treating the entire prefix as one sealed workshop deposit. The source audit also retains suffix conflicts between a derivative tablet registration and an original gold-bead entry.

Belcher's 1991 Mound E fish deposits and 2018 fish-remains synthesis supply independent fish-resource observations. Their published deposits have no recovered registration crosswalk to H-912's Area F collection. This is a missing join. It contributes neither a fish-content confirmation nor a fish-content contradiction.

We did recover a separate setting-linked frame from Belcher 1991's plan, Figure 8.2, printed p. 109. Its fourteen numbered objects join uniquely to fourteen export families with 21 recorded faces. Five carry modified fish-family signs in their transcriptions: H-1799, H-1867, H-1868, H-1869 and H-1908. None carries plain W220. Eight objects carry plain W700, and none has the exact H-912 short-face construction. The plan places most of the fish-bearing tokens in or beside Room 3; it does not assign them to the street fish-remains lots in Figure 8.8. The [post-outcome eligibility screen](fish-frame-screen-v1.json) retains every registration and positional qualifier. This is a source worklist for obtaining excavation lots and photographs, with no enrichment test or contents label.

## Frozen comparison

The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [predictions](PREDICTIONS.json), [freeze notes](FREEZE-NOTES.md), code and learned probabilities were frozen before one logged confirmation read. The [bench freeze](bench-freeze-v1.json) and [inspection](bench-inspect-v1.json) record that procedure. Earlier work examined the whole corpus and fish-number statistics; this is a frozen evaluation on historically exposed material. The private ledger retains that exposure.

The primary population uses complete, clean Harappa tablets with plain W700, a strict short- or tall-stroke group first, and an optional item sign before or after V. Whole-carrier copies are collapsed; scores are averaged within the shared loader's groups of aliases and identical texts. This grouping puts the three four–V–fish records in one component and the four–fish–V record in another. The confirmation half contains three catalogue entries, two whole-carrier prototypes and **one component**.

The fixed-four prediction assigns probability 0.9 to four and distributes 0.1 across the other values from one to 24. Two quantity references were learned from non-fish discovery tablets. The second conditions on stroke family, which matters because the four core fish forms use short strokes while most other quantity faces use tall strokes. The strongest allocation rival uses the same fixed-four probabilities and changes the proposed use of the token.

These are the [exact reserved outputs](confirmation-v1/comparison.json). Neither comparison passes. The small group count prevents inferential support; it does not falsify the reading. A meaning-map calibration of the appended fish is infeasible because discovery supplies only one qualifying component and no other appended item class. We recorded that before confirmation instead of treating copied examples as independent classes.

The broad sensitivity preserves M-928, a Mohenjo-daro seal transcribed with one tall stroke, an internally marked V and a modified fish. Its held photograph was inspected. It falls outside the frozen plain-V Harappa-tablet scope. It prevents extending the four-value claim to every V-family and fish-family combination.

## The strongest readings

| Reading | Evidence it explains | Extra observation it needs |
|---|---|---|
| Four vessels containing fish | The visible numeral, V's vessel resemblance, fish shape and the four-entry series | An individually registered content or allotment relation, plus independent unit calibration |
| Standard four-unit allocation indexed to a Fish category | Repeated quantity-shaped faces, fixed four in this series and fish on both faces | Evidence of issue, presentation, redemption or allocation, with independently identified categories |
| Four vessel offerings addressed to a fish-designated recipient | The same quantity-shaped forms and the person-and-vessel comparison on other tablets | An independently established recipient or religious-use context for the relevant series |
| A fixed designation with a fish rebus | Repeated strings, related longer legends and stable combinations | External lexical or name correspondences predicting new observations |

H-246 supplies a useful check on grammatical role. Its comparable three–V construction has a human-like form in the following slot; H-1921 has the same transcription but no held image. This makes a recipient or account category a concrete rival to contents. It does not establish that W125 means recipient, and it does not authenticate a star or divine-name reading for fish.

The absolute capacity also remains unresolved. Rao 2018 repeats a roughly forty-litre estimate from the vessel literature. The project's [existing metrology audit](../../research/literature/metrology/README.md) already traces its selected fitting vessels, long strokes, reconstructed capacities and measurement-to-object conflicts. It has no independently measured short-stroke unit applicable to H-912. We cannot turn its four short strokes into 160 litres from that citation.

Counting vessels and measuring capacity are distinct physical readings. The first predicts four physical containers; it needs no litre calibration. The second predicts an amount equal to four standard measures, possibly held in a different number of containers, and needs an independently established measure. A registered group of four fish-containing vessels could therefore test the count reading directly. The two nearby jars in Vats's narrative have no stated tablet link, so their number adjudicates neither reading.

## Working reading and deciding test

The frozen rule leaves the literal four-vessel fish reading at **0.35**, because no physical prediction could be evaluated. Our separate post-outcome working model is **a standard four-unit record indexed to the Fish category**, credence **0.40**. Fish here names the unresolved graphic category; it can denote contents, an account, an institution or a recipient. The proposed model keeps the quantity and category roles separate and predicts that the denomination remains four in new instances of this tightly defined construction. [New conjecture](../../knowledge/conjectures/C-0012-fish-category-four-unit-records.md) and [proposed C-0009 update](C-0009-UPDATE.md).

The decisive next witness is an individually registered member of the series attached to a container, allotment or documented use event. Before opening its outcome, define the unit, capacity or mass tolerance, the test for fish contents and the physical relation that makes the association valid. A single secure linked non-fish transfer would favour a recipient or account interpretation over literal fish contents. New plain-fish short-stroke W700 constructions with a securely different value would overturn the fixed-four category claim. The boldest current reading is **a standard four-unit Fish record**; delivery of four vessels of fish remains its strongest specific content hypothesis.
