# Proposed working-model addition

H-912 B belongs to a standard four-unit series indexed by a fish-designated category. The four recovered catalogue entries preserve three four–V–fish forms and one four–fish–V form, with related fish-bearing legends on their other faces. We assign the category-and-denomination model credence 0.40. The literal four-vessel fish proposal remains at 0.35. [Conjecture C-0012](../../knowledge/conjectures/C-0012-fish-category-four-unit-records.md), [C-0009 update](C-0009-UPDATE.md).

The text alone leaves contents, account and recipient roles tied. The primary context is a broad excavated collection, not a closed group of fish containers. An individually registered tablet-to-container, allotment or use relation must decide the role. Absolute capacity remains uncalibrated; the long-stroke forty-litre literature supplies no independent short-stroke unit here.
