## Competing readings

1. **Measured contents:** the strokes count vessels or vessel-units, V names the unit, and fish names literal contents. A productive measurement phrase should admit quantity variation and item substitution where sufficiently sampled. Physical attachment, residue, measured capacity or an independently described allotment would distinguish this from the alternatives.
2. **Standard allocation or entitlement:** the tablet identifies a prescribed quota, service or exchange category. Four can be a fixed denomination. Fish can name contents, an issuer, a beneficiary or a category; the model does not need every token to accompany a physical fish container. This is the strongest economic rival to a record of delivered contents.
3. **Designation or cultic name:** fish has a lexical or rebus value in a name or institutional designation, V is a shared ending or other conventional sign, and the strokes classify the designation or form part of it. A specifically celestial interpretation needs evidence beyond fish shape and later homophony.
4. **Graphic construction:** some apparent numerals or V–fish combinations form fixed graphic expressions. The test must preserve short and tall strokes, fine V variants, face polarity and uncertainty before assigning constituent roles.

The offering subtype of reading 1 adds a religious-use premise. A general economic association cannot establish that premise. A 40-litre unit, if sourced in a rival publication, will be traced to its actual pot measurement and sign association rather than adopted from a repeated citation.

Candidate tests are predictive quantity models and a calibrated association between the appended sign and the number on its tablet face. The quantity reference must include a standard-quota model, a global tablet-value model and the paired descriptor where support permits. Any meaning-map test uses `python -m bench meaning-map`, discovery reliability, re-split variance, plausible-effect power and a component-influence rule. If the construction has too few independent groups or matched signs for calibration, record that infeasibility and use exact source comparisons without inventing a significance claim. A synthetic success supplies no archaeological evidence.

Physical links receive an explicit evidence grade: attachment or measured association; same registered assemblage; shared locus without a closed assemblage; site only; no join. Only the first grade can test contents or a physical quantity correspondence directly. Negative searches measure current coverage. An absent source join neither confirms nor kills a reading.

The starting credence for the literal four-vessel fish reading is 0.35. Structural evidence alone can change it by at most 0.05. Three independent physically anchored agreements can raise it to 0.65 under C-0009's rule; an incompatible secure linked association lowers it to 0.10. A fixed allotment will be stated separately from a delivery record, with its own prospective discriminator. No sound or language value receives an update from a structural score.
