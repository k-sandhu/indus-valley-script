# Post-outcome uncertainty and coverage amendment

Recorded 7 October 2026 after `main-2026-10-07-v1` completed. Status: **Exploratory**. The frozen results and their code are preserved. The following additions change uncertainty estimation and add descriptive context; they do not replace the primary permutation endpoint.

The site-stratified bootstrap often omitted a rare object type at a sampled site. Only 1–3 of 499 replicates retained all cells in three site-balanced frequency comparisons. Those intervals are unusable. The supplemental sampler resamples complete physical components within their selected site-by-type signatures, keeping each sampled cell represented. A component's signature contains all of its selected site/type cells, so a mixed component is never split. Time analyses use type signatures only. This conditions on the sampled cell frame and its component counts; it excludes uncertainty about unobserved sites and object classes.

Sparse plug-in Jensen–Shannon estimates also showed bootstrap bias: several percentile intervals lay above the observed effect. Save both the supplemental percentile interval and the basic centered interval `2*observed - [q97.5, q2.5]`, clipped to [0,1]. The basic interval is a post-outcome sensitivity without established coverage calibration for this sparse corpus. Use permutation p-values for the specified reference, and keep this interval limitation near numerical claims. Signed length and prediction differences retain percentile intervals. The supplement also recalculates model intervals with the support-preserving sampler, conditional on the already fitted models.

Add descriptive component-presence intervals and first/last observed bounds separately for each Harappa object type. Add raw and equal-type summaries of conditional internal-bigram entropy for unambiguous 3B and 3C, with their clear-record denominators; entropy remains sample-size dependent and descriptive. These tables make object-type support explicit in the attestation and sequence results.

Add an external coverage table by site, region, exact type and direction, and a site-level literature checklist. Record match losses by exact type and length. Provide descriptive pooled/type-model scores for all 16 clear external faces where those models have at least one training record. This unpaired extension can locate unsupported candidates for future acquisition, but cannot establish an external-minus-core effect. Full record-level details remain local.
