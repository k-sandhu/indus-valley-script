# Adjudicator: settle differences from the page

Inspect the page, row context and chart before choosing a field value. Your packet shows frozen readings A and B in randomized order; the labels carry no role or model identity. Record a full row for every disputed item, including a concrete `adjudication_basis` explaining the relevant visible marks. Use candidate sets or unresolved fields when the source cannot settle a distinction.

Open only this adjudication delivery root. Do not inspect proposal files, coordinator files, original reader folders or model/run identities. Follow the same visual left-to-right drawing conventions as the readers. Preserve five-position classification blanks and inscription suffixes. Keep editorial loss spans as one modern drawing and uncertainty underlines with their glyph. Use `sign`, `hatched_span`, `loss_rectangle` or `unresolved` for unit kind. Original chart images settle high/centered ticks, stroke stacks and positional hatching; ambiguous drawings retain candidate sets.

Differences can concern target assignment, source ordinal, continuation attachment or complete-column counts as well as numeric fields and units. For a column-inventory conflict, read the full assigned column and return a complete corrected `columns` inventory. Supply full corrected rows for every item included in that packet. A double locator or false detection must be explained with `valid_printed_target=false`. Do not make a source candidate unique merely by intersecting A and B's sets.

The column inventory includes `observed_page_columns` and `unassigned_columns`. Count all visible page columns. If the count differs from the packet's detected count, record the unmatched columns and their bounds. Additional source columns require separate packets; the coordinator cannot mark this page complete from the present packet alone.

Return the supplied JSON envelope with `evidence_status=Frozen evaluation`, packet hash, provider/model/run, independence attestation, UTC times, task seconds and actual tokens/billing when available. Unknown usage remains null with a reason. Every row needs `adjudication_basis`; uncertainty and field reasons remain in the record. Main units count the numbered drawing row. `continuation_rows` lists full attached unnumbered lines with y coordinate, count, units, source observations and unresolved fields; its y coordinates match `continuation_y_pdf`. For visible gaps of at least 16 PDF points, record only the zero-based preceding unit as `{"after_unit_index":0}` in `blank_gaps`. The coordinator preserves all initial readings and your return, then runs merge and coverage checks.
