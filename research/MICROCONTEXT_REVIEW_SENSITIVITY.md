# Known-period excavation-context sensitivity

Registered 2026-10-01 23:55:28 UTC (file creation timestamp) after independent review and original outcomes, before the additional run. Exploratory only.

Unknown period (`-`) is a missing-data class, not a known common archaeological date. Repeat all four original excavation-context variants with only nonmissing period metadata. Retain area, period and exact-length strata and the same 9,999 permutations. Report every variant. A curatorial period label remains an imperfect dating control. The unique-text version discards every text that repeats in the selected population; it asks about singly attested texts and is not the same estimand as the full population.

The original weighted null additionally assumes individual block labels are exchangeable despite duplicate production cohorts. Inverse text-frequency weights and exclusion of identical-text pairs do not repair that dependence. Interpretation should rely on the singly attested sensitivities and remain limited to recorded excavation contexts.
