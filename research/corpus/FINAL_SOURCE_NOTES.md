# Source inventory and limits for the final report

The reliable contribution of the corpus work is a pinned observational dataset, primary-source provenance, and explicit controls on graphic uncertainty. It does not assign a language, sound, lexical meaning, or numerical value to an Indus sign.

| Resource | What is available | Principal limit |
|---|---|---|
| `data/normalized/lipi_observed_faces.jsonl` | 5,680 observational export rows; raw strings, physical CISI identities, object/site metadata and separate normalized direction | Modern derivative export, no verified reuse license; translation and decipherment columns excluded. Numeric families are safe only within this pinned export. |
| `data/normalized/mayig_faces.jsonl` and `mayig_lines.jsonl` | 179 M1–M199 unicorn seal faces, 186 annotated lines; 94 faces after explicit P000 and zero-damage/uncertainty filtering | A second annotator of the same CISI photographs, not new archaeological evidence. P000 can mean an unknown span, not one glyph. |
| `mayig_wells_class_crosswalk.json` | 396 P-class records, 498 Wells IDs in direct compatibility arrays | Many-to-many catalogue compatibility, not certified fine-code conversion or transitive equivalence. Whole-class arrays discard feature information. |
| `primary/koskenniemi_parpola1979.pdf` | Original 180-page Helsinki PDF; stable-code chart, plotted corpus, editorial conventions; official portal CC BY4.0 | Raster drawings rather than a machine-readable coded corpus. The source-only fresh20 reference SHA256 is `061130347c5feb52acb79427e672fade16b553e10aab00fb6c1fca493172e279`. It remains unchanged after source chart reinspection. The internal consistency audit validates selection, crop hashes, and cited chart coordinates; it does not independently validate palaeographic assignments.

A subsequent source-only acquisition audit found18 of20 fixed fresh20 crops cut into their target row's upper ink (14 clear,4 marginal). Fourteen also include the following row's ink. All20 PNGs reproduce their frozen metadata exactly. Fixed row-spacing drift therefore confounds an interpretation of the original failure as purely glyph-shape generalization. `primary/font_fresh20/geometry_audit/` preserves the diagnosis and source-only clean bounds for a labelled post-reference development sensitivity. No frozen score or reference was changed.

Subsequent original-source numerical reinspection corrects this agent's PDF20 c2r1 from359 to369 and c4r13 from350 to360. At c4r21, literature prefers465 while this annotator still favours486; this annotator preserves the candidate set `[465,485,486]`. The historical gold files remain unchanged, and the explicit overlay is `primary/chart_complete/subsequent_adjudication.json`. These adjudications followed comparison and must not improve a previously frozen benchmark silently.

The final consolidated numerical reference contains687 distinct expected chart cells and16 lower-code ambiguities. `primary/chart_complete/consolidated_atlas_audit.json` verifies current source/adjudication overlays, source pins, preservation of the frozen chart and fresh20 gold, and exact original-source pixels for all687 atlas images. It reports no coverage, metadata or pixel-provenance mismatch. This verifies source correspondence rather than expert reading accuracy; the images remain context crops.

The independent-annotator structural test can use direct P→W candidate events. It measures compatibility with a model trained on another transcription of largely the same source tradition; it is neither fine-code transcription accuracy nor an independent archaeological discovery. Matching by physical CISI identity and exclusion of repeated whole-text components are necessary. Direction was checked from source photographs: stored visual left-to-right normalized layouts are reversed for computational right-to-left lists. Multiline/multisided concatenations require separate boundary treatment.

The fish-adjacent primary images distinguish short and tall paired bars, including both on M91. They expose catalogue disagreements on M140 and M62, but they do not assign numerical coefficients or establish a metrology. H2238's middle face has three short bars and a diamond-like unit; the opposite long-bar count remains unresolved. H697B has a primary-author reverse photograph; H697A's proposed substitution frame still lacks an acquired primary photograph. Current H904 matches the coarse proposed three-long/U versus two-long/circles pattern and J213(D)477 metadata, but its identity with historical Fuls2020 ICIT3786 remains unconfirmed without Figure16.

No new unattested exactly-one-glyph restoration passed the primary-photo constraints. M61 is an uncertain historical reading and M62 is catalogue recoding. The lack of a suitable case is a documented negative result, not missing model output. No open independently coded full Mahadevan/Wells/EBUDS corpus was acquired from the searched primary supplements.

Full provenance, licensing, conventions and negative acquisitions are recorded in `CORPUS_REPORT.md`, `primary/1979_extraction_assessment.md`, normalized source definitions and audit JSON files. Raw sources and historical annotations have been preserved.
