# Primary stroke-height audit

The fixed frame contains every clear, single-line Mayig seal in the pinned subset having a W002-compatible or W032-compatible stroke class next to a fish-family class. It comprises 13 physical objects and 14 adjacencies; M46 has a fish on both sides of one pair. The frame and source code candidates were known during observation, so this is an AI source audit rather than a blinded expert transcription.

Unretouched CISI1 impression photographs support ten clear short-pair cases, two tall-pair cases, and one short-pair case with unresolved graphic grouping (M43). M91 contains both tall and short paired bars in the same inscription, although only the tall pair met the fish-adjacency selection. Approximate pair-to-adjacent-fish height ratios are 0.291–0.500 for the short cases and 0.883–1.139 for the tall cases. These are relief/shadow pixel bounds; they are not measurements of the artifacts. Conservative edge uncertainty remains separated in these selected examples. No confidence interval or corpus-wide two-class accuracy is asserted.

The main catalogue discrepancy is M140: Mayig P145 lists full-height W032, while Lipi W002 and the primary impression show two short upper-band bars. The raw transcriptions remain unchanged. A later missing-slot audit also finds source short bars at M62 where Lipi uses W032, illustrating that neither annotator can be treated as universally correct.

`selection.json` preserves the fixed frame; `band_manifest.json` preserves source locators, crop coordinates and hashes; `observations.json` records the manual observations and approximate pixel bounds. The scripts reproduce the selection and rendering. These are local research excerpts from the official IGNCA scan, whose reuse license was not explicitly established.

Height, count and adjacency are graphic facts here. They do not establish numerical values, grammatical roles, sounds, meanings, or whether these forms were distinct linguistic signs rather than allographs.
