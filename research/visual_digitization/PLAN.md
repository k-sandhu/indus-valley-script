# Primary plotted-glyph digitization prototype

Started 2026-10-02 at the file-creation time recorded in the final manifest, after viewing the 1979 sign chart and corpus pages. This is an exploratory acquisition method, not decipherment.

The 1979 corpus uses a standardized plotted glyph font. Extract image templates from its sign chart, preserving the lower stable Finnish code and upper rank as distinct fields. Use geometric template matching to propose glyph identities in corpus rows. Conventional OCR may locate numerical labels but may never be used to turn glyph-shaped OCR punctuation into sign tokens.

Preserve source hash, page/crop coordinates, candidate alternatives, segmentation and matching scores. Missing/damaged/uncertain symbols and inter-side or line blanks require separate annotation. A printed row is not necessarily one original text line. Do not add provisional sequences to the statistical input.

Acceptance requires independently checked numerical template labels, independently checked glyph segmentation, and manual comparison of sampled proposed sequences to the chart and primary source. Calibrated error rates must accompany any release. A prototype that only recognizes a few obvious glyphs is an extraction demonstration, not a completed independent corpus.
