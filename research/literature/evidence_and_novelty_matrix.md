# Evidence and novelty audit

Version 3, updated 2 October 2026. This is a research audit, not a decipherment claim. “Observation” means a corpus result or documented artifact relation; “interpretation” means the named author's proposed explanation. Statistical order does not uniquely identify language, semantic category, or phonetic value. Work appearing in 2026 as a preprint or independent project is labelled accordingly. No such claim is treated as established merely because it is recent.

## Research implications

No replicated position class should be presented as a new decipherment. The promising distinct contribution is a combined artifact/variant/dependence audit that states exactly which semantic models its observations exclude. An improvement in held-out prediction is a structural result until an independently known referent connects a sign or productive sign sequence to a specific meaning. A phonetic reading needs stronger anchors still. A failed null comparison or unresolved graphic identity remains part of the result, rather than being omitted to preserve an attractive candidate.

## Reproducibility and access

The metrological/numerical source audit is in `metrology/README.md` and `metrology/numerical_source_audit.md`. The reported H370–H372 capacities are prior fitted examples, with no independently eligible same-module held-out vessel. Their approximate40.4L per graphic stroke cannot distinguish the numerical value of a stroke from the litres per numerical unit: only the product is identified. The4.04L standard is obtained after imposing a ten-valued long stroke, rather than independently fixing ten. Preserve the unresolved excavation-number mismatch between the catalogue and the dimensional table, and the author's commodity-specific scope.

Fuls's exact numerical equations and proposed10/8/6 values are published prior. Exact algebra verifies the solution conditional on the equalities and assumed ranges; original M331 photographs verify coarse marks and surfaces but supply no addition, total or commensurate units. The historicalICIT3786 identifier is not the current corpus's object with that number. No numeral/phonetic decipherment follows from this audit. The useful contribution is explicit premises, graphic-coefficient sensitivity, source identity and a frozen external prediction requirement.

The original1979 chart's numeric-label audit is under `chart_complete/`:175 owned cells on PDF20 and all337 occupied cells on PDF21. The independently entered full PDF21 table was saved before post-comparison adjudication; fresh20 validation remains separate. Current source reinspection preserves fourteen lower-code ambiguities onPDF21 afterlaterduplicate-label reinspection and all original crops. This is a numeric reference audit; it does not certify glyph identity, allography or a sign's value.
