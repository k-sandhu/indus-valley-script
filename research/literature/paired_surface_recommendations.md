# Paired-surface constraints and what they can establish

Prepared 1 October 2026. These are research constraints, not deciphered readings.

The copper-tablet substitution argument predates this project. Parpola first analyzed this category in 1975, presented the updated typology in 1992, incorporated it in his 1994 book, and published the standalone chapter in 2008. His Fig. 1 contains 46 selected types and 217 classified tablets. The observations support a relationship between label and reverse device; literal lexical equality is an additional hypothesis. [Parpola 2008](https://tuhat.helsinki.fi/ws/portalfiles/portal/127257447/Parpola_A_2008._Copper_tablets_from_Mohenjo_daro._ECL_During_Caspers_Vol.pdf).

The strongest potential negative constraint is Parpola's classification of the C5 and C6 reverse devices as one pictogram opposite the goat-family and archer-family labels. This only excludes a universal literal animal-species mapping if the devices are independently shown to be the same relevant sign and the pictures depict distinct literal referents. Mahadevan disputed the broad sign-124 grouping because outer leaf/person elements and internal feet distinguish forms. The 2005 drawings assign M600 and M604 comparable leaf-ended forms with separated internal marks, but the available photographs do not securely resolve every internal stroke. See `copper_variant_adjudication.md`. Shared divinity, institutional identity, generic category, or context-sensitive use remain possible. Religious explanations should be attributed to Parpola; administrative explanations remain alternatives.

## Minimal inferential ladder

1. **Artifact association:** a particular inscription and reverse device coexist on authenticated objects.
2. **Reproducible class association:** the pairing recurs on independently made artifacts, not merely multiple photographs or casts of one object.
3. **Functional interchangeability:** two reverse devices occur opposite the same complete label within a controlled object class. This supports an interchangeable role, not necessarily identical lexical meaning.
4. **Semantic category constraint:** recurrent many-to-one substitutions rule out an overly narrow interpretation and identify a latent category without assigning a word.
5. **Lexical or phonetic value:** requires another anchor, productive substitution across unrelated labels, or independent linguistic evidence. None follows from steps 1–4 alone.

## Concrete tests worth running

- Build an artifact-level graph whose edges are exact complete inscription matches, same reverse sign, same reverse motif class, or partial reconstruction. Keep those edge types separate. Compute the smallest grouping of motif classes required by each proposed sign interpretation.
- Reject a one-to-one literal animal-name model only if one securely identified sign connects two securely distinct motifs. Count CT05/CT07 as one shared-sign test, not 37 independent confirmations; classify unresolved feet and leaf/person differences separately.
- Evaluate whether the entire front label, a subphrase, or the last sign predicts the reverse class on a leave-label-family-out split. Predictive performance within repeated copies of one label tests replication, not compositional interpretation.
- Preserve object support and size classes in a permutation null. In Parpola’s typology, A/C types often carry signs on the reverse while B types usually carry images; a free permutation can therefore mistake object-class effects for semantics.
- Treat mirrored labels as the same sequence only after photograph inspection. M599/M600 are the reversed-writing counterpart of the goat family in Daggumati/Revesz’s primary analysis. [Daggumati and Revesz 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-021-00713-0).
- Test candidate mappings against every compatible group and report the residual many-to-one relations. A useful result may be a disproved semantic assignment rather than a new reading.

## Harappa quantity-slot counterpart

Yadav 2013 pp. 93–95, Fig. 12 already identifies a fixed M77 inscription `(48,342,176)` opposite alternatives `(89,328)`, `(87,328)`, `(95,328)` and occasionally `(267,402,149)`. The three similar two-sign alternatives do not appear opposite one another. Three-sided exceptions and site/type concentration are documented. This is prior evidence of face-specific roles; a new result must extend or rigorously challenge it. [Yadav 2013](https://www.harappa.com/sites/default/files/201311/Nisha%20Yadav_Scripta%202013.pdf).

Rao’s ration-token proposal and Mukhopadhyay’s license-slab proposal explain overlapping tablet evidence differently. Neither is a known reading. A fixed-label/variable-quantity schema is compatible with rations, fees, offerings, or standardized entitlements. [Rao 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.00049), [Mukhopadhyay 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-023-02320-7).

## Novelty boundary

An independent September 2026 project already reports seven copper image/sign equations, eleven repeated scene-associated labels, fifteen scene-associated pairs, and failure of associations to transfer between incised and moulded registers. This page is unreviewed; its claims need auditing, but repeating them is not a novelty claim. [Bourdeau 2026](https://dbourdeau.github.io/cyphersolver/indus.html).

The potentially substantive contribution is an explicit, source-adjudicated constraint graph that includes uncertainty, object-family dependence, and many-to-one exceptions, with transparent semantic falsification tests. It should be described as a constraint advance only after its new empirical results are known.
