# Source-review retrospective

The user's approximate date and spelling were enough to locate Steven C. Bonta's channel. The watch pages confirmed twelve numbered lectures and exact upload dates. Search results also located the 2023 and 2024 editions of his partial-decipherment proposal. Crawl dates were not used as publication dates.

The YouTube transcript panel and transcript-export method did not return usable text. The player loaded automatic English captions, which were read from its observed caption response. Those captions supplied timestamped passages in the browser session. They were not saved into this tracked package. Caption errors affect proper names, language names and Sanskrit words. The notes retain this limitation and avoid assigning catalogue sign identities from incomplete text. The Academia page's extracted article text omitted sign glyphs; a full figure-by-figure comparison remains open.

The review followed the proposed chain from fields to weights, anchor values, dictionary cross-checks and historical interpretations. A comparative grammar source contradicted the categorical absence of Dravidian noun compounds. The reported ICIT check was recorded as an attempted validation step, with unresolved object overlap and selection details. No chance baseline or new corpus test was run.

The review preserves links and authored paraphrases; it adds no third-party paper or caption payload to Git. Frozen research endpoints were not changed.
