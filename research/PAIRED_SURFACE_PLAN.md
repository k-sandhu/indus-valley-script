# Paired-surface and numeric-module investigation

Created: 2026-10-01 23:28:24 UTC (filesystem creation time). Exploratory extension following initial corpus inspection; not a preregistered discovery.

Primary literature already describes Harappa objects with an invariant text on one side and alternative stroke-group plus simple-U expressions on another (Yadav 2013; Rao 2018). We will formalize these as physical-object constraints and extend the audit to the available observation corpus.

## Questions

1. Which complete sign sequences occur together on distinct surfaces of the same object, counted once per physical object rather than once per pair of repeated surfaces?
2. Which main-face templates pair with multiple stroke values? Do two unequal values ever co-occur on one object?
3. For the independently shape-identified two/three/four full-height strokes (W032/W033/W034), does the associated physical object's geometry vary with stroke count after conditioning on the other-face template and object class?

The third question is a possible external metrological check, not an assumption that volume is value. Dimensions are observational but may be incomplete, error-prone, rounded, or determined by carving requirements. No proportionality is required by a voucher/denomination hypothesis, so a null relationship cannot disprove numerical function. Width can respond mechanically to stroke spacing; thickness offers a less direct inscription-layout confound.

## Scope and controls

- Use complete, single-line, known-sign faces; report original-artifact direction and preserve mirror-order numeric expressions as separate variants.
- Count unique physical-object families; identify repeated CISI IDs and distinguish absent catalog IDs.
- Restrict scalar mapping to W032=two, W033=three, W034=four, independently documented by glyph descriptions and primary M77 examples. These numbers are counts of strokes, not phonetic readings.
- A numeric expression is exactly (stroke-group, W700 simple-U), or its mirror-order equivalent for graph membership. Do not conflate simple-U W700 with terminal jar W740.
- Keep numeric expressions on different faces separate from adjacent numerals within a text.
- Identify the main-face template as the longest complete nonnumeric inscription, stable source-ID tie-break.
- Report missing, conflicting, and implausible dimensions, and every exclusion.
- Test geometry only in template/object-type strata with more than one stroke count and at least five physical objects.
- Use within-stratum permutations for three dimensions; apply Holm multiplicity control. Describe this as exploratory evidence.
- Report log dimensions and raw stroke count effects, with no conversion of volume to mass or commodities.

## Evidential interpretation

Repeated categorical text with a variable quantitative-shaped expression provides a field-role constraint. It does not name the category or establish whether the scalar is a ration, fee, denomination, count, rank, or other code. A compelling decipherment advance needs an external observation that distinguishes those interpretations.
