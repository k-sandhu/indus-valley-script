# Indus script research plan

Started: 2026-10-01 23:07:54 UTC. Minimum research duration: four hours (until 2026-10-02 03:07:54 UTC).

## Objective

Seek a substantive, reproducible advance toward decipherment. A result must distinguish observable sign structure, archaeological function, language identification, sound values, and translation. No translation will be represented as established without independently testable grounding.

## Work streams

1. Acquire pinned public corpora and audit transcription conventions, numbering, missingness, object families, licensing, and orientation.
2. Audit primary scholarship and recent computational claims to prevent restating known results as discoveries.
3. Test explicit, falsifiable structural hypotheses under controls for sign frequency, position, length, duplicate inscriptions, site, and artifact type.
4. Seek external archaeological grounding for sign classes; assess whether candidate functional interpretations predict independent data.
5. Reproduce the strongest result with sensitivity analyses and an independent critical review.

## Evidential rules

- Treat numerical IDs as catalog labels, not phonetic values.
- Keep separate numbering systems separate unless a verified crosswalk exists.
- Preserve damaged, ambiguous, multiline, and complex graphemes in raw data; document exclusions.
- Reserve independent evaluation records before model fitting; group artifact variants and identical sequences to reduce leakage.
- Distinguish exploratory findings from confirmatory tests; use appropriate nulls and multiplicity controls.
- Report failed hypotheses and data limitations.
- Statistics alone cannot identify a language or fix a sign's sound or meaning.

## Deliverables

Pinned corpus provenance, executable analyses, machine-readable outputs, a clear research report, and a prioritized falsification agenda. The report will state the actual elapsed research duration and whether any result qualifies as a decipherment advance.
