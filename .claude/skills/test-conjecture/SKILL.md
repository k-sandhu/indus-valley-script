# Test conjecture

Read the relevant card, [conjecture rules](../../../knowledge/conjectures/README.md) and, for inscription corpus tests, [two-stage design](../../../experiments/2026-10-12-conjecture-shots/DESIGN.md). Follow the discovery and confirmation split or the card's frozen withheld-sample and prospective rules. Record every result in your owned package and propose the card's credence and record update for maintainer integration. Keep the frozen outcome and kill condition visible.

For inscription-corpus shots, use `python -m bench shots` to register the whole round and freeze every participant before confirmation opens, check the spending ledger and record prior exposure. Meaning maps measure discovery reliability, re-split variance, plausible-effect power and a rank or component-influence criterion before freezing; site construction strips identity and order proxies. Attach the relevant bench result and one joint credence rule for predictions sharing objects. For archaeological, urban, archaeobotanical, comparative or historical conjectures, freeze the sample, coding rules, controls and joint update rule before opening new material.
