# Reuse and publication status

The repository's research record is published under the owner's explicit October 11 request. Public visibility does not adopt a project-wide open-source or open-content licence for the newly authored material. Existing source-specific licences and notices remain in force; see [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and the [asset review](experiments/2026-10-11-public-release/ASSET-REVIEW.md).

Third-party terms must be retained individually. Mayig records an MIT licence; the institutional 1979 chart source records CC BY 4.0. Lipi did not expose an explicit licence in the pinned source audit. Downloaded papers, archaeological photographs, catalogue plates and third-party text are not covered by any project-wide future licence.

The original source corpus and downloaded witnesses are preserved locally with manifests. The public payload retains reviewed aggregates, source pointers, licensed chart crops and bounded attributed examples. Two mixed-source/access files are excluded from public branch histories and remain in the local backup. The chart atlas retains its transcription uncertainty and crosswalk qualifications. See [source-specific review](knowledge/provenance/REDISTRIBUTION.md).

The manuscript is an AI-assisted research draft based on reproducible analyses, not a submitted, accepted or peer-reviewed article. Named authors, affiliations and publication venue are intentionally not invented. The repository records contributors by research role until human authorship is finalized.
