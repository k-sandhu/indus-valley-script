# Reuse and publication status

This private repository has not adopted an open-source or open-content licence for its newly authored material. A future release can choose appropriate terms for code and prose after authorship and source review. Absence of a licence is not a prohibition on the project owner's authorized private use, and it is not a grant of public redistribution rights.

Third-party terms must be retained individually. Mayig records an MIT licence; the institutional 1979 chart source records CC BY 4.0. Lipi did not expose an explicit licence in the pinned source audit. Downloaded papers, archaeological photographs, catalogue plates and third-party text are not covered by any project-wide future licence.

The original source corpus and downloaded witnesses are preserved locally, with manifests, rather than uploaded wholesale. Some analytic outputs contain source-derived sign sequences, identifiers and crosswalks: review them before public release. The chart atlas uses the attributed institutional source and retains transcription uncertainty; it is not an expert-certified sign inventory. See [source-specific review](knowledge/provenance/REDISTRIBUTION.md).

The manuscript is an AI-assisted research draft based on reproducible analyses, not a submitted, accepted or peer-reviewed article. Named authors, affiliations and publication venue are intentionally not invented. The repository records contributors by research role until human authorship is finalized.
