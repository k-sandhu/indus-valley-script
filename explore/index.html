  • Reviewed sign catalogue

    Fifty-six selected Sanskrit senses and twenty bounded inscription examples support inspection; all 713 earlier codes remain searchable.

  • Chart atlas

    The chart atlas shows the 687 sign drawings and numbers in Koskenniemi and Parpola's 1979 Corpus of Texts in the Indus Script, each linked to its page and marked where the reading of the chart is uncertain.

  • Indus living atlas

    A map of 115 places where Indus inscribed objects were found and 16 towns inhabited today, with reconstructions of cities and buildings and a review of which places stayed occupied. The website copy uses the offline basemap only.
