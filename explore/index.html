Additional visual packages on GitHub

These packages either load online map tiles or bundle slide decks and builder scripts; read their reports and datasets on GitHub.

  • Comparative study · 22 Bronze Age societies

    Indus beside its Bronze Age contemporaries

    Compares Mature Harappan territory, city area, population, writing and urban institutions against 21 contemporary societies of 2600–1900 BCE. The interactive map requires online tiles, so its report and data live on GitHub.

  • Simulation package · 48 finite renewal worlds

    The teaching relay (renewal animation)

    Replays 48 trajectories of our societal-persistence model showing how brick-built services and specialist skills survive shocks across generations. Includes derivation code and frozen trajectories on GitHub.
