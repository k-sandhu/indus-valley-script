Reuse and publication status

Reuse and publication status

We decided on 11 October 2026 to publish the repository's research record. Since 13 October 2026 the project's own material has carried the component licences we chose on 8 October 2026 for the public dataset release, as set out in LICENSE: code written for the project under the Unlicense; prose, manuscripts, protocols, reports, cards, registry records, aggregate results, generated controls and documentation under CC0 1.0; and material derived from the 1979 Helsinki edition under CC BY 4.0 with its attribution. Contributions are accepted under the same terms. Existing source-specific licences and notices remain in force; see third-party notices and the asset review.

Third-party terms must be retained individually. Mayig records an MIT licence; the institutional 1979 chart source records CC BY 4.0. The audit of the pinned Lipi source found no explicit licence. Downloaded papers, archaeological photographs, catalogue plates and third-party text receive no project licence. The CC0 dedication does not cover the attributed Lipi examples and key entries, the Cologne Monier-Williams fragments (CC BY-SA 4.0) or the Copernicus and ESA WorldCover derivatives (CC BY 4.0) described in the third-party notices. Precise locations of candidate archaeological anomalies from the satellite screening are withheld from the repository.

The original source corpus and the downloaded copies of primary sources are preserved locally with manifests. The public repository keeps reviewed aggregates, source pointers, licensed chart crops and a limited number of short attributed examples. Two files, one mixing Mayig and Lipi records and one containing a signed download URL, are excluded from the histories of the public branches and remain in the local backup. The chart atlas retains its transcription uncertainty and crosswalk qualifications. See source-specific review.

The manuscripts are AI-assisted research drafts based on reproducible analyses; none has been submitted, accepted or peer reviewed. Named authors, affiliations and publication venue are intentionally not invented. The repository records contributors by research role until human authorship is finalized.
