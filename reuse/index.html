Reuse and publication status

The repository's research record is published under the owner's explicit October 11 request. Since 13 October 2026 the project's own material carries the component licences the owner confirmed on 8 October 2026 for the public dataset release, as set out in LICENSE: code written for the project under the Unlicense; prose, manuscripts, protocols, reports, cards, registry records, aggregate results, generated controls and documentation under CC0 1.0; and material derived from the 1979 Helsinki edition under CC BY 4.0 with its attribution. Contributions are accepted under the same terms. Existing source-specific licences and notices remain in force; see third-party notices and the asset review.

Third-party terms must be retained individually. Mayig records an MIT licence; the institutional 1979 chart source records CC BY 4.0. Lipi did not expose an explicit licence in the pinned source audit. Downloaded papers, archaeological photographs, catalogue plates and third-party text receive no project licence. The CC0 dedication does not cover the attributed Lipi examples and key entries, the Cologne Monier-Williams fragments (CC BY-SA 4.0) or the Copernicus and ESA WorldCover derivatives (CC BY 4.0) described in the third-party notices. Precise locations of candidate archaeological anomalies from the satellite screening are withheld from the repository.

The original source corpus and downloaded witnesses are preserved locally with manifests. The public payload retains reviewed aggregates, source pointers, licensed chart crops and bounded attributed examples. Two mixed-source/access files are excluded from public branch histories and remain in the local backup. The chart atlas retains its transcription uncertainty and crosswalk qualifications. See source-specific review.

The manuscript is an AI-assisted research draft based on reproducible analyses, not a submitted, accepted or peer-reviewed article. Named authors, affiliations and publication venue are intentionally not invented. The repository records contributors by research role until human authorship is finalized.
