F-0032: Imperfect-face reference calibration

status class

area

Finding

At nominal 5%, matched face-vector references rejected 5% of populations, against 11% for record-wise references.

Evidence

  • experiments/2026-10-02-six-hour/null-calibration/noisy-faces/main/results.json, JSON field /aggregate/noisy_faces~1face_vectors/rejections/0.05/rate.
  • experiments/2026-10-02-six-hour/null-calibration/noisy-faces/main/results.json, JSON field /aggregate/noisy_faces~1record_columns/rejections/0.05/rate.

Scope

The generated observation law uses the specified paired-error mechanism. Whole vectors and individual-record columns are competing references for the saved statistic. The calibration is conditional on that known mechanism.
