F-0026: Most seal texts that recur are found at more than one site

status class
frozen evaluation
area
seals

Finding

Among 1,430 eligible seals, 65 texts occur on more than one seal, covering 174 seals, and 41 of these recurring texts occur at more than one site.

Evidence

  • experiments/2026-10-12-seal-text-identity/run-2026-10-12/summary.json, JSON field /eligible_seal_objects.
  • experiments/2026-10-12-seal-text-identity/run-2026-10-12/summary.json, JSON field /shared_texts.
  • experiments/2026-10-12-seal-text-identity/run-2026-10-12/summary.json, JSON field /seal_objects_in_shared_texts.
  • experiments/2026-10-12-seal-text-identity/run-2026-10-12/summary.json, JSON field /shared_texts_spanning_sites/all.

Scope

Recurrence is exact identity of a text of at least two signs, using the selected sign codes and one face per object. Eligible seals are complete, single-line and from named South Asian sites. Sites and motifs come from catalogues. A sensitivity analysis with second faces and merged variants is separate.
