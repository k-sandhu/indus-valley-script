F-0026: Recurring seal texts span sites

status class
frozen evaluation
area
seals

Finding

There were 65 recurring texts on 174 of 1,430 eligible seals; 41 of the recurring texts spanned sites.

Evidence

  • experiments/2026-10-12-seal-text-identity/run-2026-10-12/summary.json, JSON field /eligible_seal_objects.
  • experiments/2026-10-12-seal-text-identity/run-2026-10-12/summary.json, JSON field /shared_texts.
  • experiments/2026-10-12-seal-text-identity/run-2026-10-12/summary.json, JSON field /seal_objects_in_shared_texts.
  • experiments/2026-10-12-seal-text-identity/run-2026-10-12/summary.json, JSON field /shared_texts_spanning_sites/all.

Scope

Recurrence is exact text identity under the selected fine-code transcription, with at least two signs and one face per family. Site and motif labels come from catalogues. A second-face and merged-variant sensitivity are separate from this population.
