F-0017: Copper groups do not transfer to seal animals

status class
failed
area
seals

Finding

Copper class-specific sign groups occurred on matching-animal seals 3 times against 3.01 expected, with p = 0.62.

Evidence

  • experiments/2026-10-12-structure-first/step2-image-text/run-2026-10-12-v3/summary.json, JSON field /2d_copper_to_seals/matching_hits.
  • experiments/2026-10-12-structure-first/step2-image-text/run-2026-10-12-v3/summary.json, JSON field /2d_copper_to_seals/null_mean.
  • experiments/2026-10-12-structure-first/step2-image-text/run-2026-10-12-v3/summary.json, JSON field /2d_copper_to_seals/p_value.
  • experiments/2026-10-12-structure-first/step2-image-text/run-2026-10-12-v3/summary.json, JSON field /2d_copper_to_seals/seal_text_animal_pairs.

Scope

The permutation comparator tests transfer of copper image-specific groups to matching field animals on seals. The recorded power is higher for fivefold than twofold enrichment. The result leaves smaller effects unresolved.
