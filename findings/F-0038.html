F-0038: H-912's source depth is 4–7 feet, while the export gives its midpoint

status class
source review
area
archaeology

Finding

The primary Vats tabulation records a 4–7-foot depth range below the surface for 8650c, catalogue no. 448. The H-912 export gives 5.5 feet below the surface, equal to the range's midpoint. The primary range must be preserved; the point is not an independently measured object depth.

Evidence

The excavation audit inspected Vats's narrative pp. 58–60 and tabulation p. 348 in rendered pages. Its source observations retain the original range, collection reference and page coordinates. The normalized export value remains preserved in the local tablet register. The arithmetic midpoint is 5.5; the source audit does not establish how the exporter generated that value.

Scope

The narrative places the collection across squares H12/21 and I12/1 and describes recovery over three months. These observations identify a broad recovery series. They do not identify a closed jar, a single depositional event or the tablet's contents. Stratum labels for some other objects in the series conflict and remain separate assertions. No calendar age is derived from the depth. Verification tier: submitted.
