F-0008: At least 68 of 114 museum records match objects in our corpus

status class
source review
area
data

Finding

Of 114 online museum records of original inscribed objects that we inspected, 68 could be linked with confidence to objects in our corpus; 46 remain unlinked.

Evidence

  • experiments/2026-10-06-evidence-census/synthesis/join-summary.json, JSON field /museum_original_records.
  • experiments/2026-10-06-evidence-census/synthesis/join-summary.json, JSON field /confirmed_identifier_joins.
  • experiments/2026-10-06-evidence-census/synthesis/join-summary.json, JSON field /unconfirmed_records.

Scope

A link rests on a reviewed identifier or a published concordance. The first comparison pass found fewer links, and later links lack the full field-by-field comparison. The 68 is a minimum for the inspected records, and the 46 are unresolved within this selected set.
