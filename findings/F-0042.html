F-0042: L-164 has conflicting published jar-stopper and wooden-box interpretations

1 min read

status class
source review
area
seals

On this page

  • Finding
  • Evidence
  • Scope

Finding

Rao 1985, p. 319, describes overlapping cords on excavation object 1864's flat underside and interprets it as a flat-stoppered jar closure. CISI 1, p. 373, identifies that object as L-164. Frenez and Tosi 2005, pp. 78–79, include L-164 in their twelve-object group tentatively interpreted as wooden-box sealings.

Evidence

  • experiments/2026-10-24-office-seal-evidence/REPORT.md.
  • experiments/2026-10-24-office-source-verification/REPORT.md.

Scope

The separate L-164 source review checked five primary page images and four source hashes. The common source observation is a cord-fastened flat underside. The later authors call box shape and size conjectural and leave packaging strings versus a fastening device unresolved. Registered, calibrated underside and edge views would decide the support geometry. The review establishes a disagreement between published reconstructions and leaves the physical specimen, die attribution and office conjecture outside its scope.
