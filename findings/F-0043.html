F-0043: Rao assigns Plate CLXI F to two different Lothal objects

status class
source review
area
data

Finding

Rao 1985 assigns Plate CLXI F to excavation object 1864 on printed p. 319 and to 1830 on printed p. 325. CISI 1, p. 373, distinguishes them as L-164 = 1864 = LTH SRG 1232 and L-161 = 1830 = LTH SRG 1195. The inspected sources leave the photograph's object identity unresolved.

Evidence

  • experiments/2026-10-24-office-seal-pass3/REPORT.md.
  • experiments/2026-10-24-source-review-pass3/REPORT.md.
  • experiments/2026-10-26-object-pilot-review/REPORT-v2.md.

Scope

The third-pass source review, Lothal row, checked the contradictory references. The object-pilot review v2, section Register and unresolved identities, inspected fresh renders and the thematic index. Neither source selects the correct panel identity. The warehouse, layer and phase fields beside p. 325's entry cannot be transferred securely to L-164 through this panel. An original labelled negative register or specimen card would decide the binding.
