A finding card records a result, its exact evidence, the population it covers and its status class. Start with a theme, then follow a card to the versioned output or primary-page source check. Counts and conclusions keep the date and scope of the original result.

The status vocabulary distinguishes verified computations, evaluations frozen before their reported outcome, exploratory refinements, failed tests, source reviews and synthetic controls; proposed, superseded and record classes preserve plans and research history. Verification tiers describe contributor review separately. The project's interpretations live in the conjectures and working model.

Sign order and prediction

Preceding signs help predict a text, and closing positions draw from a smaller sign stock. Restoration accuracy and probability loss measure different properties, so both remain visible here.

  • F-0001: The previous sign helps predict the next one

    verified computation

    Knowing the previous sign improves prediction by 0.848 bits per noninitial sign across 1,596 distinct texts.

  • F-0002: Two-sign context restores hidden signs better than one-sign context

    verified computation

    Two-sign context ranks the hidden sign first in 37.27% of 8,009 artificial gaps, against 34.59% for one-sign context.

  • F-0003: The two-sign model gives the correct sign less probability

    verified computation

    Probability loss worsens from 5.1194 to 5.2472 bits per hidden sign when the restoration model uses two signs of context.

  • F-0004: Choosing by probability loss returns the one-sign model

    exploratory

    All five validation folds choose the one-sign model when smoothing is selected by probability loss, retaining 34.5861% top-one accuracy.

  • F-0022: Indus texts begin with many different signs and end with few

    frozen evaluation

    Initial-minus-final sign entropy is +0.260 across 1,859 distinct texts, against −0.017 to +0.018 under within-text shuffling.
