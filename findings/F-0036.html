F-0036: The vessel-and-fish tablet in Parpola's 2010 Figure 6 is H-912 B

status class
source review
area
data

Finding

Figure 6 in the acquired version of Asko Parpola's 2010 A Dravidian solution to the Indus script problem matches H-912 B on CISI 2 printed page339. That paper's caption and prose call the object H-902. The H-902 photographs on the preceding page show a different tablet.

Evidence

Source observations S2–S3 give the figure and plate locators. The source note records the distinguishing signs and source SHA256. The downloaded paper, catalogue pages and image crops remain local. The pinned corpus already separates both objects, so no frozen sequence or score changes.

Scope

This is a bibliographic object-reference correction for the acquired paper version. It was checked visually by one AI reader and remains at the submitted tier. Its proposed four-vessel-unit reading has a separate conjecture and physical test.
