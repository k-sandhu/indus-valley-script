F-0007: 166 of 493 checked CISI volume 1 numbers have no corpus match

status class
source review
area
data

Finding

Of 493 numbers in 19 sections of volume 1 of the Corpus of Indus Seals and Inscriptions (CISI), for Gujarat and north India, 327 matched identifiers in our corpus and 166 did not: 75 in pottery and graffiti sections and 91 in sections for seals, impressions, tablets and other objects.

Evidence

  • experiments/2026-10-06-evidence-census/synthesis/coverage.json, JSON field /summary/CISI1_BC_entries.
  • experiments/2026-10-06-evidence-census/synthesis/coverage.json, JSON field /summary/CISI1_BC_matching_keys.
  • experiments/2026-10-06-evidence-census/synthesis/coverage.json, JSON field /summary/CISI1_BC_absent_keys.
  • experiments/2026-10-06-evidence-census/synthesis/coverage.json, JSON field /summary/CISI1_BC_absent_graffiti_or_pottery_sections.
  • experiments/2026-10-06-evidence-census/synthesis/coverage.json, JSON field /summary/CISI1_BC_absent_seal_impression_tablet_other_sections.

Scope

The census compared these sections with a fixed version of the Lipi export, our working corpus, accepting exact matches and source-recorded aliases. An unmatched number may be an object under another number or a missing one. The counts cover only these sections.
