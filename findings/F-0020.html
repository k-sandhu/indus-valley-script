F-0020: Mean segmentation accuracy at Indus scale

status class
frozen evaluation
area
methods

Finding

The best mean boundary F1 was 0.531 for the Dirichlet-process segmenter, against 0.485 for a boundary everywhere.

Evidence

  • experiments/2026-10-12-structure-first/step5-rehearsal/run-segmentation-2026-10-12/summary.json, JSON field /mean_boundary_f1/dp_unigram.
  • experiments/2026-10-12-structure-first/step5-rehearsal/run-segmentation-2026-10-12/summary.json, JSON field /cohorts.

Scope

F1 combines boundary precision and recall against edited word divisions. The mean weights the four cohorts equally; it does not estimate Indus word-boundary accuracy. The recorded cohorts omit the acquired Linear B sample.
