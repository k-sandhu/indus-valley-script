F-0023: In Ur III seal legends, edge asymmetry reverses from signs to words

status class
frozen evaluation
area
comparative

Finding

In Ur III seal legends, edge asymmetry (normalized entropy of the first unit minus that of the last) was −0.204 counted by sign and +0.079 counted by word.

Evidence

  • experiments/2026-10-12-structure-first/step6-language/run-2026-10-12/summary.json, JSON field /profiles/sumerian_seals/sign_level/edge_asymmetry/asymmetry_initial_minus_final.
  • experiments/2026-10-12-structure-first/step6-language/run-2026-10-12/summary.json, JSON field /profiles/sumerian_seals/word_level/edge_asymmetry/asymmetry_initial_minus_final.

Scope

The sign and word sets differ by one eligible legend. Because the asymmetry reverses with the unit, language comparisons need corpora matched in unit and genre.
