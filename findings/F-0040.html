F-0040: H-1657 has a published late Period 3C floor context

status class
source review
area
archaeology

Finding

HARP slide 56 places H-1657 on the floor of Room 591 in Trench 43 and assigns it to late Period 3C. The 2010 manuscript's PDF page 3, footnote 4, supplies the catalogue-to-excavation identity link. This was the sole new object-context assertion in a five-case pass.

Evidence

  • experiments/2026-10-24-harappa-context-pass2/REPORT.md.
  • experiments/2026-10-24-harappa-context-pass2-review/REPORT.md.

Scope

The separate source review checked the original identity footnote and seven primary captions. Its reviewed scope is the identity and published floor attribution. The adjacent Period 4 vessel has a different key, H99/8763-503. The caption and manuscript belong to the same excavation lineage. Lot 8796's floor and wall sequence, phase argument and dated-sample link remain needed before this attribution can supply an independently established chronology label; no manufacture date was recovered.
