F-0044: Tell Brak REG257 is Matthews catalogue 308 and TB1058

status class
source review
area
comparative

Finding

Matthews 1997, printed p. 265 (PDF p. 280), equates Tell Brak REG257, catalogue 308 and TB1058 and describes an irregular bulla fragment with crushed grooves on its back. Eidem's Appendix B, printed p. 308 (PDF p. 323), publishes a cup-bearer legend with part of the final name sign restored. The pass admitted zero legends to the comparison corpus.

Evidence

  • experiments/2026-10-24-readable-seal-pass3/REPORT.md.
  • experiments/2026-10-24-source-review-pass3/REPORT.md.

Scope

The third-pass source review, Tell Brak row, checked the catalogue identity, carrier description and edition. Catalogue image number 257 is a different identifier from excavation registration REG257. This card records a source resolution; the originating acquisition attempt retains its exploratory status. Strict language classification, completeness and duplication against the frozen baseline remain open before this candidate can enter the readable-control population.
