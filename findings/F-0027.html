F-0027: Long seal texts do not repeat

status class
frozen evaluation
area
seals

Finding

No two of the 445 seals with six or more signs shared their complete text.

Evidence

  • experiments/2026-10-12-seal-text-identity/run-2026-10-12/summary.json, JSON field /S3_whole_text_reuse/by_length.

Scope

The count uses exact identity of complete texts among single-line seals from named sites, one face per object. It covers the recorded corpus and selected faces, not damaged texts or unselected second faces.
