F-0046: The town register separates architectural axes and building episodes at 34 towns

status class
source review
area
archaeology

Finding

The town register preserves 1,380 line and system records, 118 dated-part assertions and 620 radiocarbon assertions across 34 candidate towns. Wanzke's published Mohenjo-daro survey distinguishes nearly cardinal Moneer axes from the turned HR First Street axis, so one town-wide bearing would combine different architectural systems.

Scope

The report's Primary-source checks section cites Wanzke printed pp. 33–44 and records 35 published rows with their original gon values and north distinctions. Audit v5 checks 207 input hashes and 3,009 references; these are author record checks. Earlier independent orientation and plan-registration reviews are linked in the report, and their failed validation and error warnings remain attached to inherited values. Assertion counts include repeated uses of samples. No new radiocarbon determination or scientific orientation statistic is claimed. A surveyed wall requires its own construction-phase join before use in an orientation-date test.
