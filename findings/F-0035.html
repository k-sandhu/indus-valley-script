F-0035: Legends closing in 001, 595, 679 or 820 sit on larger seals in a held-out half

status class
frozen evaluation
area
seals

Finding

On the confirmation half, the 11 seals whose legend ends in sign 001, 595, 679 or 820, a set chosen on the discovery half before the confirmation half was read, average +0.346 in residual log face area against −0.007 for the other 571 seals of the same type, site class and length class (p = 0.021, frozen one-sided permutation test). Measured against the null mean of +0.056, the excess is ×1.35 in face area.

Evidence

  • results.json, field /P4.
  • verification.json: independent recomputation, p = 0.0220.
  • shot-a-robustness.json, field /confirmation/variants: influence analysis.

Scope

The result depends on three seals ending in 820 (M-6, M-16, M-649): dropping any one gives p between 0.066 and 0.138, and a rank version gives p = 0.087 to 0.093. A Holm correction over the shot's six scored predictions does not keep it. Sizes come from catalogue dimensions, mostly converted from inches. Report: Shot A.
