F-0030: Sealings match seals from other cities

status class
verified computation
area
seals

Finding

Of 16 matching sealings or tags, 13 matched only seals found elsewhere. Those 13 carried 4 of the 6 distinct matching texts.

Evidence

  • experiments/2026-10-12-seal-text-identity/run-2026-10-12/review-response.json, JSON field /G_S5_recount/rows.
  • experiments/2026-10-12-seal-text-identity/run-2026-10-12/review-response.json, JSON field /G_S5_recount/rows_matching_only_other_site_seals.
  • experiments/2026-10-12-seal-text-identity/run-2026-10-12/review-response.json, JSON field /G_S5_recount/distinct_texts.
  • experiments/2026-10-12-seal-text-identity/run-2026-10-12/review-response.json, JSON field /G_S5_recount/texts_matching_only_other_site_seals.

Scope

A sealing is a seal's impression in clay. The review recount corrects the original summary's count of other-site matches. A match means identical catalogue texts at recorded find sites. Whether the seal, the goods or only the text travelled remains open.
